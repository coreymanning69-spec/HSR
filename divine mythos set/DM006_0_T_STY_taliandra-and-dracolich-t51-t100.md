## NPC ACTION CLOSURE — TALIANDRA AND SOLIERE-DZAN

The city does not wait for a later author to make it real. After the divine
departure, Taliandra stays in contact with the seven Houses through the
Sanctum's ordinary channels. She refuses a throne and instead establishes a
rotating table: one speaker from each House, one open seat for the unaligned,
and one record-keeper who cannot hold office. Her first order is practical —
food stores, tunnel safety, and the return of children taken as collateral.

Korin escorts the first supply train but does not govern it. Niv leaves tools,
repair patterns, and a standing way to request help without turning the city
into a garrison. Letha teaches the new record-keeper how to distinguish a
promise from a miracle. Sera's renaming remains socially disruptive, but the
city's people make it theirs by using it when they want the Houses to remember
what changed.

This closes the missing-action problem at T100: Soliere-Dzan has a living
political response, Taliandra has a chosen function, and the next expansion is
not waiting on an unresolved decision. Future faction conflict or cultural
development may still occur, but it is a later event rather than a backlog
item.
