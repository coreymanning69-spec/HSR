# DM043_0 — SUZU HIKARI AND THE PLUM TITAN

## Retrieval role

This is Suzu's dedicated character sheet. DM022_0 retains the T4 ecosystem and power
matrix; DM039_0 owns the played TR-JP cold open. When older staging notes conflict with
this file, this file controls Suzu-specific entry geometry and characterization.

## Suzu Hikari

**Tier:** T4, mortal. Japan's modern protector-machine answer to Kiyuru's sacred
artifact line.

**Core read:** Confident, famous, careful rather than cautious, and lonely in the
specific way of someone wanted plenty and pursued never. Sera reads a small mortal
woman carrying power in her own weight class and becomes interested immediately;
Suzu's yes or no remains absolute.

**Current state:** The TR-JP-05 cold open is played. The café sketch is hypothetical
and was never played; Suzu and the Girls have not met. Time did not stop there. Suzu
remains active in the Mergeverse with Hakubai, fighting, training, and continuing her
Japan-hub relationships. Kiyuru and other T4-connected figures have spoken with her.
Awareness of the Girls has spread through much of the T4 community, while the wider
world knows the transformed Oak City contact as the **Oak City Experiment**. Any later
on-camera meeting begins from that evolved worldstate while preserving Suzu's agency
and the first-meeting register above.

## Plum Titan — Hakubai

**Program:** the **Amagatsu Initiative** — Japan's national belief-construct
program, named for the *amagatsu*, the protective effigy-doll that absorbs a
child's misfortune. The name rhymes with the mechanism: a hand-built doll standing
between the country and the rest of the weight class. **Personal name:** **Hakubai**
(白梅, "white plum") — Suzu's own name for the unit, matching its plum plating and
white calligraphy.

A 120-foot plum-plated belief construct with white calligraphic designs. Suzu's
hand-built physical model is anchor and fuse: modifying the model modifies the
titan; destroying it removes the titan until Suzu builds another. The titan's
belief-intent source can match divine-scale outcomes without making Suzu divine,
infinite, or categorically equal to Sera.

The titan can recognize divinity and act on that recognition before Suzu commands
it. Its personality must remain ambiguous. Never resolve on page whether it is
sentient, a perfect projection of Suzu, belief acquiring reflex, or some mixture.

## Titan entry lock — two independent options

There are **two separate entry methods**. They are alternatives, not steps in one
chained procedure:

1. **Foot airlock:** a recessed door in the foot/ankle opens into an internal
   elevator that carries Suzu to the cockpit.
2. **Hand pickup:** Suzu steps onto the titan's flat palm; it either lifts her whole
   or takes her in a careful two-finger pinch and places her at the cockpit.

Do not write the foot door followed by a hand lift. Choose one method for a given
entry scene. The unused method remains equally canonical capability.

## Render and voice locks

- Plum, white calligraphy, bespoke rather than generic military hardware.
- Suzu remains the person in the scene, never merely the titan's operator.
- Curiosity survives surprise. Institutional handlers may be tense; Suzu does not
  inherit their fear automatically.
- The titan's uncommanded bow is a tell, not proof of a settled personality theory.
