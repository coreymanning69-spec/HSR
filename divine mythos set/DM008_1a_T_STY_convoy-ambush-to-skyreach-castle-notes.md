### Convoy Ambush to Skyreach Castle: Notes
notes: >
  This file covers the first act of HotDQ as a module inversion sequence.
  The module's crisis structure (burning town, desperate defense) is
  dissolved before it can become an event. Narrative tension shifts from
  survival to consequence — what does the Sword Coast look like after
  administration at divine tier?

  Talis the White (T154-T155) is NOT Taliandra. Different character.
  Do not conflate.

  Ember does not exist in this range. Any Ember reference is a continuity
  error.

T141: Convoy Ambush (The Shockwave) #LOC-GREENFIELDS #CONCEPT-COMBAT-OPENER #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CONCEPT-ORIGIN-CRATER-STRIKE #CONCEPT-ORIGIN-FOOT-SKULL #CONCEPT-MODULE-INVERSION
– The party intercepts the 200-strong cultist caravan bound for Greenest before it reaches the town.
– Sera attacks first—her entry creates a shockwave that annihilates kobolds and cultists alike in a single indiscriminate pulse.
– Birth of the Crater Strike. Mid-arc above the column, Sera twists her back toward the ground, claps once with her arms behind her, and uses the recoil to jettison herself straight down into the densest cluster of cultists.
– The impact craters a clean thirty-foot ring into the Greenfields road; everything inside is paste, everything within fifty feet is concussed and bleeding from the ears. She didn't plan it — she just did it, it worked, and it became hers. Every later Crater Punch in the campaign traces back to this exact moment on the road north of Greenest. Only Soliera can stand inside the impact zone.
– First use of Foot→Skull as execution method. After the shockwave settles, Sera walks the perimeter of her own crater, barefoot in the kicked-up dust, finding cultists who survived the impact but can't stand anymore — broken legs, ruptured organs, eardrums gone, eyes still tracking her.
– She doesn't draw, doesn't Flick, doesn't bother. Small pale foot, skull, flesh, ground. No skull. Move to the next one. Repeat. Drizzt watches from horseback at the treeline and doesn't interrupt; the math of what he's looking at has settled in him by the third or fourth stomp. The module's HotDQ opener — scripted as a desperate Greenest civil-defense crisis — gets resolved thirty miles up the road as a roadside processing event. Two hundred bodies, one crater, one set of footprints walking out of it.

T142: Blue Dragon Encounter (The Halt) #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-FEAT-FLIGHT-STOP #CONCEPT-DRAGON-SURRENDER #CONCEPT-DRAGON-COERCION #CONCEPT-ORIGIN-DRAGON-RECRUITMENT #CONCEPT-INTERROGATION-BEAT
– A young blue dragon, coerced by the cult, swoops in. Soliera halts it midair with a silent gesture. Drizzt speaks with the dragon, convincing it to surrender and depart.
– Sera is already at the landing zone when the dragon arrives — she ran ahead from T141's crater. The dragon opens with "WHO DARES—?!" and gets "Sera dares!" followed immediately by her launching herself into its ribcage. She hits it with her jump, breaks ribs, doesn't kill it. It goes flying and lands hard. Soliera stopped its flight; Sera stopped its attitude.
– Grounded and in pain, the dragon flinches. Sera crouches at eye level and asks whether the "slaughtering children thing" was its own idea or just the cult gig. Soliera heals it without being asked — Sera glanced at her, said "Soli? Can you…?" and it was done before the sentence finished.
– Drizzt takes the thread from there, confirms the dragon was coerced, and negotiates its release. It departs under its own power. This is the first instance of the coercion-reveal-and-release pattern that defines Soliera's stance on dragons — not every dragon is the enemy, the cult is. The prototype for every Sanctum dragon recruitment that follows.


T143: Loot and Rescue #THEME-MERCY #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-PELL #CHAR-DRIZZT #CONCEPT-LOOT-RECOVERY #CONCEPT-ORIGIN-PARTY-DOG #CONCEPT-CHILD-RESCUE #THEME-DUALITY-OF-SERA
– The caravan's treasures are reclaimed; prisoners (including a rescued child and the party's signature stray dog) are saved. Sera is seen as an angel of both terror and mercy.
– The wagons are searched and catalogued — cult treasures, stolen merchant goods, prisoner manifests all tagged for Sanctum logistics. In one supply cage: a starving dog, ribs showing, too tired to bark. Sera opens the latch and it walks straight to her without hesitation. Animals don't process the Velvet Spiral as threat — they read it as the correct order of things. It bonds equally with Pell, who immediately claims organizational custody of its feeding schedule. The dog travels with them from here forward, a small piece of domestic texture trailing a divine warpath.
– The surviving prisoners are pulled out one by one. The child is found last, locked in a transport crate. Sera's register shifts the moment she reaches them — gentle hands, hair brushed back, soft joke about being strong. The child isn't afraid. Children never are. They don't process the uncanny valley the way adults do; they just see whoever stopped the bad thing and is now warm.
– The mortals present who watched the crater and the foot-and-skull sequence are now watching the same hands hold a child. Nobody has a word for it. Drizzt comes closest — he's seen paladins, he's seen darkness in holy symbols — but this isn't either. It's genuine, which is the part that takes longest to settle. The witnesses who make it to Greenest ahead of the party carry this account with them. The town has already heard a version before the party arrives at T144, and the version is accurate. That's rarer than it sounds.


T144: Greenest Arrival (Saviors) #LOC-GREENEST #CHAR-LETHA #CHAR-PELL #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CONCEPT-SOCIAL-REACTION #CONCEPT-MODULE-INVERSION #THEME-DIVINE-WELFARE-ONSET #CONCEPT-LOGISTICS-BEAT
– The group enters Greenest as saviors—constructs bearing supplies, wounded carried in, townfolk in awe. Letha and Pell help distribute food and treat the wounded.
– The entry is processional without intending to be. Constructs at the front carrying supply loads, road survivors walking in beside the wounded on stretchers, Drizzt recognizable to anyone who knows the stories, Sera in the middle of the road with the dog at her heel. Road survivors who got to town ahead of the party have been talking for an hour already. The townspeople who line up at the gate don't know whether to cheer or kneel — they end up doing a version of both that looks like neither. The module scripted this town as a smoking ruin full of desperate defenders. It is instead an intact, nervous community that just learned the road north of it is a different kind of place than it was this morning.
– Letha reads the room on entry and moves immediately. She's a tiefling in a small Greenfields town — she's done this before, earning trust through visible competence before anyone gets comfortable with the horns. She goes to the wounded first, coordinates with the town's healer, keeps her voice warm and her register grounded. She is the translation layer between "divine logistics apparatus arriving in your town" and "people who need to feel like people are helping them." She is very specifically built for exactly this moment and she knows it.
– Pell has food distribution running within twenty minutes of arrival. No permission asked — she identifies the largest open space, points constructs at it, and works the supply manifest against the town's visible population. People stabilize faster when they're fed and given something to hold. By the time formal introductions are happening elsewhere, Pell has a line, a system, and three local volunteers who believe they are helping her organize. She will have a working household census of needs before dark, which she will hand to Niv the moment they're back in range.


T145: Infiltrator Justice (The Pointing) #LOC-GREENEST #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-KNIFE-THOUGHT-EDGE-USE #CONCEPT-VELVET-SPIRAL-CONFESSION #CONCEPT-CROWD-JUSTICE #CONCEPT-MODULE-GATE-BETRAYERS #THEME-ORDER #CONCEPT-BEAT-CORRECTION
– Sera identifies cult infiltrators among Greenest's survivors while supplies are being distributed in the town square — planted gate-openers who were going to hand the town to the convoy from inside.
– She doesn't investigate. She doesn't pull them aside. Knife-Thought Edge has been active since T114; hiding intent from Sera in T145 is like hiding fire from someone who can smell smoke at a hundred miles. She's standing in a crowd of people who just watched her crater a road and walk out of it barefoot, and she starts pointing. "Okay. You. And you. Annnnnd you." Casual. Like she's picking teams.
– They confess immediately. The Velvet Spiral is running, they know what she just did to two hundred people on the road north, and the aura of something categorically realer than them is two feet away with its head tilted and its expression cheerful. There is no version of this where they hold the line. The information comes out fast and complete.
– Sera steps back. The crowd handles it. These people were going to let their neighbors die for cult coin, some of them weren't even from here, and the town's anger has had nowhere to go since the threat dissolved on a road they didn't even see. It goes there now. This is the distinction the beat summary missed — Sera didn't execute them. She identified them and got out of the way. The community needed to do this one themselves, and she understood that without being told.


T146: Town Restoration (Divine Welfare) #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-PELL #CONCEPT-INFRASTRUCTURE-REBUILD #THEME-MERCY-AS-INFRASTRUCTURE #CONCEPT-SANCTUM-IN-ACTION #THEME-DIVINE-WELFARE-ONSET #CHAR-DRIZZT #CHAR-KORIN #CONCEPT-SANCTUM-RESOURCES-REBUILD-UNDER-NIVS-DISTANT-EYES

– Drizzt mingles with the crowd, his presence almost making the Girls seem more reasonable; "Of *course* the Drow Ranger would be beside these ... these Heroes?", etc. Letha, Pell and Korin help spread out the supplies and teach people roughly how to use the various weapons or items given to them. Sera wanders and enjoys herself playing with the children, Soliera relaxes.
– Soliera commands her constructs: homes rebuilt, walls strengthened, streets cleaned. The town experiences "divine welfare"—poverty, hunger, and fear erased in days. They continue working after Sera and Soli head towards the next beat of the Tyranny of Dragons modules.
– Soliera doesn't sequence this. Resources don't get requisitioned — they appear. Stone piles up where walls need thickening. A well that's been fouled for a generation runs clean. Constructs move without being directed at each task individually because direction isn't how this works. Niv had supply convoys repositioning before the party left the road north of town — he reads Soliera's trajectory and pre-loads the logistics. By the time constructs need timber, timber is already stacked. By the time Pell's distribution queue outpaces her cart count, more carts arrive. The Sanctum doesn't respond to need. It anticipates it, because Niv is very good at his job and Soliera's intentions are not difficult to read if you've been paying attention.
– The Girls are not supervising any of this. Soliera is somewhere being still. Sera has found something interesting three streets over, or is bothering Drizzt, or is eating something she picked up from a stall without paying because the concept didn't occur to her. Neither of them is managing Greenest's recovery because management is not their function. What the town gets isn't aid — it's an installation. Poverty doesn't improve; it stops being a condition that applies here. Fear doesn't heal; the things that generated it have been removed and something that makes fear structurally difficult has settled into the walls and streets around them. The constructs will still be learning this town's rhythms long after the party leaves. Greenest will be different a hundred years from now and it won't be because anyone maintained it.


T147: Children and Orphans (Pell's Care) #CHAR-PELL #CHAR-SERA #THEME-PROTECTION-OF-INNOCENTS #CHAR-SOLIERA #CONCEPT-SANCTUM-WELFARE-ACTIVE #CONCEPT-SERA-WITH-CHILDREN
– Pell establishes care for displaced children. Sera runs "combat games," turning training into play. Greenest's youth idolize her.
– Pell builds the system fast because she's done this before at smaller scale inside the Sanctum — identify the children, identify who has family left and who doesn't, create a physical space that belongs to them specifically, establish a rhythm. Routine is the first thing that works after a crisis. She knows this the way a carpenter knows grain. Within a day the space has rules, a schedule, and enough food that nobody's counting portions.
– Sera's version of "helping with children" is to start doing things near them until they come over. She doesn't run a program. She just starts moving — flipping, catching thrown objects before they land, running a short distance and stopping so fast the air pops behind her. The kids migrate toward it the way kids migrate toward anything that seems impossible and fun. By the second hour it's a game with invented rules that only Sera fully understands, and everyone's trying to keep up. She's slower than she could be. Not slow — just calibrated to the point where the fastest kid almost catches her once, and that almost is the thing they'll talk about for years.
– Children don't process the uncanny valley that adults do. They didn't watch her crater the road. They know she stopped the bad thing and now she's here making the air pop. The ones who grow up in Greenest carry both versions — the stories their parents tell about the road, and the memory of almost catching her in a footrace. The Sanctum will see some of them eventually. The idolization has a long half-life.

T148: Departure and Legacy #LOC-GREENEST #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CHAR-LETHA #CHAR-PELL #CHAR-KORIN #CONCEPT-PARTY-SPLIT #CONCEPT-WORLD-CHANGE #CONCEPT-HUSH-INSTALLATION-COMPLETE
– With Greenest secured, Sera, Soliera, and Drizzt prepare to move on. Letha, Korin, and Pell stay behind to help rebuild—ensuring the town's hush endures.
– Soliera doesn't do staged goodbyes. The constructs stay — that's the point, that's always been the point. The Girls leave and the infrastructure doesn't, and the town will spend the next decade slowly understanding that distinction. Letha, Korin, and Pell have their roles and their competence and they don't need managing. The partition is clean. Sera probably says something to Pell that sounds small and lands heavier than intended. Then they're gone.
– What Greenest has now isn't protection in the sense of a garrison or an alliance. It's a condition. The hush is in the walls and the streets and the constructs still moving through it, and things that generate fear have been structurally removed. Drizzt has been traveling with Soliera and Sera for several beats now and has said nothing about what he's observed in total — the convoy, the dragon, the square, the children, the departure. He doesn't say anything now either. He rides north and processes it at whatever pace a man with his particular history processes things. The road ahead still has a cult on it.

T149: Rumors Spread #CONCEPT-LORE-RUMORS #CONCEPT-REPUTATION-SYSTEM #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CONCEPT-WITNESS-ECONOMY #CONCEPT-CULT-INTELLIGENCE-FAILURE #CONCEPT-SWORD-COAST-INFORMATION-NETWORK
– Merchant caravans, road travelers, and the handful of survivors from Greenest's outer farms carry the account south and east. The stories fragment almost immediately — some accurate, some not, all of them extraordinary enough that listeners assume embellishment even when they aren't.
– What stays consistent across every version: the convoy didn't arrive. Two hundred cult soldiers and a coerced dragon went north on the Greenfields road and nothing came back. No survivors with a cult-side account exist, so the Cult of the Dragon is working from inference and missing persons rather than intelligence. They don't know what they lost it to. That's worse for them than knowing.
– Drizzt's name in the account gives it traction it otherwise wouldn't have — he's a known quantity that people can triangulate on. If Drizzt Do'Urden was there, something real happened. What he was doing standing next to whoever the small pale woman is, and what she did to the road, gets told and retold by people who weren't there based on accounts from people who mostly were at the back of the line when it happened.
– News of the divine intervention, the blue dragon's surrender, and the vanished cult force spreads rapidly across the Sword Coast.


T150: Plans for the Next Mask #CONCEPT-OBJECT-DRAGON-MASKS #CONCEPT-PLOT-HOOK #LOC-WATERDEEP #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA
— News reaches them in Waterdeep with the usual mortal delay: three reports, two contradictions, one terrified man who keeps apologizing to Soliera despite her not looking at him. The black mask trail is rumored below the city, buried somewhere in the sewer routes where cult remnants, smugglers, Xanathar contacts, and frightened opportunists have all begun stepping on each other's lies. The white mask trail points north, toward Talis the White and the hunting lodge in the Greypeaks. Drizzt listens without interrupting, measuring the difference between a mission briefing and a weather report about divine inevitability. Sera leans over the table, taps the two mask leads with one finger, and says, "So the cult has a sewer clue and a snow mask. That's honestly kind of gross branding."
