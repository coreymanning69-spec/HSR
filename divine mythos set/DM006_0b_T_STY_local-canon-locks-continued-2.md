## LOCAL CANON LOCKS (continued 2)

T74: The Drow City — Description and Atmosphere #CHAR-SERA #LOC-UNDERDARK
• The city is gothic and shadowed — carved from ebon stone, lit by faerie fire, draped in webs and ambition. The air is tense, crowded with power, beauty, and the threat of upheaval. Twelve Houses line the city walls, with a large temple to Lolth in the middle, on top of it is a magical clock lit by Fae Fire. Sera really enjoys the vibe, even though it is supposed to be one of intimidation and scale. To her it's just a pretty aesthetic.

T75: To the Matron—Doubt and Determination #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK
• Sera urges Tali onward to confront her mother. Tali finds new resolve, strengthened by the presence of the Sanctum’s leaders and her own emerging sense of worth. Combine with her walking down the dark passages, heels clicking, head held high - she felt almost worthy of it. Besides that, Sera is just so insanely powerful Taliandra has almost lost her sense of scale and danger.

T76: Approaching House Zauviir — No Opposition #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #LOC-UNDERDARK #LOC-HOUSE-ZAUVIIR
• No one stops the party as they walk through the city. Old friends stare, guards hesitate, but Sera’s poise and power freeze any resistance. They reach the estate, crossbows aimed — but Sera’s predatory grin stops anyone from firing. Conversations sputter around them, spreading down the streets. The balconies fill with eyes and ears. The top families are notified already, Taliandra notices some of the highest faces she can remember moving in near open air.

T77: Family Confrontation — Arrogance Shattered #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK #LOC-HOUSE-ZAUVIIR
• Tali’s cousins insult and undermine her. Sera slaps one, setting off panic and outrage. The tension spikes—change has come, and it is non-negotiable. This happens only once, but it's mentioned twice. Ignore that and just remember that she only slaps one cousin; and it goes into the wall and almost dies. Everyone freaks. Sera is just busted. Soliera is unmoved and waiting. Soliera makes the Matrons look weak and small.

T78: The Matron’s Appearance — Streets Watching #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-NIV-SAIFFAR #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK #LOC-HOUSE-ZAUVIIR
• The Matron emerges, the city’s eyes on the drama. Sera pushes Tali forward to claim her place, while Niv and Soliera observe. The streets are thick with onlookers — predators circling for scraps. Taliandra addresses her family openly, the concept of it going well actually occurring to her before the harsh reality of the darkness inherent within these Drow societies returned and reminded her. Soliera enjoys looking at the magical fires and light sources flickering in the distance.

T79: The Challenge — Declaration of Intent #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS
• The Matron rails at Tali for returning. Tali, finally bold, asserts her right to take over, backed by force and the support of living gods. The Matron mocks her, but Tali reminds her the city will not unite behind old power, and her companions are enough.

T80: Standoff — Sera’s Warning #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-MATRONS #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-NIV
• As the Matron and her supporters posture, Sera warns that any interference will mean instant death. The city’s rules are now set by Sera’s will. This is an interesting entrapment since it's simply Sera and Soliera enforcing what is already Drow ritual. Taliandra is somehow now allowed to actually do it without the scheming and bribes and death on her hands.

T81: Through the Halls — Shadow and Legacy #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #LOC-HOUSE-ZAUVIIR
• The group enters the ancestral halls—gothic, trappy, layered with centuries of priestly and arcane power. Sera proclaims Tali’s right to rule; Niv and Soliera survey the estate, weighing potential.

T82: Sisters of Lolth — Resistance and Rhetoric #CHAR-PRIESTESSES #CHAR-MATRONS #CHAR-TALIANDRA
• The Matron’s daughters, priestesses of Lolth, try to invoke divine right. Sera shrugs off their attacks and catches a whip. The Matron demands Tali’s motives; Tali claims her right by birth and necessity, her refusal to leave old wounds open. Sera and Soliera approve.

T83: The Duel—Combat and Consequence #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CONCEPT-MATRON-FIGHT #LOC-UNDERDARK
• Tali challenges the Matron to single combat. The Matron accepts; they fight. Tali is whipped, but wins, stabbing the Matron. Magic smoke, Lolth’s intervention, and transformation follow — the old Matron is gone, the city’s fate in flux. Lolth comes herself and adjudicates that this was a proper fight. Soliera and Sera are looking at Lolth and waiting and it's a respectful encounter. The poison from the whips/snake heads is healed by Soliera after the fight.

T84: Lolth’s Judgment—New Order Anointed #CHAR-LOLTH #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK
• Lolth herself appears through the flames, approving Tali’s victory and the newcomers’ right to reshape the city. The priestesses are left broken; rules and reality are rewritten. The Matron mother fell into the green fire, burning as she was claimed by Lolth. The city had to accept it, since it was trial by combat, but also Sera and Soliera, by default, had their hand around the entire city's throat.

T85: Aftermath — Claiming Power, Dinner Plans #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-TALIANDRA #LOC-SOLIERE-DZAN #LOC-UNDERDARK
• Sera, Soliera, and Niv joke about dinner while the city reels. Nobles gather, low houses band together. Tali is now Matron, but the city’s future is uncertain and tense. The Sanctum has been digging roads and tunnels directly to this city, which they have named Soliere-Dzan on Sanctum records.

T86: New Rule — Festivals and Fallout #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK
• Sera suggests a festival to celebrate the new order. Tali is unsure, but Niv backs the idea—a public spectacle could cement change. Meanwhile, priests and Archmages conspire in the shadows. The Festival, lights, sound and many times the foot traffic is causing many ripples through the Underdark and the miles and miles of cities and connections.

T87: Reconnection — Family and Mourning #CHAR-TALIANDRA #LOC-UNDERDARK
• Tali reconnects with her kin — grief, relief, and wary hope mix. Sera and Soliera spend the night with Niv. Morning brings the realization that the city hasn’t slept; word of Lolth’s appearance and Tali’s victory spreads. Taliandra has been busy, no sleep, working on spreading her name + the Girls + what is going on, how things are, etc. It is a very busy time but she is very confident, and for the first time in memory there was no one who planned an attack on Taliandra.

T88: New Day, New Order — Exploring After the Storm #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK
• Sera and Soliera emerge to find the First and Second Matrons watching from above. Sera commands them to come down; they obey, recognizing a new hierarchy. Face-to-face, everyone senses the city’s rules are changing. This also reflects a common theme of Sera, to her, to look down at Soliera is to be disrespectful.

T89: Equal Ground — Matron Mothers Meet the New Order #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK
• The city’s greatest powers meet the outsiders on level ground for the first time. Old postures drop; new respect and caution are born. Onlookers and travelers are openly staring, the concept of shame and shyness replaced by not wanting to miss this obviously status-changing event.

T90: Plans and Paradigm Shifts — Festival for the Future #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK
• The Matrons posture and talk; Soliera remains silent. Niv, respected even in male humanoid form, proposes a festival to cement peace. Sera is delighted; the city is confused but cannot refuse. They are worried about some of the noise and light, but there's nothing to be done now. The Matron Mothers were trying to be slightly rude, but Niv reminds them with simply his aura that he can erase them both; the only thing that literally matters to this trio is your actual strength - not that of implied force. All ants can be smushed in an entire colony.

T91: Reluctant Accord — Hollow Acceptance, Seeds of Change #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK
• The Matrons accept the festival, recognizing the futility of resistance. Sera rejoices, Soliera grins, and Niv senses deeper powers at play beneath the city. By now, two days have passed, and plenty of people are aware of what's happening in the city, how the girls have basically renamed it and claimed it, and how the city itself is experiencing the highest drive of sex and art, which is due mostly to Soliera's passive presence of just trying to expand life and Her creation.

T92: Descent — For the Forge and the Future #CHAR-NIV #CHAR-NIV-SAIFFAR #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #LOC-UNDERDARK #LOC-FORGE
• Sera, Soliera, and Niv follow the pull of an ancient forge deep below. Above, the Sanctum’s banners appear in the tunnels, physically linking the two realms. The proto-forge, webbed and cold, awaits their touch. These were some of the signals Niv saw before; he is excited to go down and see what could cause such interesting ideas and almost a cry for attention.

T93: Above — Negotiation, Below — Awakening #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-MATRONS #LOC-UNDERDARK #LOC-FORGE
• Above: The Matrons meet Tali, recognizing her authority — no one plots to overthrow her. Below: The forge stirs, resonating with Soliera’s creative spark, inviting her to shape new destinies. The Priests and such of Lolth are contented now, since they have talked with Lolth and others and understanding of their place is solidified.

T94: Duel of Creation — The Blank and the Doll #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CHAR-LOLTH #LOC-UNDERDARK #LOC-FORGE
• Below: A blank, white humanoid avatar forms in the forge, raising its hand in challenge. Soliera conjures a black twin; a silent duel of creation and will begins. Above: Tali is blunt — she was chosen, the world is changing, and even Lolth approved.

T95: Test of Wills — Construct Combat #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA
• The blank (white) construct defeats Soliera’s first (black) construct. Forge escalates, summoning a second white. Soli crafts a Hothgar-inspired barbarian warrior — he crushes the two white avatars, but the forge keeps escalating.
Eventually, a massive spider construct appears and impales the Hothgar. Sera intervenes, stopping a strike with her palm and launching the spider across the forge. The spider construct was incredibly powerful; it had one-shot the Hothgar construct, but this shows Sera's incredible density and speed. She is there before he finishes the blow, moving before he can understand it, and stops it by letting it hit her. It was too weak to move her, so it simply blew into the ground, cratering around her and cracking outward.
The forge’s power peaks — its “soul” manifests: a white-outlined, godlike proto-being. Niv translates its intent: it recognizes Soliera as a peer, not a foe.

T96: The Proto-Forge’s Sentience #CHAR-PROTO-FORGE #LOC-FORGE #LOC-PROTO-FORGE #CHAR-TALIANDRA
• Sera asks if Tali can claim the forge. Niv confirms. They summon Tali down. The forge, exposed to a Drow, shows loneliness and regret—centuries unused. It manifests a smaller, impish avatar: a quasi-AI, able to run Tali’s estate and forge operations.
The forge now serves Tali, weaving the city’s fate with the Sanctum’s. It watches the city, and later, Soliera gives it a small army of Divine Constructs to power directly. Its personality is witty and sad, but happier lately now that it has a purpose. The forge helps power various constructs and is another layer of defense for the House of Soliere, the new de facto rulers of the city (which Sera has informed everyone has been renamed Soliere-Dzan). The constructs also function as city defense, cleaning, and labor.

T97: Festival Preparations & Intrigue #CONCEPT-LORE #CHAR-JARLAXLE #CHAR-VELIN
• Several days pass. Festival plans form; Houses tense but curious. Drow from other cities and Jarlaxle's return.
Sera, Soli, and Niv roam the city, supporting Tali. Velin coordinates diplomatic outreach, explaining the Sanctum’s intentions: “This city is the example — embrace it, or be left behind.” The Sanctum has arrived now and streets are cleaned, Night vision/Darkvision potions and such are openly available and it's easy to travel in the city.

T98: Threat From Below — The Dracolich’s Plot #CHAR-PRIESTESSES #CONCEPT-BACKGROUND-LORE-REVENGE
• As the festival ignites, rumors of unrest and plotting grow. Priests and archmages from low houses plot vengeance.
Suddenly, rumbling and worms/young dragons scuttle along the cavern roof; a growing threat builds as something greater stirs below.

T99: The Dracolich Attacks #CONCEPT-FESTIVAL #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-DRACOLICH
• The festival is disrupted. Medium-sized monsters attack; Sera zips around slaughtering them.
The great dracolich — an ancient, named, CR 30 threat—erupts from the depths, devouring and destroying. Buildings collapse; panic spreads. Wyverns spread from the hole, Sera goes on a rampage, killing hundreds of them. What should have been a city ending threat barely got out of the hole they attempted to swarm from.

T100: Judgment & Ascendance #CHAR-DEASHI #CONCEPT-TALI-ARC-END #CHAR-DRACOLICH #CHAR-TALIANDRA
• The dracolich targets Soliera. Deashi materializes, intercepts its breath weapon. He erases half the dracolich with a gesture, then, with an enormous sword strike, cleaves a circle through the Underdark—ending the threat absolutely.
The city is saved, but forever changed. The arc ends with Tali’s new rule solidified, the forge bound, and the Sanctum’s influence undeniable. The city is secured and Soliera and Sera head back to the Sanctum; leaving the agents and most importantly; leaving Taliandra behind.
