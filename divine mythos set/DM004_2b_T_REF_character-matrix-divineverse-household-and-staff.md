#CHAR-LETHA

IDENTITY: Letha
VERSE: T (Divineverse, primary); referenced in Travel Arc T516+
FACTION: Sanctum (Scout/Hunt/Bard / Velin-sanctioned Reader)
ROLE: Scout | Bardic Support | Emotional Anchor | Primary Reader (Travel Arc)
STATUS: Active
INTRO: T20 (first morning, witness) | FORMAL PARTY INTEGRATION: T112 (Neverember Estate / Waterdeep expansion) | LAST: T516+ / TR

READING GAG ROLE:
- Letha is the designated Velin-sanctioned Reader for the Divineverse Travel Arc (Luskan, Giants, Coastline route).
- She reads the itinerary dossier aloud to Sera at each new location. This is her explicit logistical function in travel beats.
- In the Mergeverse, Velin or Cassian take the Reader role; in domestic Sanctum scenes, Ember fills it ad hoc.
- AI must NOT have Sera read the itinerary herself, glance at a map, or reference written text. The Reader always vocalizes it.

ORIGIN NOTE:
- Letha joined the active party in earnest during the Waterdeep expansion / Neverember Estate sequence (T112–T114).
- Her presence in earlier beats (T20–T32) was as a peripheral companion, not a full party member.
- AI should NOT place her in Underdark scenes (T34–T101) unless explicitly written.
- Letha is the first person in existence to choose **Sera over Soliera**. She joins
  primarily to be with Sera, before witnessing the morning devotions. The order is
  load-bearing: she chooses the person before seeing the fanatic. This is Sera's
  first evidence that she can be loved as a destination rather than merely as a
  route to her god, and it becomes the template for later girl attachments.

APPEARANCE:
- Tiefling female
- Red horns, tail
- Bright lavender eyes
- Wields "Sunset" — a mythic lute

ITEM — SUNSET:
- Extraordinary lute created by Soliera (T32). Member of the Blessed tier — see DM047_0.
- CURRENT MECHANICS [RULED 2026-08-09]: Sunset casts three spells simultaneously, of whatever level Letha chooses, as a single action. At Bard 20 that is three ninth-level castings at once. Range 300 feet.
- Slot cost [RULED 2026-08-09]: the triple costs ONE slot, not three.
- Save DC 30. This is a FLAT ITEM DC. It is not derived from Letha's sheet and must never be recalculated from one — a Bard 20 tops out near 24 even at divine-tier CHA 30. Store flat; recompute nothing.
- HISTORICAL, NOT SUPERSEDED (T32): as granted, Sunset stacked two or three tiered bardic effects at once and carried music 200–300 feet with magical clarity (DM005_0 line 552). The T32 record stands and is not overwritten. The distance between that entry and the current mechanics is the Blessed tier's growth property on the record — the object moved because Letha did. The retroactive-correction reading was considered and closed by Corey on 2026-08-09.

ITEM — VESPER (Working Name; Subject to Rename):
- Magnetic rail accelerator. Forged by Niv with Soliera's touch on the barrel lattice — Divineverse metallurgy through Mergeverse engineering. Niv's craft; Soliera's permission baked at the rail level.
- Member of the Blessed tier — see DM047_0. Vesper is the reference case for the permission test: it qualifies on Soliera's permission, not on authorship. Under an authorship test it would be ambiguous.
- Aesthetic: obsidian-black rails inlaid with bronze runework. Stock shaped to Letha's shoulder. Smart scope resolves friend-positions BEFORE target — civilian-blank filter clears the combat space at glance speed.
- Mechanical: no powder, no muzzle flash. Audio signature: deep bass whump on trigger pull; thunderclap downrange when the slug breaks its own shockwave. Divine-steel penetrator slug defeats dragonscale, plate, stone, and the concept of a wall.
- Range: surgical at 600 ft against soft targets (tested). Beyond that untested.
- Loading: tail-curl move-action breech load. 12-round capacity (first field test confirmed).
- Bardic Integration: Sunset's magic extends to Vesper. Letha can SING THE SHOT — bardic effects (frightened, charmed, marked, counterspelled) stack onto the round in flight. One lute-stroke, one trigger pull, one corpse with a chord still hanging in the air.
- Operational Cost: loud, not concealable. Downstream physics are blunt regardless of point-of-impact precision (first field test: bench-front three dead in under a second, but draft horses bolted because their handler was the dead man, and the cargo became the actual problem).
- Why Vesper alongside Sunset, not as replacement: Sunset is the soft instrument — area effect, room weather. Vesper is the hard answer — single target, absurd range, one breath between trigger and consequence. The two halves of Letha: the bard who walks into a room and changes its weather, and the tiefling who learned to watch doorways. Vesper lets her protect at distance.
- Source: Canon (migrated from DM036 §B, retired 2026-05-10).

ARC-STATE: #ARC-STATE-LETHA
- Early Sanctum / Expansion (T20–T140): Nervous about her differences, but gaining confidence in real time. By the end of this phase she carries herself like someone who has seen gods bleed and decided not to.
- Travel Arc / Itinerary (T516+): Fully settled. Reads the dossier aloud with dry amusement. Watches local lords squirm with visible delight. Vesper on her back, Sunset in her hands — she is the most dangerous chaperone in recorded history and she knows it.
- Mergeverse / Veyren arc (M161+): Casual, grounded, unimpressed by geopolitics. Her partnership with Veyren is built on comfort, not devotion.

PARTNERSHIP — LETHA / VEYREN (M161+, locked at M181/M182):
- Frame: PARTNERSHIP, NOT WORSHIP. Letha does not orbit Veyren; Veyren does not pedestal Letha. They calibrate.
- Casual but valued. Letha can reassure. Veyren is allowed to be uncalibrated. Neither defaults to the divine register that frames their relationships with the Trio.
- Structurally new in the corpus: the first Sanctum-adjacent pairing that runs PARALLEL to the Trio's gravity, not shaped by it. Their morning happens because they had a night, not because Soliera approved a night.
- Operational tells: Letha's blush at Sera's "edible" compliment is affection-routed, not worship-routed. Letha's tail along the stool rung is comfort posture, not ceremonial.
- Japan separation completed: Letha entered Kiyuru's orbit while Veyren stayed. The partnership grammar held through the first separation stress test; TR-JP then closed with the party returning home.
- Source: Canon (migrated from DM036 §E, retired 2026-05-10).

#CHAR-KORIN

IDENTITY: Korin | The Old Ranger
VERSE: T
FACTION: Sanctum (Pathfinder/Moral Grounding)
ROLE: Grandmaster of the Sanctum | Elder Ranger | Tracker | Party Anchor
STATUS: Active
INTRO: T22 | LAST: T234

APPEARANCE:
- Elder human male (~80 years old when met)
- Now has "perfectly formed warrior's body" with all experience retained
- Carries an obsidian-style knife (for whittling)
- Wears sleek, dark-leather armor (reforged by Soliera) — muffled, crisscrossed, AC 24
- Druid pendant

MECHANICAL BODY:
- Level 25 player-character-scale Ranger/Grandmaster chassis.
- All six ability scores are 20 after Soliera's T25 healing.
- The numbers are not a foreign grant. Soliera settles the full integral of effort
  Korin expended across eighty years — physical, attentive, judgmental, and social
  — into his body with zero entropy leakage. Reflexes are stored effort and return
  as part of the principal. His age is prerequisite; the prior decades were escrow.
- This is the mortal curve untaxed by vessel leakage, not a claim that Korin is
  divine or beyond mortal-hero space.

ORIGIN:
- Found in a city (possibly Luskan), whittling figures and teaching young men
- Bad leg, used a crutch; resigned to his final years
- Soliera made his body young in function — all cells refreshed, muscles and bones strengthened
- Kept all reflexes, concepts, knowledge — can now "sweep all four people at once"
- The Remaking adds nothing foreign: the perfect body is made entirely of Korin,
  a bookkeeping restitution of what aging took.

KEY MOMENTS:
- T22–T25: Meeting and healing
- T27: Romantic relationship with Letha develops
- T31: Badly wounded by cultist; Sera goes "deadly serious" and massacres attackers
- T32: Armor reforged; mental repertoire deepened
- T212: Leads tracking during Chuth hunt
- T337: Listed among party members on the road

QUOTE: "Korin bows, never kneels." — DM04-T231

ARC-STATE: #ARC-STATE-KORIN
- Early arc: mortal interface layer for intimidation, duty, and world-facing
  business, buffering ordinary people from direct divine contact all the way to
  Waterdeep.
- Later: steps back once the girls are universally known and semi-retires in place
  into what he loves — training people. A teacher's vocation is restored at full
  power in a perfect demonstration body, completing the founders' payout pattern.

#CHAR-TALIANDRA

IDENTITY: Taliandra Zauviir | Tali
VERSE: T
FACTION: Underdark (Matron of Soliere-Dzan) / Sanctum Ally
ROLE: Matron Mother | First Outsider Elevated | Exile-to-Queen Arc
STATUS: Active (Ruling Soliere-Dzan)
INTRO: T34 | LAST: T101

APPEARANCE:
- Drow female
- Obsidian regalia (as Matron)
- Silver staff

ORIGIN:
- Drow scout from a lesser house
- Sent to the surface as exile — "her mission more exile than assignment, sent above ground to die"
- Sera noticed her stealthy presence and brought her before the group
- Sera called her "Tali" for convenience; the party didn't care about her house

ARC SUMMARY (T34–T100):
- T34–T44: Arrives at Sanctum; treated as beneath notice
- T45–T50: Used as literal footstool by Sera; stepped on nightly
- T50–T51: Soliera pauses, steps BESIDE Tali instead of on her — divine math shifted
- T51: Invited to the table, then to the bed — accepted into inner circle
- T52–T100: Offered revenge on her Underdark city; becomes Matron with divine backing
- T100: Arc ends with Tali's rule solidified; Soliera and Sera leave her behind

STATUS AS OF T101:
- Matron Mother of the Drow city (renamed Soliere-Dzan on Sanctum records)
- Before contact the city was a modest roughly seven-House settlement. Sera
  never learned its original name; the later Soliera-centered culture grew
  downstream of her casual renaming.
- Her decision to stay behind paralyzed Soliera and Sera for nearly two days
- The Sanctum has dug roads and tunnels directly to her city

NARRATIVE FUNCTION:
- First test case of Soliera's "middle ground" developing
- Proves that exile and abuse can become elevation
- Her absence = genuine emotional wound for the divine

ARC-STATE: #ARC-STATE-TALIANDRA
- Sanctum arrival (T34–T50): Meek, terrified, steeped in shame and self-pity. Accepts being used as a literal footstool because she has decided she deserves it.
- Underdark reclamation (T51–T100): Emboldened by divine backing. Challenges her Matron Mother with quiet, resolute conviction. Transitions from victim to queen in a single arc. Her choice to stay behind — after everything — is the wound the divine never speaks of.
