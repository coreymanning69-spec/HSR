### LOCATION 05: PORT LLAST
**[MAP POSITION]** Small fishing village, south of Luskan on the coast, north of Neverwinter. On the High Road.
**[STATUS]** FIRST VISIT — Small, unimportant by most measures. On the itinerary because it’s on the road and Ember noticed it on the map and said “that one looks small and cozy.”
**[TRAVEL ACCESS]** Coastal road (partially-improved Sanctum infrastructure). One day south of Luskan.
**[WORD OF MOUTH]** Regional/road network. Small communities hear everything from passing travelers. They know the institution and trio; details vary by caravan and season.

**Setting Changes (5 — small location)**
1. Port Llast’s harbor is too shallow for anything larger than a fishing skiff or small coastal trader. The Ark cannot dock here. The girls arrive by road.
2. The fishing community here has been benefiting indirectly from improved Luskan road traffic — better goods, better prices, more travelers passing through. They are not Sanctum-affiliated but consider the Sanctum road to be a local improvement they didn’t have to pay for.
3. There is an old lighthouse on the promontory north of town. The light mechanism broke forty years ago. The lighthouse keeper maintains the tower anyway, out of habit. She is 78 years old, calls herself Marta, and has strong opinions about the North Sword Coast that she shares freely with anyone who sits still long enough.
4. A group of five young men from Port Llast have applied to become Sanctum constructs-crew — they want to work on road maintenance. Their applications are in Niv’s queue. They don’t know this yet. They will be delighted.
5. The village maintains a fishing superstition: on the third day of any month that begins with the letter F, you don’t fish. This has no divine basis. It has been practiced for four generations. No one remembers why. Ember will ask. No one will have an answer. She will sit with this for several minutes.

**Hooks & Quests (TR-12)**
* **TR-12:** Marta the lighthouse keeper saw something in the fog offshore seven days ago. Something large. Moving southeast. She didn’t report it because there’s no one to report to and she wasn’t sure it wasn’t a trick of the light. She will tell the girls if they ask. It’s relevant to Neverwinter.


### LOCATION 06: NEVERWINTER (The Jewel of the North)
**[MAP POSITION]** Central North Sword Coast, on the High Road, south of Luskan and Port Llast. The magically-heated Neverwinter River runs through it.
**[STATUS]** FIRST VISIT — Not Visited in canon. The girls have skirted this area but never entered the city proper.
**[TRAVEL ACCESS]** High Road from Port Llast. The road is not Sanctum-built here (the Sanctum road cuts east toward Mirabar before this point), but the High Road is well-maintained and Neverwinter sends patrols.
**[WORD OF MOUTH]** Detailed institutional. Neverwinter is politically sophisticated and has extensive trade contact with Waterdeep. It has detailed intelligence on the trio. Lord Neverember — who has personally been healed and blessed by Soliera in Waterdeep — has briefed his Neverwinter contacts. The city is expecting this visit and is prepared to perform its best version of itself.

**Setting Changes (22+)**
1. Neverwinter is in active mid-rebuild following the Mount Hotenow eruption of 1451 DR (roughly forty years before current T515 timeline). The eruption destroyed the south half of the city. The rebuild is ambitious, optimistic, and slightly chaotic — three different architectural styles coexisting because three different funding coalitions had different ideas.
2. Lord Dagult Neverember controls Neverwinter as Lord Protector, leveraging his position as Open Lord of Waterdeep (he holds both titles simultaneously, to the irritation of several political factions in both cities). Since his healing and blessing by Soliera in Waterdeep, he has been a quiet but consistent advocate for Sanctum commercial relationships.
3. The Neverwinter River — heated by fire elementals living under Mount Hotenow — has moderated slightly since the eruption. The river still runs warmer than any natural river should, but the temperature is no longer at the edge of scalding in high summer. Residents consider this an improvement.
4. The post-eruption rebuilding has created an unusual economic condition: no guilds to restrict trade or construction. Anyone can set up a business in Neverwinter. This has produced a gold-rush atmosphere in the merchant quarter — rapid enterprise, frequent failure, but more innovation per square block than any other city in the North.
5. Neverwinter has a formal relationship with the restored dwarven city of Gauntlgrym (deep below ground, accessed through specific routes in the Neverwinter Wood). Neverember has been brokering trade agreements between Neverwinter and Gauntlgrym — essentially civilizing the supply chain of deep-forge dwarven goods to surface markets.
6. The Blacklake District (wealthy residential) survived the eruption almost intact. It is the social center of old Neverwinter money — the estates of the surviving noble families, the rebuilt city zoo, and the eccentric elven wizard’s fortress estate (Aganazzar’s Hold, technically outside city limits but considered part of Blacklake culture).
7. The Beggar’s Nest (the historically hard-hit district) is in the deepest phase of reconstruction. The eruption compounded decades of neglect. A Neverwinter social reformer named Anya Coldwater has been organizing community rebuilding crews using a model she claims she learned from “travelers who came from the Sanctum.” She has never been to the Sanctum. She learned from a merchant who met a Steward in Waterdeep who described how the Sanctum organizes community labor.
8. The Docks District is fully operational and heavily active. Post-eruption, Neverwinter leaned into its coastal trade position harder than ever. The docks are cleaner and better organized than Luskan’s, with actual municipal oversight — a harbormistress named Telden Rau who runs the district like a military operation.
9. Neverwinter’s map situation: the city officially distributes no maps to prevent Luskan intelligence gathering. A thriving black market for city maps exists. The quality varies enormously. A Sanctum Steward in Waterdeep, acting on Velin’s instruction, once purchased sixteen black-market Neverwinter maps and compared them. Three were accurate. The rest were creative fiction. The accurate three are in Velin’s files.
10. The Neverwinter Zoo — in the Blacklake District — is one of the few intact cultural institutions from pre-eruption Neverwinter. It currently houses: several large felines from Chult, a pair of displacer beasts (heavily secured), a beholder in a specially warded glass enclosure (the public is uncertain whether this is ethical; the beholder appears to have no opinion), and a creature that the zookeepers cannot identify and that arrived during the eruption from underground.
11. The unidentified zoo creature is the size of a large dog, moves like smoke, has too many limbs for any known animal, and has not eaten anything staff have offered it in three years. It also has not died. The senior zookeeper has written fourteen reports about it. None have been answered by any biological authority.
12. Neverwinter’s precision crafting culture — famous for water clocks and colored-glass lamps — survived the eruption in the form of its craftspeople, if not all their workshops. The clock-makers’ guild (the one guild that did survive the no-guild reform, on the grounds that clock-making requires consistent standards) operates from a rebuilt hall in the upper market. Their clocks are sold across Faerûn.
13. The city maintains a sophisticated water management system — cisterns, channels, and pressure-fed fountains, all connected to the Neverwinter River’s heated flow. The system was partially damaged in the eruption and partially rebuilt with Gauntlgrym dwarven engineering assistance. Several sections are still improvised and technically unsound. The city engineers know this and are slowly fixing it.
14. Neverwinter’s light — the famous colored-glass lamps — runs on a city-maintained enchantment system applied to standard lamp oil. The enchantment produces colored light without changing heat output. Different districts have traditional colors: Blacklake is amber, the Market District is white, the Docks are blue. The color scheme was established by a civic vote in 1389 DR and has not been changed since.
15. Lord Neverember’s political position in the city is stronger since his blessing. He has attributed his recovery from a significant political and health challenge (around the Waterdeep events) to Soliera’s direct intervention. This is both true and strategically deployed. His opponents are not sure whether to mock it or be afraid.
16. The Harpers maintain a visible presence in Neverwinter — more visible than in most cities, because Neverwinter’s rebuilding chaos created information vacuums that Harper agents stepped into as “civic helpers.” Several Harper agents currently hold legitimate city administration positions. This is known to Neverember. He tolerates it.
17. Zhentarim agents in Neverwinter are fewer than in Waterdeep or Luskan but more sophisticated — they operate almost entirely through legitimate business fronts. The most significant front is a lumber consortium that controls most of the wood supply for the city’s reconstruction. This is economically important enough that exposing the front would cause significant construction delays.
18. A community of Neverwinter natives who lost everything in the eruption and did not return to the city have formed a settlement called New Neverwinter on the coast south of the ruins. They reject the official city and Neverember’s governance. They are not hostile, but they are not cooperative. They trade independently and have their own local governance. They are not aware of the Sanctum beyond the broad rumors.
19. The eruption opened several access points into the Underdark under and around Neverwinter. Most have been sealed by the city’s mage contingent. Two were too structurally complex to seal and are now guarded by a dedicated garrison unit. Things occasionally come up. The garrison handles it. They are tired.
20. Mount Hotenow — the volcano northeast of the city — is now permanently active at a low level. It does not erupt. It smokes. It glows at the peak at night. It heats the river. The fire elementals who live under it have not communicated with any surface representative since the eruption. Neverwinter’s senior mage sent a message down via a summoned fire elemental two years ago asking if they were all right. The response was: yes. No further communication has occurred.
21. The thing Marta saw offshore from Port Llast: it was a Kraken, moving through the coastal waters south of Luskan. It has not approached Neverwinter. It has been sighted twice by Neverwinter harbor patrol. The harbormistress Telden Rau has not publicized this because she does not want a panic. She will tell the girls privately because they are the only people she’s met who seem unlikely to panic.
22. Neverwinter’s children’s schools were rebuilt as a priority after the eruption — the city made a specific cultural decision that education infrastructure came before commercial infrastructure. The result is that the rebuilding-era generation (currently young adults) is the most educated cohort Neverwinter has ever produced. They are entering commerce, government, and the crafting trades simultaneously, and the city is feeling the positive effects.

**City Flavor (Street Level)**
Neverwinter smells like the river — warm, slightly mineral, with the faintest edge of sulphur on hot days when the wind comes from Hotenow. The streets are a patchwork: sections of old cobblestone that survived the eruption alternating with new-cut stone from Gauntlgrym quarries, which is slightly lighter in color and more regular in cut. The colored lamp posts haven’t all been replaced — some districts have the new glass, some still have old oil lamps, one street has both. The market district is loud with the kind of energy that comes from knowing a city is actively rising: there is visible optimism here. People here build quickly and argue about what to build next. The river runs through it all, warm enough that you can feel the temperature change standing on a bridge over it.

**Embassy Status**
NO FORMAL EMBASSY — relationship via Waterdeep. Neverember has been in informal diplomatic contact with Velin through the Waterdeep embassy. A full Neverwinter embassy is the next logical step in Sanctum diplomatic expansion. Velin considers this the most achievable near-term embassy establishment. Neverember is likely to accept. The Harpers may try to influence the terms.

**Hooks & Quests (TR-13 through TR-16)**
* **TR-13:** The unidentified zoo creature. Ember will find it immediately. It will be interested in Ember in a way it has been interested in nothing else for three years. What is it?Only Soliera or Deashi might actually know, and the answer may surprise the zookeepers.
* **TR-14:** Telden Rau’s private briefing about the Kraken. She wants to know if the girls are going to do something about it or if it’s going to be “one of those divine things where you say it will sort itself out.” This is said with more direct bluntness than most people employ with the girls. She means no disrespect. She’s just exhausted and needs an answer.
* **TR-15:** The Underdark access points that couldn’t be sealed. The garrison has been handling regular incursions. A new type of creature has started coming up — not the usual Underdark fauna. It’s organized, intelligent, and retreats rather than fighting when encountered. The garrison can’t identify it. The garrison’s senior officer wants eyes from someone more capable than city mages.
* **TR-16:** Anya Coldwater and her community rebuilding crews. She wants to meet the Sanctum people she’s been modeling her work on, without quite knowing that’s what she’s been doing. The meeting has emotional weight: someone took a Sanctum model and implemented it from scratch, on their own, out of necessity, with no support. That’s worth acknowledging.

**Parallel Threads**
* **Velin:** Pre-positioned here, arriving two days before the girls via teleport from Waterdeep. She has already met with Neverember, Telden Rau, and the senior mage. She has a briefing package ready for the girls.
* **Niv:** Monitoring the Kraken report from Bryn Shander. She sent one construct survey bird to the coastal zone. It has not returned. She has sent two more. She has not told the girls yet.
