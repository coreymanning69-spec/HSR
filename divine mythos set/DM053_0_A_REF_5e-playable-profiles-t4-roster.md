## Cassian Ward

**Source owners:** `DM014_3` and `DM022_0`. Gravemaker Doctrine is an
institutional nickname, not Cassian's self-title.

**5e translation:** Rogue 12 (Mastermind) / Fighter 6 (Battle Master), level
18. Dexterity drives precision weapons; Intelligence represents tactical geometry.

### Proposed core

| Ability | Score | Mod |
|---|---:|---:|
| Strength | 12 | +1 |
| Dexterity | 20 | +5 |
| Constitution | 16 | +3 |
| Intelligence | 18 | +4 |
| Wisdom | 14 | +2 |
| Charisma | 10 | +0 |

- Proficiency bonus +6; HP 147; AC 19 in operative gear; speed 30 ft.
- Saving throws Dexterity and Intelligence; expertise in Investigation and Stealth.
- Skills: Acrobatics, Insight, Perception, Sleight of Hand, Investigation, Stealth, Deception, and Company tools.
- Use Sneak Attack 6d6, Cunning Action, Uncanny Dodge, Evasion, Reliable Talent, Stroke of Luck, Master of Tactics, Action Surge, and Extra Attack normally.
- Battle Master maneuvers: Brace, Disarming Attack, Precision Attack, Riposte, and Trip Attack; four d10 superiority dice per short rest.

### Gravemaker Doctrine — proposed feature

When Cassian rolls initiative, he may designate up to four visible creatures or
environmental features. Until the end of his first turn, he has advantage on the
first attack or ability check against each designated target, and one designated
target cannot benefit from cover against him. Once per short or long rest. This
is positioning and preparation, not foresight or teleportation.

### Equipment

- MK-Sliver: finesse, light, +2 weapon; 1d8 piercing, plus once per turn 2d6 force damage against a studied or designated creature.
- Resonant-frequency pen: DC 17 Constitution save on a tuned target; failure removes reactions and imposes disadvantage on its next attack. This is the Veyren sparring exploit, not universal shutdown.
- Localized EMP: once per short rest, 15-foot radius; DC 17 Constitution or tools save, disrupting constructs/electronics for 1 round on failure.
- Dark combat slacks, Company kit, concealed tools, and improvised weapons.

## Veyren

**Source owners:** `DM014_3`, `DM022_0`, and `DM004_3`.

**5e translation:** Barbarian 15 (Totem Warrior) / Fighter 3 (Champion), level
18. The subclass names are approximations; bespoke features carry the identity.

### Proposed core

| Ability | Score | Mod |
|---|---:|---:|
| Strength | 24 | +7 |
| Dexterity | 18 | +4 |
| Constitution | 24 | +7 |
| Intelligence | 8 | -1 |
| Wisdom | 12 | +1 |
| Charisma | 14 | +2 |

- Proficiency bonus +6; HP 233; speed 40 ft. unarmored, 30 ft. in plate; AC 21 in plate, 18 unarmored.
- Saving throws Strength and Constitution; advantage on initiative checks.
- Skills: Athletics, Acrobatics, Perception, Intimidation, and Survival.
- Use Rage, Reckless Attack, Danger Sense, Extra Attack, Fast Movement, Feral Instinct, Relentless Rage, Persistent Rage, and Primal Champion normally.
- Rage damage +4; suggested Totem choices are Bear at 3rd and Tiger at 6th level.

### Regeneration — locked fiction, proposed timing

At the start of each turn, Veyren regains 20 HP if he has at least 1 HP. It
stops until the end of his next turn after radiant or blessed/greater-belief
damage. He cannot die from ordinary damage while it functions, but direct divine
action and greater belief remain valid exceptions. If reduced to 0 HP, he makes
death saves normally; a natural 20 or successful stabilization restarts it.

### Two-phase armor

- **Armored charge:** full 360-pound plate rig; AC 21, speed 30 ft.; resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks; cannot be pushed while raging unless he chooses.
- **Plate drop:** bonus action; loses armor benefits, gains speed 40 ft. and advantage on Acrobatics, and may move through one hostile creature's space per turn if it is Large or smaller. Plates remain as an obstacle.
- **Crater jump:** after moving 20 ft., creatures within 10 ft. of landing make a DC 19 Dexterity save or fall prone and take 2d10 bludgeoning.

### Attacks and counterplay

- Two Hooked Gauntlet attacks, or one gauntlet and one bite while raging. Gauntlet: +13 to hit, 1d10 + 7 bludgeoning plus 1d6 piercing. Bite: +13 to hit, 1d8 + 7 piercing and grapple option (escape DC 21) against Large or smaller creatures.
- A creature grappled by Veyren has disadvantage on escape checks and cannot willingly move more than 5 ft. away.
- Once per long rest, Maximum Effort doubles his drag/lift capacity for 1 minute and adds 30 ft. to a Strength-based jump. Historical test numbers remain descriptive benchmarks, not damage conversions.
- The tuned resonant-frequency effect can exploit his neural sensitivity: failed DC 17 Constitution save removes reactions and imposes disadvantage on attacks until the end of his next turn.

## Suzu Hikari

**Source owner:** `DM043_0` for Suzu, Hakubai, consent, names, staging, and
entry geometry; `DM022_0` for the Japan T4 belief-intent system.

**5e translation:** Artificer 15 (Armorer) with a bespoke pilot package. Suzu is
the person; Hakubai is separate.

### Proposed core

| Ability | Score | Mod |
|---|---:|---:|
| Strength | 10 | +0 |
| Dexterity | 16 | +3 |
| Constitution | 14 | +2 |
| Intelligence | 20 | +5 |
| Wisdom | 14 | +2 |
| Charisma | 16 | +3 |

- Proficiency bonus +5; HP 108; AC 18 in Amagatsu field gear; speed 30 ft.
- Saving throws Constitution and Intelligence; tools: tinker's tools and Vehicles (land).
- Skills: Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Persuasion, and Perception.
- Use the normal 2014 artificer spell progression; spells represent engineering, protection, communications, and repair.

### Pilot package

- **Model anchor:** the hand-built Hakubai model is an attended vulnerable object in the cockpit. Modifying it modifies Hakubai; destroying it removes Hakubai until Suzu builds another.
- **Calibrated confidence:** advantage on initiative and Wisdom saves while protecting civilians or operating within the prepared Amagatsu staging network.
- **National protector:** once per long rest, call a prepared route, carrier, staging crew, or emergency support within the established apparatus. This is logistics, not teleportation.
- **Mortal pilot:** cockpit breach, model destruction, or direct attack on Suzu affects her normally; the titan does not make her divine or infinite.
- Suzu remains confident, careful rather than cautious, and consent-locked. Hakubai's bow to divinity is a tell; its exact personality remains unresolved.

## Hakubai — Plum Titan

**Source owner:** `DM043_0`, with belief-intent context in `DM022_0` and `DM022_0b`.

**Profile type:** separate Gargantuan construct, not a PC level, familiar, or
ordinary summon.

### Proposed construct profile

- Gargantuan construct, unaligned; height 120 ft.; speed 60 ft., climb 30 ft.
- AC 23; HP 480; Strength 30, Dexterity 10, Constitution 30, Intelligence 10, Wisdom 16, Charisma 16.
- Immunity to poison and psychic damage, and to charmed, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, and restrained.
- Resistance to all damage from ordinary nonmagical weapons and siege effects. Falls and conventional weapons do not meaningfully threaten it; greater-belief effects remain valid counterplay.
- Acts on Suzu's initiative immediately after her. If Suzu is incapacitated, it follows its last protective instruction and may protect without settling its personality.

### Actions

- **Plum-plated strike:** +13 to hit, reach 20 ft., 4d12 + 10 bludgeoning.
- **Calligraphic beam:** 120-foot line, DC 21 Dexterity save, 10d10 radiant or force damage; structural effects follow the single visible act Suzu intends.
- **Protective reposition:** move half speed and interpose its body, granting total cover where appropriate.
- **Visible-act outcome:** once per long rest, perform one clearly visible protector act at divine-scale outcome: remove a building, clear an obstacle, or change a battlefield feature. This is finite and cannot duplicate Sera's bottomless capability.

### Anchor and entry rules

- The cockpit model can be targeted as an attended object: AC 17, HP 30. Destruction dismisses Hakubai until Suzu rebuilds it; damage is not automatically titan damage.
- Hakubai may bow or alter posture near recognized divinity without a fixed combat effect. Never resolve whether it is sentient, a projection, belief reflex, or a mixture.
- Suzu enters by either the foot/ankle airlock and elevator or the titan's palm pickup. These are alternatives, never a chained sequence.
