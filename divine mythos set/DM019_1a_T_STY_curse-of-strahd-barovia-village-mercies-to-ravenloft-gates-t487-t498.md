## Barovia Village Mercies Through Ravenloft Gates (T487–T498)

T487: The Burgomaster's Manor #LOC-BURGOMASTERS-MANOR #CHAR-IREENA-KOLYANA #CHAR-ISMARK-KOLYANOVICH #CHAR-KOLYAN-INDIROVICH #CONCEPT-CONSENT-FIRST-DIVINE-INTERVENTION
— Stanimir directed them to the burgomaster's manor — where Ireena and Ismark sheltered with their father's body.
— MODULE DEVIATION: In standard Curse of Strahd, the burgomaster (Kolyan Indirovich) is dead and stays dead. His death is a fixed plot point — the inciting incident that forces Ireena to seek escape.
— Ireena met them at the door. Young, dark-haired, visibly exhausted, two bite marks on her neck partially hidden by collar. She recognized Stanimir, was confused by the rest.
— Sera's first assessment: a woman trapped, hunted, and slowly losing ground. This intersected every protective axis Sera possessed.
— Ember saw the body in the parlor (coffin, candles, grief-arrangements) and asked the obvious: "Is he dead? Can Soli fix him?"
— The room went silent. Ireena's eyes went wide. Ismark's hand tightened on his sister's shoulder.
— Sera, casual: "Yeah, Soli, can you fix him?"
— Soliera moved to the coffin. Touched Kolyan's forehead. Reality adjusted.


T488: Kolyan Resurrected #LOC-BURGOMASTERS-MANOR #CHAR-KOLYAN-INDIROVICH #CHAR-SOLIERA #THEME-MERCY-AS-INFRASTRUCTURE
— Kolyan Indirovich drew breath. Opened his eyes. Looked up at Soliera with the expression of a man who had been dead and now wasn't.
— "I was dead." Statement, not question.
— Soliera inclined her head. Yes.
— "And you brought me back."
— Another nod.
— Kolyan sat up slowly. Looked past Soliera to the doorway where his children stood clutching each other, both crying openly.
— "Ireena. Ismark." They crashed into him — both collapsing against their father, sobbing into his shoulders, holding him like they were afraid he'd disappear.
— "I'm here," he murmured. "I'm here. You're safe. I'm here."
— Sera watched from the side, arms crossed, expression complicated. Ember stood close to Sera.
— "That's... that's really good," Ember whispered.
— "Yeah," Sera agreed quietly. "That's the good part."
— First module-breaking miracle: the dead stay alive.


T489–T490: Gratitude and Briefing #LOC-BURGOMASTERS-MANOR #CHAR-KOLYAN-INDIROVICH #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA
Beat Ledger:
T489: Kolyan's restoration becomes gratitude, shock, and witness-economy ignition inside the manor.
T490: The family briefing reframes Strahd's predation as a solvable jurisdiction problem rather than an inescapable gothic condition.

— Kolyan settled into his chair, Ireena and Ismark flanking him, and provided intelligence: wolves, fog patterns, Strahd's behavioral rhythms, valley geography.
— Soliera entered the study. Stanimir hesitated. Sera giggled and jumped to her feet. Ember slid off her chair.
— Mortals approached to thank Soliera. Sera became visibly too happy — too excited to see her God receive praise. Sera's praise kink isn't hers; it's praise for Soli. Praising Sera, to her, is just praising reality but ignoring the creator.
— Sera's physical devotion response to Soliera being acknowledged by mortals: heightened energy, kinetic joy, the Velvet Spiral running warm.


T491: Marks and Mercies #LOC-BURGOMASTERS-MANOR #CHAR-IREENA-KOLYANA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CONCEPT-CONSENT-FIRST-DIVINE-INTERVENTION
— Ember noticed Ireena's neck. "Can we take the bites away?"
— Everyone stopped.
— Ember pointed at the scars: "The marks. From when he bit you. Why leave them? Soliera fixed your dad being dead. Can't she just... make your neck not bitten?"
— Ireena's hand flew to her throat, covering the wounds instinctively. "I... could you? Would that..."
— Sera: "Oh yeah. Obviously. Soli?"
— Soliera crossed to Ireena. Fingers touched her neck, gentle as breath, over the puncture scars.
— The marks unmade themselves. Not healing — unbecoming. As if reality consulted its records, found the error marked "vampire bite x2," and corrected the mistake. Skin smooth. No scars. No trace.
— Ireena gasped: "They're... they're gone. Completely gone. I thought I'd carry those forever."
— Sera: "You're not marked anymore. Fresh start."
— Sera hopped off the desk: "Alright. Info downloaded, neck un-bitten, family reunited. What else we got on the village to-do list?"
— Kolyan: "The church. Father Donavich. His son..." He looked at Soliera with desperate, fragile hope.
— Sera: "We'll handle it. Screaming vampire kid in a basement sounds exactly like our kind of problem."


T492: The Village Shifts #LOC-BAROVIA-VILLAGE-SQUARE #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #THEME-INFRASTRUCTURE-AS-FREEDOM
— They exited the manor. Sera first, already moving with purpose. Ember trailing, clutching remaining bread wrapped in cloth. Soliera followed at her own pace, and the world noticed.
— Sera waved off Ireena: "You guys just stay here. Hang out with your miraculously-not-dead dad. Process stuff."
— The village square had changed. Subtly. Cobblestones cleaner — not scrubbed, just less tired. Gray stone showing hints of warm amber, pale cream beneath decades of grime. Fountain running clear and steady.
— Ember noticed first: "Oh. It looks a little less sad now."
— Sera's gaze snagged on the church steeple — a faded banner hanging limp. "Does this mean this area belongs to the Sanctum now? Because if so, we need to hang up our flag and take down that ugly one."
— Ember: "Yeah. Probably. But let's go talk to the owner guy first, and then we'll see what we wanna do."
— Sera: "Owner guy. You mean Strahd?"
— Ember, seriously: "Seems polite to introduce ourselves before we start changing his stuff."
— Sera grinned: "I like your version of polite. It's very 'we're absolutely going to wreck your shit, but we'll say hi first.'"
— They crossed toward the church. From beneath it: screaming. Intermittent, ragged, throat-raw. Someone who'd been screaming for weeks.
— Ember's glow flickered, distressed. Moved closer to Sera.
— Sera's expression went flat: "Yeah. That's fucked up."
— Stanimir knocked.


T493: Basement Salvation #LOC-BAROVIA-CHURCH #CHAR-FATHER-DONAVICH #CHAR-DORU #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-EMBER #THEME-MERCY-AS-INFRASTRUCTURE
— Father Donavich opened the door. Gaunt, gray-bearded, exhaustion beyond physical into spiritual. Priest's vestments hanging loose on a wasted frame.
— "Please. Please, if you're here to mock me, I cannot — I have nothing left to —" His voice cracked completely.
— Sera stepped forward, expression gentling: "We're not here to mock you, Father. We're here to help with your son."
— Donavich crumpled: "There is no help. He is damned. Lost. I pray and pray but the Morninglord does not answer—"
— Sera: "We can fix it. That's what we do. Fix things."
— Donavich stared at her — barefoot woman in a scandalously short seafoam slip, beside a girl made of fire and a silent figure who looked like every icon of divinity he'd ever prayed to.
— "Who... who are you?"
— Sera grinned, but it was kind: "The cavalry. Can we come in?"
— Inside: dim candles, dust-covered pews, incense over despair.
— Ember stopped at the doorway, ember-glow dimmed. Staring at the floor — the stone leading to stairs leading down to the screaming. Her expression was tight, uncomfortable.
— Sera: "Don't even come down here, Ember. Just hang up here. It's gonna be fine."
— Sera turned to Soliera, who stood near the altar like she'd always been there: "Actually, Soli, can you just heal him from here?"
— Donavich: "Heal him? He's undead. He's a spawn. There is no healing—"
— Sera: "Oh, of course it's gonna work." She looked at Soliera with absolute certainty. "It's already done now, right?"
— Soliera lifted one hand. Reality adjusted.
— From below: the screaming stopped. Complete silence. Then a voice — young, male, hoarse, human. Confused. Frightened.
— "Father? Father? Where... what happened? Why am I — Father!"
— Donavich's knees gave out. Stanimir caught him. The priest dissolved into shaking sobs.
— "Doru. That's... that's my son. That's his voice. Not the thing — that's—"
— Sera: "That's him fixed. Soli just kinda asked reality to rewind the 'being a vampire spawn' part."
— Father and son reunion in the basement. Chains undone, desperate embraces, sobbed apologies from both directions.
— MODULE DEVIATION: In standard CoS, Doru is a vampire spawn the party must fight or avoid. There is no cure. Donavich's grief is permanent. Here: healed from upstairs without entering the basement.


T494: Questions Without Answers #LOC-BAROVIA-CHURCH #CHAR-STANIMIR #CHAR-DONAVICH #CHAR-DORU #CHAR-EMBER #THEME-WITNESS-ECONOMY
— Donavich and Doru emerged from the basement. Doru gaunt, filthy, blinking, but alive and human. No red eyes, no fangs.
— Doru found Soliera's gaze immediately: "You. You're the one who... I felt everything just stopped being wrong."
— Stanimir: "What are you? I've traveled with Vistani all my life. I've seen Madam Eva read fates in cards, watched the mists part for our caravans. But this... Reversing vampirism. Resurrecting the dead. Making the mists bow."
— Donavich: "The Morninglord doesn't answer prayers here. The gods are silent in Barovia. And you just... undid damnation like it was nothing."
— Ember spoke first, genuinely confused: "Well, then why'd you send somebody to the Sanctum if you didn't want help?"
— Everyone stopped. The silence stretched.
— Stanimir, slowly: "We wanted help. Desperately. But wanting and believing... We've lived in Strahd's domain for generations. Nothing gets better. Nothing can get better. The best we hoped for was... witness."
— Sera: "Soli's not from this land. Different jurisdiction. Way higher clearance."
— Two families fixed in under an hour. Strahd's authority pre-emptively destabilized — his spawn unmade without confrontation.
— Sera, looking toward the castle: "Now we go introduce ourselves to the vampire lord who thinks he owns this place and explain that management's changed."
— Her grin was sharp, delighted, and absolutely predatory. "Road trip to Castle Ravenloft. Let's go be terrifying."


ROAD TO CASTLE RAVENLOFT (T495–T498)

T495: The Road to Castle Ravenloft #LOC-ROAD-TO-RAVENLOFT #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-STANIMIR #CHAR-LADY-VODA #THEME-SERA-JOY-FIRST-VIOLENCE-SECOND
— Left the village. Road east wound upward through dense forest, bare trees reaching overhead like skeletal fingers.
— Sera remembered Lady Voda — reminded Stanimir to keep detailed notes for his report. "She's going to want every detail. Write it down. All of it."
— Stanimir's hand was already cramping.
— Six dire wolves attacked from the treeline. Pack coordination, flanking maneuver, supernatural aggression. Standard module encounter.
— Sera used airwave pressure manipulation — not inversion, not geometry. Raw compressed atmosphere channeled through intent-speed action. The wolves didn't redirect. They splattered against the treeline. Messy, brutal, thorough. Bodies crumpled and left behind as the group kept walking.
— Bodies positioned as a statement: "This territory is claimed. Your authority is revoked. Do not test this further."
— Ember uncomfortable but processing. "Does it hurt them? When you do that?"
— Sera: "Nope. Intent-speed. They're dead before pain could register. I'm not cruel, Em. Just thorough."
— Ember settled slightly: "I just... they looked scary, but then they just stopped. And you didn't even seem worried."
— Sera: "Because I wasn't. They couldn't hurt us. They were just confused about the food chain. We're not in danger here, Ember. We're the danger."
— Stanimir experiencing complete category collapse. Everything he believed about reality being systematically deleted.
— The forest went very, very quiet after the demonstration.
— Bridge crossing. Castle Ravenloft revealed through the mist: Gothic oppression architecture, perched on its plateau like a crown of thorns against the gray sky. Designed to crush hope before you reach the doors.
— Sera's grin widened: "There it is. Home office of the guy who thinks he runs this place."


T496–T498: Approach and Sentinel Contact #LOC-ROAD-TO-RAVENLOFT #LOC-CASTLE-RAVENLOFT #CHAR-STRAHD-VON-ZAROVICH
Beat Ledger:
T496: Road approach tightens; gothic atmosphere fails to dominate the party's emotional baseline.
T497: Sentinel / threshold contact establishes that Ravenloft sees something it cannot categorize.
T498: Strahd assesses the trio at the gates: Sera as pressure, Ember as warmth, Soliera as terrifying nothing.

— Three vampire spawn sentinels on the road near the castle gates. Standard module guardians.
— Sera announced plainly that they were here to talk to Strahd.
— The sentinels received a psychic message. Paused. Stepped aside. "The master acknowledges your presence."
— Strahd von Zarovich waited at the castle gates — first direct contact. Tall, dark-haired, red-eyed, dressed in immaculate noble clothing. The kind of presence that had controlled this domain for four centuries through a combination of supernatural power and practiced aristocratic authority.
— He assessed them. Felt Sera's presence as physical pressure. Felt Ember's divine warmth. Felt... nothing from Soliera. Which was worse than anything he could have sensed.
