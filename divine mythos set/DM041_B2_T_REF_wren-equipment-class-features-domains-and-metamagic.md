### Robe of the Archmagi — brilliant white and gold

The gray Sanctum robe is retired. At T537.0 the Robe of Stars' magic was carried
forward into a **Robe of the Archmagi in brilliant white and gold**, the white
pattern that answers to a good-aligned wearer. Wren and Doran now match instead
of inverting: both in white, both unmistakably carrying the Sanctum's mark.

**As a Robe of the Archmagi:**

- **AC 15 + DEX** while she wears no armor. With cloak and Blessing this is
  **AC 18**, or **23** under *shield*. *Mage armor* is now redundant and no
  longer needs to be maintained.
- **Advantage on all saving throws against spells and other magical effects.**
- **+2 spell save DC and +2 spell attack**, already included in DC 22 / +14.

**Carried forward from the Robe of Stars:**

- +1 to all saving throws.
- Six removable stars. Each uses an action to cast *magic missile* at 5th level:
  **seven** automatic-hit darts for 1d4+1 force each. All six regenerate daily at
  dusk. No metamagic applies. The stars visibly leave the garment when spent.
- As an action Wren may enter the Astral Plane with everything worn or carried;
  as an action she returns to the exact space she left. No save, roll,
  concentration, daily limit, hunger, or aging applies.
- **It holds *crown of stars* permanently.** Seven motes ride above her at all
  times with no slot and no concentration, refreshing daily.

The Astral door is not travel and cannot carry Doran. Every use is a choice to
leave him in the room. Githyanki and astral dreadnoughts make repeated use
narratively consequential.

The permanent crown is the loudest thing she owns. She cannot be inconspicuous
while wearing it, and it announces what she is to anyone who can read the sight.

### Cloak of Protection

+1 AC and +1 to all saving throws. Doran wears the matching cloak.

### Blessing of Protection

+1 AC and +1 to all saving throws, stacking with robe and cloak. Blessing of
Magic Resistance is rejected as too strong. This blessing and the Personal
Blessing below are mechanically distinct parts of Soliera's personal T532
remaking package Wren receives when she wakes.

### Personal Blessing

+1 to INT and WIS saving throws only. This is separate from the Blessing of
Protection and the sigil's +5. It is already included in INT +12 and WIS +18.

### No attunement

Attunement does not apply to either trainee's gear. It was divinely made for
them; nothing occupies a slot or can be attuned away by someone else.

This follows Wren's no-rites, no-rituals cleric structure. Her divine channel
runs to the literal new overgod, outside constraint systems negotiated by older
Realms gods. When a limitation would apply, Soliera says **yes**. The metamagics,
live cleric list, doubled slots, unified casting stat, dual domains, and
no-attunement kit are one architecture, not piecemeal exceptions.

### Component-free casting and Doran-only healing maximization

Wren requires no material component, costly consumed component, focus, holy
symbol, or channeling tool for any spell on this sheet. Verbal and somatic
components remain observable unless she elects Silent and/or Still. If a scene
wants a diamond, bowl, incense, or other physical ritual prop, the Sanctum
supplies and replenishes it without cost or limit; its absence never blocks the
cast in the field.

Wren's healing rolls normally except when it targets Doran. His partial
Blessing of Wound Closure maximizes all healing he receives, including healing
from Wren; this is his gear acting on him, not a property of her casting.
Empowered Healing is redundant only on Doran and remains live and useful on
every other target.

### Staff of the Magi

The Wand of Fireball is retired. At T537.0 Soliera personally remade it into a **Staff of the
Magi**: divine white wood, with a black bird standing on a metal perch at the
tip. It reads as a matched set with the Black-Bird Sigil, and it is the most
recognizable object either trainee carries.

The staff is not a replacement for the wand. It is the same object at scale.
The Wand of Fireball was small and slender, white, and carried in one hand;
the staff is that silhouette grown into. Same wood-white, same hand, more of
it. The black bird on the metal perch continues the wand's line rather than
introducing a new mark, which is why it reads as a matched set with the sigil
instead of as a third emblem.

The wand's magic did not go into the staff. It went into Wren — *fireball*
moved onto her known list at T537.0. The wand recharged only through the
Sanctum and refused metamagic; the staff regains 4d6+2 daily on its own and
absorbs spells cast only at her. She needed the Sanctum to refill her, and now
she refills herself. The object grew; the dependency did not survive.

**Indestructible.** It will not bend, break, splinter, burn, corrode, or shatter.
No force in play destroys it. The single exception is retributive strike, which
requires Wren's deliberate intent — nothing can shatter it *for* her, and it
cannot be broken as a way to disarm her.

**Physical interposition.** Because nothing in play cuts, bends, or shears the
staff, Wren may hold it crosswise and stop a weapon with it. An axe, a sword,
a giant's blade, a falling edge — the edge does not reach her.

The force still does. She is driven back, knocked prone, hands numbed, ribs
cracked; a giant's swing moves her whether or not it cuts her. Blocked is not
unharmed.

**This grants no AC and no damage reduction.** It is a described action in a
described moment, adjudicated at the table, never a standing number. Wren's
softness is load-bearing to the pair's design and must not be quietly eroded
by a passive that makes her harder every round.

**50 charges**, regaining **4d6+2 daily at dawn**. Expended charges no longer
require a Sanctum return.

| Cost | Spells |
|---:|---|
| 0 charges | *detect magic, enlarge/reduce, light, mage hand, protection from evil and good* |
| 2 charges | *flaming sphere, invisibility, knock, web* |
| 5 charges | *dispel magic, fireball, ice storm, lightning bolt, passwall, telekinesis* |
| 7 charges | *conjure elemental, plane shift, fire storm* |

- **Spell absorption.** As a reaction to a spell targeting only her, she negates
  it and gains charges equal to its level. If this would exceed 50, the staff
  holds and the excess is lost.
- **Retributive strike.** She may break the staff deliberately: 16d6 force in a
  30-foot radius, destroying it. This is the only thing that unmakes it.
- **Its +2 spell attack bonus is declined.** See the At a Glance note.

***Fireball* is now one of Wren's own known spells** and no longer depends on the
item. It scales with her slots and accepts metamagic normally, which closes the
Ironslag frustration beat — Energy Substitution can now move it off fire when
something is immune. That failure was a real arc and it resolved through the
uplift rather than being patched away.

**Physical utility.** Because it cannot break, the staff is genuine dungeon
equipment and should be used that way: propping a gate, jamming a portcullis,
wedging under a descending ceiling or a giant's foot, spanning a gap, or handed
to Doran as a lever he can put his full STR 27 against. It holds. Reach for this
before reaching for a spell when the problem is mechanical.

## CLASS FEATURES AND DOMAINS

**Cleric 20:** Channel Divinity **3/rest** · Destroy Undead (CR 4) · full cleric
list live at all times without preparation. Wren has no percentile Divine
Intervention roll and no replacement button, and the Cleric 20 automatic-success
version stays excluded for the same reason: when she does the impossible, she
does it with the power already given to her. If the situation genuinely exceeds
her, she asks Sera, Ember, or Soliera — and that is a relationship and a scene,
not a feature with a cooldown. The dramatic unit remains Wren and Doran.

**Divine damage — every spell, every attack.** Everything Wren throws carries
**DIVINE**. It rides *alongside* the printed damage type rather than replacing
it: a radiant spell is radiant *and* divine. Existing vulnerability interactions
are preserved unchanged, and resistance to the printed type no longer fully
blunts her. Her cleric-side source is Ember's ability; her Divine Soul source is
**Soliera Herself**, which makes this a theology fact and not only a sheet fact —
see `DM042_0` and `DM047_0`.

**Sorcerer 20, Divine Soul:** Font of Magic · Metamagic · Favored by the Gods
(2d4 to a failed save or missed attack, 1/rest) · Empowered Healing (1 SP to
reroll healing dice; redundant only on Doran, where his blessing maximizes) ·
Otherworldly Wings (**fly 60 ft**; manifesting and dismissing is now free, no
action) · **Unearthly Recovery** (bonus action, once per long rest, while below
half HP: regain **110**) · **Sorcerous Restoration** (regain **4 sorcery points**
on every short rest).

**War Caster:** advantage on concentration saves · somatic components with
hands full · spellcasting as an opportunity attack.

**Alert:** +5 initiative · cannot be surprised while conscious · unseen attackers
gain no advantage solely from being unseen. Taken at Sorcerer 16 to fix the worst
number on her sheet: at +1 she acted last in every fight, which contradicted a
doctrine built on repositioning and preserving an exit before things worsen.

**Resilient (DEX):** +1 DEX and proficiency in DEX saves, carrying her from +4 to
**+10**. STR +3 is now her only shallow save.

**Skilled:** retained from the source build. Its proficiencies are already
included in the current skill and tool list above; do not add three more on top
of that list.

### Portal domain

Granted power: Wren senses active portals and gates within 60 feet, permanently.

#### Channel Divinity: Dimensional Shift

**Action.** Wren and up to six willing creatures within 30 feet teleport to any
point she can see, or any place she has physically stood within the last 24
hours, up to one mile away. No concentration, no components, and it does not
provoke.

This is the pair's exit button. It costs one of three Channel Divinity uses and
it moves everyone, which is the entire difference between Wren leaving and the
party leaving.

#### Portal 14 — Unbarred

Her teleportation cannot be stopped by *dimensional lock*, *forcecage*,
anti-teleport wards, or any effect of 8th level or lower. She knows on sight when
a space is sealed against travel, and she knows what it would take to leave.

She also casts *misty step* as a **bonus action without a slot, five times per
long rest**.

#### Portal 17 — The Open Door

**Anchor.** At the end of a long rest Wren sets an anchor where she stands. Once
within the next 24 hours, as a **reaction**, she and every willing creature
within 30 feet return to it. She may do this while restrained, grappled,
incapacitated, or at 0 HP — the only requirement is that she is alive.

**Toll.** Anything attempting to teleport into or out of a 60-foot radius around
her must make a **CHA save against her spell save DC** or the attempt fails and
is wasted. She chooses who is exempt.

The Toll is the most quietly powerful thing on her sheet. Nothing escapes her,
and nothing reinforces against her. Use it to make a fleeing boss stay in the
room, and remember that enemy casters will start opening with attempts to remove
her specifically once they understand what it does.

The Anchor is the mechanical form of her doctrine: the exit exists before the
fight starts, and it works even when she has already lost.

#### Portal Shear ("The Eraser") — signature

**Conjuration (teleportation) · entire turn (planting) · 1/long rest ·
force-lane use · range 50 ft · a straight line leaving a 1 cm RADIUS circle in
anything at all, angled freely · CON DC 22, binary.**

**Activation change, landed knowingly as a nerf.** Shear is planted over the
whole turn. At action speed she could Shear and quicken something else in the
same round; she can no longer do that. This is deliberate — it makes Portal
Slash her only fast erasure. The geometry field is a *radius*, twice the prior
implied width. On a successful
save nothing happens; on a failed save the full line resolves.

- The line pierces every body, object, structure, and piece of terrain along
  its exact straight path for the full range. Nothing inside that line—not the
  first target, a fortified wall, a mountain, or any amount of intervening
  mass—stops or absorbs it. Only matter beyond the line's 50-ft endpoint is
  untouched; with Distant, that sole cutoff moves to 100 ft.
- Every crossed creature or object takes **8d20 force/teleportation damage**.
  The excised slice is transported whole to an abyssal locus and dissolved;
  nothing remains to retrieve. Unattended objects and structures receive the
  full effect automatically, with no save.
- Elemental resistance, bracing, wards, and shields do not mitigate the
  relocation. Only genuine teleportation immunity or resistance blocks it.
- If the damage totals at least **65% of a creature's current HP**, it makes a
  second save: flat **CON 25, no modifiers**. Failure kills instantly, with no
  death saves and nothing to stabilize.
- Enlarge/Distant costs 1 SP and doubles the line to 100 ft without changing
  its piercing behavior.

**Level-10 Eraser capstone — not yet learned.** Wren has not acquired this
mode and it is not a runtime option. Once learned, she may treat Portal Shear
as level 10 and apply
Split Ray, Twin, capstone-only Enhanced shaping, Maximize, Empower, Extend,
Careful, and Distant as one indivisible capstone package. The separate lines
merge into one **4 cm × 200 ft wave**. Every crossed creature or attended object
makes one CON DC 22 save: zero on success, **960 force/teleportation damage** on
failure. Unattended matter receives the failure result automatically. Normal
piercing, teleportation immunity/resistance, and the 65%-of-current-HP death
check remain unchanged.

The capstone costs **40 SP plus the day's Portal Shear**. After it is used, the
merged form requires **seven uninterrupted safe days in the Sanctum** to
recover. This recovery requirement belongs only to the capstone; ordinary
Portal Shear continues to recover on its normal long-rest cycle.

#### Portal Slash ("Slash") — its own lane

**Not a Shear mode and not the capstone.** The level-10 capstone is Portal
Shear's own maturity — every metamagic merged into one planted Shear. Slash is a
different ability with its own geometry, its own save, and its own entry in
`DM041_B`. It carries the lane formerly filed as the Nanowire Whip; that name is
retired. Everyone calls it Slash, the girls included.

**Conjuration (teleportation) · 1 action, mid-combat · DEX DC 22, binary.**
Where Shear plants a straight line, Slash throws a line crossing horizontally in
a whip arc: a cone in front of Wren, dependent on her facing. The ranks widen
from her outward — 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 squares, eight at the far end, thirty-five
squares total. It is **narrow at origin**: anything directly beside her is missed
entirely, and a square or two off her facing removes a target from the arc.

The save is **DEX, not CON**. Slash is a physical thing travelling through air
and speed evades it; Shear is CON because you endure being unmade. The split is
deliberate.

Every crossed target takes **8d20 force/teleportation damage**. The cut height is
uncontrolled — it takes whatever it crosses at whatever height it crosses it —
and it is architecture-capable along its whole path. Piercing behaviour, the
teleportation-only block, and the 65%-of-current-HP death check carry over from
the base ability unchanged.

**Cost:** Slash spends the day's Portal Shear. That keeps one erasure event per
day and stops Slash routing around the capstone's seven-day Sanctum lockout. It
is the easiest constraint to loosen if play says it is tight.

**Status:** specified, not acquired, not a runtime option. The unlock is
narrative, as she grows — no level and no mechanical trigger.

### Liberation domain

The original granted power is redundant with Wren's permanent immunities, so
the domain points outward: she removes coercion and constraint from others.
Liberation is not merely freedom from a condition. It is Wren's theological
office of protecting agency: no imposed restraint, forced movement, domination,
or coercive outcome should be treated as the natural right of a stronger will.
The mechanics below are the settled expression of that office. Wren's eventual
personal demonstration of what liberation means remains a future character beat;
this file does not invent its scene, speech, or final emotional resolution.

#### Channel Divinity: Indomitable Spirit

**Action.** Wren and every ally within 30 feet immediately end any **charmed,
frightened, paralyzed, restrained, stunned, grappled, incapacitated, or
petrified** condition on them, and cannot be subjected to any of those for one
minute. Forced movement against them fails outright for the duration.

She is already immune to most of this. The feature exists so that she can hand
her own condition to everyone standing near her, which is the whole thesis of the
domain.

#### Liberation 14 — Unshackled Host

Allies within 30 feet have the benefits of ***freedom of movement* at all
times** — no slot, no concentration, no upkeep. The standing pair practice that
used to depend on Wren remembering to cast it before an engagement is now simply
true whenever Doran is near her.

Additionally, when an ally within 30 feet drops to 0 HP, they may immediately use
their reaction to move up to their full speed without provoking. A downed body
does not have to stay where it fell.

#### Liberation 17 — Aura of the Unbound

Allies within 30 feet have **advantage on all saving throws**.

Once per long rest, when a creature within 30 feet fails a saving throw, Wren may
use her **reaction to make it a success instead** — including against effects
that would kill outright.

Read together with Doran's saves, this is the single largest defensive change
from the uplift: he sits at STR +15 / CON +11 / WIS +11 / INT +10 *with
advantage* whenever he is standing near her. The pair geometry is no longer
thematic. It is arithmetic, and separating them is now the correct enemy play.

Liberation is Sanctum doctrine, expressed through Ember's cleric-side sourcing
and routed through Soliera's direct Divine Soul relationship with Wren. Portal
is the question Wren cannot stop asking; Liberation is the answer she gives when
someone else's agency is being taken. The exact future moment in which she fully
names or embodies that answer remains intentionally open and belongs to Wren's
character arc, not to this mechanical sheet.

## METAMAGIC

This engine exists by Ember's direct consent and is routed by Soliera. It is
always available to Wren and cannot be removed, dispelled, suppressed, or
narrowed by ordinary 5e metamagic eligibility rules. The listed sorcery-point
costs, action tax, and Quicken exclusivity still bind. When an imported option
has loose geometry, use the straightforward spell-facing reading and let the
declared homebrew effect resolve; do not invent a balance ceiling.

| Metamagic | Cost | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Energy Substitution / Transmuted | 0 | swap damage type |
| Silent | 0 | remove verbal component; elective per spell through the ring and excluded from turn-tax count |
| Still | 0 | remove somatic component; elective per spell through the ring and excluded from turn-tax count |
| Enlarge / Distant | 1 | double range |
| Extend / Extended | 1 | double duration |
| Sculpt | 1 | reshape area |
| Careful | 1 | chosen allies automatically succeed on the save |
| Empower | 2 | numeric effects ×1.5 |
| Split Ray | 2 | a ray hits a second target |
| Seeking | 2 | reroll a missed spell attack |
| Maximize | 3 | maximize numeric effects |
| Widen | 3 | double area |
| Chain | 3 | arc to secondary targets |
| Repeat | 3 | take effect again next round without another action |
| Twin | 4 | spell takes effect twice |
| Quicken | 4 | cast as a bonus action, maximum once per round |
| Heighten | variable | raise the save DC; pay the difference |

Persistent metamagic is excluded. Twin may target the same creature and may
apply to a multi-target spell.

**Quicken exclusivity:** Quicken is the bonus-action cast and can never combine
with another elected metamagic on the same spell. It must be the only
Wren-performed metamagic on that cast. Ring-elected Silent and Still are not
counted as Wren-performed metamagics.
