# DM051_0 — CHAMPION TIER DOCTRINE AND ROSTER

## Displacement Spine

Hostile divine champions are displaced counterparties, not offended worshippers. The Sanctum's structural success makes old leverage obsolete: Umberlee loses protection-racket revenue when Sanctum ship protection makes safety non-scarce; Shar loses oblivion-as-mercy when loss becomes recoverable through resurrection and the Open Door. High-INT characters may derive this pattern independently in play; it is never announced as a lecture.

## Certified Encounter Reading

Legendary Resistance answers only its owner's failed saving throw. It is not a
general counter to Wren's attack-roll damage, an attended object's separate
saving throw, or a fixed-cost refusal layer. Champion survivability is therefore
priced through visible action denial, positioning, and encounter structure rather
than unbounded hit-point inflation.

## Proposed Extensions — Not Canon

The three-lane patron-backed, national, and relic-bearing taxonomy, further roster entries beyond Ithreva, and any Shar/Mystra/Selune/Tyr development remain PROPOSED. They do not create a ceiling, admit another champion, or authorize a new encounter.
