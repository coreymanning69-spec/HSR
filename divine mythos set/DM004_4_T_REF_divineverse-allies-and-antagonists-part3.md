# Undermountain Arc Companions world-state


## ALFIA
Current post-arc: Sanctum Chaplain, Undermountain Outpost
Selûne's touch left a mark Alfia can't measure and won't stop trying to. She didn't follow the girls down to level 20 — she wasn't ready, and she knew it, which is its own kind of maturity. What she did was stay.
When Velin and Niv arrived with constructs and goodwill to establish the base at level 20, Alfia was already there. She'd been there. She'd quietly organized the upper levels into something functional — rotating patrol routes, a small infirmary, a space kept clear for prayer that nobody asked her to build but everyone started using.
Velin noted her. Filed it. Two weeks later Alfia received a letter with a black bird seal she didn't recognize yet.
She runs the Undermountain outpost's spiritual infrastructure now. Chaplain isn't quite the right word — she doesn't sermonize, doesn't recruit. She just keeps the channel clear. Sanctum soldiers rotating through Undermountain know there's a woman on level 8 with white hair and tired eyes who will sit with you without asking what's wrong. That's the whole job description.
She still prays every morning. The channel is considerably less quiet than it used to be.
Re-entry point: Any party moving through Undermountain post-T429. She's the face of the outpost for mortals. Velin trusts her with more than she's told her yet. She also travels and does work to spread her gospel and help those she can.


## MIRA

Current post-arc: Sanctum Research Adjunct, Dungeon Architecture Division
Velin didn't recruit Mira. Niv did. He read three pages of her notebooks over her shoulder while she was still writing them, said nothing, and had a formal offer drafted by morning.
She's the only person alive with a comprehensive structural survey of Undermountain's first fifteen levels. Halaster found out the Sanctum hired her and had feelings about it that he couldn't quite articulate and eventually just redirected into redesigning level 11 out of spite. Mira catalogued the changes within a week. Halaster has started leaving things for her to find. She's not sure if it's a threat or a collaboration. It might be both.
Her official title is Research Adjunct. Her actual job is being the person who understands what Undermountain is structurally — how Halaster's madness shapes space, where the dungeon's logic breaks down, which walls are load-bearing in ways that shouldn't be physically possible. The Sanctum uses this for outpost placement, emergency routing, and occasionally just because Niv finds her theories about spatial dream-architecture interesting and wants someone to talk to about it.
She published the book. Seventeen footnotes reference conversations with Ember. None of them explain who Ember is. It became something of a scholarly mystery in certain circles.
Re-entry point: Any Undermountain arc, any Sanctum research thread, or whenever Ember inevitably asks Soliera what happened to the girl with the glasses.


## RENA

Current post-arc: Sanctum Intelligence Contractor, Upper Undermountain

Nobody formally hired Rena. That's the point.
She has a working arrangement with Kessla — not employment, not a retainer, not a contract with signatures. An arrangement. Rena moves through Undermountain's upper levels the way she always has. She notes things. Occasionally a report surfaces at a Sanctum outpost desk written in handwriting nobody can attribute. Kessla reads them. Acts on them. Leaves a small pouch of coin at level 3, position 7, behind the third loose stone from the left in a corridor only Rena knows about.
They've met twice. Kessla respects her enormously and would never say so. Rena finds Kessla fascinating in the specific way she finds anything fascinating that she can't quite get a read on.
She hasn't forgotten Sera. Doesn't expect to. It's not longing exactly — more like having met a fixed point. Something that recalibrated her sense of scale and then walked through a portal into another universe. She kept the personal rule she made afterward. She's still working on what it means.
Re-entry point: Any intelligence thread, any Undermountain arc, or whenever Kessla needs someone deniable in a dungeon. Also — if Sera ever comes back through that portal, Rena will know before anyone else does. That's not a plan. It's just how her probability works. The Undermountain has become a city under a city, the portal to the Mergeverse an open secret in the Sanctum. Passage is available, you simply have to get to level 21 of Undermountain. The area itself doesn't bother Sanctum; others are free game.
