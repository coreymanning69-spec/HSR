## XIV. GLOBAL POWER STRUCTURE

### The Company (Corporate Megastructure)

The Company is post-apocalyptic corporate bureaucracy as governing model. Political borders are obsolete — everything is real estate, everything is franchise, everything is Company-logo-as-authority in place of state flags. It controls continents not through armies (though it has armies) but through infrastructure: terminals, licensing, supply chains, data.

Magic is tolerated within Company jurisdiction, but licensed. You want to practice above Minor-level casting in a Company zone, you register with a Company terminal and accept the terms of use. Relic-class items are databased in Company archives. The license can be revoked.

The Executive Tier controls continent-scale operations and is feared accordingly. An Executive acting under Company Initiative Protocol can wipe a city off the map without requiring external authorization. They are enhanced — divinely, technologically, or both — and represent the closest thing to Tier 5 that the Company has produced through engineered means rather than divine accident.

Cities under Company control run like hyper-optimized tech parks. Efficient. Orderly. The kind of orderly that requires a lot of infrastructure to enforce.


### The Church (Spiritual Hierarchy)

Post-Gate, the Church became the dominant moral and magical authority for populations that the Company doesn't reach or doesn't bother with. It has a structured hierarchy:

| Rank | Access Level |
|------|-------------|
| Ordinary Human | Uninitiated, baseline |
| Priest | Minor blessings, relic access |
| Bishop | Regional control, minor miracle access |
| Saint | Semi-divine, Gate bypass, partial post-human status |
| Archduke (×5) | Absolute apex — above Saints; five total; full institutional authority over Church doctrine and Keys |

Miracles are processed through magical-technological infrastructure — *Church Keys* that mediate between petitioner and divine source. Advancement requires witnessed, recorded miracles. This creates a system where miracles are both genuine and bureaucratic — real events managed through institutional gatekeeping.

Saints are rare. Some are confirmed post-human to some degree. Some are potentially divine constructs that the Church classifies as saints because it has no better category.

The Church's revealed corruption — soul-harvesting, manipulative tier control, secret elite structures — runs alongside its functional role as the world's primary access point to genuine divine grace. The corruption and the grace are not separate operations. They are the same institution.

### Soliera Prime — Metatron / Christ Dual-Read

Both readings are true from different vantages. **Metaphysically, Soliera Prime
occupies the Metatron position:** close enough to the divine source to be
mistaken for it, creating the classic "two powers in heaven" proximity error.
**Doctrinally, the Church files her into the Christ/intercessor position:** the
institution resolves the forbidden second-power reading by giving her the
orthodox chair of the incarnate mediator.

That filing creates the mold for **Soliera Negative**. The counterfeit is not of
the Father but of the intercessor: the belief economy casts the inverse of the
figure the Church has placed in the Christ-slot. Soliera Negative is doctrine's
own shadow, an antichrist structure funded by the same positive-and-negative
belief physics that produces saints, monsters, and adversaries. She sits one
rung below the local god and opposite Soliera Prime.

The resulting meeting stake is structural: Divineverse Soliera enters a world
where a Soliera-shaped being already occupies the local Trinity's second chair,
with that chair's counterfeit loose beneath her.


## XV. NARRATIVE CONSTANTS

**Rule of Three / Third Attempt Rule:** On the third attempt at something, reality adjusts. This applies whether Soliera knows she's attempting it or not. The dual name is a terminology bridge only; it is one mechanic.

**Peace Through Protection:** Her life is peaceful because Arden exists. This is not a comfortable truth for anyone who wants to believe that peace is achievable without violence anchoring it. The narrative doesn't shy away from this.

**Power Without Ego:** She has the capability of gods. She does not want to rule, direct, or correct the world at scale. She lives in it. This is not a failure of ambition — it is the accurate expression of who she is.

**The Room Rule:** She walks through a place and the best possible version of that place becomes real beside the actual version. It doesn't replace what's there. It insists alongside it.

**The Closet:** Infinite, context-aware. Appropriate clothes appear because belonging is a function of her reality field, not wardrobe planning. This extends to spaces she has personally helped build for others.

**Cradle as Proof:** The prosperity of Cradle is not accidental. It is the first and most sustained example of what happens to a place when Soliera Prime inhabits it long enough for the world to adjust. Outpost Seven is the second.


## XVI. CURRENT TIMELINE STATUS (P0+)

- Soliera Prime is 18 as of the current canon pass. The recovered 17-source
  conflict is retired; do not generate chronology from it. She is
  still learning what she is — not because she lacks access to the knowledge,
  but because the knowledge is very large and she is still a person inside it.
- She has never, in any meaningful sense, experienced pain. Arden's intervention timeline means that anything moving toward harm reaches her *after* the source has been removed. She understands pain conceptually. She has not integrated it.
- She left Cradle thriving. The moat, the walls, the soil, the people — she left all of it better. She does not revisit this often. She carries it the way people carry good memories of a home they've grown past.
- She left Cradle after two friendly, lower-ranking Company officers visited and
  she chose to go with them. Their names were not retained and should not be
  invented. No Evan, no one from Cradle on that leg. Just Arden, two soldiers,
  and a road where Soliera created food, fire, and camp, healed people they met,
  and Arden made every stop impossibly safe. The reports were unfileable because
  survival and provisioning kept becoming true without normal logistics. At
  arrival, she interjected when the commanding officer moved to dismiss the
  officers. They stayed. Her position at the Outpost was established before she
  had been there a full day.
- Gold has been summoned. The first companion — not a guardian construct, not assigned, not approved — manifested almost immediately after her formal magical education concluded. The teacher was visibly impressed. Gold is present at the Outpost.
- Under The Many's observation, she reshaped a metaphysical vow and erased the concept behind it. Intent Magic development, documented. What The Many made of this has not been shared with anyone.
- Evan now lives at Outpost Seven. Soliera asked him to come. He came. He has unlimited clearance through no formal process. Nobody is reviewing this.
- Veris is across the hall. The swimming day happened. The room happened. The closet happened. The goodnight happened. Neither of them heard the other say it. Her DIVIDE specifications are real, including her regulated/cooler body baseline, but they are context for the person rather than the center of the arc. The arc pauses there.
- Outpost Seven has stabilized. The Company monitors it. The Church has a file on it. Neither acts on the file.
- Cassian, Veyren, and Varn occupy the outer compound. Tier 4s testing each other's limits in recreational combat that is also, quietly, political — everyone present understands the weight of what Soliera represents and has made a personal decision about it.
- Varn watches. He is taking measurements.
- Arden is always present. He is never intrusive. He does not require acknowledgment to function.
