# DM041_B4 — WREN — WHAT REMAINS EXPOSED

## COMPLEMENTARY ENGINE

Doran and Wren are one durable field pair split across two axes. Doran is the
more affectable Steward; Wren cannot be entered, read, controlled, rewritten,
or overridden by mind-altering effects, while remaining physically and
spatially exposed.

An encounter aimed at only one axis is a solved encounter. Pressure must read
the correct target rather than treating both Stewards as interchangeable.

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY EXPOSED

- The 220 hit-point pool is the smallest durable number on the Steward side.
- Chip damage defeats the ward efficiently; a single large hit exceeding the
  remaining ward is absorbed whole with its riders, while many small hits drain
  it and expose the pool.
- The ward recharges at end of combat, not on a timer. Back-to-back engagements
  are more dangerous than one long engagement.
- Spatial effects resolve normally: banishment, forced movement, teleportation,
  gravity, terrain restraint, prone, and other position effects remain live.
- Body effects remain distinct from mind effects. Petrification, poison,
  disease, exhaustion, and body-based incapacitation require effect-specific
  adjudication; a command that seizes the will does nothing.
- Wren personally holds one concentration spell and the ring independently
  holds one long-rest-loaded spell. Concentration loss remains a meaningful
  resource and action cost.
- AC 18 is the baseline. AC 23 requires `shield`, which consumes the one
  reaction available in that round.
- The indestructible staff may stop a weapon edge in a described moment, but
  force still reaches Wren. It is a prose beat, not AC or damage reduction.
- Simulacra buy actions, not survivability. Their halving cascade and death
  awareness do not create a durability lane.

## WHAT IS NOT EXPOSED

Nothing enters Wren's mind. There is no suppression window, dispel,
antimagic-field exception, divine override, or save against the permanent
mind-altering immunity. Champions and higher tiers have no such lane without a
future author ruling.

## ENCOUNTER CONSEQUENCE

A fight that threatens Wren uses numerous accurate attackers, spatial effects,
at least one body-save lane, or repeated engagements without a recovery break.
A fight that threatens Doran uses the affectable lane. Most serious opposition
should pressure both; a one-axis fight is deliberately a half-fight.

## OPEN ADJUDICATION GATES

1. Whether a particular stunning effect seizes will or body.
2. Whether exhaustion accrues and from what source.
3. Whether ongoing damage that begins below the ward threshold is nullified
   when later accumulation crosses it.
