# Sera's Itinerary (Part 4: Expansion Destinations and Meta Protocol)


### LOCATION 13: EVERMEET (The Isle of the Elves)
**[MAP POSITION]** Far west, in the Trackless Sea. Accessible by Ark (the Ark can navigate to it).
**[STATUS]** NOT VISITED — Extraplanar-adjacent. The elven island home of the High Elves, a place most mortals never see.
**[TRAVEL ACCESS]** The Ark. Multiple days of sea travel from Baldur’s Gate.

**Key Data Points**
1. Evermeet is protected by the most powerful elven magic in the world. Outsiders are not permitted without explicit invitation from the Seldarine (elven pantheon) or the elven monarchy. This has never been a meaningful restriction to the girls.
2. The elves of Evermeet have been aware of the girls’ activities. Their assessment is complex: the girls’ power is acknowledged; the girls’ methods (infrastructure, consent-based architecture) align more closely with elven values than most divine interventions in history; and Soliera’s silence as authority resonates with certain elven philosophical traditions.
3. Sera and Soliera have both had contact with individual elves — Drizzt (partial), the Silverymoon integration community, individual High Forest wood elves — but never with elven civilization at its source. Evermeet is where elven culture is most concentrated and least adapted for external contact.
4. The Seldarine (Corellon Larethian and the elven gods) are aware of Soliera. Their response has been silence. Not hostile silence — simply uncharacteristic quiet. The girls visiting Evermeet might be the event that ends the silence.
5. Evermeet’s invitation, if extended, will come in the form of a light at sea that the Ark’s constructs will notice first. Following the light is not required. But Ember will already be pointing at it.


### LOCATION 14: CHULT / PORT NYANZARU
**[MAP POSITION]** Far south, across the sea from Baldur’s Gate. Tropical continent. Port Nyanzaru is the primary coastal city.
**[STATUS]** VISITED — T527 landing; that played record is closed in DM038_N1. T537–T550.0 narrative and the recorded T550.1 departure boundary are in DM038_N2/N4/N5/N6; the current live edge is DM038_L.
**[TRAVEL ACCESS]** The Ark. Significant sea voyage south from Baldur’s Gate.

**Local fame version:** Chult knows the Sanctum and divine trio, but distance,
merchant retelling, and local priorities make the version less intimate than
Waterdeep's. Courtesy and relationship still matter because recognition never
substitutes for consent, trust, or a reason to cooperate. Doran and Wren remain
individually unknown.

**Key Data Points**
1. Chult is relevant to the tour because of the Death Curse: a magical affliction that was causing raised dead to waste away across Faerûn. The curse targeted everyone who had been resurrected — which includes a significant number of people the Sanctum has blessed, healed, or raised over the course of T0–T515. The curse has been resolved by this timeline, but the source (the Soulmonger, in the Tomb of Annihilation) and its creators (the atropal and Acererak) left permanent scars on the region.
2. The Tomb of Annihilation — the dungeon where the Death Curse was resolved — is somewhere in Chult’s interior. The specific location is known to Port Nyanzaru’s merchant prince council. The girls visiting Chult without addressing the Tomb is narratively possible but creates a “isn’t someone going to ask about that” tension.
3. Port Nyanzaru is a city of merchant princes — seven powerful trading families share governance in a rotating council system. They are pragmatic, welcoming to outsiders who can pay or contribute, and deeply aware of Chult’s unique position as a source of materials (dinosaur-related goods, rare herbs, deep jungle resources) that nowhere else in the Realms provides.
4. Dinosaur racing is Port Nyanzaru’s primary entertainment and a significant economic driver. Sera’s presence at a race would be an event.
5. The Tomb’s resolution left a specific magical echo in the region: a zone of heightened necrotic energy in the jungle interior that is slowly dissipating. It will be fully gone in approximately twenty years. In the meantime, undead in the jungle interior are more numerous than they should be.

### DM META NOTES — PERMANENT REFERENCE

**The Tour’s Emotional Arc**
The itinerary is structured to move through three emotional registers across its geographic flow:

* **REGISTER 1 (North): Comfort and consequence.** The girls are at home in the North. They see what they’ve built. They see what it cost and who benefited. They encounter the scale of their influence from the outside — as others experience it. The goal is not self-congratulation but perspective.
* **REGISTER 2 (Central — Silverymoon to Waterdeep): Complexity and accountability.** Here the girls encounter the full spectrum of responses to their existence: academic analysis, diplomatic calculation, genuine friendship, and the specific weight of decisions that cannot be undone. The Undermountain sequence is the emotional peak of this register.
* **REGISTER 3 (South and Extraplanar): Discovery and the unknown.** The girls leave their established zone of influence. Everything here is first contact. The extraplanar stops push beyond any framework that exists in the established world. The tour ends facing something genuinely new.

**Character Function by Location**
* **SOLIERA:** Infrastructure audit lens. She is checking what she built. She is silent but present in every beat. Her moments of visible engagement (Silverymoon’s scholar’s question, the restricted Candlekeep section) should feel earned and rare.
* **SERA:** Social and combat lens. She is the one who talks to Luskan. She is the one who wants to see the play. She is the one who answers Telden Rau’s blunt question with an equally blunt answer. Her joy-first framework means she’s the most legible character to most NPCs — she’s the one who seems most like them.
* **EMBER:** Moral and philosophical lens. Every location has an Ember beat. She asks the question no one thought to ask. Her “elder abuse” framework (Curse of Strahd) is the template: apparently absurd, internally consistent, impossible to fully refute. Let her lead in locations where the expected questions have been asked.
* **VELIN:** Logistics and political lens. She is already at most locations before the girls arrive. She is managing five things when the girls get there. She is doing her job. This is her tour as much as theirs — she built most of these relationships.
* **NIV:** Home base continuity. He checks in via construct messenger. His reports to the girls should be brief and calm regardless of what is actually happening. If Niv sounds alarmed, something significant is wrong.
* **THE ENGINEER:** The unnamed Mirabar engineer appears at Mirabar and sends notes to every other location. Her presence is felt even when she’s not present: her projects, her questions, her refusal to be impressed by things that aren’t engineered correctly. Do not assign her Pale's name.

**Tone Calibration**
The tour should never feel like a victory lap. The girls didn’t do things to be celebrated — they did them because the problems required doing. The celebration that other people attach to their actions is texture, not validation. When characters express gratitude or awe, the girls’ responses should be warm, matter-of-fact, and slightly confused about why anyone would be surprised.

The comedy of the tour is the inverse of cosmic horror: everywhere the girls go, something enormous is being treated as normal. Sera sitting on Soliera at a diplomatic meeting is normal. Ember eating diplomatic desserts during a sensitive political briefing is normal. Constructs reorganizing a city’s traffic patterns at 3am because Sera slept there is normal. The NPCs’ responses to this normalcy are where the comedy lives.

The genuine tension of the tour comes from whichever destinations are actually
selected. The Primeverse portal, the large thing in the forest, and the forged
manuscript's coordinate remain optional material that requires attention rather
than raw power. Slarkrethel belongs to the Storm King's Court endgame route, not
to a generic itinerary mystery.
