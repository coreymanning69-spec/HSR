# DM002_1 — DIVINEVERSE SPINE (T0–T260)

# ARC 1: ORIGIN & FIRST MIGRATIONS (T0–T50)
#CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-DEASHI #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-YUIUM #CHAR-VELIN #CHAR-MARRIK #CHAR-HOTHGAR
#LOC-SPINE-OF-THE-WORLD #LOC-FIRST-VILLAGE #LOC-SANCTUM
#CONCEPT-YUIUM-FRAGMENTATION #CONCEPT-THE-REMAKING #CONCEPT-SANCTUM-WHEEL-ARCHITECTURE
#CONCEPT-CONSTRUCT-INFRASTRUCTURE #THEME-DIVINE-DOMESTICITY


**Where:** Spine of the World. Frozen north. First village south of the mountain.

**Why:** Soliera and Deashi emerge and walk south. No directive — discovery in motion.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Quiet wonder at mortal fragility. Learning what worship means without having sought it. Warmth that precedes policy.
- *Deashi:* Silent and watchful. First violence is clean and purposeful. No commentary needed.
- *Reva (pre-Sera):* Devoted before she understands what she's devoted to. The Remaking is an act she chooses, not something done to her.
- *Marrik:* Practical optimism. Sees logistics where others see gods. Quietly ambitious, no malice in it.
- *Velin:* Young and eager, absorbing everything. The diplomatic voice forming in real time.
- *Supporting cast:* Hothgar is consolidating tribes and not asking questions. Thorin and Cale provide security and then drift — they're not built for permanence. Early villagers oscillate between reverence and normal human need.

**Compression note:** This span is approximately 10:1 compressed against played
sessions. Horror survives the summary more easily than warmth and witness-
interiority; exact recovery belongs in DM005_0 and the Rev8 reread deltas.

**Sanctum Population:** T0: 3 (Yuium, Soliera, Deashi) → T30: ~30 → T50: low hundreds, construction underway

**Ember:** Does not exist.

# ARC 2: EXPANSION NORTH (Ten-Towns / Bryn Shander)
#CHAR-LETHA #CHAR-KORIN #CHAR-AURIL #CHAR-NIV-SAIFFAR
#LOC-TEN-TOWNS #LOC-BRYN-SHANDER
#CONCEPT-AURIL-REDEMPTION-ARC #CONCEPT-FIRST-WORSHIP #THEME-POWER-AS-MERCY


**Where:** Ten-Towns, Bryn Shander, Icewind Dale.

**Why:** Auril's predation escalating. Cold cult incidents drawing attention north.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Present but not driving. Observes the north's suffering and moves to correct it without announcement.
- *Sera:* Unimpressed by mortal greed and cult mechanics. The violence is functional, not exciting yet.
- *Letha:* Newcomer. Night of the Doll is her entry point. Still processing what she has joined.
- *Korin:* Joins after Letha's recommendation. Grounds the group in practical human knowledge of the region.
- *Niv-Saiffar:* Functioning as the Sanctum's Firemind — logistical nerve center, not field presence.
- *Supporting cast:* Northern tribes consolidating. No single dramatic loyalty moment — a slow gravitational pull toward the Sanctum.

**Sanctum Population:** Growing through T100. ~300,000 by arc close — monster races and humans fully integrated.

**Ember:** Does not exist.

# ARC 3: TALIANDRA / SOLIERE-DZAN REVERSAL (T51–T100)

#CONCEPT-DIVINE-CLIENT-STATE #THEME-JUDGMENT-AS-SILENCE


**Where:** Underdark. Soliere-Dzan — faerie-fire gates, house banners, Lolth's temple.

**Why:** Taliandra was captured, exiled, and survived. Sera offers her city back.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Silent authority. Her presence alone restructures the political calculus underground. No speech required.
- *Sera:* Engaged. The politics of the drow are interesting to her. She enforces the duel's terms and removes the dracolich as an afterthought.
- *Taliandra:* Determined. The offer to reclaim her city is not something she hesitates on. The duel is a statement, not a gamble.
- *Jarlaxle:* Calculates, recognizes the situation, steps back. Mutual recognition — no interference.
- *Lolth:* Backed into acknowledgment. She cannot deny Taliandra's claim once Sera is standing there. Pragmatic capitulation.
- *Supporting cast:* House factions recalibrate overnight. The population does not flee — they watch what power looks like when it enforces instead of threatens.

**Sanctum Population:** No significant surge from this arc — population node is underground and politically aligned, not absorbed.

**Ember:** Does not exist.

**Where:** Waterdeep — beach, tavern, sewers, Neverember estate, Frostfell.

**Why:** Beach trip. Pell mentions Waterdeep. Sera says yes immediately. Escalates from there.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* A rare arc of relative softness before correction becomes unavoidable. The beach is real enjoyment. The Burning & the Cost incident shifts her from passive to acting.
- *Sera:* Delighted by Waterdeep. Treats the city like a theme park until the Auril cult children appear. Then the delight turns into something colder and more precise.
- *Pell:* Hit by a Mind Flayer trap — the personal cost of being close to divinity in a dangerous city. Soliera undoes it; Sera takes offense.
- *Kessla:* Background presence. Exists in the guild node before she is formally introduced. This is her origin arc.
- *Vajra Safahr:* Teleports in expecting a confrontation. Leaves with a diplomatic framework. Adjusts faster than most.
- *Supporting cast:* Neverember is blessed and remade — a demonstration, not a reward. Xanathar yields, which is the only thing Sera required. Auril cult members learn the cost of using children.

**Sanctum Population:** Waterdeep embassy established. Population node added — not absorbed into Sanctum census.

**Ember:** Does not exist.

#CONCEPT-SILVERYMOON-ALLIANCE #CONCEPT-MOONBRIDGE-SALVATION #THEME-GODS-WANDER


**Where:** Silverymoon, eastern interior. Moonbridge, High Court.

**Why:** Traveling. Diplomatic expansion northward. Shar makes it complicated.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Curious about the Moonbridge. The encounter with Mystra is one of the few times another divine entity talks to her like a peer rather than backing away.
- *Sera:* Treats the arrival alarms as fanfare. Flirts with both Alustriel and Mystra. Genuinely enjoys this arc more than most.
- *Alustriel:* Oscillates between awe, political instinct, and maternal hospitality. Adjusts faster than expected.
- *Mystra:* Manifests, takes a beautiful shape, treats Soliera with unmistakable deference. Warns Shar correctly that she's underestimating them.
- *Drizzt:* Witnesses the destruction, recognizes non-mortal energy. Alustriel later sends him a direct message. His trajectory redirects from this arc forward.
- *Supporting cast:* High mages split between kneeling and pointing spellbooks. Half cry. None enter the zone around Alustriel's manor. Shar takes the hit, leaves. Smart call.

**Sanctum Population:** No surge. Silverymoon becomes diplomatic node — not absorbed.

**Ember:** Does not exist.
