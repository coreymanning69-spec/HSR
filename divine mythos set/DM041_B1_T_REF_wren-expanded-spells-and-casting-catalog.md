> **Her reaction is now the tightest slot on the sheet.** Seven options contend
> for one reaction, and *shield* and *counterspell* are both usually the right
> answer. This mirrors Doran's reaction asymmetry deliberately: both of them can
> do enormous work on their own turn and must choose exactly one thing between

**Crown of Stars is permanently active.** Seven motes, no slot, no
concentration, refreshed daily. A bonus-action mote is **+14 for 4d12 radiant**,
spent on hit or miss. It is also the most visible thing about her — a ring of
stars over her head at all times, which is intentionally showy and does nothing
to help her hide.

**Portal Shear is not reflectable by Reflective Carapace.** Its force-lane
resolution proceeds from the source specification; no reflection interaction is available.

**Spell access.** Wren knows every normal published Cleric and Sorcerer/Divine
Soul spell. *Wish*, Karsus-origin magic, and individually NPC-exclusive spells
are excluded. Named spell rows are reference examples, not a closed access list;
resolve each cast using the 2e or 5e version selected at the table.

### Permanent immunities

These are properties of Wren after the T532 transformation, not item effects.
They cannot be dispelled, suppressed, or removed.

Wren is immune to **all mind-altering effects**, regardless of spell school,
damage type, source, wording, or whether the effect uses the charmed condition.
Nothing may alter, suppress, override, control, rewrite, disable, enter, read,
or involuntarily access her mind.

The lock includes, but is not limited to:

- Charmed.
- Frightened as a condition; Wren still experiences ordinary fear.
- Compelled action, domination, confusion, mental paralysis, or mental stunning.
- Possession.
- *Feeblemind* and equivalent ability/personality destruction.
- Unconsented access to or alteration of thought, memory, identity, emotion,
  perception, belief, desire, or decision-making.

This is a mind boundary, not blanket immunity to geometry. A non-mental effect
that physically cages, banishes, relocates, or strands her—such as *forcecage*
or *maze*—resolves under its own spatial rules.

## GEAR

### Sanctum Black-Bird Sigil — arcane and divine focus

Amulet, pendant, necklace, sigil, Insight pendant, and black-bird sigil all
route to this one current object. At T532 Soliera's personal restructuring consolidates
the earlier T522 Insight pendant into the full sigil rather than creating a
second worn focus.

- +5 to Wren's INT saving throws.
- +5 to Wren's WIS saving throws.
- 75-point all-type damage ward with full rider immunity while it holds.
  Burning, freezing, acid, and condition progression do not advance through
  a warded damage instance.
- If one damage instance is no greater than the remaining ward, subtract it
  from the ward normally. If it exceeds the remaining ward, set the ward to
  **0** and nullify that entire instance, including overflow and attached
  riders. The **next separate hit or damage instance** resolves normally.
- The ward recharges at the end of combat.
- +15 stored sorcery points.
- +1 spell save DC.
- +1 to spell attack rolls.
- Sealed *imprisonment*, 1/long rest, full action, visible tell.
- Force lane: one independent cast per spell per long rest of *wall of force,
  Bigby's hand, Otiluke's resilient sphere, Mordenkainen's private sanctum,
  forcecage,* and *Otiluke's freezing sphere*.
- May serve as Wren's visible casting focus; never required.

The sigil may be used as a visible focus, but Wren does not mechanically need a
focus, material component, costly component, or channeling tool. Her casting
route is intrinsic to the grant.

The +1 Arcane Grimoire and Pearl of Power are not part of the current T532
runtime. Their physical disposition is not supplied and must not be inferred.

### Left ring — elective Silent and Still

The ring permanently grants access to Silent and Still; it does not apply them
automatically. Wren elects either or both at the start of a cast. They cost zero,
use no action, and do not count toward the turn tax because the ring performs
them rather than Wren's metamagic engine.

Election requires intent. Surprise, panic, or reflex can leave the effort absent
until training makes it native. When elected, she can cast undetected in
conversation or inside her own *silence*. The same asymmetry works in
*darkness*. Doran's helm now lets him see normally through magical and
nonmagical darkness to 120 feet, so Wren may use the spell as a screen he can
fight inside. The helm is not truesight.

### Right ring — concentration

**Ring of Concentration** remains a working name. It holds one concentration
spell. Wren may load or swap it only during a **long rest**, or during relaxed,
safe, out-of-combat downtime that Corey explicitly treats as the equivalent of
a long rest for gameplay. It cannot be swapped during combat, imminent danger,
or another tense active scene.

- Damage cannot break it; no concentration save is ever made for the ring.
- Printed duration does not apply. The spell lasts while loaded.
- Wren's unconsciousness, 0 HP, or death does not end it.
- Wren's own concentration remains free for one second spell.
- Any end-of-*haste* lethargy is contained inside the relaxed long-rest swap and
  never creates a later combat-round tax.

Default load is *haste* on Doran unless play establishes another choice.
*Stoneskin* is the obvious high-defense alternative and should be chosen as an
in-character decision, not silently assumed.

### Weave-sight glasses

- Read magical structure: spell architecture, enchantment seams, active-effect
  lattices, and Ember's substrate edits rendered as Weave-legible information.
- Extended darkvision.
- Not Truesight; they do not reveal mundane ambushes, lies, pits, or nonmagical
  hidden creatures.
- Invisible creatures appear as green gleams or shapes. Wren targets them with
  no disadvantage, and they gain no advantage against her solely from
  invisibility.
- The gleam shows shape, location, and count, but not face, expression, identity,
  or an Insight-readable presentation.
- Wren cannot see through magical darkness, but she reads the shapes inside it
  and always recognizes the darkness as magical.
- The glasses expose that an illusion, magical disguise, transformation, or
  Ethereal effect is magical work, but they do not see through it, reveal the
  underlying form, identify the creature, or grant Ethereal sight. They are not
  Truesight.
