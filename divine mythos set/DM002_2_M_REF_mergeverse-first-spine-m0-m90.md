# ARC M1: OAK CITY ARRIVAL (M0–M56)
#CHAR-TITAN-CONSTRUCT #CHAR-COMMANDER-BUCKY #CHAR-OLD-MARA #CHAR-REEVE-TAMSIN
#LOC-OAK-CITY #LOC-TAVERN
#CONCEPT-OAK-CITY-ARRIVAL #CONCEPT-BRING-ME-YOUR-KING #THEME-DIVINE-DOMESTICITY


**Where:** Oak City, Primeverse. Asphalt plaza. A modern world with no framework for what just arrived.

**Why:** The Undermountain portal opened. They stepped through. No plan — discovery in motion, same as T0.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Studies the rules of this reality from the moment her feet touch asphalt. Silent. Cooling the pavement underfoot is the first thing she does — passive care, zero announcement.
- *Sera:* Calls the world "slow and cute." Immediately at home in a place that has no idea what she is. Demands lunch because lunch is holy. Bucky submitting delights her completely.
- *Ember:* Melts a mailbox. Apologizes immediately. This is still her register — accidentally breaking things and being genuinely sorry. But she is Sera's size now, which makes the apology more alarming for bystanders.
- *Bucky:* Survives by recognizing the game before it kills him. Kneels and offers himself as "King." The right call. Becomes the primary liaison because of it.
- *Old Mara:* Performs the white-cloth rite of hospitality. The only person present with a cultural framework for divine arrival — she reaches for the closest thing she has.
- *Supporting cast:* Titan Construct bows to Soliera, confirming hierarchy to every watcher. Reeve Tamsin, Jory, Sela, Haim approach cautiously. Church convoy arrives to assess the theological implications. City mood shifts from terror to paralyzing awe in hours.

**Sanctum Population:** Combined Divineverse + Primeverse/Oak City total: ~1.2M by M0–M80 (constructs and personnel both counted).

**Ember at M0:** Physical equal to Sera. Obsidian skin, kintsugi fracture lines (heat-spectrum; coal-red at rest, gold/amber at elevation), wildfire hair. Accidental heat flares ongoing but reducing. Emotional state: soft curiosity, still processing a world she didn't expect.

**Where:** Neon City Slums, Primeverse. Densely impoverished urban labyrinth past a fortified wall.

**Why:** Exploration. Passive divine correction. The wall was in the way.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Passive healing ripple cures sickness, disease, and malnutrition as she walks. No announcement. The slum simply becomes healthier in her wake.
- *Sera:* Treats the slums like an amusement park. Completely oblivious to systemic poverty as a concept — not cruel, just outside her reference frame. Amara corrects her orbit gently.
- *Ember:* Asks devastatingly simple, innocent questions about why people are forced to live like this. The questions are the intervention — they don't require answers to land.
- *Amara Windbreaker:* Ember's grounding human hand in the chaos. First mortal in this world to form a genuine connection with the divine rather than observing it.
- *Cassian:* Realizes the Tier System doesn't apply to these entities. They are category-breakers. Begins calculating implications rather than countermeasures.
- *Executive F:* Begins calculating the inevitable collapse of Company authority in the face of true divinity. Veyren watches Sera's movements on monitors and recognizes an apex predator's body language.
- *Supporting cast:* Risa and Tehilum represent the slum's survivalist hierarchy. Hale aligns his church faction to "do no harm and observe." Malachar's faction begins splitting. Slum population shifts loyalty overnight from Company/Church to Sanctum.

**Sanctum Population:** Divineverse side growing. Primeverse integration beginning — post-scarcity logistics overwriting local poverty.

**Ember at M57–M150:** Fully settled in physical form. Accidental heat flares effectively resolved. Begins building relationship with Amara as first genuine peer-bond in this world — mirrors the Mira dynamic from Undermountain.
