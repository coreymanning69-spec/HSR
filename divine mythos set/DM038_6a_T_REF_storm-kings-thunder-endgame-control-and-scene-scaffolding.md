## Storm Court Procedure Flow

Run Maelstrom as legitimate court contact, not as a dungeon room the girls dominate.

| Step | Scene function | Divineverse handling |
|------|----------------|----------------------|
| **1. Standing and petition** | The conch gives the arrivals standing inside storm giant jurisdiction. | Doran gives names, origin, and purpose to Uthor; Wren carries the evidence packet. The petition is addressed to Serissa. Their mistakes remain theirs to repair. |
| **2. Serissa opens the hearing** | The regent chooses the room, audience, and order of testimony. | Serissa speaks first and decides whether Mirran, Nym, and the advisor remain. The girls honor that choice unless an immediate attack makes containment necessary. |
| **3. Claims entered in order** | Separate evidence from conclusion. | Present: Hekaton lives and was abducted; Kraken Society agents carried the chain; Neri's murder was engineered; “Ymira” is Iymrith. Give Serissa the facts before asking her to accept the verdict. |
| **4. Serissa tests the record** | The court answers the claim under its own authority. | Serissa questions; Uthor supplies security records and his knowledge of Neri's recovery; Mirran and Nym answer for what they were told and did. Iymrith may respond but does not control the floor. |
| **5. Exposure and containment** | The disguised advisor becomes legible as the architect of the fracture. | Serissa orders the arrest or exposure. Sera enforces the boundary; Ember prevents the sisters' compromised grief from being flattened into Iymrith's culpability; Doran and Wren keep their evidence separate from inherited small-folk blame. |
| **6. Rescue mandate** | Convert the finding into lawful action. | Serissa names Hekaton's recovery as the court's priority, turns over the Golden Goose lead, and directs Uthor's support. The girls may move immediately, but they act with the regent's mandate rather than as replacement sovereigns. |

**Guardrail:** Serissa is not weak, decorative, or replaced. The girls give her missing truth and remove the criminal obstruction. They do not become storm giant sovereigns by proxy.


## Scene Scaffolding - Endgame Sites

### Maelstrom

**Arrival beat:** The party enters a court where everyone is waiting to learn what everyone else believes. Deference to Serissa exists, but it has become measurable: courtiers look before bowing, delay before answering, and treat silence as a political position. Uthor is the one steady point. Iymrith is present as the advisor whose calm has been mistaken for wisdom.

**Court pressure:** Mirran and Nym should be played as daughters inside factions, not as faction banners with no interior life. They are angry, grieving, and deceived. Their positions are dangerous because they contain partial truths. Ember's sort holds that complexity in place: compromised, not culpable in the same category as Iymrith.

**Critical moment - the reveal:** Iymrith's exposure should make the court understand that the crisis was engineered. Neri's death, Hekaton's disappearance, anti-small-folk framing, Serissa's isolation, and the sisters' rivalry were not separate facts. They were one architecture. Soliera finds the architecture interesting. The interest ends when the architecture is fully understood.

**Resolution shape:** Serissa retains authority. Iymrith loses court cover and exits, is contained, or is forced into the finale path according to live pacing. The Kraken Society chain becomes actionable. Current route, unresolved court pressure, and durable court or artifact state → the active live ledger.
