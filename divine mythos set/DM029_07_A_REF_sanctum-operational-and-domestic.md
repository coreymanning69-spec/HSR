THE SANCTUM: OPERATIONAL REFERENCE BIBLE

Status: Post-Scarcity Utopia / Opt-In Theocracy Population: ~2.1 Million (Expanding) Governance Model: Divine Corporation (Executive Board + Hyper-Competent Administration)

IV. THE LEGAL FRAMEWORK (The Consequence Zone)

The Sanctum is an Opt-In society. You enter voluntarily; you are removed if you break the code.

1. The Prime Directive: No Initiation of Force.

    Rule: You cannot be the aggressor.

    Enforcement: Sera’s Knife-Thought Edge detects the initiator. If you start a fight, you lose (usually your head).

Exception: Self-Defense is Legal. If someone attacks you, you are permitted to kill them to survive. Sera distinguishes between "Malice" and "Survival".

2. The Third Rail: Protection of Innocents.

    Rule: Harm to children or the vulnerable is instant death.

    Mechanism: There is no "Self-Defense" against a child. This intent triggers immediate erasure by Sera.

3. The Thought Filter: Actionable Intent.

    Rule: You are judged on Intent to Harm, not Thoughts of Desire.

    Nuance: A man lusting after Soliera is "amusing/validating" (Safe). A man plotting to stab Soliera is "Threat" (Dead).

V. THE ECONOMY & LABOR (Post-Scarcity)

    The Labor: "Hundreds of constructs" handle the grunt work (mining, farming, building).

The Talent: Citizens act as the "Soul" of the workforce—artisans, teachers, cooks, specialized crafters.

The Currency: While gold exists, the true currency is Novelty and Status. Access to the "Chilling Room" or proximity to the Triad is worth more than money.

The Supply: Infinite. Soliera provides; Niv distributes. There is no scarcity.

VI. THE USER EXPERIENCE (Therapeutic Theocracy)

    The Vibe: It is not a military camp; it is a Spa.

    Daily Life: Public baths, free food, education, weekends off (Soliera's decree).

Religion: "Visible Theology." You don't pray; you watch the Goddess walk by. Faith is replaced by eyesight.

The "No-Go" Zone: The Antechamber. The only place forbidden to the public, guarded by the conceptual horror of Deashi.

This is the machine.

    Input: Refugees/Wanderers.

    Process: Therapeutic healing via Stewards -> Integration via Work/Culture -> Protection via Sera/Deashi.

    Output: A loyal, thriving, artistically vibrant population that worships Soliera because she "turned the heat on."


VII. THE SANCTUM HOUSE — DOMESTIC OPERATIONAL CANON (M182 Lock)

### Confirmed T550.2 house texture and locations

The house repairs damage but not use: linens remain perfect and surfaces clean,
while hand-worn dishes remain in bunk-frame corners and staff brush around chalk
drawings rather than restoring them. Goldenfields is publicly broadcast through
Marrik's east-hall projection, with a live household audience. The grass courtyard
is a seamless mountain-side exterior: sunlit cropped grass beneath real snow,
low wide trees, and worn pale-stone benches.

The Sanctum house — Soliera and Sera's residence, where Ember sleeps, where Velin runs the kitchen, where Niv keeps the atelier — operates by its own household rules. These overlap with but do not duplicate the Sanctum-wide policy above. Sealed at M182 (the Morning After). All apply going forward.

1. The Sound Curtain.
   ~80ft perimeter passive silence enchantment. Self-enforcing. Localized to the estate. Town goes about its business outside; the household has its own audio space inside. Always on. Not gated by ritual. The town does not hear what is not for the town.

2. Velin's Second-Pot Rule.
   Velin does not sit at the table until the second pot of coffee is brewing. House rule. Functions as a soft-priesthood marker — the cook holds the room until the room is settled, then takes a place. Apron-off transition is the marker of the rule's release. Restated explicitly at M182.

3. The Atelier (Niv's Studio).
   Hand-makes the girls' outfits when Soliera does not manifest them directly. The atelier is NOT magical — it is deliberate craft. That is the point. Outfits made there carry a different weight than manifested clothing because they were chosen, cut, and stitched by hand. The girls notice the difference. They wear both. (Niv's red-dragon paperwork dominion sits at §II — same dragon, separate signature.)

4. The Chickens.
   Eight in number. Named and renamed by Ember according to the day and her
   mood. Kitchen supply chain. No fixed roster should be recorded. Per the M182
   lock: the chickens are the household's most reliably uneventful institution.

5. The Dressing Ritual.
   Locked: Sera dresses Soliera on the bedroom floor before either leaves the room. Once Soliera is dressed, Sera can wander nude through the house. Ember wanders nude until somebody wrangles her into clothes. Modesty inside the house is a public-facing courtesy, not an interior household constraint. Guests and extended household members (Veyren, Letha, Cassian) adapt to this grammar; they do not import outside norms into it. M182 prose is the canonical execution beat in DM021_2.

6. House Interior Dimensionality.
   Sanctum logic applies inside the house. Bigger inside than outside. Upper-floor windows from Pell's original wish. Capacity is not a household constraint — guest count never strains the structure. The room you need is the room that's there.

VIII. SANCTUM STAFF — FLAVOR RECORD (Steward-Era)

Evergreen character notes and Thayan-arc-adjacent beats for Sanctum senior staff, recorded here because they carry no specific T-spine anchor and apply across the operational era.

**Niv-Saiffar**

The whole staff watched the Thayan arc on large projection scrying. Niv was standing in the back. When it was over, nobody spoke for about thirty seconds. Then Niv said the exact number of spell slots expended. Someone laughed. The room breathed again.

Niv ran a post-deployment audit after the Thayan operation without being asked — seventeen items, fifteen already handled by Velin, two he fixed himself. He didn't leave a note.

Niv has a standing meeting with Pell every fifth day. It was Pell's idea. He thought it was unnecessary. He hasn't missed one.

**The Hidden Record.** Niv is ultimately responsible for the authoritative
T/M/TR/P-beat record, with Velin producing and maintaining it beside him. The
records are exact, not fallible character testimony: Niv does not make mistakes.
The girls do not know their activities are being organized into the record.
Neither Niv nor Velin announces the joke or exposes it through divine dialogue;
it sits quietly in the corpus in the same family as Sera's Itinerary.

**Velin**

Velin's version of a compliment is completing a task one step before you expected to need it done. Doran figured this out at T521. Wren at T534. She has never confirmed the theory.

She's started running Ark logistics at the morning staff breakfast. Niv adjusted his schedule to arrive before she starts. He says the tea is better in the morning.

**Pell**

Pell keeps a handwritten list of every Sanctum member who's had a hard month. She's never shared it. Twice she's told Niv that a certain person might need a lighter load for a week. Niv has never asked why.

After the Thayan battle-scrying, three Sanctum members came to Pell's office. She'd seen it coming. The chairs were already pulled out.

**Kessla**

Kessla doesn't arrange to be in crowds when Sera returns. She just ends up there. Sera has never not found her.

The principle Kessla runs her network on: the most useful thing you can know is what someone is about to ask before they ask it. The Sanctum never asks twice. Malharon and Daire have both internalized it without being told.

**Marrik**

Marrik and Velin were the ones who noticed the Triad had gone quiet for twelve hours during the Thayan arc. They didn't escalate — they knew the difference between quiet because something went wrong and quiet because Soliera is working. They waited. They were right.

Marrik keeps the field deployment logs in a format that looks informal — shorthand, margin notes — but reconstructs perfectly under any audit. It's intentional. He learned early that neat paperwork gets copied by people who haven't earned the context.
