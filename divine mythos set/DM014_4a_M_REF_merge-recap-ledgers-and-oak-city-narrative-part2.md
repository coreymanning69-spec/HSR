  * *Divine Bleed* — lingering normalization after presence withdraws.
  * *Witness Belief* — civic compliance multiplier; ritual → resilience.

* **Primeverse vs Divineverse shifts**

  * Prime leans rules/tech/optics; Divine leans etiquette/awe/logistics.
  * Surveillance blurs under "regard"; outcomes reframe jurisdiction.

* **Sanctum appearance/provisioning**

  * Arrives like a utility: lanes, food/water lines, clinics, fiber, stewards.
  * "No draft—only volunteers"; blessings bind by use, not prestige.
