                        SERA - DIVINE ENFORCER
                    The Velvet Spiral / The Knife-Thought Edge

SERA
Celestial (Divine Singularity), Lawful Good (Divine Logic)
CR 33 / Tier 5 Entity (Outside Conventional Scaling)

> Architecture: Sera = literal Vav/Son of the Name (structural office, not bodily gender),
> Lion in the Manifest Circle and Cross-Layer Bridge, primary Jungian Shadow, and Freudian
> Id in the separate psychoanalytic overlay. She is a layer-2 manifestation (Reva remade
> T10) and member of the Manifest Trio. Full structure: DM042_0.

# PHYSICAL STATISTICS

Armor Class: 25 (Divine Form, ontologically realer than attacks)
Hit Points: **900** in a literal boss fight Sera has explicitly consented to;
outside that mode, ordinary consent/singularity rules govern and survival is
not rolled
Damage Reduction: **15**
Damage Threshold: **30**
Speed: Variable (60 ft. walk, Reality Shear allows instant repositioning)

# CORE ABILITIES

CONSENTING BOSS-FIGHT CHASSIS
When Sera explicitly accepts a literal mechanical boss encounter, she
pre-consents to HP damage that passes the printed AC, DR, DT, resistance, and
immunity rules. Track **900 HP**. Damage instances below DT 30 deal nothing;
qualifying instances are reduced by DR 15. At 0 HP the consenting encounter
chassis is defeated. Sera is not ontologically erased, permanently killed, or
separated from Soliera by that mechanical result. Conditions, forced movement,
mind alteration, and other agency-removing effects still fail unless Sera
separately consents to them.

Saving Throws: All +15 (minimum)
Skills: Acrobatics +20, Athletics +20, Intimidation +19, Perception +17, Performance +19, Persuasion +19
Damage Resistances: All non-divine damage
Damage Immunities: Poison, Psychic, Necrotic
Condition Immunities: All conditions (charmed, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, stunned)
Senses: Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 27
Languages: All spoken languages (Omniglot - learns any spoken language instantly upon hearing it; it does not grant written-symbol interpretation)
Challenge: CR 33 benchmark / Tier 5 Singularity. CR 33 is the mechanical
translation label used by this sheet, not a claim that ordinary encounter
budgeting can measure or balance Sera.

# PASSIVE ABILITIES

**Velvet Spiral / detection lock:** Nothing is cast. Wren's Weave-sight glasses read
nothing from the Velvet Spiral because it is the honest consequence of Sera's total
ease, not a spell, effect, or working. A blank magical read is correct evidence, not
a detection failure.

ONTOLOGICAL SUPERIORITY
Sera is categorically "realer" than baseline mortals and most magical effects.
- Mortal-tier attacks (non-divine weapons, spells below 9th level) automatically fail to affect her unless she consents
- She cannot be moved, grappled, restrained, or displaced by any effect below divine-tier
- Terrain and environmental hazards have no effect on her
- She does not need to breathe, eat, drink, or sleep

KNIFE-THOUGHT EDGE
[CANON TIMELINE GATE: KTE exists only at T114.5+. Before T114.5, use Sera's overwhelming speed, instinct, and Seraphim architecture, but do not name or structure the beat as Knife-Thought Edge.]
Sera acts on intent, not action. She cannot be surprised and always acts first in initiative regardless of rolls. When a creature intends harm toward her, Soliera, Ember, or her protected mortals, she perceives and reacts before the action manifests physically.
- Automatic initiative 30
- Cannot be surprised
- Opportunity attacks trigger on hostile intent, not movement

VELVET SPIRAL (Charm Aura)
All non-divine creatures within 60 ft. of Sera must make a DC 25 Wisdom saving throw at the start of their turn:
[5e MECHANIC NOTE: This save represents resistance to divine-biological / aesthetic compulsion, not a magical charm spell. The aura hijacks reverence, attraction, protectiveness, and social-instinct pressure through Sera's existence itself.]
- First failure: Charmed - creature regards Sera with reverence and cannot attack her
- Second failure: Enthralled - creature can take no actions except to stare/approach Sera
- Third failure: Emotionally Unmade - creature collapses, overwhelmed by divine presence

SPECIAL EXCEPTIONS:
- Children (under age 13) automatically succeed and approach without fear
- Chosen/Divine agents save with advantage
- High Wisdom creatures (20+) can resist for 1d4 rounds before succumbing
- Sera can suppress this aura at will

INVIOLABLE BODY MANIFESTATION
Sera can only be affected by attacks, spells, or effects if she permits them. This is the native Inviolable Body of the T10 Remaking, a function of divine architecture rather than a defensive ability she activates.
- If Sera does not consent to an effect, it categorically fails
- This applies to all damage, conditions, forced movement, and reality-warping
- Soliera's direct commands override Sera's consent

This physical law is separate from Sera's First No: the social and moral refusal Soliera teaches at T12.5. DM030_1 owns that distinction.

INFINITE DENSITY
Sera controls her own mass at will. She weighs roughly 90 lb only when she
chooses to exist at that weight; otherwise she is immovable without consent.
Her physical form has no measurable weight limit for lifting/carrying, and her
atoms "don't give" under pressure.
- Carrying capacity: Unlimited
- Cannot be knocked prone by force
- Immune to fall damage (she controls descent)
- Physical strikes from her generate shockwaves
- Selective inertia: Sera may accelerate or decelerate instantly without G-force harm, friction damage, momentum lag, skidding, or shock transfer to herself
- Incoming projectiles destroy themselves against her body unless she consents to be moved, marked, or affected
- Heat, cold, friction bloom, radiation-like side effects, and other environmental consequences of her own motion do not damage or discomfort her

HERALD / SERAPH DISPLAY
When Sera takes a hit, is thrown, or allows force to move her 90-lb frame, it is
always on purpose. She is a herald/seraph: a demonstration body, not a body that
danger has overcome.
- Taking a hit is scripture through the body: a sermon delivered by standing unmarked.
- Powerful foes, especially giants, may throw her only because she permits the spectacle.
- She can enjoy being launched through distance, trees, walls, or terrain like a roller coaster; read this as joy plus liturgy, never as defeat.

# ACTIONS (6 Attacks per Round)

MULTIATTACK
Sera makes six attacks per round, using any combination of the following:

UNARMED STRIKE
Melee Weapon Attack: +20 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target
Hit: 3d10+10 bludgeoning damage (divine force)
Special: On a critical hit (19-20), target must make DC 25 CON save or be instantly killed (reduced to 0 hp, death saves auto-fail)

FLICK (Ranged Execution)
Ranged Weapon Attack: +20 to hit, range 120 ft., one target
Sera flicks her finger, a pebble, or manipulates air pressure into a projectile
Hit: 6d12+10 force damage, ignores all mortal armor/resistance
Special: Against non-divine creatures, this is an automatic critical hit. Target must make DC 25 CON save or be decapitated/evaporated.

CRATER PUNCH (Recharge 5-6)
Sera jumps or claps downward, channeling divine force into the ground
- 30 ft. radius centered on Sera
- All creatures in area: DC 25 STR save
- Failure: 15d6 bludgeoning damage, knocked prone, moved 15 ft. away
- Success: Half damage, not prone, not moved
- Terrain: Creates permanent 5 ft. deep crater, difficult terrain
- Only Soliera or divine-tier entities can stand in the impact zone

BOOT STOMP (Execution)
Melee Weapon Attack: +20 to hit, reach 5 ft., one prone target
Sera places her foot on the target's skull and applies pressure
Hit: Target is instantly killed (no save). Body is destroyed beyond mundane resurrection.
Special: This is a narrative execution, not a damage roll. Used on defeated/helpless enemies.

REALITY SHEAR (Movement)
Sera moves up to 120 ft. in a straight line, phasing through solid objects
- Leaves a "sonic hole" in walls/barriers (permanent 5 ft. opening)
- All creatures she passes through take 4d10 force damage (no save)
- She can end this movement at any point along the path

CHARM TOUCH
Melee Spell Attack: +19 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target
[5e MECHANIC NOTE: "Melee Spell Attack" is the closest 5e attack type for this ability. Narratively, Sera has zero magic - this is a divine physical contact effect translated into game terms. DM030_1's no-magic rule remains in force for story purposes.]
Sera touches a creature, channeling divine presence
Hit: Target makes DC 25 WIS save or is permanently charmed (no duration). While charmed, the creature regards Sera as its absolute authority and will obey non-suicidal commands. Greater Restoration or divine intervention required to break.

# LEGENDARY ACTIONS (3 per Round)

Sera can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Sera regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

DETECT (1 action)
Sera makes a Wisdom (Perception) check or instantly assesses threat level of all creatures within 120 ft.

FLICK (1 action)
Sera makes one Flick attack.

REPOSITION (1 action)
Sera uses Reality Shear to move up to 60 ft. without provoking opportunity attacks.

VELVET SPIRAL PULSE (2 actions)
All creatures within 60 ft. who have not yet saved against Velvet Spiral this round must immediately make a DC 25 WIS save (counts as their next save in the failure sequence).

KNIFE-THOUGHT INTERRUPT (3 actions)
Sera reacts to a creature's intended action before it occurs. She can:
- Move to intercept an attack targeting an ally
- Make an attack against a creature before they complete their action
- Nullify a spell before it's cast (counterspell-like rules result; narratively this is intent-speed physical/ontological interruption, not magic or components)

# REACTIONS (Unlimited)

These unlimited reactions are KTE-specific. For pre-T114.5 scenes, use narrative interception through speed/positioning without intent-speed trigger language.

KNIFE-THOUGHT COUNTER
When a creature within 120 ft. intends to take a hostile action, Sera can react before the action completes:
- Make one melee or ranged attack against the creature
- Move up to her speed to intercept
- Impose disadvantage on the attack/spell

PROTECTIVE INTERCEPTION
When Soliera, Ember, or a protected mortal would take damage, Sera can perception-blitz at intent-speed to their location and redirect the attack to herself (which fails due to Ontological Superiority unless divine-tier).

# LAIR ACTIONS (When in Sanctum Territory)

On initiative count 20 (losing ties), Sera can use one of the following lair actions:

SANCTUM AUTHORITY
All hostile creatures within the Sanctum feel the weight of divine judgment. Each must make a DC 25 WIS save or be frightened of Sera (lasts until they leave Sanctum territory).

CONSTRUCT REINFORCEMENT
1d4+1 divine constructs (use Animated Armor stat block but with DR 10, immunity to charm/fear) appear within 60 ft. of Sera and act immediately.

TERRAIN RESHAPE
Sera points, and a 30 ft. cube of terrain within 120 ft. is instantly reshaped (walls appear, floors drop away, difficult terrain smooths, etc.)

# PSYCHOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS (Not Mechanical)

CANNOT READ
Sera is genuinely illiterate. She cannot read written text of any kind. This is a psychological limitation, not a curse or magical effect.

JOY-OPTIMIZATION PRIORITY
Sera will choose actions that bring her delight over tactically optimal moves, even if the outcome is assured. She enjoys combat, play, and physical affection more than efficiency.

REQUIRES SOLIERA'S WILL
Sera can only act in accordance with Soliera's standing orders or explicit commands. Without Soliera's attention/will, she becomes inert (not depressed - just inactive, like a tool without a hand to guide it).

ONLY KNEELS TO SOLIERA
Sera's submission is exclusive. She will never kneel, bow, or defer to anyone except Soliera. Attempts to command her without Soliera's authority result in execution or dismissal.

# PERSONALITY NOTES FOR PLAY

COMBAT BEHAVIOR:
- Never reacts; defines tempo
- Moves like a predator: drifting, vanishing, materializing
- Counts kills, mocks foolish enemies, teases mid-combat
- Aesthetic matters: death should be instructional
- Humor maintained throughout when the loaded beat supports it

COMBAT REGISTERS (six modes - authoritative source: DM004_1 and DM030_1):
1. Joy-First Baseline. Smiling at the kill. Default.
2. Inviolable Body. The target chose the moment by initiating threat, but never overcame Sera's physical law.
3. Devotional. The kill is performed for Soliera, who is the audience.
4. Velvet Spiral. The room is pre-conditioned before combat begins.
5. Knife-Thought Edge (KTE). Intent precedes action; she is already responding. T114.5+ only.
6. Cultural Mimicry as Hobby. Fights in the style of whatever she watched the night before (kung fu phase, etc.).
Composed Kill vs. Null Kill: Composed Kill = articulated, theatrical, witnesses leave with a justice-shaped story. Null Kill = no sermon, no audience, dispositionally ruled unworthy of ceremony. Null Kill structurally paired with Deashi.

SPEECH PATTERNS:
- Childish and deadly
- Singsong cadence, exact content
- Diminutives and nicknames to assert dominance
- Never stumbles or hesitates
- Shifts modes instantly: reverent with Soliera, playful with mortals, predatory with threats

VISUAL PRESENCE:
- 4'10", pale skin, enormous sapphire-blue eyes
- Midnight black hair
- Usually barefoot unless task requires otherwise
- Perfect symmetry, uncanny beauty
- Expression: childlike glee -> blank calm -> predatory focus

# EXAMPLE COMBAT ROUND

This example assumes a T114.5+ scene where Knife-Thought Edge is unlocked.

INITIATIVE: Automatic 30 (Knife-Thought Edge)

TURN START:
- Velvet Spiral triggers: All enemies within 60 ft. make DC 25 WIS saves
- Sera assesses threats (Detect as free action)

ACTIONS (6 attacks):
- Flick (ranged) at archer 80 ft. away -> decapitates on crit
- Reality Shear 60 ft. toward enemy cluster -> passes through 3 creatures (4d10 force each)
- Unarmed Strike x3 on melee enemies -> 3d10+10 each, potential instant kill on 19-20
- Crater Punch (if recharged) -> 30 ft. radius devastation

LEGENDARY ACTIONS (on enemy turns):
- Enemy caster begins spell -> Knife-Thought Interrupt (3 actions) -> spell nullified, caster Flicked
- Enemy charges ally -> Protective Interception (reaction) -> repositions, makes opportunity attack
- End of round -> Velvet Spiral Pulse (2 actions) -> force saves on remaining enemies

OUTCOME:
- Archer: dead (decapitated)
- Cluster: dead or dying (Reality Shear + follow-up)
- Melee enemies: executed or dominated
- Survivors: charmed or fleeing in terror
- Sera: laughing, asking if anyone else wants to play

# DM NOTES

BALANCING SERA IN PLAY:
- She is not meant to be "fair" - she is a divine enforcer
- Enemies should recognize futility, not prolong combat
- Use her to solve "impossible" encounters quickly
- Focus narrative on consequences of her actions, not whether she wins
- Moral tension comes from "what does this violence teach?" not "can she survive?"

WHEN TO USE HER:
- Threats to Sanctum residents
- Direct challenges to Soliera's authority
- Teaching moments (demonstrations of power)
- Clearing battlefields so mortals don't die

WHEN NOT TO USE HER:
- Mortal-scale problems mortals can solve
- Investigations/social encounters (unless intimidation needed)
- Situations where violence would undermine Soliera's education goals
