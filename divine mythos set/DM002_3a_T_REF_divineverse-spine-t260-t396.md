**Where:** Thay — coastal village, Eltabbar. Back to Waterdeep via the Ark.

**Why:** Nymmurh (bronze dragon) murdered by Thayan undead agents. Soliera corrects the error.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* The Ark turns east without a speech. Nymmurh's death hardens something. The execution of Tarneth is administered, not celebrated — she walks behind Sera without flinching.
- *Sera:* Barely remembered which dragon Nymmurh was. Then the memory lands and hardens her. She doesn't cry. The Tarneth execution is deliberate spectacle — staged as a lesson.
- *Ember:* First arc after birth. Learning that death is permanent for some. Dragons' souls are not the same as normal humans' — Soliera doesn't raise Nymmurh. "She corrects the error instead." Growing into emotional buffer role. Accidental heat flares still happening.
- *Tirren & Daviya:* Adopted on sight. Ember leads them away from the worst of the execution. They absorb that they are now rulers — divinely chosen, overwhelmed but obedient.
- *Kessla:* Waved from the crowd on return. Sera tackle-hugs her immediately. Proof that Kessla is family regardless of arc distance.
- *Supporting cast:* Tarneth screams for Shar. Nothing answers. That door closed permanently in Silverymoon. The freed slaves, chosen successors, and compliant paperwork give Soliera three simultaneous pieces of the alignment.

**Sanctum Population:** No direct census event this arc. Thayan coastal village rapidly becomes a logistics hub — constructs, missionaries, freed populations integrating. Numbers grow but don't anchor to a specific T-beat here.

**Ember — Development Beat:**
- Nymmurh teaches her that death is real and permanent for some entities. Dragons are not humans. Soliera's call not to resurrect is a lesson, not cruelty.
- First time Ember explicitly performs the emotional buffer role — leads Tirren and Daviya away, explains "This had to happen," copies Soliera's calm and Sera's certainty in a softer register.
- Learning what it means to be feared just for existing (crew on the Ark).
