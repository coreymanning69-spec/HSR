### SECTION II — THE ARK SAILS NORTH: SEA OF MOVING ICE (T170-T179)

The Ark is 900 feet long and 120 feet wide, with three imposing pure-white sails
that each rise 140 feet. It is a city on the ocean, impervious to damage and
weather.


T170: Decision — North to the Sea of Ice #LOC-SEA-OF-MOVING-ICE #CONCEPT-QUEST-ACCEPTANCE #CHAR-DRIZZT
— The decision to go north is not a debate. Arauthator has been a problem in the old accounts for long enough that the question is not whether he gets addressed, only when. Maccath is somewhere in his lair, which makes the timeline non-negotiable. Drizzt, who has his own history with the region and knows the Ice Hunters' situation better than anyone else in the party, maps the route without being asked. The Ark is ready. The Frostskimmr is provisioned. The party points north.

T171: Fleet North (The Ark & Frostskimmr) #CONCEPT-VEHICLE-THE-ARK #CONCEPT-VEHICLE-FROSTSKIMMR #CONCEPT-TRAVEL-NAVAL #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA
— Soliera creates the Ark — not assembles, not commissions, not orders. Creates. Living wood from something that was not a forest and is now a ship, built at the scale where conventional maritime terminology starts to fail. It is a flagship the way a cathedral is a building. The Frostskimmr leads, handling the channels the Ark's draft makes complicated, and ten auxiliary vessels follow carrying supplies and additional Hush Agents. The journey north is marked by an unnatural calm on the water — not flat, not still, just ordered in a way the ocean does not naturally manage. Sera spends most of the voyage at the prow because she finds moving water interesting in the specific way of something that doesn't have to worry about what it might do to her. Maccath, now aboard, watches the two of them and begins taking notes she will never stop taking.

T172: Arrival — Ice Hunters' Awe #CONCEPT-FACTION-ICE-HUNTERS #CONCEPT-SOCIAL-REACTION #CONCEPT-INFRASTRUCTURE-BUILD #CHAR-SOLIERA
— The fleet arrives near the Ice Hunter camps on the margin of the moving ice. The locals have seen travelers before, but not this. The Ark's scale alone is enough to suspend ordinary reaction; the constructs disembarking in orderly formation with supply loads is the second thing that stops processing. Soliera does not announce herself. She doesn't need to. The supplies go directly from the ships to the camps — food, medicine, materials for sheltering against the specific brutality of the northern winter. Constructs begin breaking frozen ground for permanent shelter foundations without being directed to. The Ice Hunters have survived by being extremely pragmatic; they recognize practical improvement when they see it and they are not too proud to accept it. Some of them try to understand what they are looking at. Most of them decide that understanding is a secondary problem.

T173: Sera's Icebreaking #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-ENVIRONMENT-DESTRUCTION #CONCEPT-FEAT-STRENGTH #CHAR-SOLIERA #CONCEPT-PHYSICS-MATTERS
— The passage north is blocked by ice — not a skim or a seasonal freeze but the permanent, building-sized architecture of the moving ice shelf, chunks the size of city districts ground into channels that the Frostskimmr cannot navigate and the Ark will not fit through. Sera asks. This is worth noting — she asks Soliera before she does this, because the scale of what she's about to do to the water will affect everyone on the fleet. Soliera extends the shield before Sera moves. Then Sera moves. She hits the first iceberg kinetically and the sound arrives at the fleet approximately three seconds after the visual. Ice the size of towers becomes ice the size of rubble. She redirects the ocean flow with the same method she uses to redirect everything — intent applied directly to physics. The sea lane that results from this sequence is permanent. The passage through the moving ice is different from this point forward. The Ark sails through it. The Ice Hunters will use it for generations and tell their children something enormous made the way.

T174: Tribal Alliance #CONCEPT-DIPLOMACY-AID #CONCEPT-CONSTRUCT-GUARDIANS #CONCEPT-FACTION-ICE-HUNTERS #THEME-MERCY-AS-INFRASTRUCTURE
— The Ice Hunters' relationship with the divine has historically been adversarial — Auril's winter is not kind to those who depend on the sea. The Sanctum's arrival is not adversarial. It is practical, generous, and without condition. Medical miracles: the healers in the camps address the chronic cold-injury accumulation of a lifetime in the far north, things the tribes had accepted as permanent. Food: without the constraints the region imposes. Warmth: structural, built-in, not temporary. Five construct guardians are left when the fleet departs — enormous, sleepless, and without political alignment. Some hunters are overwhelmed enough to kneel. Others shake hands with the constructs, which does not do much but which the constructs accept without comment. The Sanctum will not ask anything of the Ice Hunters in return. That is the part that takes longest to believe.

T175: Confronting the Great White Death #CHAR-ARAUTHATOR #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-COMBAT-AVOIDED #EXILE #THEME-ABSOLUTE-POWER-ETHICS
— Arauthator is ancient. He has the specific arrogance of something that has been the largest, oldest, and most dangerous thing in its environment for long enough to have confused longevity with invulnerability. Sera calls him down from his elevation with the same tone she uses for everything: direct, cheerful, slightly impatient. He comes because the alternative presented itself clearly. She scolds him for his centuries of cruelty in the North — the raids, the captives, the ice-locked hoarding — with the matter-of-fact displeasure of someone addressing a long-standing civic problem. Soliera speaks once. The ultimatum: exile or annihilation. It is not theatric. It is not a negotiation. He feels afraid. This is, by any reasonable accounting, the first time Arauthator has felt afraid in centuries, possibly since before the current calendar. He flees north into the deeper ice. He does not return. Maccath, watching from the Ark's rail, writes down what she observed as precisely as she can. She knows she will not have adequate language for it. She writes it anyway.

T176: Maccath's Rescue #CHAR-MACCATH #CONCEPT-RESCUE-SUCCESS #CONCEPT-LORE-KEEPER #LOC-ARAUTHATOR-LAIR #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA
— Arauthator's lair in the ice — on Oyaviggaton, the iceberg that serves as his territory — is large the way a
  wound that's been accumulating for a long time is large. Maccath is found in a chamber the dragon designated as
  her workspace, which is a compromise between captivity and the practical reality that the dragon needed her
  knowledge more than he needed her broken. She is battered, underfed, and completely alert — she has been keeping
  records of everything she's observed since her capture and she hands them to Sera before she allows herself to
  be carried. She is healed immediately. Soliera's healing on this scale is not gradual — damage stops being a
  condition that applies. Maccath stands up straight for the first time in what she estimates is years, looks at
  the two women who just exiled an ancient dragon in the time it takes to have a conversation about one, and says
  something to the effect that she would very much like to come with them. She is offered a high position in the
  Sanctum's information hierarchy. She accepts because she is extremely good at evaluating situations. Her
  knowledge of the Dragon Masks, the cult's arcane methodology, and draconic lore is treated as the institutional
  resource it is. She becomes the Sanctum's living archive.

T177: The Ark — Divine Vessel #CONCEPT-VEHICLE-THE-ARK #CONCEPT-CREATION-FEAT #CHAR-SOLIERA #LOC-SEA-OF-MOVING-ICE
— Soliera expands the Ark's capacity for the return voyage — more rescued persons, the materials from Arauthator's lair that Maccath has flagged as worth recovering, the Ice Hunter delegates who have decided they would like to see Waterdeep at least once. The Ice Hunters still gathered at the shore watch this happen. Several of them are going to build a story about it and tell it every winter for the rest of their lives. The Ark moves south through the lane Sera opened and the moving ice closes very slowly behind it, the way geography closes around something it still can't quite explain.

T178: Divine Aftermath (The Sea Lane) #CONCEPT-ENVIRONMENT-TERRAFORMING #LOC-NORTH #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA
— What remains of the northern intervention: five construct guardians in the Ice Hunter camp, a permanent navigable passage through the moving ice shelf, Auril's lingering winter magic transmuted from predatory to protective — it still winters hard in the North, but the cold now drives game toward the camps rather than away from them, and the killing blizzards that used to come without warning have a different character now, something that feels more like a season than an attack. The Sanctum does not announce these changes. They are simply the conditions that persist after the fleet leaves. Sailors charting the passage will note the opened lane for centuries without knowing what made it.

T179: The Return South #CONCEPT-TRAVEL-NAVAL #CHAR-MACCATH #CONCEPT-REFLECTION #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CONCEPT-VEHICLE-THE-ARK
— The voyage south gives everyone aboard time to be whatever they are when the immediate problem has been resolved. Maccath fills three more notebooks. Drizzt is quiet in the way of someone who has been revising a mental model and has not yet decided what the new version looks like. Sera is at the prow most of the time, or she's bothering Maccath, or she's doing something physically absurd on the Ark's rigging because the ocean is large and movement is more interesting than stillness. Soliera is wherever she is, which is never quite where you expect. The ship is not a tense environment. It is the post-exertion phase of something enormous, and there is a specific kind of peace that comes from that — not rest, not victory, just the moment after consequence when consequence is still settling. Maccath writes about the difference between mortals and the divine and then crosses most of it out because she realizes she is writing about the difference between people who age and people who don't, and that is not actually the distinction she observed.
