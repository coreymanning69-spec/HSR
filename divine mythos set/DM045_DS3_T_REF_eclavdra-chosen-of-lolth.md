# ECLAVDRA — CHOSEN OF LOLTH

#CHAR-ECLAVDRA #CHAR-LOLTH #THREAD-ECLAVDRA-SHADOW #LOC-HOUSE-ZAUVIIR
#ITEM-ZAUVIIR-SIGNET #ITEM-BROKEN-CRESCENT-BLADE

## WHY LOLTH CANNOT ACT DIRECTLY

Ao's settlement bounds every god by portfolio. That is what makes a
non-aggression posture stable: a god can scheme sideways, but direct action
remains inside the domain arrangement.

Soliera sits outside that arrangement. She was never a signatory, and the
Sanctum accrues devotion inside other gods' territory without claiming their
portfolios. Direct retaliation has already failed. Shar took a hit at
Silverymoon and left; Mystra manifested, deferred, and told Shar she was reading
the situation incorrectly. Shar and Myrkul remain publicly faith-damaged.

**Champions exist because direct action is closed.** A mortal instrument is the
available way to act without reopening the divine settlement.

## WHY THIS IS LOLTH'S WORST CASE

Bane's grievance is theological: dominion was achieved and loved. Lolth's is
more specific.

At T75 Sera renamed a seven-House drow settlement without learning its original
name, and the civilization reorganized around the gesture. No betrayal, coup
cycle, or correction followed. It held. Lolth's theology says that should be
impossible, and the result sits in her own Underdark with a surface goddess's
name on it.

Subversion also has no useful purchase on the Sanctum's standing defense.
Non-sapient, disposable constructs cannot be seduced, split, or turned. They
can only be spent.

So Lolth chooses a student who reports.

## WHY ECLAVDRA

She has been doing the work since TR2 without the title.

- She predicted divine intervention specifically and called it a good test.
- She is not reacting to the gods. She is studying them.
- She coordinated a multi-type giant alliance across the Savage Frontier and
  signed the invasion order herself.
- She was absent from all three sites when the girls arrived, or watched from
  somewhere the modules do not account for. Whatever she learned, she kept.

Elevating her closes an open thread instead of inventing a replacement.

### The find

The useful conclusion is that the Sanctum is not a rival faith operating inside
the domain system. It is outside it. That is the piece of intelligence Lolth
would elevate someone to obtain, and Eclavdra is already positioned to get it.

## THE GRIEVANCE IS FAMILIAL

House Zauviir ranked fifth of seven. Its scouts treated Taliandra as disposable
and sent her above ground to die.

She returned with divine backing, killed the Matron Mother in a duel Lolth
permitted, and took the city. A priestess who tried to break the duel was put
through a wall. Taliandra took whip poison and gutted her mother anyway.

Lolth did not accept the loss. She took the mother and made her a monstrosity.
The Spider Queen does not lose people; she collects them.

### The Proto-Forge

Standing in the Zauviir estate at T68, Niv registered magical signals from
miles underground. The party eventually found an ancient forge, webbed and
cold, that proved sentient at T96.

It sat under Zauviir ground for generations and never opened. Taliandra walked
in with two gods and it answered.

That explains Eclavdra better than pride does. She studies the gods because
something she was owed responded to them and never to her.

The webbed Proto-Forge is part of Eclavdra's final-operation architecture. Its
exact use and discoveries remain for play; this does not retroactively identify
the ancient webber.

## THE CLEAR-LANE

The Ordinant takes you. Eclavdra stops a team from being one.

Every tool in the kit converts a party into individuals: forced impostor reads,
redirected protection, obedience that looks like allegiance, and the death or
removal of the member everyone else was covering. A team that fights her well
can still leave not trusting itself.

That is the result she reports. Corpses are incidental.

## THE TELL

The Ordinant's tell is visible in one glance: no shield, low AC, and a man who
has never needed to block.

Eclavdra's is visible afterward. **She does not lie.** Not to enemies, allies,
or Lolth. Drow who assume inversion act against what she said, which lets her
move them without touching their minds. Survivors work it out weeks later.

## APPEARANCE

- Drow, ageless, unhurried. No regalia of office because she holds none.
- Working clothes of extremely high quality. Nothing announces rank.
- The Zauviir signet on her left hand: a dead house's ring, worn openly.
- No spiders anywhere on her person. She considers the iconography beneath a
  serious practitioner.
- Against seven feet of mirror-white plate, the Ordinant absorbs the light.
  Eclavdra is the woman who was never in the room.

## THE KIT

### The Zauviir Signet — worn left

The only thing she kept. A fifth-ranked house's seal, functionally worthless as
authority.

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lineage | Attunement restricted to Zauviir blood; she is the last |
| Recall | Stores one location per tenday; she may return to it from any plane as an action |
| The Ledger | She always knows the current disposition of every living Zauviir. Most are inside the Sanctum orbit. |
| Attunement | Chosen of Lolth only in its current state |

Recall is her escape valve and the mechanical form of the Exit Lock. She is not
trapped by ordinary containment.

### The Broken Crescent Blade — carried, never drawn

Two-thirds of an Eilistraeean sword believed capable of killing Lolth, broken
in the Demonweb Pits. She carries it wrapped at her hip, visibly.

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| State | Broken; cannot be used as a weapon in its current condition |
| Presence | A drow who recognizes it must make a WIS save or be unable to act against her for one round; recognition is the trigger |
| Proof | Physical evidence that the Chosen contest happened and someone survived it |
| Unwritten | How she got it; whether Lolth knows; whether it can be reforged |

A Chosen of Lolth carrying a god-killing sword in public is either the most
trusted servant in the Underdark or the beginning of something. That question
is deliberately unresolved.

### The Skein — Lolth's grant

Not a spell and not an item. Ambient webbing is simply present wherever she has
stood for more than a minute, and it was not there before.

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| Terrain | A 30-foot radius becomes difficult terrain that ignores flight and incorporeality |
| Anchor | A creature that starts its turn in the Skein makes a STR save or loses its reaction |
| Skein-Step | Bonus action: she moves to any point in the Skein. No teleport tag; she was already there. |
| Withdrawal | She may close the Skein and leave through it. No current counterplay exists. |

## OPERATIVE RUNTIME BLOCK — T537

**Build:** Drow Overwizard — Wizard 20, School of Illusion, with Chosen grafts
supplying effects the class does not grant. "Overwizard" is the corpus term in
DM026_0 and wins over the published priestess read.

**Format gate:** this is an operative compact 5e runtime, not a full spellbook.
The fixed suite below is what she brings to a scene; other legal Wizard spells

### Signature spell load — the disaggregation suite

- **Forcecage** — removes one member without killing them
- **Maze** — attacks the line-holder's Intelligence check rather than a save
- **Feeblemind** — leaves the party to decide what to do with permanence
- **Mass Suggestion** — obedience that reads as allegiance
- **Simulacrum** and **Clone** — support years of proxy operation
- **Mind Blank**, self, permanent-standing
- **Illusory Reality** — one illusory object becomes real for one minute

The suite is a party-breaking baseline, not a claim that every containment tool
works on every target. Wren's Portal 14 defeats *forcecage* and other
eighth-level-or-lower teleport blocks; Doran remains the better spatial target,
especially for *maze* because its escape is an INT ability check.

### Chosen grafts

- **Yor'thae Candidacy.** She is a candidate, not the mantle. The mantle was
  spent on Danifae. Lolth's grant is real and revocable, and Eclavdra knows the
  revocation terms better than anyone alive.
- **Handmaiden's Regard.** 1/day, force a reroll of any d20 in line of sight. A
  yochlol answers. It is not subtle and she hates using it.
- **The Sentence.** When she reduces a creature to 0 HP, she may instead offer
  it to Lolth. The creature does not die; it is taken, and what returns is not
  it. This is the mechanical descendant of Taliandra's mother's fate, not a
  ruling on the mother's current disposition.
- **She Is Not Here.** Up to three reactions per round: when a creature targets
  her with an effect, she may move to an **unoccupied space within 5 feet that
  she can see** and make that targeting invalid. It cannot answer an effect
  that does not target her. Occupying or denying every adjacent space defeats
  the graft; grapples, narrow rooms, terrain, and area denial are therefore
  real counterplay. This is why AC 19 is not the encounter's central number.

### Design seam — GM side

She should never win a damage race and should never be in one. If a session
reduces to attack rolls against her, the encounter was framed incorrectly.

Her defeat condition is **information denial**. A party that gives her nothing
new has beaten her, whether or not anyone is standing. She leaves those rooms
early and does not return to them.

## CHAMPION-TIER DOCTRINE — SESSION LOCK 2026-08-03

1. **Solo operators.** One person clearing teams for a god, not a party leader
   and not an opponent designed around the field Stewards.
2. **Peer band.** Level 20 with artifact-grade kit. An average champion is peer
   to senior Sanctum staff, not to the divine family.
3. **The Sanctum cannot be attacked directly.** Its embassies, Underdark
   waypoints, population, constructs, and supply network make a direct move a
   declaration against the infrastructure surrounding it.
4. **If a person comes, it is a Korin.** A level-20 grandmaster can enter the
   champion's own room past ordinary detection, deliver the warning in the
   open, and leave.
5. **A viable hostile champion needs nothing findable.** No seat, city, or
   throne. Eclavdra qualifies.
6. **Not all champions are hostile.** Mystra's warm Chosen relationship with
   the Sanctum is the counter-example; deployment is a spread, not a villain
   roster.

## CURRENT PLOT-STATE BOUNDARY

DM050_0 owns all changeable Eclavdra plot-state. The settled boundary is that an
unnamed non-Xorlarrin house hosts her drow, priest, and spider force; the
Proto-Forge and Taliandra's transformed but recoverable mother feed the final
operation; and Taliandra herself is absent. Exact geometry, discoveries, and
the mother's recovery remain for play and do not alter this runtime.
