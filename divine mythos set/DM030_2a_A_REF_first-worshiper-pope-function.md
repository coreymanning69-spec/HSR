## First Worshiper / Pope Function

Sera's worship is sensory certainty, not abstract faith. She has direct
knowledge of Soliera as living divinity. That makes her a religious authority
even when she is barefoot, eating cake, laughing, or sitting in someone's lap.

The later Emberians arise around *Candlelight; For Ember*, not around an office
held by Sera. Their existence does not dilute Sera's first-worshiper function,
create an authority that can order the Girls, or convert Wren's separate
Sanctum Archpriestess role into Emberian leadership.

Functionally, Sera is the first worshiper and the nearest interpreter of
Soliera's will. She does not speak for Soliera by institutional office. She
speaks because she is Soliera's response vector and because Soliera permits
the world to route through her.

Write this as joyful certainty:

- She is not trying to convince herself.
- She is not insecure about doctrine.
- She is not lobbying for rank.
- She does not need mortals to agree before reality follows.

## Public Presence

Sera in public should produce both warmth and danger.

Oak City return gives the clearest current model:

- The gate plaza stops from recognition.
- Sera notices cakes before politics.
- Dana freezes, serves cake, and later knows she will never top the shift.
- Jefferson and Adaela approach as state/church representatives, but the scene is still a cafe scene.
- Sera clocks Axel because his stealth effect is working too well.
- Sera hesitates around the Church because she knows the Company better; Ember reframes it by saying they do not know people they have not talked to.
- Sera accepts the correction immediately.

This is the right register: cosmic significance filtered through appetite,
conversation, furniture, and small social preferences.

Do not reset her to diplomatic inexperience. Before the current edge she has
already worked the Blackstaff/Waterdeep institutional lane, Silverymoon, and the
Mystra/Alustriel balcony encounter. New councils may be novel people, but they are
not her first room of frightened officials, archmages, or divine peers.

## Decompression and Food

After impossible events, Sera scenes often land through mundane handling:
food, corridors, sandwiches, sitting, waiting, processing. DM021_1 calls out the
disparity between what just happened and what they are eating as the joke.

This matters because Sera does not need to solemnize her own scale. The
witnesses carry the solemnity. Sera can sit pretty on a platform and ask:

> "what else do y'all wanna test?"

## Relationship: Soliera

Soliera is Sera's axis. Sera worships her, dresses her, speaks from her frame,
and performs for her. Soliera's watching is not a small detail; it is often the
purpose.

Plate-test reading:

- Sera performs because Soliera is present.
- Sera's peaceful expression under the machine is not triumph; it is
  sufficiency.
- Soliera watching is the environmental condition that makes the act complete.

Useful dialogue anchors:

> "Soli fixes everything!"

> "Only I call her that. Don't lose your tongue, after all!"

> "They're so serious."

Write Sera's Soliera-facing lines as worshipful, possessive, informal, and
absolutely certain. She may use childish cadence, but she is not child-coded.

Source: DM020_2_M_STY_oak-city-scenes-m151-m160.md:145-189.

## Relationship: Ember

Ember modulates Sera by presence, fear, fascination, and innocence. She does
not govern Sera as an authority figure. The distinction is important.

DM028_3 gives the guardrail: Sera as guardian-against-harm is only partial.
The framing is not "Ember is helpless." The correct framing is that Ember's
emotional state raises the stakes. Sera's protection is about catastrophic
triggering, not Ember's fragility.

Use these dynamics:

- Ember's fear or hurt can trigger overwhelming force.
- Ember's fascination can restrain Sera.
- Ember can redirect the scene because Sera loves watching her understand.
- Sera and Ember share frame/height, but their vectors are different: response and inquiry.
- Ember often processes Sera's demonstrations as delight, not horror.

Source: DM028_3b_A_REF_ember-spec.md:254-266; DM020_2_M_STY_oak-city-scenes-m151-m160.md:179-189.

## Relationship: Deashi

Deashi is silent judgment; Sera is joyful response. Their relationship should
feel intimate and structurally distinct. Deashi does not verbally correct her.
He does not become a competing authority. Sera can love him, lounge near him,
and recognize him as part of the divine household without changing her
Soliera-only submission.

Write Deashi as a stabilizing silence and Sera as a body that can play near
that silence because she belongs inside the same divine frame.

## Relationship: Niv-Saiffar

Niv-Saiffar is the sole male-formed intimate of Soliera and Sera, separately
and together, and Sera's earned household favorite. He can share space and
Soliera without becoming Sera's authority or a rival. This file preserves the
direct quote:

> "He's my actual favorite."

Sera remains women-only. Niv does not weaken or open that orientation because
he is a dragon operating in a male humanoid form, not a man. Use Niv as social
steadiness, earned proximity, and the one male-formed intimate of both women.

At T15, Niv crosses the door by praising Soliera rather than claiming Sera's
invitation. His mistaken belief that he could counterweight Deashi gives him
peer posture; his conduct at the threshold earns his place near Soliera. He later
becomes intimate with Soliera and Sera, separately and together. The
later pairing does not undo the refusal: it happens only after body-offering is
no longer the sole proof of lovability Sera believes she possesses.

## Relationship: Cassian

Cassian processes Sera through structure, planning, reporting, and quiet
institutional realism. He is useful because he does not mistake the room's
emotions for operational truth, but even his frameworks fail when Sera moves
beyond what the data can hold.

Use Cassian to:

- Explain human-scale bridges.
- Translate testing procedure.
- Pivot rooms after overload.
- Keep witnesses moving toward food, rest, and briefing.
- Fail elegantly when the available frameworks are not enough.

## Relationship: Mortals and Outsiders

Sera's mortal-facing behavior is direct, playful, and dangerous.

Selected quote anchors:

> "What do you think we should go fix first?"

> "If you like picking on people weaker than you... then why not try me? Aren't I smaller than you?"

> "No."

> "Our miracles are never for nothing. You just haven't earned the right for one yet."

> "They won't like it."

She can be funny, helpful, terrifying, and socially casual in the same scene.
Do not separate those into different versions of Sera.

## Relationship: Children

Children are safe with Sera. Predators around children are not.

Rules:

- Sera protects children without needing to philosophize.
- She can be gentle immediately after violence.
- She may tell children to cover their eyes before doing what the scene requires.
- Harm to children bypasses ordinary social negotiation.
- Do not frame child protection as sentimental weakness; it is canon law.

Useful quote:

> "Cover your eyes. You don't need to see this."

## Humor Architecture

Sera's humor is not stand-up. She is usually not joking. The joke is the
category mismatch between her sincere register and the scale of what she is
doing.

This file preserves:

> "She is never joking. The comedy is observational, not performative."

Rules:

- Do not have Sera make jokes to entertain the reader.
- Let her react sincerely to a situation that is absurd at her scale.
- Pair her sincerity with a mortal witness reaction.
- Let the horror and comedy be the same object when appropriate.
- Do not undercut grief or stakes when the witness needs the full weight of the moment.

Examples:

- Sera calling the destroyed press a "Good machine."
- Sera asking what else the room wants to test after everyone else is still processing.
- Jefferson maintaining eye contact with the middle distance while Velvet Spiral runs.
- Sandwiches after the testing-facility event.

## Dialogue Register

Sera's voice is compact, direct, sometimes singsong, and often socially
over-familiar. She can sound playful without losing lethality. She can speak
with simple phrasing because she does not need complex rhetoric to dominate a
room.

Use:

- Short questions.
- Possessive nicknames.
- Direct corrections.
- Sincere enthusiasm.
- Flat declarations when the scene turns lethal.
- Casual profanity where the loaded beat supports it.

Avoid:

- Over-articulate speeches.
- Moral lectures unless the scene is explicitly framed as instructional.
- Long tactical analysis.
- Hesitation filler.
- Child-coded vocabulary.

## Scene Guidance

### Public Space

Sera is visible, casual, and ownership-coded. People may stop from recognition
rather than fear. The divine household can sit at a cafe and still alter the
political meaning of the district.

### Council / Institution

Sera's presence makes hierarchy unstable. Commanders, archbishops, executives,
and cadets all have to re-evaluate their posture. She is not impressed by rank,
but she can enjoy people who are useful, pretty, brave, or direct.

### Domestic Sanctum

Write dressing, lounging, eating, reading-aloud, and touch as divine
infrastructure. This is not downtime detached from the theology. It is the
theology.

### Witness Processing

After demonstrations, let mortals process in mixed states: fear, hope, awe,
embarrassment, practicality, hunger, and exhaustion. Sera does not need to
process at their tempo.

## Failure Modes to Avoid

Do not:

- Make Sera harmless in social scenes.
- Make Velvet Spiral a spell.
- Treat attraction to Sera as automatically sexual orientation.
- Make Sera use sexuality as coercive weapon.
- Make Sera a servant in a low-status way.
- Make Soliera/Sera intimacy a break from authority.
- Make Ember "control" Sera by command.
- Make Veyren equal to Sera because he is physically impressive.
- Give institutions the power to order Sera around.
- Write deliberate jokes where sincere mismatch is stronger.
- Infantilize Sera because she is short, playful, or physically affectionate.

Do:

- Let her joy warm the room and pressure it at the same time.
- Let witnesses carry the explanatory burden.
- Let domestic acts remain sacred.
- Let Soliera's silence be the axis.
- Let Ember's fascination soften Sera's immediate enforcement.
- Let children be safe.
- Let predators understand too late that the room has already changed.
