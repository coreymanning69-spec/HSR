This file documents the institutional and mechanical scaffolding of the Primeverse: the timeline of how the world arrived at its present state, the two megastructures (Company and Church) that share it, the technology baseline, Project DIVIDE / Countersign, and the magic-and-items logic that governs combat and miracles. The narrative core (Soliera Prime, Arden, locations) is in DM014_1; the character profile roster is in DM014_3; current Merge continuation is in the M-beat files. DM014_4 is a standalone archive.


Timeline – Soliera Prime (Canon Confirmed Events)

Before the Gates – Primeverse begins as an ordinary Earth, indistinguishable
from any other world, until the first Gates open.

Gates Emergence – Gates and dimension doors begin opening very sparsely, over
roughly ten to twenty years, with no pattern binding them to land; most
surface on solid ground, but at least one later opens under the ocean. A Gate
is not a functioning portal the moment it appears — belief activates it.
Until enough people associate a site with folklore, it sits inert; the Gates
that open unnoticed stay closed longest. As Gates open and belief accumulates
around them, technology, medicine, and witchcraft-type effects all begin
working noticeably better, unevenly and without anyone yet able to explain
why.

The incursions were indiscriminate: aliens, goblins, zombies, and other made-real
nightmares killed without regard to wealth, race, or status. Regional fear then
minted additional local monsters through the belief-intent system, making the
threat self-catalyzing.

The Great War – Around 1930, roughly twenty years after the first Gates open,
a single Great War breaks out. This timeline has only one world war, not two.

The Brazil Gate – A late-opening Gate in Brazil produces something actively
hostile; tens of thousands die before anyone can organize a response. This is
the event that turns "Gates are strange" into "Gates are a threat," ahead of
any institution built to answer it.

A New Kind of Enemy – A hostile category emerges that wants to kill all races
indiscriminately, human or animal. The chupacabra becomes real in this
window: a man in Mexico shoots one, and unlike every prior cryptid claim,
this one is not debunked.

Soliera's Enchantment – She enchanted her dagger early on with a tagged item system ("Cursed Steel Dagger").

The Church's Rise – It formed a tiered clerical structure post-Gate, becoming the dominant moral + magical authority.

Company Expansion – In response to chaos, the corporate "Company" began securing continents under private rule.

The Company began as survival infrastructure rather than a belief scheme: a
battery-maker proposed a division of labor in which one group supplied power
cells, another held ground, and both shared the cost of hiring people willing to
fight monsters. Escalation and consolidation absorbed the corporations that
would otherwise have become separate industrial giants into one Umbrella-grade
megastructure with continent-scale holdings and extreme-depth bunkers.

Arden's Dual Modes – Her protector, Arden, has a binary state: dormant guardian vs. overwhelming war mode.

Village Tragedy – Her home village was destroyed, catalyzing her transformation into the central myth.

The Church's Corruption – Revealed to operate as a soul-harvesting or manipulative regime with secret elite tiers.

Evan's Separation and Return – Church contact kept Soliera's father figure away
for a time. Soliera later decided the separation had lasted long enough and
brought him to Outpost Seven, where he now lives as a regular human inside the
world's most secure base.

Post-Gate Society – Society is rebuilt around power hierarchies, with magic, corps, and religion forming a broken triumvirate.

Arrival at Outpost 7 – Soliera Prime reaches the frontline edge of civilization, at a minor Company-Church outpost.

Project DIVIDE / Countersign – Company, Church, and independent operators attempt a cross-faction Tier-escalation answer to Soliera Prime's unclassifiable influence. One product survives: Veris. The recovered label "Countersign Divide" refers to this same program; "Cosign" and "Cosan" are discarded drift.

Global Power Structure
The Company (Corporate Megastructure)
Executive Tier controls entire continents like CEOs.

Local governance is franchise-based: outposts, labs, bunkers.

Political borders are obsolete; everything is real estate.

Magic is tolerated but licensed via Company terminals.

Company Logos serve as authority in lieu of state flags.

Cities operate like hyper-optimized tech parks—efficiency, labor drones, commerce.

"Secular" does not mean belief-free in an intent-formed world. Company culture
redirects faith toward human capability, national myth, technology, and the Tier
taxonomy. The same engine pays out in T4s even when nobody calls the process
religion.

The Church (Spiritual Hierarchy)
Tiered System:

Tier 1 – Ordinary humans, uninitiated.

Tier 2 – Priests: access to minor blessings and relics.

Tier 3 – Bishops: regional control, minor miracle access.

Tier 4 – Saints: semi-divine, allowed to bypass Gates.

Tier 5 (Church) – Archdukes: five total, absolute apex of Church authority. Above Saints. Full control over Church doctrine, Church Keys, and miracle classification. Rarely visible at operational level.


Miracles are processed through magical tech, controlled via Church "Keys."

The divine entity at the root is Yahweh: the God of the biblical account made
literal and demonstrably active by accumulated human thought and belief. He is
not a substitute wearing the name, but neither is He the world's original
creator. The Primeverse belief engine instantiated the biblical account as a
local divine force.

The Church contains and routes that force as belief logistics. Church Keys are
trunk-line metering rather than the source of miracles, and the five Archdukes
are the last on-chart tier rather than the divine ceiling. Clergy are conduits,
not sources: initiates receive a trickle, bishops a provisioned line, and
archbishops a trunk connection. Access can be throttled by the entity at the
root. These are directional bandwidth relationships, not fixed combat numbers.

Scripture remains open and living because its active author can still produce
visions and current pages. The Abrahamic split was unified by demonstration and
paternity claim — visions identifying Jews, Christians, and Muslims as children
of the same source — rather than conquest. Institutionally the result is
European-Catholic in structure with a visible merged Judeo-Christian spine.

Project DIVIDE / Countersign (Cross-Faction Program)
Project DIVIDE was not a normal Company project and not a Church sacrament. It was the place where institutional panic became cooperation: Company enhancement infrastructure, Church miracle-class access, and independent myth theory all aimed at one impossible requirement — produce a counterweight to Soliera Prime's influence without touching Soliera or triggering Arden.

Countersign Divide is the recovered institutional synonym for this same program. Use Project DIVIDE as the live-canon label. The counterweight was the goal; Veris was the result. That distinction matters institutionally because every file that treats Veris as a successful divine-scale answer is repeating the program's ambition, not its outcome.

DIVIDE's prayer methodology was literal. The program prayed nightly for test
results to land within 0.001 of tolerance, and the accumulated belief made that
precision increasingly real. Primeverse science has therefore been
faith-assisted throughout the modern era. Veris is partly answered prayer: a
body engineered by hands and animated through institutional liturgy, while
remaining a person rather than the divine-scale counterweight the program named.

Technology and Spellcasting
Technology Level
Post-collapse retro-futurism: Batteries, terminals, drones exist alongside magical foci.

Power grids are often replaced with arcane ley extractors.

Communication is fragmented, but Companies run short-range tower hubs.

Medical tech is strong, but enhancement is rare outside Executives or Saints.

"Relic-class artifacts" are tagged and databased in both Church and Company archives.

Spellcasting & Item Logic
**The Six Magic Schools:** Incantation, Rune, Alchemy, Spiritual, Gateborne, Primal Intent. These are the six recognized major schools. Most practitioners specialize in one or two. Soliera Prime has studied all six and can cast each at a basic level; her Intent (Primal Intent) track is developing fastest and is the most anomalous in terms of ceiling.

Uses modular tagging system:

Example: "Cursed Steel Dagger [+Poisoned Edge][x2 Bleed Mod][Unstable Wound Roll]"

Magic is not freeform. You slot spells/items into conduits or "runlines."

Combat is structured around:

Spell Anchors (places where magic was burnt into the ground)

Tags (metadata-like properties: curse, radiance, echo, temporal)

Cooldowns/Recharge Routines (some tied to Church rituals, others to item timers)

Meta & Narrative Style Details
Soliera Prime is the mortal girl version; separate from divine Soliera (who is Yuium-aspect).

Focus is personal myth through corporate divine decay—a girl's rise amidst monopolized authority and nightmares made real. However, the good things are made real; but in the form of Powers given to humans.

Combat, trauma, and wonder are all intimate, not cosmic—until they are.

Narrative logic follows realism-leaning pacing: magic costs, morality twists, no assumed goodness. When introduced, the D&D aspects of combat will be applied but just in a "flow form", buffs and initiative still exist, just in "realistic flow of combat/action".

World System Definitions
1. Combat Tier System
This is a 5-level framework that governs power levels:

Tier 1 – Baseline humans

Tier 2–3 – Trained operatives, high-tier casters, priests

Tier 4 – Narrative battlefield dominators (e.g., Ashe)

Tier 5 – Singular, time-free entity; divine-level threats (Arden is the clearest example — the Company's working classification)

Soliera – Outside of the tier system. Sera and Deashi also operate beyond these tiers by metaphysical rule.

2. Church Hierarchy
The Church structure moves from:

Normal Human Priest Bishop Saint

Each rank gains access to different levels of miracle work, divine support, or company-sanctioned blessings.

Saints are semi-mythic: rare and possibly post-human (or partially divine constructs).

This structure is rigid and formalized—miracles must be witnessed and recorded to qualify advancement.

3. Company Executive System
The Company controls continents and operates as a post-apocalyptic corporate bureaucracy. Power is divided as follows:

Executives = Nation-scale controllers, divinely or technologically enhanced

Mid-level Agents = Operatives who enforce and influence regional policy

Clerical Support = Data scrubbers, intelligence feeders, and miracle archivists

Soliera Prime (from the Arden line) was originally part of this world, making her emotionally tied to the fallout of this system.

Each Executive is feared like a minor deity—capable of wiping cities off the map under "Company Initiative Protocol."

Modular Magic and Spellcasting
Magic is modifier-based and modular, not Vancian:

Example: Cursed Steel Dagger [+Burning] [+Tracking] — each item can be tagged with qualifiers.

Spells and abilities are cast through conceptual invocation, not memorized lists.

Spell canceling or nullification is instinctual for divine beings. E.g., Sera's Reflexive Null passively rejects any spell—even those that usually bypass saves.

Miracle & Item Classification
Minor Miracle – Observable but plausible events (e.g., healing, enhancement)

Major Miracle – Reality-breaking (e.g., Soliera rewriting poison out of a species)

Relic-Tier Items – Anything that has been witnessed by multiple factions and survived divine touch. Typically one-of-a-kind or world-reactive.

Relics are often modular and evolve with the wielder's divine alignment or sin.
