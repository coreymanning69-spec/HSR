### META & RECAP — FOR TIMELINE/CHARACTER FILE USE

• **Kessla's Arc Thread:** She is now permanently in the divine orbit. Not as servant, not as victim — as someone who was chosen and chose back, and does not yet fully understand what that costs. Her mind will eventually reach its ceiling. The thread is open.
• **Elia's Function:** Political liaison, draconic observer, gentle conscience. She will not fight Chuth. She is here to witness and to connect the metallic council to the Sanctum's reality. Her presence legitimizes and complicates in equal measure.
• **Soliera's Silence:** Absolute throughout. She acts, she looks, she smiles. She speaks for DM-level rulings only. Her silence is louder than anyone else's words.
• **Sera's Velvet Spiral & Combat Profile:** Visible in both social and physical registers across these beats. She is the same person at the tea table and in the lair entrance.
• **The "Gods Came and Left" Theme (T210):** This is the real consequence of divine intervention in Altand, and it will echo. The elves are healed. Now they have to live in a world that God improved and walked away from. That question — what do you do with grace you did not earn and cannot repay — is what follows every miracle.
• **ROT Module Inversion:** The standard Rise of Tiamat framing positions the party as the last line of defense. The Sanctum inversion is that Soliera and Sera are the last line of offense — the world is already defended. What happens next is administrative.
