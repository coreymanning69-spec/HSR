## Northern Court Diplomacy: Part 2

SERA'S REPUTATION:
She is a local legend. Mortals whisper about her disarming joy, catastrophic
violence, and care—the paradox of joy-first enforcement made real.

SOLIERA'S APPROACH:
She visits whatever she missed the first time. People chat with Sera and some
even talk AT Soliera—Sera allows this, which is notable.

WORSHIP DYNAMICS:
Worship is one of the few things Sera lets people do that gets fairly close to
Soliera. The other main exception: children can simply climb on Soliera or do
whatever they want, as they won't do anything super unruly by definition of being
children in this context.


T127: Final Blessing & Preparation #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #LOC-WATERDEEP

Before departing, Soliera grants a final blessing on Waterdeep's orphans and city
guard. The city is stable; enemies are few.

INFRASTRUCTURE EXPANSION:
- The wall of the city is expanded in size and scope
- The docks expand outward
- The Embassy is already working on building better housing and educational areas

SOLIERA'S DECREE:
The populous should be educated and content to provide the best entertainment,
education, and concepts for Sera (and later Ember) to look at and enjoy being
involved with. This is the explicit purpose of Sanctum expansion.

The party prepares for magical travel to Silverymoon.


                         ERA: SILVERYMOON DIPLOMATIC ARC
                              (T128-T134)


T128: Teleportation to Silverymoon #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-PELL #CHAR-LETHA #CHAR-VAJRA-SAFAHR #LOC-BLACKSTAFF-TOWER #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Blackstaff opens a teleport circle. The party (Soliera, Sera, Letha, Pell) steps
through, entering the heart of the Sword Coast's arcane city.

TRAVEL ORDER:
- Letha and Pell go first
- Sera and Soliera go second

PELL'S ANXIETY:
She's nervous about being remade somewhere else—the existential fear of
teleportation. Letha is excited. Sera is over the top with enthusiasm.

THE FIRST ATTEMPT:
The first time Soliera and Sera attempt to pass through, it doesn't work. Soliera
didn't allow it. She looked around, assessing.

Sera announced: "Okay, she said it'll work now!"

And it did.


T129: Arrival and Alarm — The Mythal Reacts #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Arrival triggers the Silverymoon Mythal—alarms sound, the city senses divine
presence. Soliera's arrival nearly crashes the ancient magic.

DETECTION ASYMMETRY:
- The high priestess of Mystra and various high-level adventurers are alerted by
  Sera's arrival—she reads like a bonfire in an empty field
- They cannot sense Soliera directly, but notice the absence of sensing anything
  where she stands

CITY RESPONSE:
- Ward-keys flare
- Scrying nodes lock
- Diviners panic
- Guard lines form
- Wizard cadres brace for a divine incursion they can't name

SOLIERA'S PRESENCE EFFECT:
Her presence collapses all aggressive protocols gently. The wards settle around
her as if recognizing a higher authority.

SERA'S INTERPRETATION:
She interprets the alarmed chatter as "fanfare" and beams like she's being welcomed
with trumpets.


T130: Silverymoon Hospitality #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-ALUSTRIEL-SILVERHAND #CHAR-METHRAMMAR-AERASUME #CHAR-JORUS-AZUREMANTLE #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Alustriel Silverhand, High Lady of Silverymoon, arrives to personally welcome
Soliera and Sera. Tensions ease; divine guests are given quarters and guides.
This newer placement remains authoritative: Alustriel has already met them before
the Moonbridge walk.

SERA'S BEHAVIOR:
She enjoys various snacks and teases a few of the guides. One—a younger, bearded
man—falls smitten instantly. Sera enjoys the doting.

THE ESCORT:
Methrammar Aerasumé and Jorus Azuremantle are among the senior figures who remain
with the visitors after Alustriel's welcome. When Methrammar addresses Soliera
too easily, Sera steps between them with a smile that is corrective rather than
hostile:

> "Ah, don't talk to her so comfortably. We don't know you yet. Talk to me."

The correction passes as soon as it lands. Sera is happy, curious, and ready to
see the city, but she still asks the question the officials have not answered:

> "Well, we can go to the bridge—but you haven't told me yet what you all want…"

They agree that the purpose of Silverymoon's interest can be discussed while they
walk. Sera brightens immediately:

> "Cool, let's go!"

SOLIERA'S EFFECT:
Soliera walks by and the rest of the men fall in line. The city itself almost
seems to want to look better and more presentable for the visiting Goddess.


T131: Divine Tension — The Moonbridge Approach #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-METHRAMMAR-AERASUME #CHAR-JORUS-AZUREMANTLE #LOC-MOONBRIDGE #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Soliera and Sera tour Silverymoon, crossing toward the enchanted Moonbridge.
Sera delights in the city's lights, mixed peoples, and magic, drawing a crowd.
The alarms are fading, yet the citizens are not behaving like people facing an
army. She asks whether Silverymoon is used to gods, then offers her own comparison:

> "Soli and I went to Auril's plane and talked to her there, so we know gods too!"

THE REVEAL:
The Moonbridge comes into view above the River Rauvin as a span of living silver
light. Elves, mages, dwarves, half-elves, halflings, and other residents stop or
step aside to watch. Their attention is disbelief and caution, not a declaration
that the city is human-only or empty around the visitors.

SOLIERA'S QUESTION:
Soliera studies the bridge and the magical field around the city. Corey supplies
the rare, question-shaped word:

> "Mythal?…"

Sera turns to the escorts and asks what a mythal is. Methrammar identifies the
ancient magical field protecting Silverymoon and its interaction with the
Moonbridge; no additional source-world mechanism is locked by this recovered
exchange.

THE CROWD EFFECT:
Guards start to head into the plaza but—like many others—stop along the way.
Something in the air ahead, especially around the barefoot woman, changed their
minds for them.


T132: Moonbridge Pressure — The Ground Shatters #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-METHRAMMAR-AERASUME #CHAR-JORUS-AZUREMANTLE #CHAR-MYSTRA #CHAR-SHAR #LOC-MOONBRIDGE

They approach the Moonbridge. Each step doubles the force applied against Soliera
by the defensive enchantments.

THE BREAKING POINT:
The pressure cannot move or injure Soliera, but it still has physical consequence.
Flagstones crack beneath her as the defensive force bears against an existence it
cannot redirect. Methrammar steps forward far enough to warn her:

> "Lady, it's not supposed to do that."

Soliera raises one foot a few inches above the bridge. She understands that the
span, not she, would fail if the step completed. She withdraws the half-step and
looks back at the escorts. The newer quote placement remains intact:

> "Oh, that didn't work."

The ground is shattered around her. The Moonbridge pushed with all its might and
the force simply slid off her.

SERA'S QUESTION:
Sera turns the problem back on the officials:

> "But why can't she walk on it?"

Methrammar's answer is practical rather than a challenge: the bridge cannot hold
her and was not made for what she is.

SERA'S SUGGESTION:
Sera walks roughly twenty feet along the bank and points across the river:

> "Soli, can you just make another one here?"

THE CITYWIDE TEST:
Soliera moves to the indicated place and raises her hands. Moonlight begins to
coalesce above the water into the possibility of a second span. The Mythal reads
the act as pressure against its long-held order and resists with everything
available to it. Gravity thickens across Silverymoon; the air heats without
burning anyone; lanterns strain; the River Rauvin and the existing Moonbridge
tremble. The force is felt citywide, but Soliera is not attacking, which is why
the resistance can exist at all.

Methrammar and Jorus are forced down near the center of the effect. The current
high priestess of Mystra and the Knights in Silver are alerted.

Soliera ends the attempt voluntarily. Her hands lower, the gathered moonlight
dissolves, and the pressure releases. Methrammar, Jorus, the guards, and the city
begin recovering with the understanding that she chose to stop.

DIVINE RESPONSE:
The magical activity has nevertheless drawn divine attention. Mystra begins to
form—and as Mystra forms, so does Shar.

The city is freaking out.


T133: Shar and Mystra Descend — The Slap #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-MYSTRA #CHAR-SHAR #CHAR-ALUSTRIEL-SILVERHAND #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Mystra has finished forming and is descending into the street. Lady Silverhand
works with guards to evacuate everyone. Shar forms beside Mystra.

DETECTION FAILURE:
Shar and Mystra chat. They can't sense Soliera—only Sera. But to them, Sera
appears as just a high-level adventurer.

SERA'S APPROACH:
She walks over and chirps: "Hey, don't ignore me!"

SHAR'S RESPONSE:
She swats Sera through multiple streets, cracking her into a large brick wall
in a pile of rubble.


T133.5: Sera Versus Shar's Avatars #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SHAR #CHAR-MYSTRA #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Sera hops out of the rubble and looks toward the goddesses. Soliera hasn't moved.

SERA'S COMPLAINT:
"That was uncalled for. I was just saying hello~"

SHAR'S ESCALATION:
She doubles down and forms seven avatars surrounding Sera.

SERA'S RESPONSE:
She raises a hand with conspicuously polite, almost teasing formality.

They strike.

THE CRATER:
The ground is cratered around her. Various weapons have hit her, but she's
literally unharmed—standing in a crater. Her left dress strap has been ripped
by a large warhammer cracking on her.

THE COUNTERATTACK:
She flashes around. After each flash, a different manner of death occurs to
each avatar.


T133.9: Sera Versus Shar Herself #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SHAR #CHAR-MYSTRA #CHAR-SOLIERA #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Soliera smiled some, amused at her creation doing so well.

MYSTRA'S OBSERVATION:
She notices Soliera now—not by power, but by the lack of it. A void where sensing
should work.

SERA'S APPROACH TO SHAR:
Shar is in a 9-foot-tall form. Sera looks up at her, neck straining.

THE FLICK:
She flicks Shar's knee.

THE CONSEQUENCE:
Shar's robe ruffles. Behind her, the glass shatters in all buildings within a
half-mile cone. The cobblestones fly free, crashing into each other.

MYSTRA'S INTERVENTION:
As the shockwave moves forward, Mystra raises her hand and calms it. The Plaza
is in ruins. No one died due to the earlier evacuation.

Shar looks upset. Mystra suggests she just go.

She does.

QUOTE ANCHOR — MYSTRA (to Shar):
"You're underestimating them. The stronger one hasn't moved yet."


T134: Mystra's Gift — Divine Tea Time #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-MYSTRA #CHAR-ALUSTRIEL-SILVERHAND #LOC-SILVERYMOON

Mystra visits privately, gifting Soliera a weave-threaded token (arcane focus or
mark of peace).

THE BALCONY SCENE:
Sera, Alustriel, Mystra, and Soliera enjoy time on a balcony above the Moonbridge's
flowing charge. Sera chats idly with the other goddess and Lady Silverhand.

SOLIERA SPEAKS:
She says more words than ever before in this scene. The vibe is bright, cheerful.
Mystra answers questions from Soliera—who is Tiamat, who is hurting the villagers,
etc.

RESPECT BETWEEN GODDESSES:
Sealed here. Mages of Silverymoon begin studying Soliera's magic from afar.

INFRASTRUCTURE:
The Sanctum officially opens a teleportation circle in the city.


T134.5: Moonflower Garden Interlude #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-ALUSTRIEL-SILVERHAND #LOC-MOONFLOWER-GARDEN #LOC-SILVERYMOON

During the later city wandering already established by the newer chronology,
Sera and Soliera visit the Moonflower Garden, a Divineverse-specific crescent
terrace where cultivated moonflowers glow among the night plantings. The named
garden is campaign canon; it is not asserted as a published Forgotten Realms
location.

Alustriel rejoins them there after the Moonbridge crisis and formal audience.
This is not their first meeting. In the quieter setting, Sera's liking for
Alustriel becomes immediate and legible: Alustriel's calm authority, warmth, and
unforced steadiness remind her of Soliera without making the two women equivalent.

When Soliera turns her attention fully toward Alustriel, the Chosen recognizes a
difference deeper than spell power. Soliera feels more actual than most gods
Alustriel has met or communed with: not a distant portfolio pressing through an
avatar, but a presence whose reality does not depend on worship, display, or
proof. Alustriel keeps her composure and answers that recognition with respect.

T137: Preparing to Adventure — Alustriel's Feast #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-ALUSTRIEL-SILVERHAND #CHAR-PELL #LOC-SILVERYMOON

T140.5: The Sanctum Breathes — Concurrent #CHAR-DEASHI #CHAR-YUIUM #CHAR-NIV-SAIFFAR #CHAR-VELIN #CHAR-MARRIK #CHAR-HOTHGAR #CHAR-KORIN #LOC-SANCTUM #LOC-ANTECHAMBER #LOC-SPINE-OF-THE-WORLD #CONCEPT-SANCTUM-FOUNDERS

While the sleigh moves south toward Greenest, the Sanctum does what it has always done: runs without her.

THE ANTECHAMBER:
Deashi sits on his throne. The crystal pillar holds Yuium's slow black current — moving the way deep water moves, deliberate and without direction. The sconces burn at their usual height, but the quality of the light is different when Soliera is absent. Quieter. Not dimmer. Quieter.

The construct rotation continues. Guards cycle in and out of the corridor. None of them look directly into the chamber. They have learned, over the years, that Deashi's absence of expression is not an invitation to read his mood. He has no mood on offer. He simply is — a nine-foot stillness that the mountain has organized itself around.

Yuium's current shifts behind him in the crystal, slow and ambient. The Veiled Eye is open across every location Soliera and Deashi have ever walked. The Antechamber knows where she is. Deashi knows where she is. Neither of them moves.

> **REFERENCE-LAYER CLARIFICATION (2026-07-22):** “YUIUM's current” and “the Antechamber
> knows” name the distributed Whole's ambient substrate, not a separate dormant person.
> Deashi is the atemporal knower of the enrolled domain. Soliera remains excluded from the
> feed. “Neither moves” preserves the scene's image of current and sentinel at rest; it does
> not create a second observing agent. See DM042_0 §§2 and 8.

The Antechamber does not feel empty. It feels withheld.

NIV-SAIFFAR — THE LOGISTICS CORE:
Niv sits in humanoid form at the Trade Hall with four ledgers open simultaneously, spectacles pushed up into his hairline. 430,000 residents require constant calibration — food rotation, ore processing, textile supply from Luskan, the steady trickle of mortal grievances that somehow still find ways to exist in a place with infinite bread. He handles them all. The surplus report is stamped. The next quarter's projection is already open.

He is the only being in the Sanctum capable of a conversation with both Soliera and Deashi. He does not seek it. He understands that the Sanctum runs best when the staff does not require validation from their principals.
