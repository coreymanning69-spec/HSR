## T537.0 — The Sanctum Uplift

*Recorded event, not a played scene. No dialogue is authored here. If this is
ever played in FORGE, it is written then, and Soliera's lines come from Corey.*

Sera and Ember came to Soliera together and said they both believed Doran and
Wren had passed their test. Their acceptance as full Sanctum field Stewards and
their uplift happened together. Sera and Ember asked Soliera to do for them what
she had once done for Korin.

Soliera did not answer in words. She did it.

The two candidates were taken to the Sanctum and returned to Waterdeep the same
hour; Soliera's transit is delete-and-remake rather than travel, so no time was
spent crossing anything. What came back was not a pair of promising
steward-candidates. It was two full Tier-3 field Stewards finished to the top of
the mortal band, still below Velin and Letha's settled inner-circle Tier 2.

**What the uplift did.** Both were carried to level 20 outright. It was not
awarded as experience and no threshold was crossed — their campaign XP is still
165,000 and no longer means anything about their level. Neither was made a
generic perfect twenty. Each was given the specific things they had earned and
the specific things they lacked:

- **Doran** was finished as a Battle Master: 370 HP, four attacks, two Action
  Surges, his superiority dice raised to d12, and the Intelligence seam that
  Bane's chosen had opened at T531 closed for good. His armor class was fixed at
  a flat 25 rather than lifted — he is meant to be hit. The uplift also carried
  every one of his ability scores up by two at once, which is why his whole sheet
  moved rather than one number.
- **Wren** was carried to a full Cleric 20 / Sorcerer 20 stack: 220 HP, her ward
  raised to 75, fifty sorcery points, a ninth tier of spells opened, and the four
  domain features her Portal and Liberation stacks had been owed for beats
  without ever being written. Her gray robe was replaced with a Robe of the
  Archmagi in brilliant white and gold, and her Wand of Fireball was remade into
  an indestructible Staff of the Magi — divine white wood, a black bird standing
  on a metal perch at its tip. She was not given *wish*. She does not need it;
  she can ask.

**What it looks like.** They are both in white now. Doran's mirror plate was
already white; Wren's robe was gray and is not anymore, so for the first time the
pair reads as matched rather than inverted. Both of their masteries are open,
both carry the Sanctum's blessing, and both carry the Divine Trio's direct stamp
where anyone who can read such a thing will see it. A permanent crown of stars
sits over Wren's head and does not go out.

They cannot be mistaken for unsponsored mortals any more. That is a cost as much
as a gift, and it has not been paid yet.

**Still open.** Whether either of them has understood what was done to them,
what they say about it, and what Vajra makes of two Sanctum field Stewards walking
into Blackstaff Tower looking like that — none of it is authored. The Deep Road
report below has not been delivered.

### T537.5 — Niv's update
When the Steward elevation went through, Niv sent a one-line update to the outer network: *Status change: Doran and Wren are no longer probationary.* Then he made tea.

### T537.6 — Korin's moved face
When the official report on the Steward elevation came through, Korin read it once, nodded once, and went back to his deployment map. That's his moved face.

## T537 — Blackstaff Report Stage

Doran and Wren are rested at Blackstaff Tower with Vajra Safahr present. They
carry the played T536 Deep Road report: a sealed Menzoberranzan approach, eight
Xorlarrin dead simultaneously without wounds or magical signature, and the iron
flask containing Maegera missing without a release event. Soliera, Sera, and
Ember are at the Sanctum.

The report was later delivered and Vajra selected the northbound route recorded
below. The report's unresolved causes remain unresolved.

### T537.7 — Letha in the Mergeverse
In the Mergeverse, Letha is doing what she always does: learning what the local music sounds like before she plays anything herself. Veyren says she'll perform in two weeks. She says a month. There's a bet.

### T537.8 — Letha's scouting
Letha already knows the route to the Oak City portal — scouted it twice before Korin mentioned wanting to go. She hasn't told him. She wants to watch him figure out she'd already been there.

### T537.9 — Korin's contingent
Korin's 150-knight contingent doesn't know they're Sanctum-adjacent. They think they're working for a regional lord. He prefers it that way — fewer expectations, better information.

### T537.10 — Veyren's map
Veyren found a tracking route through a mountain pass that Korin missed twenty years ago. He's mapped it and is waiting for the right time to tell him. He thinks Korin will find it funny.

### T537.11 — The merged assessment
Malharon and Daire haven't been in the same room in three weeks. The threat assessments arrive pre-merged. Kessla has stopped asking who wrote which section.

### T537.12 — Daire's decision
Daire's five station chiefs flagged the Blackstaff Tower situation before it became a named arc beat. He reported it to Kessla. Kessla chose not to send it forward — Soliera had it. Sometimes the right intelligence decision is recognizing you don't need to act.

## T538.2–T539.2 — Blackstaff to Amphail

Vajra sends Doran and Wren through Amphail toward Silverymoon, Harshnag, the Eye
of the All-Father, and its Oracle. The old tower is deferred by Doran, not
abandoned: the one-use ring direction to Keltar Dardragon remains unused.

At Amphail, Wren distinguishes a promoted person from a rewritten one and admits
that no internal test can settle which happened to her. She does not evade the
question: she decides that being able to help people now, as someone once helped
her, is enough to act as Wren. This resolves the question for herself, not for
Vajra; their conversation remains unplayed.

Wren sends Velin the fire-giant-dig report with exact coordinates by trade mail.
Velin's answer is a standing order: say nothing to Zalto until she holds the
letter, and assume nothing about the crew's owner. Ilva Dunmar supplies a
Zhentarim road-contract dossier: collection signatures do not match contract
signatures and three hill estates armed within one month. Her half-name is held
for the return leg. Letha's reply is domestic and warm.

### T538.5 — The deployment table
Korin was reviewing deployment maps alone when Soliera walked in and stood behind him looking at the table. She didn't say anything. He didn't turn around. After a few minutes she pointed at one area and said *that one.* He moved a marker. She left. He's not sure if it was advice or just her being curious. He moved the marker anyway.

## T539.3–T539.9 — Amphail

Doran repairs the ditch behind the Roaringhorn wall and clears three elms from
the north track. Wren treats local injuries and needs, then publicly calls
herself the first and only priestess of the Sanctum: no liturgy, rite,
ordination, or authorising body accompanies the declaration.

Doran gives the Sanctum's open-door doctrine to civilians: membership is by
wanting in; someone bearing Sera's enclosed black-bird sigil can be asked; once
in, the person is protected wherever they are. The Sanctum is not a place one
must reach. The promise is public and ungated.

At Tylandar Roaringhorn's banquet, three unarmoured hill giants arrive for food.
There is no initiative. Doran takes the lone under-shepherd's spear without
comment, offers food and safe departure, and sets the Giant Cleaver point-first
in the dirt with his helmet on its crossguard. The lead giant recognizes the
gesture and sits. Giants and villagers share the square before the giants leave
fed, armed, and unharmed.

The giants report that tribute now moves north to a distinct "Big Boss," whose
counting has worsened. Wren cuts Sera's black bird, wings out and fully enclosed
by white, into a slate token. It holds a Glyph-of-Warding *sending* keyed to
Guh's touch: twenty-five words each way. She sends the token north with the
giants and states that Nosnra is alive, fed, and answers to the mark. The channel
is open and unanswered.

Wren later tells Doran that she is Wren and the first and only priestess of the
Sanctum. The village phrasing "priest of Lady Ember" remains a spoken fact as
spoken, but her own settled understanding is priestess of the Sanctum — she is a
girl, and priestess is the settled corpus term. Ember has no independent
power; she exists through Soliera's continuing permission. This is unspoken by
the characters and does not authorize a divine voice or new theology.

## T540.0 — North from Amphail

Amphail is left materially better: a filled ditch, cleared elms, repaired and
treated people, and seven Sanctum potions on the storeroom's high shelf. Arleosa
Starhenge's near-reveal advances one notch and is not elaborated here. Doran and
Wren leave north in the morning. Half the village sees them to the road; no new
speech is made.
