# ARC 6: HOARD OF THE DRAGON QUEEN (T141–T190)
#CHAR-REZMIR #CHAR-CLOUDGAZER #CHAR-TALIS #CHAR-KESSLA #CHAR-DRIZZT
#LOC-GREENEST #LOC-SKYREACH-CASTLE #LOC-MERE-OF-DEAD-MEN
#CONCEPT-HOARD-OF-THE-DRAGON-QUEEN #CONCEPT-MODULE-INVERSION #THEME-SERA-JOY-FIRST-VIOLENCE-SECOND


**Where:** Greenest → Sword Coast road north → Mere of Dead Men → Naerytar → Skyreach Castle.

**Why:** Tiamat cult active. Masks in transit. Following the trail.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Steady. The arc is management and correction — she drives the direction, Sera drives the execution.
- *Sera:* Engaged and functional. The burnt caravan and doll are the ignition point. After that she is efficient and occasionally delighted by the chaos.
- *Kessla:* First real field arc. She watches Sera turn the Talis lodge into a surrendered intelligence node and begins processing what being close to these entities actually means. Not traumatized yet — that comes at the Well.
- *Drizzt:* Competent and grounded. Functions as field scout and mortal anchor. Recognizes power he cannot measure but doesn't freeze.
- *Cloudgazer (module name Cloudchaser):* Submitted. The bluff failed the moment Sera looked at him. Genuine moment of recalibration — he goes to the Sanctum or goes free, both options are the same to Sera.
- *Supporting cast:* Rezmir dies without ceremony. Blagothkus is turned into paste for refusing to descend. The cult's organizational structure collapses backwards from the top.

**Sanctum Population:** ~500k range. Road networks expanding.

**Ember:** Does not exist.

# ARC 7: RISE OF TIAMAT (T191–T326)
#CHAR-TIAMAT #CHAR-ELIA #CHAR-CHUTH #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-KESSLA
#LOC-WELL-OF-DRAGONS #LOC-MISTY-FOREST
#CONCEPT-RISE-OF-TIAMAT #CONCEPT-EMBER-BIRTH #CONCEPT-TIAMAT-EXECUTION #THEME-DIVINE-INEVITABILITY


**Where:** Misty Forest → Waterdeep council → Mustering Fields → Well of Dragons.

**Why:** Tiamat's summoning ritual must not complete. The mask arc closes here.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* The war preparation scenes show quiet contentment in Sera's joy. The Well of Dragons is her most decisive moment this arc — compressing the sky is not rage, it's administration.
- *Sera:* Joyful through the mustering fields. The Black Death attack snaps her briefly. The Well is where she catches Ember's first strike midair. No hesitation, no drama.
- *Kessla:* Traumatized arc. The Black Death attack breaks the illusion that being near the girls is safe for mortal observers. She is loved but she is not protected from witnessing what they are.
- *Elia (silver dragon):* Observer and mediator. Warns about the metallic dragon council. Flies overhead as the army returns. Steady moral compass that isn't divine.
- *Ember (born T299):* Ember birth beat: see T299 (DM010_2) — obsidian skin, wildfire hair, coal-light kintsugi lines. Soliera speaks once. Niv carries her out.
- *Supporting cast:* Mortal armies are existentially unsettled — the constructs and divine blessings leave them questioning their own purpose. Kessla's vomiting after Chuth's erasure is the best diagnostic for how mortals experience proximity to godhood.

**Sanctum Population:** ~500,000 at T300.
