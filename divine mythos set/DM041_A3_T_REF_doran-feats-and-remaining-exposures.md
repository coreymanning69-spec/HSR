## WHAT REMAINS EXPOSED

These are intended pressure lanes. Do not patch them merely because play finds
them.

- **No exit:** the Cape of the Mountebank remains declined. Twenty feet of flight
  is not an extraction. If Wren is down, occupied, or out of range, Doran remains
  in the room until the room is finished. This is the load-bearing lane now.
- **Cannot withdraw:** speed 30 in kit leaves no reliable fighting retreat.
  Winning or being taken are more likely than disengaging cleanly.
- **Gas:** thermal exemption does not prevent poison, particulate, or
  suffocation. Wren's *heroes' feast* can close this for twelve hours if she
  thinks of it.
- **One reaction:** opportunity attack, Deft Answer, and Riposte contend for a
  single reaction while his action economy runs to thirteen attacks. Anything
  that punishes him for being able to answer only one thing per round between
  his turns is working as designed.
- **Flat AC 25:** nothing raises it any more — not planting, not both daggers,
  not Wren's *haste*. The tier-4 band lands on him 40–65% of the time, and
  dropping the kite for Wren takes him to 21. Attrition is the live threat even
  at 370 HP.
- **Ranged pressure:** thrown daggers at 30/70 plus 20 feet of flight narrow
  the gap but still do not fully answer a cloud giant that can misty step. The
  lane is partially closed, not gone.

**Closed at level 20:** the INT save seam. Resilient (INT) plus the uplift carry
him to **+10**, so Nihiloor and mind-flayer aim points no longer find an open
door there. Do not write encounters that assume the old +2.
