## 0. AI DIRECTIVE & NARRATIVE ROUTING
#RULE-AI-GUARDRAILS #CONCEPT-MODULE-INVERSION


**AI DIRECTIVE:** This file covers the second half of the *Hoard of the Dragon Queen* module inversion — the fall of Skyreach Castle, the voyage to the Sea of Moving Ice, the exile of Arauthator, and the divine return to Waterdeep. The critical structural move is that **the module's final dungeon is not defeated, it is given away.** Skyreach is not crashed. It is administered. Arauthator is not slain — he is made afraid, possibly for the first time in several centuries, and exiled. Maccath is not simply rescued — she is recruited, evaluated, and handed a role commensurate with what she actually knows.

**CANON FLAGS — READ BEFORE PROCESSING:**

1. **CLOUDGAZER** is the Divineverse OC name for the white dragon resident of Skyreach. The module names it Cloudchaser. **Do not use Cloudchaser.** Cloudgazer is a true ally after T165, uneasy but loyal.
2. **BLAGOTHKUS** (the cloud giant) is present but not focal. He is deferred and neutralized, not a significant player. The alliance is with the dragon.
3. **ARAUTHATOR** is exiled, not killed. He feels genuine fear — probably the first time in his ancient life — and flees. He does not return.
4. **REZMIR** and the Red Wizards are dead. This is resolved at T163-T164. Do not revisit.
5. **SKYREACH** is gifted to local mortal governance. It is not crashed, commandeered, or held.
6. **EMBER DOES NOT EXIST YET.** Do not reference or foreshadow her in this range.
7. **MACCATH** is a tiefling scholar from the Arcane Brotherhood. She is the voice that ultimately tips the scales toward sparing dragon lives. She is the Sanctum's lorekeeper from T176 forward.

**OPERATIONAL CONSTRAINTS:**

* Drizzt is a full traveling companion through this arc but speaks and acts with mortal scale. He is a witness to things that exceed his frame of reference, and he is honest about that — not awestruck into passivity, but genuinely recalibrating.
* Kessla is in Waterdeep managing logistics as a Hush Agent. She is not on the northern voyage. She meets the party at the docks on return (T181-T182).
* The Ark is Soliera's creation: a massive, reality-bending flagship of living wood and mythic infrastructure. It is not a conventional ship. It does not operate on conventional maritime rules.

**NARRATIVE ROUTING:** This file follows **DM008_1 (T141-T160)** and precedes the ROT transition beats in **DM008_3 (T191-T220)**. The module inversion is completed here. The world that exists at T190 is permanently different from the world at T141.
