## T533 — IRONSLAG APPROACH

### T533.1 — Blackstaff Briefing and Departure

Vajra Safahr briefed Doran and Wren at Blackstaff Tower with Neverember, Sera,
Ember, and silent Soliera present. The trainees were ordered to confirm
Vonindod assembly progress and what Zalto still lacked, confirm Maegera's
containment, free the captives, and avoid giving the North a story in which the
Sanctum broke a mountain. Zalto's fate was left to their judgment.

The Dessarin watershed and the rebuilding towns below Ironslag were the mortal
stakes. Resurrection was stated openly in the room, so the test was not whether
the recruits could be brought back. Sera proposed letting the titan finish so
she could fight it; Ember brought the focus back to the recruits' assignment,
and Sera yielded. The room laughed when Sera's safety-net version turned the
question of death into a question of when she would arrive afterward.

Doran asked Vajra for the summary once more. Wren worried the transformed sigil
at her chest; the low-grade anxiety was visible and unremarked. Sera offered to
move them directly. The departure was instead set for Triboar because
Neverember's concern was the story produced by the approach: two recruits on a
road rather than the Sanctum arriving at the mountain. Sera cast nothing. Every
transit she offers is Soliera granting it, and Soliera granted this one without
comment.

### T533.2 — Triboar, Days One and Two

The pair arrived roughly three hundred feet from the Long Road and walked into
Triboar. Wynter Ilmani of the Twelve and Ranulf met them on the road and
recognized the Sanctum immediately from Svardborg. Darathra Shendrel recognized
Remallia's silver band on Wren's wrist and told her to keep it visible between
Triboar and Silverymoon.

Wren worked from Alagh's ledger in Darathra's order and healed ninety-one
people, worst-first. Ranulf's aunt remained fourteenth even with the boy
present. Wren also blessed two fields; barley rose green through ash. Doran
planted the Giant Cleaver in the cobbles, hung his helm on it, and spent two
days moving beams, roofs, and freight. Three men could not pull the Cleaver
free.

At dusk on the second day, Niv's column arrived: twenty constructs and seven
loaded wagons. Doran and Wren promised further Sanctum dispatches—constructs,
masons, healers, and raise-dead support—inside ten days. Darathra accepted the
arrangement, called them children, and the whole square saw them off at dawn.

The image that left Triboar was now larger than either recruit: Wren's
unnecessary wings, crown of light, burning sigil, and daylight above Doran's
seven-foot mithral silhouette reflecting her light. The town had no other
vocabulary for it. That picture began traveling the Dessarin Valley even
though Neverember's narrower bar—not breaking a mountain—remained intact.

### T533.3 — The Road, Days Three Through Nine

They covered roughly fifty miles a day, with Wren flying and Doran moving
without fatigue, and deliberately skirted Everlund. On the second road night in
the Evermoors, a wisp used a two-month-old wrecked freight wagon as bait over a
troll larder. Wren spent a sixth-level *greater dispel magic* and found nothing
to grip because the wisp was anatomy, not a spell. She opened sixty feet of
*daylight*, spread the wings she did not need for flight, crowned herself in
motes, and maintained *haste* on Doran through the Ring of Concentration.

Three trolls recognized the Sanctum from the Svardborg story but did not know
Doran and Wren. They negotiated and were allowed to go north. Wren held
*Portal Shear* throughout and did not spend it. Later long rests restored the
road expenditure before the pair reached Ironslag's approach.

### T533.4 — Yakfolk Village Threshold

On day nine out of Triboar, Doran and Wren stand on the last high shelf before
the ice mountains north of the Silver Marches, looking down and east at the
yakfolk village above Ironslag. Terraced fields, a turning mill wheel, thatched
roofs, and cultivated green sit in country that should not support them.
Figures with the wrong shape for men walk the perimeter. Coal and hot iron ride
the wind; the forge's vibration carries through the rock. No approach has been
chosen, nothing has been rolled, and they have not been seen.

Doran and Wren remain the ordinary rolling field pair. Wren arrives after road
rests at full HP, ward, slots, and sorcery points; her Ring of Concentration
holds *haste* on Doran, Crown of Stars and Robe of Stars are active, and
*Portal Shear* is unspent. The Ironslag run begins from this threshold.

## T535 — IRONSLAG: NEGOTIATED DESCENT AND DIVINE RESOLUTION

### T535.1 — The Negotiated Descent

Five consecutive contacts resolved on talk: Brimskarda, the feasting-hall
goblins, the slave-pen hobgoblins, Kruvarr of the ninth shift, and Zaltember.
Doran attempted diplomacy first in every case, including with monsters. The
single kill in the dungeon was a salamander in the ore rooms, killed because it
was the first thing encountered with no shared language.

Zaltember, spared and apologized to after being physically put down, walked
point voluntarily as a cooperative guide. Berrik, a shield dwarf rescued from a
smelter after refusing to beg, followed barefoot up an iron grill stair over the
foundry and suffered for it silently. Neither Doran, protected by thermal
immunity, nor Wren, concentrating on *protection from energy*, was touched by
the heat. Wren deliberately declined to spend resources on Berrik.

### T535.2 — Zalto's Workroom

Zalto was alone, hammering the eleventh failed attempt at one shoulder plate
for the Vonindod. He had not come down in six days. The adamantine forge he
needed could not be lit, so the finest smith in the Sword Mountains was working
at an ordinary forge.

His transactional opening posture came apart in sequence. Doran's silent white
plate had no compromised seam, and the Cleaver had no grain, fold pattern, or
forge scale: it had not been worked, but decided. Doran set it into the basalt
floor without effort, and Zalto read the workmanship correctly. Wren then told
him that most chromatic dragons were dead and that Soliera had personally
killed hundreds on the march to Tiamat's ritual. Doran added that Tiamat was
dead and that her essence was Ember. Zalto's two-year dragon-slaying enterprise
was a monument to a finished war.

Zalto then reached the last conclusion himself. House Xorlarrin had asked
whether the Vonindod would be thorough, never whether it would be enough,
because there was nothing left for it to be enough for. The drow were buying a
weapon against the surface North while letting him fund it under the name of a
dead war.

### T535.3 — Declined Leverage and Invitation

Doran and Wren had held Zalto's son, walked past his wife, and arrived
empty-handed. Zalto said hostage leverage would not have worked: he would have
chosen his destiny over his son and let them keep him. Zaltember heard this
from around the corner and fled. Doran and Wren told Zalto to go after him and
offered to wait. Zalto left his maul behind, ordered his hell hounds to protect
the pair, returned with the boy, put his own key ring in Zaltember's hand, and
sent him to tell Brimskarda that the dragons were dead.

Doran asked Wren to *sending* Ember, then raised a hand and asked Zalto whether
that was acceptable. The question answered Zalto's objection about whose forge
it was. Zalto formally invited Ember with his hand over his chest and guaranteed
her safety under his mountain.

### T535.4 — The Forge

Sera arrived first, then Ember, then Soliera between moments. Zalto went to both
knees involuntarily. Sera told him to stand and declared friendship because he
had treated her recruits correctly. Ember's presence reheated all eleven failed
plates and woke the banked forge.

Ember put one hand on the adamantine forge and lit it in ten seconds. Every
flue drew, every fire roared, and every scattered Vonindod fragment rang in
sympathy. The forge had been cold for 140 years; neither Zalto's father nor his
grandfather had seen it lit.

As the forge climbed past survivable heat, Soliera corrected the room rather
than the flame: colossus-melting heat in the forge and spring-morning air around
it, simultaneously. She healed Berrik completely and restored his clothing and
boots. Berrik knelt and wept. The smallness of the act beside the forge was the
point: the slave Zalto had never looked at directly was seen and restored.

### T535.5 — The Hall and the Vonindod

Soliera lifted a hand as a courtesy so mortals could watch and enlarged the
assembly hall from a 50-foot ceiling to 120 feet. Walls and floor extended
without a seam, and two years of chalk marks remained undisturbed. Zalto
understood that Ostorian construction had never been a lost technique. Somebody
had simply decided the room was larger; nine generations of his family had died
holding the wrong question.

Ember and Sera proposed giving the Vonindod a mind, and Soliera assented. Pieces
came from across the mountain through halls and vents. Four fragments that had
never been in Ironslag simply became present, including two Zalto knew had been
at the bottom of Klauthen Vale. The torso stood at full height before its feet
arrived, and a sword manifested in its hand.

The completed Vonindod stands 110 feet tall. It is animate, sentient,
cooperative, and silent, with a tracking socket-eye. On Ember's instruction not
to smush anyone, it laid its roughly 90-foot sword flat with the point away from
every living thing. It knelt to Zalto, lowered its head to fire-giant eye level,
and lifted him in an open palm.

### T535.6 — The Whole Fortress

Sera intercepted fifty charging fire giants in the doorway, introduced herself,
told them to come in and be quiet, and walked back rather than teleporting so
they could follow her with their eyes. Giants, ogres, hobgoblins, goblins, and
four hundred people emerging from opened slave pens assembled in the hall. No
violence followed.

The contact lasted for hours. Worship was mass and disorganized. Ember taught
the shoulder join to two dozen fire giant smiths and spent two hours talking
with Cinderhild. Sera played tug of war against twenty-one fire giants anchored
by Kruvarr, did not move an inch for eleven seconds, and snapped the four-inch
iron-core ore hawser. That, more than the forge or the colossus, is the story
the giants will retell.

### T535.7 — Departure and Standing Consequences

Nineteen yakfolk descended and lost their benevolent guise within seconds of
Ember's proximity; more were arriving above and more were expected. Sanctum
constructs deployed and began counting, listing, and establishing water and
medical priority for the four hundred freed workers.

Doran told Zalto that Niv or Velin would arrive to negotiate binding terms:
long, written, enforceable rules handled neither by the goddesses nor by the
trainees. Zalto accepted without resistance. Doran, Wren, Soliera, Sera, and
Ember departed at day's end; their immediate destination was not supplied.

Ironslag closed with no deaths among its people. The salamander in the ore rooms
was the site's only casualty. The forge is lit without Maegera. Zalto and fifty
fire giants are allied, four hundred captives are free, the Vonindod is complete
and sentient, and Ironslag stands under Sanctum protection pending the written
settlement.

Draac and Taal Xorlarrin have not arrived. The two drow mages and six elite
warriors are six days overdue with an iron flask containing Maegera. Their
client no longer needs the primordial or the missing fragments, and now knows
what they concealed. Their arrival at an allied fortress, a living Vonindod,
and a Sanctum protectorate is the hottest unresolved Ironslag thread.

## T536 — THE DEEP ROAD

### T536.1 — Hunt the Drow

Doran and Wren descended through Ironslag's twelve mine levels into the
Northdark and turned east toward House Xorlarrin's seat. They were hunting Draac
Xorlarrin, Taal Xorlarrin, six elite warriors, and the iron flask containing
Maegera.

Wren cast *discern location* on Maegera and received a null return. She
recognized the result as neither ward nor block: a creature contained in an
iron flask occupies no plane and has no coordinates until the stopper is drawn.
The physical flask remains trackable. Her spoken conclusion was, **"Stop hunting
the fire. Hunt the drow."** A follow-up *divination* returned weal and woe
together—the black cloak ahead and slowing, and the destination city watching
the road.

They traveled openly for three days. Wren flew on manifested white wings with
the Crown of Stars lit and the Robe of Stars active; Doran walked with 120 feet
of helm-sight. At a seep on day one he cut eight sets of sign in two files, one
heavy print repeatedly shifting the weight of a long-carried belt.

### T536.2 — The Reception

A twenty-drow reception held a quarter-mile gallery: an elite captain, a mage,
four more elites, fourteen drow, and two giant lizards. They had watched Wren's
Crown approach for an hour and chose reception rather than ambush. The officer
opened, **"You're three days early, and you're going the wrong direction to be
them."** Wren caught the slip. A *sending* had gone east minutes before contact.

Doran asked directly about the primordial and read the officer cleanly—Insight
19 against 2. She knew exactly what he meant and showed relief, as if she had
expected a worse conversation.

The consequential fighting lasted twelve seconds. Doran traded one attack for
Commander's Strike, and Wren's Crown mote killed the mage who had sent the
report. An eight-bolt volley landed once, reducing Wren's ward from 50 to 46.
She spent 3 SP to Widen *reverse gravity* down the road; fourteen unanchored
bodies struck the seventy-foot ceiling. Eight drow and the dead mage were
destroyed on impact while the captain and four elites remained pinned.

Doran deliberately unplanted, rode the field upward, took 21 bludgeoning on the
ceiling, and crossed one hundred feet of inverted stone. His three ordinary
Cleaver swings resolved miss, 31, and 37. Wren raised the short white Wand of
Fireball and discharged its 9th-level cast into the cluster for 50 damage at DC
20. The captain and three elites failed and burned; one elite saved. Doran stood
inside the blast unharmed by fire. Wren released concentration, the gallery
turned over, the final elite died to 31 falling damage, and Doran's vind-rune
*feather fall* carried him down cleanly.

Fourteen of twenty drow were dead. Group morale held on 16, but two individual
break checks failed. Two drow fled east on foot; a lizard-rider had already gone
east with the report. Three laid down their weapons and knelt.

### T536.3 — The Answers and the Bolt-Hole

Wren spoke Undercommon without a spell. She deliberately named the Sanctum,
Soliera, and Soliere-Dzan. Her three reads were clean—27, 28, and 25
against 20, 6, and 11—and no answer was a lie. Nothing had passed the picket
going east in eleven days. Menzoberranzan had sent out no envoy, patrol, trade,
or slaver for more than a tenday. The picket itself had been sent sealed, with
no courier, relief, or route home. When told they could leave, one survivor's
unanswered question was, **"Leave to where?"**

The three surrendered drow were released without conditions, promise, escort,
or assigned destination. They kept their lizard and water and left their
weapons on the stone. Their destination is deliberately unrecorded.

Six hours west and forty feet off the Deep Road, the pair found eight bodies in
an undisturbed bolt-hole. Weapons remained sheathed. Two sat; one cup still
stood in the dust beside a hand. There were no wounds, blood, burns, bruises,
venom signs, or postures of defense, and no magical signature. Taal had not cast
*shield*. All eight had stopped simultaneously inside a fraction of a second.

Draac lay face-down at the back wall. His belt and clasp were intact; the closed,
undamaged loop for the two-pound iron flask was empty. The room had not been
slagged or opened by a Dawn Titan. Maegera and the flask had been removed, not
released. Doran and Wren recorded positions, postures, the cup, empty loop, and
absence of signature, then left the dead where they lay.

### T536.4 — Return and Handoff

The pair spent two days returning from the Underdark and took long rests. Every
tracked spend restored: Doran returned to 308/308 with 16 superiority dice,
Action Surge, Second Wind, and *feather fall* ready; Wren returned to 197/197,
ward 50, all 106 slots, 30 sorcery points, seven Crown motes, and ten wand
charges. The Ring of Concentration reloaded *haste* on Doran and *Portal Shear*
remained unspent. T536's durable costs are information and exposure.

### T535.5 — Common hall evening
Letha was playing something soft in the common hall when Ember came in and sat cross-legged three feet away, watching her hands. Velin arrived for a completely unrelated reason, checked the room, and stayed. Nobody spoke for a while. Letha eventually asked Ember what she wanted to hear. Ember said she didn't know yet. Letha kept playing. Velin made notes. That was the whole evening.

### T536.5 — Kessla holds the seal
Kessla knew about the Menzoberranzan seal before Velin's desk saw the report. She held it for four days — not because she was slow, but because the shape of the silence was telling her more than the facts.

### T536.6 — Letha's archive
Before she left for the Mergeverse, Letha recorded the lute arrangements she'd written during the Ironslag and Deep Road deployments. She left them in the archive unlabeled. Pell found them. They've been playing in the common hall since.

### T536.7 — Daire and Ember
Daire was alone drafting a network summary when Ember sat down across from him uninvited and started reading one of his older reports upside-down. He didn't say anything. She read for several minutes, then asked one question — specific, about a location name — that implied she'd understood the whole thing. He answered. She nodded and left. He updated his assessment of what she retains. He hasn't mentioned it to anyone yet.
