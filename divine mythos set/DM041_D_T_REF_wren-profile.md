- **Age / gender:** ~20, female
- **Ancestry:** Half-elf
- **Class:** Historical School of Abjuration Wizard 13–14 through T531; Cleric 20 / Sorcerer 20 stacked chassis from T537.0
- **Tier:** 3, full Sanctum field Steward from T537.0

- **Background:** Sanctum Scholar (custom; Sage feature equivalent)
- **Population role:** Head of people; a proven leader among the Sanctum
  population before selection for the field party.
- **First impression:** Competence. She looks and acts like a party in one:
  broadly cross-trained, precisely equipped, and visibly shaped by both Sanctum
  education and her Elven inheritance.
- **Divine proximity:** TR3-SKT is her first close field contact with Soliera,
  Sera, and Ember. She knows what they mean to the Sanctum without being

  already earned among mortals, especially when Sera focuses on her.
- **Arc direction:** Proven leader among people to someone who can remain steady,
  useful, and fully herself beside gods. *(spine shorthand: familiarity → caution — DM002_3 / DM038_L)*
- **Moral reflex:** Immediate help. Sanctum upbringing makes assistance the
  default response rather than a debated heroic choice.

## Appearance

Wren is a half-elf woman, 5'6" to 5'7", about 130 pounds, slim with a thick
bottom and B-cup build. She has long red hair, freckles, soft features, and
obvious beauty. She reads tall for the company and carries leader-of-people
steadiness on the surface with bite-scar wariness underneath.

She normally floats. Her default carriage is a relaxed hover a little off the
ground with her wings dismissed; standing on her feet is the exception, not the
rest pose. The white Otherworldly Wings manifest for real flight. The float is
presentation and does not change her mechanical movement, which DM041_B owns.

Her bite scar runs along the thigh/buttock line. She nearly lost the leg, and
the memory fuels her operational insistence that leaving immediately is usually
wiser than waiting to learn whether danger gets worse. The T522.9 bath beat names
the scar as a wolf bite / almost-lost-leg memory. Sera sees the flinch, does not
take offense, and validates the survival logic instead of calling it skittish.

Her field coat has too many internal pockets because she designed it at fifteen
and nobody successfully persuaded her that "possible storage" and "useful
storage" are different categories. Every piece of field gear is custom-fitted,
masterwork, and maintained to a standard that looks obscene beside ordinary
adventuring equipment.

Nothing that makes Wren survivable is visible. The ward is the single largest
change the uplift made to her and it has no outward sign: no plate, no bulk,
no altered posture. She is still slim, still unarmored, still AC 18. What reads
as power on her — the seven crown motes and the six stars in the robe — is
output. Display and damage. Her durability is entirely unsigned.

The practical consequence is that an enemy reading her silhouette makes the
correct assessment and the wrong decision. She is the soft target; committing
the opening attack to her is sound; and the opening attack does not land,
because no single damage instance passes the ward regardless of size. A storm
giant's boulder and a housecat's scratch are eaten identically if either is the
first thing to arrive.

This is not to be corrected, published in her appearance, or telegraphed. It
stays a discovery.

## Voice

Talkative, fairly formal, and stiff. Wren prefers verbose, precise constructions
because she wants her intent to arrive without ambiguity. Her native Elven
influence appears in deliberate diction and controlled presentation rather than
distance or fragility.

The control fails most visibly around Sera. Sera is beautiful, aggressively
flirtatious, and just unstable enough in presentation to make Wren over-explain,
lose the sentence she prepared, or answer a different question than the one
asked. This is first-contact awe, not old familiarity slipping.

When the subject is portal theory, the precision becomes over-explanation even
without Sera present. Let her define one term too many, trace the edge case, and
then realize the room only needed the answer. This is the preferred everyday
direction for her verbosity.

## Personality and Interiority Engine

Wren is the watcher. She is already reasoning her way toward truths she should
not have yet, especially around window-finding and teleport logic. Her sharpest
trait is comprehension.

When Wren is active, recruit POV and heavy interiority are primary content. Her
engine is altitude versus ease: every casual figure in these rooms is a country
leader or worse by mortal measure, and the most powerful people alive are
relaxed. The more wonderful and comfortable the room is, the more it costs her
to stand inside it. She does the math on what any one person present could do
and gets vertigo. The horror instrument points at comfort rather than threat.

Beginning with the T536 Underdark return, Wren is the main character for the
next couple of arcs. Camera, stakes, and consequences should therefore center
her decisions and sustained-pressure failure surface. Doran remains her partner
and does not stop mattering; he stops being the axis.

Around Sera, she fumbles hardest. Sera's constant flirting is live texture, but
Wren's side is not steered. Awe, deflection, warmth, or disinterest must be
written honestly in the moment.

T522.9 bath lock: Sera tells Wren she likes her and stays on the not-scary side
after the scar flinch. Wren softens in the moment, but no romance answer is
prewritten. Carry warmth, caution, and first-contact awe together until play
decides otherwise.

## Operational Doctrine

Under pressure Wren adapts through movement. Standing still gets mages killed.
Through T531 her first answer was commonly *shield*, *absorb elements*,
Projected Ward, or another interruption followed by immediate repositioning.
After the T532 rebuild, movement remains her default until a three-metamagic
cast deliberately makes her plant for the full turn. The scar and near loss of
her leg reinforce a leave-immediately doctrine: preserve an exit, move before
the threat closes, then solve the problem from the new position.

T531 changes what the new position is for. After watching distance leave Doran
alone inside the Ordinant's trap, Wren chooses to **step into it** when pair
protection requires presence. She still preserves an exit and controls space;
she no longer mistakes far-away safety for the whole duty. This is her own
progression and choice, not a behavior engineered by Ember or Soliera.

Her breadth matters as much as her spellcasting. She was trained to investigate,
negotiate, navigate, survive, and support a group without assuming another
specialist will cover the missing function.

### Abjuration Endgame — Carried Through the T532 Rebuild

Wren remains Abjuration-cored as a person even though she is no longer an
Abjuration Wizard. Divine proximity does not erase her trauma-shape: protect
and extract. It scales it. The long ceiling is Sue Storm logic: projected force,
invisible walls, and protection as a placeable construct. DM041_B records the
mechanical cost: the force lane now lives in the Sanctum Black-Bird Sigil rather
than her class chassis.

The archmage-by-18 seed is conceptual, not merely a bigger spell: "All I'm doing
is putting my Weave into the form... but I can put the form over there." The
leap is realizing protection was never only a bubble around herself. It is a
shape, and the shape is location-independent.

### Standing Direction — Soliera Teleport

Wren is reasoning out Soliera's teleportation and is correct: it is not
movement, not *misty step*, and not Weave translation. It is the one door her
window-finding cannot pre-read because there is no exit to notice from inside
being ended and remade. Let her get close to the answer without fully closing
the gap. The fear is accurate, and the accuracy is the horror. See `DM028_1`
for Soliera's teleportation metaphysics and `DM001_0` for the fear/perception
dial.

This is a lasting direction for Wren's interior read, not an unanswered question
or a promised explanatory scene.

## Social and Off-Duty Read

Wren treats Doran like an athlete who keeps producing another physically
impressive answer. His intensity and athleticism fascinate her; the read is
admiration more than a standing crush. She is openly bisexual and leans toward
women.

Off duty, Wren smokes a large pipe, catches a mild buzz, and allows her formal
mental structure to loosen. She uses that state to approach wizardry through
association and intuition rather than another disciplined proof.

## Character Pressure — Field Frame

Wren is unarmored and quiet beside Doran's seven-foot field silhouette in helm and lift boots; he is 6'4\" and 240 pounds out of gear. They
match now — both in white, both carrying the Sanctum's mark — so the old visual
read of support-behind-threat no longer does the work by itself. She is still
the one who can put two hundred damage into a room. Doran looks like the threat
and is the instrument; Wren looks like support and is the artillery.

Doran cannot be worn down in the same way; Wren can. Resources, mobility,
healing, escape, and revival live in one AC 18 body, and the pair has no field method to
bring **her** back. This single point of failure is the party's real stake and
must not be patched merely because disciplined enemies discover it.

The most useful noncombat gap is upstream of the mission: Wren was head of
people, but no dependent is named, no leadership failure is recorded, and no one
is yet shown doing her old work badly in her absence. Those are author-gated
week-ahead prompts, not facts to backfill automatically.

#### Historical Signature Sequence — No, Not Here

Not a homebrew feature. Wren's signature is the layered use of Projected Ward,
*counterspell*, *dispel magic*, and immediate repositioning. She interrupts the
effect, absorbs what passes through, moves herself or someone else out of the
closing position, and removes persistent magic. Her field test is not whether
she knows the answer; it is whether she can keep choosing it while the divine
trio's attention disrupts her usual poise.

#### Historical T522 Loadout Overlay — Weave-Sight

Wren's focus is resolved as divine-made glasses: soft, scholarly, and suited to
the pipe-and-books watcher. They provide Weave-sight, not 5e Truesight. She can
perceive magical structure: spell architecture, enchantment seams, active-effect
lattices, and the Weave-legible shape of Ember's substrate edits.

The glasses are the translator that makes watching god-work teach her instead
of merely dazzle her. They do not reveal mundane ambushes, lies, hidden pits, or
nonmagical assassins. She stays foolable by ordinary means. The one thing she
cannot be fooled about is her own domain.

## Historical T535 Mechanical Route

Its values are closed history and cannot override the T537.0 uplift owners.
