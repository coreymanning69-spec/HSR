# DM045_DS2 — The Writ Six and Hollowmark Reverse-Simulation Findings

#CHAR-SER-HALVARD-RUUN #CHAR-ILYTH-MARRAVEL #CHAR-AMBRIS-TEAL
#CHAR-HURN-BLACKMARROW #CHAR-SISTER-ONORA-KELT #CHAR-RELL-ASHGROVE

> **Hard boundary:** Hollowmark was a reverse-role sandbox. Doran and Wren held
> a Sanctum relay post as opposition against a six-person party of levels 16–18.
> Nothing about the location, writ, missing woman, combat, deaths,
> revivifications, or escapes is played canon.

## ROSTER IDENTITY

All six believed they were the good people in the scenario. None was wrong to
ask. That moral position remains part of the reusable roster even though the
Hollowmark premise does not. They are competent people who are correct to stop
you.

**Forward use:** elite hired palace guards: a retained house company rather
than a wandering party. The new frame makes them prepared and emplaced, which
is the only configuration in which they meaningfully threaten post-uplift
Stewards.

## T537 ELITE-GUARD REBUILD

The rebuild has two separable layers.

**Format gate.** These are compact encounter cards, not six full character
sheets. Their operative combat values are fixed below; prepared spell suites
are intentionally short and a full spellbook remains encounter-selected. That
is a load discipline, not a missing-build error.

**RAW scaling.** Every member advances exactly two levels. The band moves from
16–18 to 18–20. Gained features, slots, proficiency bonuses, and progressions
are rules-as-written for the class.

**Flat overlay.** Every member gains +100 HP and +1 AC on top of the RAW build.
This is homebrew, used because the roster was tuned against pre-uplift Doran.
Both figures remain visible in each line so the overlay can be removed without
rebuilding the character.

### Ser Halvard Ruun — human, Paladin 19, Oath of Vengeance

**HP 175 base, 275 with overlay.** d10, CON 16.  
**AC 20 base (plate and shield), 21 with overlay.**  
Prof +6. STR 20, CHA 20. Spell save DC 19. Attack +12 with a +1 longsword.

Gained at 18: **Aura of Protection extends to 30 feet.** Gained at 19: ASI.
Slots 4/3/3/3/2. Divine Smite through 5th level. Improved Divine Smite adds
1d8 radiant to every weapon hit. Vow of Enmity, Relentless Avenger, and Soul of
Vengeance remain live.

*Threat lane.* Radiant passes divine plate at full value. The real weapon is
the aura: every ally within 30 feet gets +5 on saves. Killing or separating the
aura is worth more than killing Halvard for damage.

### Ilyth Marravel — sun elf, Wizard 20, School of Conjuration

**HP 122 base, 222 with overlay.** d6, CON 14.  
**AC 15 base (*mage armor* and DEX 14), 16 with overlay; 21 while *shield* is up.**  
Prof +6. INT 20. Spell save DC 19. Spell attack +11.

Gained at 18: **Spell Mastery** with *shield* and *misty step*. Gained at 20:
**Signature Spells** with *counterspell* and *hypnotic pattern*. Benign
Transposition remains live for moving allies out of a Cleave arc.

*Threat lane.* *Forcecage* is not an answer to Wren: Portal 14 lets her leave
any eighth-level-or-lower teleport block, including the seventh-level cage.
It remains a no-save containment tool against Doran when Wren cannot
immediately extract him. *Maze* is the cleaner Doran lane because escape is an
INT ability check, not the +10 INT save that the uplift closed. At-will *shield*
raises Ilyth's effective dagger-facing AC to 21. Her free *counterspell* lane
contests Wren's opening without consuming ordinary slots.

### Ambris Teal — tiefling, Warlock 19, Fiend

**HP 155 base, 255 with overlay.** d8, CON 16.  
**AC 16 base (+1 studded leather and DEX 16), 17 with overlay.**  
Prof +6. CHA 20. Spell save DC 19. Spell attack +11.

Four *eldritch blast* beams. Agonizing Blast and Repelling Blast. Mystic
Arcanum through 9th. Gained at 18: invocation. Gained at 19: ASI. Dark One's
Own Luck and Fiendish Resilience.

*Threat lane.* Her darkness lane stays retired because Doran's helm sees
through it. **Repelling Blast is the separation engine.** Four beams can push
Doran out of the 30-foot radius where Wren grants him advantage on every save.
Ambris is setup for Onora and Ilyth, not the roster's primary damage dealer.

### Hurn Blackmarrow — half-orc, Barbarian 18, Path of the Zealot

**HP 221 base, 321 with overlay.** d12, CON 20. Effectively 642 against
bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing while raging.  
**AC 18 base (Unarmored Defense, DEX 16 and CON 20), 19 with overlay.**  
Prof +6. STR 20. Attack +12 with a +1 greataxe.

Rage damage +4. Brutal Critical 3 dice. Gained at 18: **Indomitable Might.**
Divine Fury adds 1d6+9 radiant on the first hit each turn. Relentless Rage.
Zealous Presence gives up to ten creatures advantage on attacks and saves for
one round.

*Threat lane.* Zealous Presence stacked on Halvard's aura is the roster's
strongest defensive tempo: advantage plus +5 against Wren's DC 22 for a round.
The radiant rider is live against divine plate. Hurn is difficult to remove,
but removing him first is usually the wrong priority.

### Sister Onora Kelt — human, Cleric 18, Twilight Domain

**HP 147 base, 247 with overlay.** d8, CON 16.  
**AC 20 base (plate and shield), 21 with overlay.**  
Prof +6. WIS 20. Spell save DC 19.

Gained at 17: **9th-level slot.** Gained at 18: **Channel Divinity 3/rest.**
Slots 4/3/3/3/3/1/1/1/1. Twilight Sanctuary, Eyes of Night, Vigilant Blessing,
and Steps of Night.

*Threat lane.* Twilight Sanctuary is a 30-foot sphere granting roughly 21
temporary HP to every ally inside it every round for a minute, and it ends
charm and fright. Three uses. *Banishment* attacks Doran's shallow CHA save only
after separation removes Wren's advantage aura. Vigilant Blessing grants
initiative advantage, which matters against Doran's +21.

### Rell Ashgrove — halfling, Rogue 19, Assassin

**HP 155 base, 255 with overlay.** d8, CON 16.  
**AC 18 base (+1 studded leather and DEX 20), 19 with overlay.**  
Prof +6. DEX 20. Death Strike DC 19. Sneak Attack 10d6.

Gained at 18: **Elusive.** Gained at 19: ASI. Evasion, Blindsense, Slippery
Mind, and Death Strike.

*Threat lane.* The overlay does not make Rell a direct answer to Wren. Her ward
nullifies a physical damage instance regardless of magnitude while points
remain, so a Death Strike can still become one ward strip. His real value is
reconnaissance: backtracking spell-effect origins, mapping exits and cover,
and handing Ilyth the prepared containment geometry before initiative.

## HOW THE REBUILT ROSTER THREATENS THE STEWARDS

The lane that scales with preparation time is **Rell plus Ilyth**, not raw
numbers. Rell walks the palace, maps Wren's likely positions and exits, and
identifies where the invisible ward can be stripped. Ilyth turns that map into
containment geometry, counterspell positions, and a *maze* lane for Doran.

The secondary lane is **separation**. Within 30 feet of Wren, Doran has
permanent *freedom of movement* and advantage on every save. Outside it he does
not. Ambris pushes him out, Ilyth contains or mazes him, and Onora uses the
resulting window. Halvard and Hurn keep the company functional against Wren's
DC 22 while Twilight Sanctuary extends the setup clock.

The deployable kite changes the encounter outright. If Doran drops it for
Wren, she gains half or three-quarters cover while his AC falls from 25 to 21.
The six are no longer attacking one stable defensive line; they choose between
the exposed protector and the covered exit-engine. Any new simulation must
track the kite as a placed object, not a carried AC item.

### Reusable scenario hooks

These are hooks, not established history:

- Ambris and Rell can escape before seeing the resolution and believe the
  Sanctum killed everyone.
- Halvard can learn that the Sanctum raises the dead and find that more
  disturbing than the killing.
- Myrkul's future champion pressure routes through this resurrection lane around
  Halvard. The connection is a design direction, not a played event or assigned
  patronage fact.
- Hurn can owe Doran something he cannot name after a nonlethal finish.
- Onora is the one who files the proper paperwork.
- As retained guards, the company can be correct, professional, completely
  outmatched, and know it by round two.

## DURABLE DESIGN FINDINGS — EVIDENCE, NOT CHRONOLOGY

### Doran

- The former conclusion that daggers are the automatic boss lane and the
  Cleaver is visual bait is **retired pending re-simulation**. The Cleaver now
  deals its 2d8 mastery rider on every hit, for roughly 44 average damage, not
  only against giants. Its thirteen-attack burst changes the comparison.
- The darkness-blinds-Doran answer remains retired; his helm sees normally
  through magical and nonmagical darkness to 120 feet.
- Bludgeoning and radiant remain the plate's honest damage lanes.
- The 30-foot radius around Wren and the placed kite are mechanical objects on
  the battlefield. Prepared opposition attacks the geometry.

### Wren

- *Reverse gravity* is a lever rather than a damage spell. Holding targets away
  and ending the field on command won the old simulation.
- Her position can be inferred from visible spell-effect origins.
- The Wand of Fireball finding is retired. Her current Staff of the Magi,
  personal spell list, Portal 14, and reaction bottleneck route through DM041_B
  and DM044_1.
- A *forcecage* plan directed at Wren is inverted post-uplift. She leaves it.

### Runtime errors caught

- *Greater invisibility* is concentration and cannot coexist with Wren's
  personal *reverse gravity* concentration lane.
- The Ring of Concentration holds one spell; ring-held *haste* means no second
  ring-lane spell.
- Twin and Maximize do not apply to *reverse gravity* under the current spell
  ruling; it is an area spell with no damage dice.
- Quicken is once per round and exclusive of another Wren-performed metamagic.
  Two leveled spells in a turn is the ceiling under the house Quicken rule.

## SANDBOX CHARACTERIZATION TEST

In the old simulation, Doran refused entry on sanctuary doctrine, offered
nonlethal resolution before weapons were drawn, offered surrender during the
fight, grounded the Cleaver for an agreed hit-for-hit exchange, and finished
Hurn nonlethally. Wren restored the dead and healed the survivors.

The closing institutional line was: **"Just have them send some proper
paperwork."**

This is reusable evidence for the Sanctum's character — the door does not open,
people inside are not surrendered, and challengers are allowed to go home
alive — but it is not a canonical event or quote until played.

## STANDARDIZED DEPLOYMENT SHEET — T537

**Role.** Prepared palace or protected-site company. They seek containment and
capture, not a road-fight kill. Use a fireteam by default; use all six only for
a deliberately prepared set-piece.

| Fireteam | Members | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Forward | Rell + Ilyth | Scout, map exits, establish containment geometry, remove one target |
| Anvil | Halvard + Hurn | Hold the objective, survive the first contact, punish a separated intruder |
| Support / nuke | Onora + Ambris | Preserve the company, strip positioning, and create the separation window |

**Overlay.** Against the post-uplift Stewards, apply the visible +100 HP / +1
AC overlay. Against everyone else, begin RAW and turn it on only if the
encounter needs the extra setup clock. The overlay does not alter action
economy, saves, spell DCs, or signature actions.
