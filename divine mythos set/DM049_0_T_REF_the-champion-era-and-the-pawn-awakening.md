# DM049_0 — THE CHAMPION ERA AND THE PAWN AWAKENING

**Thesis.** The giants were used by a power working through proxies because
direct action was closed to it. That is the champion pattern one tier down. A
giant who works out what was done to him has derived the shape of the era
without being told it.

**Use.** Take one row. Do not deliver a section. Nothing here resolves.

## 1 · THE ERA, AS THE WORLD FEELS IT

| Venue | Drop-in |
|---|---|
| Road | Two shrines inside a day's walk where last year there was one. Both new, both well-kept, neither with a priest in sight. |
| Tavern | The mercenary board has three postings and all three are re-posts. The names on them are companies, not jobs. |
| Temple | The collection plate is embarrassing. Clergy have stopped apologising for the building and started apologising for the queue. |
| Temple | A junior priest speaks with an authority nobody granted him, and does not notice he is doing it. |
| Market | Holy symbols in metals the faith could not afford two years ago. The smith will not say who commissioned the first one. |
| Noble house | A guest wears regalia worth more than the estate, and wears it badly — like borrowed clothes, not property. |
| Letter | A factor apologises for a delay: the escort company “is not answering, and their charter house is also not answering.” |
| Letter | Contract language has grown a clause about *sole survivors* that nobody remembers negotiating. |
| Harper channel | Four companies gone in a season. No bodies, no wreckage, no ransom. Four regions. |
| Anywhere | Someone nothing in the room can account for — too well equipped, too calm, too alone — is pleasant, buys nothing, leaves early. |

**Borrowed-regalia tell.** A champion wears his god's things, not his own.
Artifact kit on someone who did not earn it reads wrong to anyone who has watched
a veteran handle gear. Wren notices before Doran does.

**Not all hostile.** Mystra's Chosen is warm to the Sanctum. A sighting is not a
threat by default; treating each as a villain flattens the era into a roster.

## 3 · THE PAWN AWAKENING — FOUR RUNGS

| Rung | Trigger | Tell | Looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 — the counting is wrong** | Tribute north, always north, more each season; a short count means somebody gets eaten | Resentment aimed *downward*, at the counter, never the cause | Beatings over arithmetic. A camp hiding food from its own boss. |
| **R2 — the beneficiary never appears** | The count is carried off by someone who never eats with them | Asked once, badly, and not repeated | “Who is it *for*.” Unanswered. Remembered. |
| **R3 — the timing** | Everything broke at once, and the breaking suited one party who was not there | Suspicion with no target; the ordning has no word for this shape | A giant stops raiding to think, which his own people read as cowardice. |
| **R4 — the missing word** | Someone outside supplies the idea, or he invents it in pain | Rage that does not know where to land | He understands *used*. |

**R4 cannot be reasoned into from inside.** The ordning frames every rank as
earned and every superior as legitimate, so there is no native concept for *used
by an outsider*. It must be given to him, or torn out of him — a gift or a wound,
never a deduction. This remains design material.

## 5 · WHAT AN AWAKENED GIANT IS

Not an ally. Vengeful and unaimed — he has learned he was used and has no target,
no standing, and no vocabulary for the grievance, so it comes out sideways on
whoever is nearest. That is a proposed stakes-carrier, not a canon outcome.

**Deception fact.** “Conscripted by a lie” describes someone who chose on planted,
true-seeming information. It is relevant to Sera's judgment, not a third sort,
mandatory mercy rule, or automatic death rule.

## 7 · BRAID POINTS

1. **Guh's Sending token.** Twenty-five words each way was established and the message was delivered. It is a historical fact only; no reply is owed and no awakening route fires from it.
2. **Told versus arrived.** Converted lords can be *informed*. Unconverted ones must climb, and the climb is the story. Do not level them.
3. **Tribute mirrors faith.** Food carried north to an unseen beneficiary is the shape of devotion drained by an unseen god. Giants can be shown their diagram.
