### Convoy Ambush to Skyreach Castle: Notes, Part 2

T151: Hunt for the Black Mask Trail #LOC-SEWERS #CONCEPT-FACTION-CULT-OF-THE-DRAGON #CONCEPT-INVESTIGATION #CHAR-DRIZZT
— The first move is Waterdeep, because the black mask being routed through the city is an insult sitting close enough to smell. Xanathar's people do not "cooperate" so much as rapidly discover an enthusiasm for living through the afternoon. Their maps come out. Their hidden doors open. Their informants become very helpful once Sera starts asking questions with that bright, delighted patience that makes every liar understand the floor has teeth. Drizzt watches the underworld fold itself into service and says nothing, because there are moments when wisdom is just letting the porcelain girl enjoy her scavenger hunt.

T152: Sewer Descent — Xanathar's Network #LOC-UNDERDEAL #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-FACTION-XANATHAR #CONCEPT-COMBAT-SWEEP
— Sera leads the descent like it is a field trip somebody scheduled in a toilet. Constructs move ahead of her in layered silence, sweeping side passages, sealing escape cuts, and lifting iron grates without a scrape. Cultists who run find construct hands waiting around corners they have not reached yet. Cultists who kneel are zip-tied, stripped of knives, and stacked in a dry room for later sorting. Sera looks at one slime-slick wall, wrinkles her nose, and says, "Waterdeep really looked at a hole full of murder-water and said, 'Let's add politics.' Bold."

T153: Black Mask Route Secured #CONCEPT-OBJECT-BLACK-MASK #CONCEPT-PLOT-HOOK #CONCEPT-INTELLIGENCE-SECURED #CONCEPT-COMBAT-SWEEP #THEME-OVERWHELMING-FORCE
— The black mask is not found behind three false walls, a drowned shrine, and a cult courier who has convinced himself he is important because no one has killed him yet. What waits there instead is the route to it: ledgers, ciphered orders, payment marks, Skyreach references, Rezmir's movement pattern, and enough courier traffic to prove the artifact is still ahead of them rather than beneath Waterdeep. The courier's belief in his own importance lasts until Soliera steps into the chamber. The papers do not flare. They do not whisper. They become objects in a room where the real power has already arrived. Sera picks up the packet with two fingers, studies it, then looks at the courier and says, "You hid the evil dragon hat directions in the poop maze. I need you to understand that this reflects poorly on everybody." No true resistance remains in that sewer operation after that; only inventory, prisoners, and the quiet administrative horror of Waterdeep realizing the gods can clear a cult route faster than the Watch can file a patrol route.

T154: The White Mask and Talis #CHAR-TALIS #CONCEPT-OBJECT-WHITE-MASK #LOC-HUNTING-LODGE #THEME-AMBITION-MEETS-DIVINITY
— The white mask trail is cleaner, colder, and more personal. Talis the White is not a sewer rat or a doomed courier; she is ambitious, charismatic, resentful, and smart enough to understand betrayal as a tool rather than a moral event. Her lodge is not just a hideout. It is a court-in-miniature, stocked with servants, guards, cult protocol, and the kind of polished hospitality villains use when they still believe presentation matters. Word reaches her before the party does, but the reports are useless in the way all reports about Soliera and Sera become useless: too impossible to plan against, too consistent to dismiss. Sera pauses on the approach and says, "Wait. Talis. Not Tali. Different Tali. Somebody should put little name tags on cultists before I get emotionally efficient."

T154.5: Talis Is Not Tali #CONCEPT-DICTATION-GUARD #CHAR-TALIS #CHAR-TALIANDRA #CONCEPT-CANON-DISTINCTION
— This beat stays exactly where it belongs: a spike strip across the road. Talis the White is the Cult figure at the hunting lodge. Tali/Taliandra is the drow arc anchor from the earlier Sanctum/Drow sequence. The names are similar enough for dictation to do crimes, but canonically they are not adjacent characters, not variants, not echoes, and not secretly the same woman. Sera would absolutely find this funny exactly once, then become irritated that mortals keep reusing syllables like there are only twelve available.

T155: Talis's Negotiation — Surrender #CHAR-TALIS #CONCEPT-DIPLOMACY-INTIMIDATION #THEME-SURRENDER #RULE-CONSENT-AND-COMPREHENSION
— Talis receives them in the lodge's best room because she is not stupid, and because a throne-like chair still feels better than being found hiding behind a pantry door. She begins with leverage: names, routes, Rezmir's movement, Skyreach's status, the white mask, Severin's mistakes, Varram's insult. It is a good speech. In another campaign, it might even work. Then Soliera enters fully, not dramatically, not with thunder, just present enough that every candle in the lodge seems to remember fire was invented for warmth before it was invented for warning. Talis stops midway through a sentence and understands that she has not been negotiating with adventurers; she has been arranging the terms under which she remains a person.

Sera wanders the room while Talis recalibrates, touching the back of a chair, peeking behind a curtain, inspecting a decorative dragon skull with open suspicion. "Do y'all practice the villain furniture thing," she asks, "or does the chair come with the job?" Nobody laughs except one servant, who makes the smallest sound in the world and immediately looks like she expects to die for it. Sera beams at her. "See? She gets it."

That is what breaks the room. Not the threat. The permission to breathe.

Talis removes the white mask herself. She does not throw it down and she does not clutch it until Sera has to take her hands off. She sets it on the table between herself and Soliera with the precision of a woman who knows rituals matter even after the god in the room has made them obsolete. "I can give you Skyreach," she says, and this time the ambition has been stripped down to its cleanest bone. "I can give you Rezmir's route. I can give you everyone who chose Severin over sense."

Sera looks at her for one long, cheerful, terrible second. "That's better," she says. "See, now you're helping."

The lodge does not survive as a cult node. Its ledgers are taken, prisoners sorted, servants released, symbols burned, stores redistributed, and every hidden compartment opened by constructs that do not care about clever carpentry. Talis survives because she chooses surrender with comprehension, not because she is innocent. She is not folded into the family, not trusted, not forgiven in some sugary nonsense way. She is useful, frightened, ambitious, and now correctly oriented toward the largest fact in the room. The white mask leaves with Soliera. The black mask remains ahead, with Rezmir. Skyreach becomes next.

T156: Skyreach Castle Looms #LOC-SKYREACH #CONCEPT-FACTION-CULT-OF-THE-DRAGON #CHAR-REZMIR #CHAR-DRIZZT #CHAR-SERA #THEME-WONDER
    – Rumors confirmed: the cult's command structure has pulled back to Skyreach Castle, a flying fortress currently rotating somewhere above the northern tundra. The party closes to visual range. Sera's first proper look at the thing — a castle that is simply floating, for no reason that respects gravity — lands exactly as you'd expect. She finds it correct. Aesthetically correct. Not impressive the way mortals mean impressive, but the right idea taken too far, which is the only direction she understands. Drizzt, who has not seen this particular fortress before, provides actual tactical context: the rotation pattern, the probable giant crew responsible for its heading, the guard positions legible even from this angle. The party takes a moment to simply look. It is the only time in this sequence that looking functions as preparation.

T157: The Castle Moves #LOC-SKYREACH #CHAR-GIANTS #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #THEME-CURIOSITY
    – Skyreach shifts its heading. Not drifting — correcting, responsive, deliberate. Something on board has noticed them or has been warned. Giants are visible on the upper battlements adjusting the castle's course; dragon shapes circle the high parapets in patterns that read as directed rather than patrol. The girls don't know yet whether the giants are steering or the dragons are guiding them — the command structure isn't clear at this distance. Sera and Soliera are not alarmed. Sera is specifically interested. The most entertaining development that could happen right now is a castle attempting to move away from her, and that is exactly what is happening.

T158: Invisible Bridge, Visible Roads #LOC-SKYREACH-APPROACH #CHAR-SOLIERA #CONCEPT-FEAT-DIVINE-GEOMETRY #CONCEPT-CULT-LOGISTICS
    – A stretch of broken, cavernous terrain opens between the party and the castle's current position. Soliera resolves it. An invisible bridge materializes — not conjured with ceremony, just geometry that wasn't there before and now is — and the party crosses without breaking stride. Below them, the road network around the castle's shadow is active: loaded wagons, hooded figures in organized clusters, pack animals moving toward the fortress from multiple directions. The cult's logistics operation is still running. Soliera reads the movement the way she reads any resource: as something that is about to change ownership.

T159: The Courier #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-FACTION-CULT-OF-THE-DRAGON #CONCEPT-INTERROGATION #LOC-GREENEST-OUTSKIRTS #CHAR-DRIZZT
    – A man moving fast in the wrong direction — away from the castle, toward the Greenest outskirts — catches Sera's attention. She asks where he's going. He panics. Attempts to run. She flicks his knee off. Not lethally — the joint simply ceases to function in the way it used to, stopped by a gesture that required less effort than the question. She asks again, same tone. He answers: he's a field courier carrying observation reports from Greenest's outlying settlements — troop positions, civilian counts, resource assessments for the cult's operational picture. Not strategic intelligence. Local bookkeeping. Sera hands the reports to Soliera without comment; Soliera reads them once and passes them to Drizzt. The courier is left on the road. He'll survive.

T160: The Ridge #LOC-SKYREACH-RIDGE #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-STRATEGY-PLANNING
    – The party gains a high ridge with unobstructed sightlines to Skyreach's current position. From here the full shape of the castle is legible against the cloud cover — stone and ice and cult engineering turned into something that has no business being airborne. Drizzt begins marking probable entry points based on the guard patterns visible from this angle. Sera watches the dragon patrols with the particular attention she gives things she is about to deal with. Soliera looks at the castle the way she looks at most problems: like the resolution already exists and the present moment is just the part where everyone catches up to it. The plan forms in the quiet. Nobody has to say it out loud.
