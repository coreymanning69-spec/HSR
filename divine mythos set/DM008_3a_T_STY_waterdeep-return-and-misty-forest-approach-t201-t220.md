### SECTION II — WATERDEEP RETURN: THE ARK ARRIVES, KESSLA IS CLAIMED (T201-T209)

T201: Arrival at Waterdeep Docks (Return) #LOC-WATERDEEP #VEHICLE-THE-ARK #CHAR-KESSLA
— The Ark, Soliera's divine water-vessel, glides into Waterdeep's working docks with the Frostskimmr leading ahead as herald. The harbor does not know how to react — she left, and she is back, and the interval between those two facts is too short and too large simultaneously. Rumor moves faster than the ship. Korin, Letha, and Pell are already in the city, having preceded them overland. Sera and Soliera stand at the bow in matching outfits — Sera's choice, naturally: silk, white-gold trim, both of them barefoot as is Sera's ritual approach to any homecoming. Kessla waits at the dock with a formation of Hush Agents, Neverember, and Vajra. Maccath is quietly escorted away to the Arcane Brotherhood's representatives. When the city's silhouette opens beneath her, Sera's greeting is exactly what everyone expected and somehow still lands: "We're baaaaaack\~."

T202: Procession through the City #SOCIAL-REACTION #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-KESSLA
— A hush precedes them through the streets — not silence exactly, more the sound of several thousand people deciding what they are witnessing before they decide how to feel about it. Kessla falls into step with practiced professionalism and immediately begins losing the battle against her own nerves. Sera reads this the way she reads everything — instantly and with full intention. She places a hand on Kessla's back, unhurried: "Don't be shy now. I saw you watching us leave." Kessla, to her credit, does not flinch. "I just wanted to come with. To help." Sera smiles. That is the whole of the negotiation.

T203: Council/Meeting Hall Entrance #LOC-COUNCIL-HALL #CHAR-NEVEREMBER #CHAR-VAJRA
— The entrance to the meeting hall is grand in the way Waterdeep always manages — carved stone, banners, the weight of institutional self-importance. Sera bounces ahead and draws Kessla in by the wrist. Soliera's chair is already in place: white, red cushion, positioned as though it has always been there and the hall was built around it. Vajra and Neverember are present with the full complement of Waterdeep's relevant lords; the room's tension is the particular kind that comes from powerful people who are deeply uncertain whether they are the most powerful people in it. They are not. Sera controls the atmosphere with the effortless dominance of someone who finds the room genuinely entertaining. She gestures to Kessla: "This is my favorite — Kessla." Then, to the assembled lords, she delivers her field report on recent events: "Blew up the ice, moved the sea, saved a bunch of cold people." The lords react with the layered expression of awe, confusion, and political recalibration that has become their default around her.

T204: Elia Revealed / Metallic Dragon Hook #CHAR-ELIA #DIPLOMACY-METALLIC #THEME-AWAKENING
— The lords introduce Lady Elia — silver-haired, composed, the kind of beautiful that feels like a held breath. Sera looks at her with honest assessment for exactly two seconds. "That's just a pretty woman." Then something shifts. "Oh!! You're like Niv! Hellooo\~" The delight is immediate and total. Elia, Otaaryliakkarnos in her mortal skin, is amused in the slow way that ancient things are amused — patient, warm, slightly watchful. She and Sera already know each other from the Chuth encounters; this is a reunion with better furniture. The assembled party and lords proceed into the substance of the session: the green dragon threat, Neronvain's identity (son of King Melandrach of the Misty Forest elves, turned Wyrmspeaker — the betrayal is both political and generational), the Council of Metallic Dragons' conditions, and the shape of what comes next. Sera's unpredictable energy keeps everyone slightly off balance, which is where she finds them most honest.

T204.5: Embassy Arrival & Casual Intimacy #LOC-SANCTUM-EMBASSY #CHAR-KESSLA #VELVET-SPIRAL
— The party retreats to the Sanctum Embassy — the city's version of home, which means high ceilings, cool air, a divine lounge built for bodies that don't get tired, and a bed large enough to serve as a room. Hush Agents open doors in reverence, not fear; there is a distinction and they know it. Sera pounces on Kessla before the door is fully closed — stripping and pulling her in with the easy certainty of someone who has decided and sees no reason to circle the decision. "Man, it feels nice to be back home!" Kessla is overwhelmed in the specific way of someone who has imagined this and discovers that imagination was insufficient. Elia and Soliera take chairs by the fire and begin talking about things that matter. It is a happy evening. Everyone is exactly where they chose to be.

T205: God and Dragon (Fireside) #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-ELIA #DIALOGUE-DIVINE
— Soliera and Elia, both composed in the way that old things are composed, speak plainly by the fire. The questions Soliera asks are not rhetorical: why is Tiamat so feared when fear has a structural source? Why does Bahamut not intervene when intervention has a cost he refuses to pay? Elia answers with the measured honesty of someone who has outlived most of the people she has told the truth to. Her analysis of the green dragon threat and Neronvain's psychology is clinical and a little sad — she knew the elven court before the corruption. Across the room, Sera is demonstrably not listening to any of this, occupied entirely with Kessla. The Velvet Spiral is in full effect — it does not coerce, it simply clarifies desire that was already present. Elia glances over once with an expression that takes several centuries of context to fully read.

T206: The Night Passes (New Bonds) #CHAR-KESSLA #CHAR-SERA #VELVET-SPIRAL
— By the time the candles are low, Kessla has lost the last of her professional composure and found something that suits her better. She is marked now — not by violence or demand, but by proximity to something that operates at a frequency mortals were not built to sustain indefinitely. She is not broken. She is permanently changed, which is different. Sera, playful and warm and completely unsentimental about it: "Guess what we did, Solii? Did you get jealous?" Soliera crosses to Kessla and rests a hand on her thigh — unhurried, present, grounding. The gesture says: you are seen, and that is not going to be taken from you. For Kessla, it lands harder than anything else that happened tonight.

T207: The Next Morning (Gathering) #CHAR-KORIN #CHAR-LETHA #PARTY-GATHERING
— Korin and Letha knock at a reasonable hour and enter after giving the gods and their company sufficient time — they have both learned the shape of the embassy's mornings. The gathering is easy, practical, joking. Pell checks in; the others account for themselves. Sera's first order of business is logistics: "Kessla, you're not really high enough to fight a dragon, are you?" Kessla, mortified and determined, says she will keep up. No one disagrees with her. No one agrees either. She is coming — this is already decided — and she will have to locate her usefulness on the road.

T208: Departure from Waterdeep #VEHICLE-DIVINE-SLEIGH #CHAR-ELIA #EXIT-SCENE
— Farewells with Drizzt and Catti-brie, who are in the city proper rather than the embassy — warm, brief, the kind of goodbye between people who are not worried about each other's survival but know the road changes everyone anyway. The party assembles in Soliera's divine sleigh: comfortable, shaded, built for a retinue. Elia travels with them as metallic dragon liaison — her presence both political credential and quiet counsel. The sleigh rolls out with the full weight of their accumulated status visible in every detail of it. Waterdeep watches them go the same way it watched them arrive: with the unsettled feeling of a city that has just been visited by something it will be explaining for a generation.

T209: En Route (Discussion) #TRAVEL-DIALOGUE #CHAR-SERA-GROWTH #THEME-MERCY
— The road south and west toward the Misty Forest is long enough for real conversation. Sera reflects aloud on something she has noticed in herself: the pause before the act. Not hesitation — she does not hesitate — but a beat of consideration that was not there before. She has apologized, recently, more than once. She has chosen mercy in situations where her earlier self would have found it redundant. The party notices without naming it. They discuss Neronvain — his use of Chuth's poison as a tool against his own people's forests, his identity as Melandrach's hidden shame, the particular cruelty of a Wyrmspeaker who could have been a king. Elia provides context from the dragon's side of the relationship. Kessla listens. The tone in the sleigh is mythic, intimate, and charged — Sera is, as she has always been, the reason you believe in the possibility of a good place to arrive.

### SECTION III — THE MISTY FOREST: APPROACH TO CHUTH (T210-T220)

(NOTE: These beats are the compressed approach sequence. Full scene expansion
continues in DM009_0, T221 onward. Both documents are valid; read DM008_3 as
the interior experience, DM009_0 as the world-facing account.)

T210: Miracle in Altand (Poison Rewritten) #LOC-ALTAND #CHAR-SOLIERA #DIVINE-INTERVENTION
— The village of Altand has been living under Chuth's lingering poison for long enough that the elves have stopped imagining its absence. Soliera rewrites it — not heals, not treats, not mitigates: rewrites. The poison stops being poison. The damage done is reversed in the bodies of everyone it touched. It is an act of pure will applied to the molecular level of the world, and it takes her approximately as long as deciding to. Sera spins and grabs her and calls her her hero in front of everyone. The elves stare. Galin's faith — the kind of faith that lives below doctrine, in the part of a person that knows without being told — is restored and will not leave him. The shadow of the green dragon is visibly less, even in the air. The story that will follow these people is not "the gods saved us" but "the gods came, fixed it, and left" — and now the elves have to decide what to do with a world that is better than they knew how to hope for.

T211: Payment, Shrine, and Plans #CHAR-GALIN #CHAR-SERA #THEME-WORSHIP
— Galin tries to offer something — timber, herbs, labor, any form of accounting that restores the balance a mortal mind insists on after receiving something enormous for free. Sera deflects with the particular warmth of someone who finds the concept of payment in this context genuinely funny: "Build Soli a nice shrine and send something to the Sanctum when you have extra. We'll call it even." Letha is practical about what the village can actually sustain, running down what they need and what they can give in the calm voice of someone who has done this logistics work a hundred times. Elia stays back, near the tree line — she has no interest in being between a village of traumatized elves and the dragon she knows is ahead of them, and she is honest about this.

T212: The Hunt Begins (Into the Woods) #LOC-MISTY-FOREST #CHAR-KORIN #ATMOSPHERE-TENSION
— Korin moves to point as they enter the deeper forest. The trees here are old and wrong in the specific way of places that have had something large and poisonous living in them for too long — the light comes down wrong, the undergrowth smells faintly of bile, and the birds are quiet in the way that means the birds left. Letha and Kessla maintain the group's center. Sera moves ahead of everyone without quite seeming to hurry, because for her there is no such thing as hurrying — there is only where she is and where she has decided to be next. Soliera walks at the rear, serene in the way of someone who has already seen how this ends and found it acceptable. Kessla's tension is specific and growing: every role she reaches for is already handled. The forest is beginning.

T213: First Encounter (Ritual Remnants) #INVESTIGATION #CHAR-LETHA #CHAR-SERA
— A blackened patch of earth — recent, circular, centered on what used to be a natural spring. The water has turned and is ringed with corrupted druidic fetishes, the kind of rune-work that started as something to protect and was inverted into something to bind. Each party member reads the scene through their own lens:
• Letha examines the runes — druidic in origin, now twisted into a containment pattern for psychic bleed. The corruption is Neronvain's specific handwriting; she has seen his work before.
• Korin finds tracks at the perimeter — cultist boots and something bare-footed that moved with unusual weight, suggesting captives forced to participate.
• Kessla crouches near the spring and feels the pool's residual psychic signal: a plea rather than a threat, something that was aware enough to want help.
• Sera wades in directly, immune to every property of the water, and disperses the illusion layered over the surface with a casual sweep of will. "Nice try! It's just water. Maybe a little spooky, but not to me." The pool's whispers clarify for a moment before they dissolve: Chuth. River. Shadows. Help us.

T214: Moving Forward (Trail Clears) #CHAR-SERA #ENVIRONMENT-CLEARING
— Sera's presence in the pool has the structural side effect of clearing the psychic and physical contamination from the immediate area — not intentional, simply the consequence of something divine standing somewhere corrupt. The forest lightens perceptibly. The party is aware, without needing to discuss it, that this is a contest of will between Chuth's accumulated influence over this territory and whatever Sera brings to it. The contest is not close. They press on.

T215: Second Encounter (The Survivor) #CHAR-KORIN #NPC-ELF #PLOT-HOOK
— Korin finds him first: a young elf, barely adult by elven reckoning, wedged into a hollow between two root systems with the particular stillness of someone who has learned that movement means being found. He is battered and carrying the specific vacancy in his eyes that comes from extended exposure to psychic compulsion — Neronvain's witch-work, which made his captives forget who they were in layers, like removing pages from a book. He is not entirely there yet. But he knows enough to speak: "Please — help. Chuth, the green dragon, and his witch — they took the others deeper, through the old river path. The witch made us forget who we were." Kessla and Letha tend to him. The party learns what the deeper chambers of the lair likely contain.

T216: Choices at the River Path #STRATEGY-DECISION #CHAR-SERA
— The river path forks. The debate is brief because Sera ends it: "Let's just go get his wife back." The tactical question of whether to circle for survivors first is valid and she is aware of this, which is why she defers it to Soliera with a grin rather than simply moving. "Or maybe you can make him a new one, Soli?" Soliera's expression in response to this is the particular look of someone who has decided that the joke is too expensive to unpack right now. They move.

T217: Velvet Spiral in Play #CHAR-SERA #SOCIAL-AURA #CHAR-KESSLA
— As the party moves through the outer villages and clearings that Chuth's reach extends to, Sera's passive aura — the Velvet Spiral — interacts with the population. Children approach before adults do, which is always true of things that are genuinely not dangerous. Adults bow, or weep quietly, or simply stand and stare with the look of people witnessing something that resolves a question they have carried for years. Kessla watches this happen around her and feels two things at once: pride, because she is walking in that wake, and a specific loneliness, because she is the only mortal in it and she knows it. She is chosen. That is not the same as not being alone.

T218: The Shadow of Chuth #CHAR-CHUTH #PSYCHIC-THREAT #CHAR-LETHA
— The dragon's psychic presence reaches the party before the dragon does — a low-frequency pressure that reads as existential dread in anyone without a divine anchor. It is the kind of fear that makes the body want to become smaller than it is. Letha is the first to name it and the first to start working against it, moving through the group with quiet practical reassurance. Sera shrugs it off with genuine indifference. "What would poison do? I don't even need to breathe." She is not performing this for anyone's comfort; it is simply true, and the truth of it is useful. Korin confirms what the tracks and the green glow ahead both suggest: the lair is close.

T219: Approach to the Lair #PRE-BATTLE-PREP #PARTY-BONDING
— The final check before the final approach. Equipment confirmed. Bonds — the informal, wordless kind that grow between people who have survived things together — quietly present. Sera makes one more round of the group: "Are you SURE you want to come?" This is directed at each person individually and means something slightly different to each of them. Pell is left behind at the last staging point; this is agreed on without drama. The atmosphere carries the specific mythic weight of a moment that everyone present knows they will remember — not because it is glorious but because it is real, and real things have mass.

T220: Lair in Sight (Endgame Begins) #LOC-CHUTH-LAIR #CHAR-ELIA #ARC-CLIMAX-PREP
— The cave entrance is ahead: broad, low, breathing out the particular cold-warm mix of a space that has been lived in by something large for a very long time. Illusion layers the entrance — Neronvain's work again, designed to turn visitors around or fill their heads with images of the forest they just came from. It does not work on anyone in the current party, which is itself a kind of message. Elia stops at the tree line. Her final word is measured and genuine: she will not enter. Her reasons are her own and she does not elaborate, because ancient things do not owe explanations for their limits. Soliera leads. Sera moves to her side. Kessla, Letha, and Korin settle into position behind them. The lair waits.
