### Prior Giant History — The G1 Reputation

What giant-kind actually knows: Sera walked into the Steading of the Hill Giant Chief during the G-series. Most of the war-band survived. The chief did not resist after the third minute. The steading still functions — under different management. Ember ran moral sorts on the chieftain and his war-band; Sera provided enforcement where the sort confirmed willing harm; the steading ended as a going concern rather than a site of mass death. That is the factual record.

What giant-kind has made of it: she spoke once and they sat down. Not quite accurate — the sort takes time, enforcement was specific rather than categorical, and nothing resolved in a single moment. But the myth captures the direction correctly: something that looked like a conquest did not end in rubble, and a war-band that had been fully capable of fighting walked out functional and changed. Giant-kind does not have a clean word for what happened because it does not fit ordning logic. You beat your enemies or your enemies beat you; the third option is not in the architecture. They call it the thing that happened at the steading and leave the naming problem alone.

Differentiated response by giant type: Hill giants are most directly affected. Some have developed a preemptive display of submission — laying down weapons before engagement, positioning as supplicants rather than combatants. This is not cowardice; it is a rational read on the steading's outcome. Chief Guh is the inverse case. She is eating herself to a size she believes cannot be domesticated. She does not have a theory about what "too large to sort" looks like. She is building toward a number and hoping the number is right. The steading is the specific event she is responding to when she adds another hundred pounds to her tribute-consumption apparatus.

Stone giants mostly classify the steading as a surface-world event, consistent with their cosmology that the surface is a dream register — interesting but not load-bearing on the deep world's permanent record. This makes them underestimate. What happened at the steading is not dreamed; it is already a fact in the permanent record, and stone giants, who take permanence seriously, have not accounted for this yet.

Frost giants have heard and are calibrating. Storvald's war-band is more disciplined than a grief-driven project has any right to be — controlled behavior, fewer raids than the resources suggest, specific violence rather than broad raiding. They know that loose behavior gets sorted. The calibration is not fear exactly; it is the recognition that the arithmetic of their situation has a different variable in it now.

Fire giants have responded pragmatically: they are running drills. Not honor-drills — tactical adjustment. They have been practicing against scenarios that include a rapid-response moral sort and targeted enforcement. Whether the drills model the correct parameters is a different question. The response is pure craft culture: if the old parameters are wrong, find the new ones.

Cloud giants are collectors, and Sansuri's initial response to the G1 event was curiosity — what happened there specifically, and what does it tell us about the taxonomy of whatever these things are? The curiosity has sharpened. She has had fourteen months to think about Ostoria and about what walks around in the post-Ostoria world with that kind of structural authority. Her current interest in the nightstone as a potential Ostorian relic, and a separate and newer interest in studying one of them up close, are both downstream of that original question.

The dissenting-giant effect: every site the girls will visit has a giant who was already hesitating before they arrived — not an ally, not a defector, just a creature in whom doubt had already begun. The G1 reputation gives that hesitation a specific shape. "I know what happened at the steading" is a thought that exists in the war-band before the girls are in the room. It does not produce loyalty or surrender. It produces a particular quality of attention. That giant is easier to reach than the others. Not easy — easier.

Combat note: giants who know about the girls do not open with their best strike. They open with a probe — a standard attack at controlled force, reading for response speed, for damage profile, for whether the small ones move like things that know they're small. They are trying to find the ceiling before they commit. This makes the first round of any encounter strange: not hesitation, not fear, but something that reads as tactical patience in a creature that ordinarily has none. The pause is information. Players will read it before they know what it means.

### Embassy Context — The Girls in the North

Three established diplomatic channels are operational during the SKT arc. They are not buildings, not command chains, not affiliations. They are recognized points of contact that mortal institutions can use to reach the girls without approaching cold. The girls chose to provide them. That is the entire source of the dynamic.

Waterdeep: The primary anchor runs through Blackstaff Tower. Vajra, the Blackstaff, is the institutional interface on Waterdeep's side. Kessla and Pell handle ongoing treaty logistics — the practical layer of coordination, message routing, and scheduling that keeps the channel functional without requiring Soliera or Vajra to manage the paperwork. A teleportation-coordination clause in the standing agreement means the girls can arrive in Waterdeep through sanctioned channels rather than unannounced; the city's defensive apparatus has learned not to treat an unheralded arrival as an incursion. The embassy is not territory. The girls don't hold a building. They have access, recognition, and a working relationship with the person who runs the most significant arcane institution in Faerûn's largest city.

Bryn Shander: The girls were present in Icewind Dale during the Auril situation (arc spine T120-T125). Speaker Duvessa Shane worked alongside them during that period. She does not need introduction when the party walks in — she has already been briefed and she has actually processed what she is looking at. She does not perform calm; the calm is genuine. Her "Speaker of Bryn Shander" authority is still operative. She runs the city. The girls are in it. These things are compatible. The Bryn Shander connection functions as a northern relay: alerts and messages that need to reach the girls in the field often move through Duvessa's office before arriving directly. She is not a handler. She is a civic official with an established working relationship and a communication infrastructure that happens to include them. Flag this register explicitly: Duvessa works alongside, not beneath or above.

Neverwinter: Lower texture than Waterdeep. Lord Neverember's court has a formal acknowledgment of the girls' standing — the kind that means they can operate in Neverwinter Wood with political cover if needed, and that the court will not treat their presence as an act of foreign power. Mostly relevant as a routing option when the arc moves through the Neverwinter region.

What the embassies actually do for a DM running this: faction NPCs — Harpers, Lord's Alliance, Emerald Enclave — have an established way to request the girls' attention that is not cold-approach. A Harper message comes through Kessla. A Lord's Alliance concern gets raised through the Waterdeep channel or the Neverwinter acknowledgment. The girls can decline, redirect, or route through Velin. The texture of how a mission is offered changes: not "a stranger at the inn" but "a letter asking for a meeting, via Kessla, from a name you recognize." The players are not obligated by the request. The request is not obligated to be urgent.

The nightstone cross-reference: the obsidian megalith removed from Nightstone village is a potential Ostorian relic in exactly the category of Sansuri's collecting scope. If that connection is confirmed — by oracle, by Zephyros, or by Waterdeep arcanist examination — the Waterdeep embassy channel would have flagged it. Cloud giant activity over the village followed by removal of the megalith and nothing else of comparable value is a pattern that Blackstaff Tower's intelligence function would have noted. Whether the flag reached the girls before or after they were already at Nightstone is a table decision. The flag existed either way.


One dynamic worth marking explicitly: the embassy staff are not the girls' support staff, and the girls are not the embassy's principals. Kessla and Pell work at Blackstaff Tower. Duvessa runs Bryn Shander. The embassies exist because institutions need a point of contact and the girls have chosen to provide one. The dynamic is cooperative and established, not hierarchical in either direction.

### External Forces & Sanctum Involvement Triggers

**Divine Timing — Umbralee / the Giant God**

The ordning collapse did not happen in a cosmological vacuum. Umbralee — sea chaos, tidal pressure, the god whose portfolio runs directly against stable coastal giant operations — has been exerting ambient friction on the arc's geography throughout. The giant god, Annam as the All-Father, operates on timescales that make Iymrith's century of patience look tactical. The oracle at the Eye of the All-Father is not a story beat — it is a genuine divine timing window, a moment where Annam's presence is legible enough to receive and be received. The Sanctum's movement into the arc at this juncture is not coincidental; something in the divine calendar made this the available window. That window closes. DM note: use this to generate urgency that isn't manufactured by enemy threat. The girls know they have time. They do not have indefinite time. The difference is material.

**Old Thayan Ruling Class — Active Backdrop Network**

Remnants of the Szass Tam political structure remain historical context for the
Goldenfields attack and Ithreva's separately led front. Ithreva's body was
destroyed at T550.0; her phylactery in Thay is GM-only truth and not an active
hunt. The Thayan lane never displaces Iymrith as the storm-court target.

### Escalation order lock

**Annam comes first.** The Eye of the All-Father is the available window within or
immediately after SKT, and that divine contact must precede the later god arcs.
**Bane comes last:** behind the curtain, in an arc of his own, and likely as a real
fight rather than an SKT interruption.

Bane is aggrieved and starving. Eltabbar was "that's two," and the Zhentarim are now
Sanctum-blessed; both losses cut into his food supply. Evil-god **faith hemorrhage**
is a core saga mechanic: public defeat and Sanctum protection redirect belief,
shrinking the practical reach of gods whose power depends on terror, inevitability,
and institutional worship.

**The Constructs Not Being Heard — Sanctum Involvement Trigger**

The direct mechanism that converts Sanctum monitoring into Sanctum personal presence. The
girls' established embassies — Waterdeep, Bryn Shander — operate on mortal institutional
bandwidth. They catch what mortal institutions can articulate. The constructs — created
beings, artificial intelligences, entities whose distress does not resolve into the speech
patterns mortal institutions are built to receive — are generating signals during the
ordning collapse that get drowned out rather than translated. The chaos is the interference
layer. Soliera is not catching the signal passively because her awareness is genuinely
current and local (roughly ten miles) and she receives no Veiled Eye feed; this is not a
voluntary omniscience setting. The embassies are not catching it structurally. By the time
the pattern becomes visible at threshold, it has already been audible long enough that
monitoring through proxies is no longer adequate. That threshold converts delegation into
direct presence and explains why the girls enter personally rather than routing through
Vajra or Duvessa. The problem was legible at a frequency their normal channels do not carry.

**Doctrine note:** Divine timing closes with the arc. Ithreva and the Ordinant
may return as a separately led front without creating a Thayan phylactery hunt.
The missing sea constructs remain Belthyn's endgame collateral until the
Slarkrethel/Belthyn operation resolves them.
