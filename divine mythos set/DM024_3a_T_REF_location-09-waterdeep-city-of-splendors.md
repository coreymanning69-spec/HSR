### LOCATION 09: WATERDEEP (City of Splendors)
**[MAP POSITION]** Central Sword Coast, south of Neverwinter on the coast. The Ark is currently anchored here.
**[STATUS]** ESTABLISHED — Ally/HQ. Kessla network. Neverember healed. Undermountain beneath.
**[TRAVEL ACCESS]** The Ark. Teleport node (primary southern hub). Road from Neverwinter (the High Road, well-maintained). The girls know this city.
**[WORD OF MOUTH]** Intimate/direct. Waterdeep has seen the trio repeatedly. Neverember has been personally touched by Soliera’s intervention. Kessla runs welfare operations here. The Walking Statues incident, the sewer scenes, and the Thieves’ Guild tunnels give the city history with the Sanctum at every level of society.

**Setting Changes (25+)**
1. Waterdeep’s Sanctum embassy is in the old Thieves’ Guild tunnels below the city: cleaned, retrofitted with construct maintenance systems, lit by stored-magic lanterns, and expanded twice since establishment. The embassy now occupies approximately 4,000 square feet of underground space and is the largest Sanctum diplomatic installation outside the Sanctum itself.
2. Kessla manages city-level welfare operations from Waterdeep: overflow housing networks, food distribution augmentation, social outreach programs that operate in parallel to city systems without competing with them. Her network is the reason Waterdeep’s historically intractable poverty problem has measurably decreased. City administrators credit “improved governance.” Kessla does not correct this publicly.
3. Lord Dagult Neverember — healed and blessed by Soliera during a period of political and personal crisis — operates as the most consistent ally the Sanctum has in any mortal government. He is not obsequious about this. He is strategic: he advocates for Sanctum interests where they align with his own, and he is honest with Velin when they don’t. This makes him more useful than a simple yes-man.
4. The Waterdeep Lords’ Alliance position on the Sanctum has evolved over time from “alarmed monitoring” to “grudging acknowledgment” to “they are part of the political landscape now and must be accommodated.” The Lords have never formally recognized the Sanctum as a political entity (because doing so would create reciprocal obligations they aren’t ready for) but they extend it practical cooperation.
5. The Blackstaff Tower — seat of Waterdeep’s master mage, Vajra — has an ongoing academic collaboration with Sanctum construct engineers on the question of construct intelligence architecture. Vajra was initially involved in an incident with Sera that ended without permanent damage to either party. The collaboration is the most polite possible form of acknowledging that the incident happened and that everyone would prefer not to revisit it.
6. The Walking Statues of Waterdeep (the enormous animated stone figures that guard the city) have been modified in a way that the city does not officially acknowledge: their patrol routes now explicitly avoid Sanctum embassy territory. The modification was not requested. It was observed. Neverember’s staff confirmed via careful questioning that the statues moved on their own initiative. No one knows what this means.
7. Undermountain — the 23-level megadungeon below the city, accessible via the famous pit in the Yawning Portal tavern — contains, at its lowest levels, a portal to the Primeverse. The Sanctum is aware of this portal. Halaster Blackcloak is aware that the Sanctum is aware.
8. Durnan, the legendary proprietor of the Yawning Portal, knows what’s been going on with Undermountain better than he lets on. He has met Velin. He served her tea. He told her two things that she found extremely useful and that she has never put in writing. When the girls visit, Durnan will serve them whatever they want, watch them with extremely careful eyes, and say approximately six words total.
9. Skullport — the underground port accessible through Undermountain’s upper levels — is the seedy underside of Waterdeep’s commercial position. It handles goods and trade that Waterdeep’s official commerce system prefers not to acknowledge. A Sanctum construct operates there as a dockworker. The construct has been there long enough that Skullport’s residents consider it a local. Its name, as assigned by regulars, is Bolts.
10. The Harpers have a major presence in Waterdeep — probably their densest concentration outside their own formal halls. They have been watching the Sanctum embassy closely for years. Their Waterdeep coordinator (a half-elf named Elara who operates from a legitimate position as a shipping company registrar) has a file on every Steward, every construct, and every significant visitor to the embassy. Velin considers Elara a professional equal and buys her lunch once a quarter to maintain the relationship.
11. The Zhentarim in Waterdeep are in the middle of a factional crisis that has been ongoing for two years: a reform wing (who want to operate as a legitimate merchant network) versus an old guard (who want traditional black-market operations). The crisis is relevant because both factions have been using Waterdeep’s dockside areas — which Kessla’s network also operates in — as their testing ground. There have been three quiet confrontations. No blood yet. The Sanctum has not taken a side.
12. Waterdeep’s fashion scene — normally a proxy for political positioning among the merchant and noble classes — has developed a “Sanctum aesthetic”: architectural simplicity, high material quality, no ornamentation for its own sake. This aesthetic is expensive because the materials are genuinely superior, which means it has become paradoxically aspirational among the wealthy class that can afford simplicity. Sera finds this absurd. Soliera has never commented on it.
13. The deep harbor — where the Ark is anchored — has been restructured around the Ark’s presence. The harbor master constructed two floating platforms to accommodate Sanctum supply operations. Several merchant captains have moved their berths away from the Ark’s section of the harbor because the Ark’s construct crew unloads cargo at a speed that makes neighboring operations look embarrassing by comparison.
14. Waterdeep’s sewer system — which the girls have operational history with — has been significantly improved in the years since their involvement. Kessla’s network identified several infrastructure failure points that were connected to chronic housing problems in the lower city. The city spent money it previously didn’t have (Neverember arranged the budget) on improvements. The connection to Sanctum influence is indirect but real.
15. The Yawning Portal’s pit — the entrance to Undermountain — now has a small sign beside it. The sign reads: “Current Operators of Note: None.” Before the sign, the pit had no sign. Durnan added the sign after the girls’ first Undermountain excursion. The “None” is updated regularly. Currently it means what it says.
16. A Waterdeep noble family (House Mhzentul) attempted, two years ago, to establish a commercial arrangement with the Sanctum via intermediary without going through official Waterdeep channels. The arrangement would have given House Mhzentul exclusive rights to distribute Sanctum goods in Waterdeep’s upper market. The Sanctum declined. Velin’s response to the family’s agent: “We don’t do exclusive.” The family is still resentful.
17. The city’s temple district has had complicated reactions to Sanctum presence. The temples of Tyr and Ilmater have been broadly positive (Sanctum infrastructure alleviates suffering — this is theologically uncomplicated). The temples of Waukeen (commerce goddess) are ambivalent (Sanctum economics is not quite free market). The temple of Bane is absent from this conversation because Bane’s temple in Waterdeep is no longer operating at full capacity since certain events.
18. Waterdeep’s City Watch has updated its unusual-entity protocols three times in the past five years. The current protocol specifies: “Sanctum representatives, constructs, and principals — do not detain, do not challenge, route any incidents to Embassy liaison Steward immediately.” The Watch does not love this protocol. They follow it.
19. Halaster Blackcloak's established portal is on Level 20, the single level from which the Girls entered the Mergeverse. The Yawning Portal remains the surface access to an Undermountain district that has since expanded into a near-city.
20. The Ark in harbor has become a Waterdeep tourism attraction. People pay boat operators to row them past it for a look. The Ark’s construct crew is fully aware of the tourist traffic and ignores it with professional thoroughness. One child threw a rock at the Ark’s hull. The rock bounced back and landed in the tourist boat. The tourist boat operator refunded everyone’s fare without being asked.
21. Laeral Silverhand (Open Lord of Waterdeep, Alustriel’s sister) has a more formally political relationship with the Sanctum than her sister does. She treats it as a foreign power operating within her city’s sphere — respectfully, at diplomatic distance, with clear mutual expectations. She and Velin have a working relationship that is genuinely cordial and entirely professional. They have never talked about anything except work.
22. The Undermountain Mergeverse portal at Level 20 remains the single established cross-verse portal. The surrounding levels and Underdark veins are now busy Sanctum-linked urban infrastructure, with slow trade and conversation developing with Oak City.
23. Waterdeep’s orphanage system has been quietly supplemented by Kessla’s network for three years: food, clothing, educational materials, and periodic construct-assisted structural repairs to the buildings. The orphanage administrators know where the support comes from. They have told the children it comes from “city resources.” Kessla has not corrected this. She considers it better for the children to believe the city takes care of them.
24. A writ exists in Waterdeep’s legal archives that Neverember quietly filed two years ago: a permanent guest-right declaration for Soliera, Sera, Deashi, and Ember, acknowledging them as permanent residents of Waterdeep for the purposes of civic law. This means, technically, the girls have Waterdeep citizenship. No one has told them. Velin found out last month and is deciding whether to tell them.
25. The Waterdeep dock quarter has a new drinking establishment that has become popular among Sanctum construct crew on rest cycles: “The Bolts and Bells.” The name is a reference to Bolts (the Skullport construct) who, when asked why constructs need a drinking establishment, responded: “We don’t drink. We watch people drink. It’s interesting.” The establishment is now also popular among locals who come to have philosophical conversations with constructs.
26. Belthyn passed through Waterdeep before the Storm King's Court endgame in a worn drowned body. At a distance the body appeared to be a beautiful woman; up close its eyes were dead and cold. She concealed her identity and used the visit for reconnaissance. No public encounter or missing city response is implied.

**Embassy Status**
ACTIVE — Primary Southern Hub. Full diplomatic staff. Teleport node. Kessla’s operational network integrated with embassy logistics. The Waterdeep embassy is considered the model installation for all other Sanctum diplomatic facilities.
