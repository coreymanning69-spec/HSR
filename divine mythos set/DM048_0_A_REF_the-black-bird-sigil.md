# DM048_0 — The Black Bird Sigil

## Form and Origin

The sigil is a **black bird, wings spread, fully enclosed within a white
outline**. It has no text or secondary charge. The white enclosure is not a
decorative border: without it, a depiction is not the Black Bird Sigil.

It is **Sera's childlike scribble**, adopted by the Stewards as the Sanctum's
mark. It belongs to the Sanctum, not Ember. Where Sera or Soliera marks a place with it, Sanctum authority and the
infrastructure of an embassy or outpost may follow. That established divine
operation is not a portable new spell system.

## Public Doctrine at Amphail

At T539.3, Doran publicly stated the following to civilians:

1. A person joins the Sanctum by wanting to be in it.
2. They find someone with the bird and ask them.
3. Once in, they are protected wherever they are; the Sanctum is not a place
   they must reach.

This was a public, ungated promise by a Sanctum field Steward. Its practical
costs are future story consequences; no later owner may silently add an
application, fee, rite, examination, or geographic exception.

## Established Carriers and Instance

- Doran bears the sigil on his divine plate and uses it as a recognition gesture.
- Wren bears the Sanctum Black-Bird Sigil in her current kit. Its force-lane
  mechanics are solely `DM041_B`; this file does not restate them.
- At T539.6, Wren cut the sigil into pale slate and bound a *glyph of warding*
  holding *sending*, keyed to Guh's touch. The token gives Guh twenty-five words
  to Wren and twenty-five back. It was delivered north by three hill giants and
  was unanswered at the chapter close. It is now historical only: no reply is
  owed and it creates no active hook.

## Boundaries

Do not define a generic tier taxonomy, divine-layer effect, alternate origin,
or an Ember-owned version of the mark. The village phrase "priest of Lady Ember"
was spoken by Wren; her settled understanding is priest of the Sanctum and does
not transfer the sigil's origin from Sera.

Open-door doctrine remains literal: no application, fee, rite, examination, or
geographic restriction. Ordinary staff verify claims and triage distant requests
through the standing Steward and knight network; this is operational care, never
a narrowing of membership or a promise of immediate divine arrival.
