# DM025_0 — TR1: LUSKAN (The City of Sails)

**Arc Status:** COMPLETE
**Party:** Sera, Ember, Soliera, Letha. Velin managing logistics from the Ark.
**Travel Method:** The Ark — coastal approach from the north (Mirabar road leg complete).
**Duration:** One day, one night. Arrival morning, departure following morning.
**Word of Mouth Tier:** B — High Captains have intelligence, performing nonchalance.

## HARD CANON NOTES

1. **Deposition Principle:** Luskan receives permanent flood correction, structural wall repair, harbor current normalization, a 60-year sealed room release, and an apprentice's destiny redirected. The girls receive fried dough and a good view.
2. **Luskan Handshake:** Political maneuvering is bypassed by Ember liking a "fish on a flag," elevating House Harker through proximity alone.
3. **Passive Blessing:** Soliera's correction of the city's flooding is non-verbal and non-theatrical. She absorbs the problem and corrects the geometry of the world.
4. **Reading Gag:** The Itinerary (from DM023_0) is read aloud to Sera by an NPC when applicable.


## SETTING CONTEXT (Pre-Arrival State)

Luskan: five islands stitched together by rope bridges and elevated walkways. Five High Captains, each controlling a Ship (political-criminal faction). The Arcane Brotherhood operates out of the Host Tower as a parallel power structure. No formal law enforcement — the Ships provide their own. No Sanctum embassy, no waystation. Velin's deliberate choice: the moment they establish there, they become Ship Six. The Sanctum road from Mirabar cuts inland before reaching Luskan, bypassing the city entirely. The High Captains know this is deliberate. Velin has not clarified whether it's punitive or logistical.

The Brotherhood's intelligence file on the Sanctum is the most complete outside of Waterdeep's Lords' Alliance. It concludes with: "Do not provoke. Observe only."

Jarlaxle has a semi-permanent presence in the city. He sent Velin a message: "I look forward to the visit. Please tell Sera I have no plans to be interesting. This is, of course, a lie." Velin has not passed this to Sera.

Population ~16,000. Harbor is the largest in the North. City smells like salt, tar, fish, and the specific smoke of a hundred fires nobody fully controls.


## TR1 BEAT LOG


### TR1-01: The Approach
**Tags:** #LOC-HARBOR #LOC-THE-ARK #CHAR-VELIN #CONCEPT-LUSKAN-HANDSHAKE

The Ark clears the last headland and Luskan opens up. Five islands, rope bridges, elevated walkways. The Host Tower of the Arcane visible from a league out — jutting from the mainland district like something that was never supposed to exist at that height, alien geometry against the grey sky.

The harbor master's bell is already ringing.

Three separate welcoming parties form on the main dock — different colored sashes, each pretending not to see the others. Red sashes: Ship Kurth. Blue: Ship Rethnor. Fish emblem, arrived last, most panicked: a minor House trying to get ahead. Lord Harker. He sent Velin three letters. She has not replied to any of them.

The Luskan handshake is in motion. All three groups trying to be first to the gangplank without visibly running.

Velin is at the Ark's rail, rolled document in one hand, jaw set in the expression she gets when she's been awake since before dawn managing exactly this situation.

Children on the Ark too — Soliera travels with children always. Sanctum crew, deckhands, people of different standings. Quite a few know Luskan. The Ark is visible to the entire harbor. This is not a subtle arrival.


### TR1-02: The Dock — Harker Gets Chosen
**Tags:** #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-HARKER #CONCEPT-LUSKAN-HANDSHAKE #THEME-WITNESS-ECONOMY

Sera asks Velin who these people are and why they're waiting. Velin briefs: competing Ship delegations, political handshake protocol, five factions running five parallel welcome bids.

Ember, at the rail, watches the dock. Points at Harker's group.

"I like the fish on that flag."

That's it. That's how House Harker — the smallest of the five Ships, two vessels currently in for repairs, controlling the eastern island district and fish market licensing — gets chosen by the divine party. Ember liked a fish on a flag.

Harker comes aboard. He is deeply aware that approaching with gods in tow is categorically different from approaching alone. He does the math in real time: two dry-docked vessels become a fleet problem somebody else solves. Three unanswered letters become leverage he didn't have this morning.

He offers to show them the city.


### TR1-03: The Walk — Street-Level Luskan
**Tags:** #LOC-MARKET-SQUARE #LOC-CUTLASS-ISLAND #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-HARKER #THEME-PASSIVE-HORROR #RULE-VELVET-SPIRAL

Harker walks them through Luskan's elevated walkways and rope bridges. Street-level observations: the city is built vertically as a response to chronic flooding — elevated walkways, rope bridges, ground level dark and cramped. Buildings marked with Ship territory symbols: knots, colors, door frames.

Ember observes the city is "written on" like a very long letter that everyone can read except visitors.

Sera's invulnerability shown through small physical details — bare feet on northern harbor stone, silk summer dress in autumn, a crab hanging from her finger while she laughs about it. The showing off is both vanity and power. She knows she's pretty and she knows nothing touches her and doesn't distinguish between the two facts.

Market square: fried dough vendor, honey and sea salt, 40+ years same family. Sera decides they're getting it. Buys two portions, eats them walking.

Harker explains the Ship system. Five captains, five factions, each controlling territory. Ship Kurth has the deep harbor. Ship Rethnor controls the northern approach roads and salvage licensing on the wreck field.

> "So you're the fish people legitimately," Sera says.
>
> "...We are the fish people legitimately, yes."

Sera points at Ember with her bread in a gesture of vindication. Ember looks extremely satisfied.

Harker explains the Brotherhood sits above or beside the Ships depending on who you ask. They control what the Ships can't — magic, intelligence, sea routes requiring magical navigation. The arrangement works because everyone understands what happens if it stops working.

> "What happens?" Ember asks.
>
> "Luskan eats itself," Harker says simply. "It's happened before."


### TR1-04: The Salted Mast
**Tags:** #LOC-SALTED-MAST #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-HARKER #CHAR-LETHA #THEME-DOMESTIC-DIVINITY

Harker takes them to the Salted Mast — his regular tavern. As Soliera enters, the entire tavern goes silent. Not gradually — all at once. Everyone stopped talking. Then Ember walks in, and whatever had just settled shifts again, because now there are two of them, and Ember's presence is a different register than Soliera's. Then Sera pops in. Then Harker walks in behind them and the room recalculates him entirely.

Harker's visibility in this moment: the man who walked in with the gods. Every person in the tavern is reassessing House Harker's position in the city's power structure in real time.

Sera eats everything. Fried kelp. Whatever else is available. Ember eats with her. They settle in like they own the place, because in a practical sense they now do — the Velvet Spiral is running and every person in the room is experiencing the passive recalibration.

Sera mentions casually to Harker that they can fix all his problems if he wants. The ships, whatever else. But let's get through the day first, she says. She wants to see everything. And maybe later they go meet the other captains.

> "We can fix all of that if you want, by the way. The ships, whatever else." She doesn't slow down. "But let's get through the day first. I want to see everything this place has. And maybe later we go meet your other friends — the other captains. I wouldn't mind talking to them."

Harker stands in the doorway doing math. Walking back toward Ship Kurth and Ship Rethnor with the Sanctum at his shoulder changes four generations of harbor politics.

Ember says wait. She has more kelp to eat. Also, didn't they say they'd spruce up his house? Sera tells her not to worry about it — they'll mention it to Velin later, she can take care of everything. Direct access to the Sanctum or whatever he wants. That way he can focus on just showing them around.

Harker's expression: a man who wanted it to happen and is now watching it happen at a speed his political training didn't prepare him for.


### TR1-05: The Rope Bridge — Crossing to Cutlass Island
**Tags:** #LOC-CUTLASS-ISLAND #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SERA

Sera suggests going across the rooftops to reach the Host Tower. Harker explains the rooftops aren't connected — the islands are still separated. The bridges are the only crossing.

Ember is delighted by this. The rope bridges sway. She wants to take the bridge specifically because of the swaying. This is why they take the bridge instead of the road. This is important — Ember chose the fun path and everyone followed.

Sera's mild disappointment about the rooftops lasts exactly one second before full commitment to the bridge option.


### TR1-06: The Host Tower — First Approach
**Tags:** #LOC-HOST-TOWER #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-CORVEL #CHAR-CASHAAN #CHAR-TEYVA #CHAR-DRUETTE

The Host Tower of the Arcane: a massive dead stone tree — central spire with four limbs reaching cardinal directions, covered in smaller spires and balconies. Built by Netherese arcanist Melathlar in -354 DR as a sacrifice to protect the original settlement. The tower is what that act became. Nobody designed the shape. Melathlar is in the walls.

The Brotherhood rebuilt it in 1486 DR after a century as a haunted ruin. Public posture: cooperative, transparent. Actual goal: unchanged.

Two apprentices on the platform. The shorter one gives his name to Ember when she asks: Corvel. Eighteen years old.

Ember locks onto the tower immediately. Asks Corvel about Melathlar.

> "He put himself into it, right? To protect people."

The kid has no good answer. His colleague has nothing for him either.

Then a door opens somewhere above them inside the tower. Not dramatically. It just opens, the way significant doors do when the person opening them doesn't need drama.

Three of them come through:

**Teyva the Gray** — Overwizard East. Half-elf enchanter, former rogue. Comes first. Reads the platform before she's fully on it. Eyes go to Ember and Corvel first, then Sera, then Harker. She and Sera are going to get along.

**Druette the Raven** — Overwizard North. Tall, contained, politically weathered. She looks at Soliera and does not look away, which marks her as either very brave or very disciplined. She doesn't try to read Soliera with arcane senses. She just watches.

**Cashaan the Red** — Archmage Arcane. Comes through his own door last because he doesn't need to be first. Older, red cloak chosen decades ago and never reconsidered. His entrance is a statement. It dissolves on contact with Soliera, who is looking at the city and not watching for it.

Ember tells Corvel to come with them. He looks at Teyva. Teyva nods.
