| Ch. 10 - Hold of the Storm Giants | 201-214 | Maelstrom court, Serissa's regency, Iymrith infiltration. | Maelstrom is a storm giant palace beneath the Trackless Sea. Serissa is holding the court together with incomplete information, two compromised sisters, and an ancient dragon advising her in disguise. The girls arrive not as supplicants requesting audience but as people who have already sorted the mess that led here — and who have the information Serissa has been trying to find. The dynamic is structured around Serissa's legitimate authority, which the girls honor. They do not step over her. They give her what she needs. Political vacuum texture: Hekaton has been absent for months and the court has not been frozen in place waiting for him. A minor functionary — supply steward, third rank — has been issuing procurement decisions outside his authority because no one with the correct rank has been available to authorize them; he knows this is improper and does it anyway because the larder does not wait for proper channels. Two noble storm giant families with no legitimate succession claim have been quietly positioning themselves in proximity to Mirran and Nym respectively, watching whether Serissa holds. Uthor has been making judgment calls he has no formal mandate to make; he is the structural spine of a court operating without its sovereign, doing his own job and everyone else's, and the weight of that is visible to anyone who knows what to look for. The court is not collapsing. It is drifting. Drift, left long enough, becomes collapse. |
| Ch. 11 - Caught in the Tentacles | 215-224 | Grand Dame lead, Kraken Society chain, Hekaton rescue. | The Grand Dame is a gambling pleasure vessel operated by Khaspere Drylund (Kraken Society Admiral) that provides the entry point into the Society's network. The trail leads from the Grand Dame to the Morkoth, the ship where Hekaton is actually held. [Source-verified: two distinct ships. The Grand Dame is a casino riverboat operating the Yartar waterway — ornately decorated, run by Drylund, the entry point into the Kraken Society chain, not the prison. The Morkoth is a separate vessel with a squid-shaped hull, captained by Tholtz Daggerdark (CE Illuskan human archmage, Slarkrethel's devoted thrall), sailing the Trackless Sea around the Purple Rocks islands. Hekaton is held in chains aboard the Morkoth. The ship is named for its squid-like hull form, not for any creature imprisoned there. Drylund, when cornered on the Grand Dame, reveals the Morkoth's location — and dies for it seconds later by Slarkrethel's telepathic kill.] Hekaton's rescue is a justice operation: Soliera removing a sovereign held by mortals who made a category error about what is removable. |
| Ch. 12 - Doom of the Desert | 225-230 | Iymrith lair and final module conclusion. | Iymrith's lair near Anauroch. The source runs this as the climactic boss encounter — ancient blue dragon in her lair, full dragon combat. In Divineverse: Soliera considers Iymrith with genuine intellectual interest. A manipulation so structurally clean that it read as natural collapse for years is the kind of problem worth understanding before it ends. Soliera understands it. Then she ends it. Iymrith had centuries of patience. She did not account for something that does not operate on the same clock she does. |
