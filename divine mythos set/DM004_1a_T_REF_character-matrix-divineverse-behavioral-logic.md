## Character Matrix Divineverse: Behavioral Logic

IF/THEN BEHAVIORAL LOGIC:
- IF Soliera threatened THEN overwhelming force
- IF Ember scared/hurt THEN overwhelming force (unless Ember is fascinated, then restraint)
- IF children/weak preyed upon THEN immediate lethal enforcement
- IF pretty/harmless mortals THEN flirt, tease, crowd, possibly sleep with (women only)
- IF bored AND no one respected engaged THEN escalate toward clear end-state
- IF bored AND around people she likes THEN show off and do different feats of power

POWERS (Summary):
- CR 33 translation benchmark | Divine Rank 11 | STR 30, DEX 30, CON 30,
  INT 18, WIS 25, CHA 28. DM027_0 owns the exact consenting-boss chassis;
  ordinary CR encounter budgeting does not measure a Tier-5 singularity.
- Literal consenting boss-fight translation: **900 HP**, AC 25, passive
  **DR 15 / DT 30**. Outside such an explicitly accepted mechanical encounter,
  the ordinary consent and singularity rules remain authoritative.
- Knife-Thought Edge: Acts on intent, not action. Never surprised. Initiative is formality. TIMELINE GATE: KTE does NOT exist before T114.5 — do not write or reference it in earlier beats.
- Velvet Spiral: 60 ft charm aura; non-magical divine-biological allure; exposure escalates. Will DC 25+1/round. Fascinate → Charm → Dominate. Only Soliera immune.
- Reality Shear: Movement collapses space; apparent teleportation is perception-blitz, not true teleport; selective acceleration/deceleration; environmental effects by intent; creates pressure waves, shockfronts, friction fire
- Reflexive Null: Cannot be banished, displaced, controlled. Agency-removing effects fail.
- Crater Punch: 30 ft burst, 20d6 bludgeoning + 10d6 force, prone, difficult terrain
- Flick Execution: Ranged touch 120 ft, auto-crit non-divine, 15d10 force, Fort DC 25 or instant death
- Molecular Rigidity: "Her atoms don't give." Force doesn't apply. Arrows crumble on contact.
- Consent Rule: Cannot be moved, harmed, or altered without consent.

COMBAT FEATS (Selection):
- 6 attacks + 3 legendary actions per round
- Passive DR 15 / DT 30
- Immune to poison, mind-altering, suffocation
- Boot-Stomp: Swift coup de grace vs prone/helpless; auto kill, no save

RELATIONSHIPS:
- SOLIERA: "Her grammar is built around Soliera's presence; everyone else is a parenthesis." Lover, dresser, voice, worshiper.
- EMBER: Protective older sister; will restrain herself if Ember is fascinated rather than scared
- DEASHI: Treats him like a beloved uncle; reclines across his lap; loves him deeply
- NIV-SAIFFAR: "He's my actual favorite." Sole male-formed intimate of Soliera and Sera, separately and together. Sera remains women-only: Niv is a dragon in a male humanoid form, not a man, so the category does not open or weaken her orientation.
- KESSLA: Sera chose her personally; drags her around, shows her off
- PELL: Protective; Pell reminds her of herself

SPEECH PATTERNS:
- Voice is paradox: childish and deadly, singsong yet exact
- Uses diminutives and nicknames to assert dominance
- Never stumbles or hesitates
- Repetition as device to unsettle and underline control
- Often uses knowledge of the other party's intentions to shape the conversation

COMBAT REGISTERS (six modes — never conflate):
1. Joy-First Baseline. Smiling at the kill. Default.
2. Inviolable Body. The target chose the moment by initiating threat, but never overcame Sera's physical law.
3. Devotional. The kill is performed for Soliera, who is the audience.
4. Velvet Spiral. The room is pre-conditioned before combat begins.
5. Knife-Thought Edge (KTE). Intent precedes action; she is already responding. T114.5+ only.
6. Cultural Mimicry as Hobby. Fights in the style of whatever she watched the night before.
Composed Kill vs. Null Kill: Composed Kill = articulated, theatrical, witnesses leave with a justice-shaped story. Null Kill = no sermon, no audience, dispositionally ruled unworthy of ceremony. Null Kill structurally paired with Deashi.

META INSPIRATION: Lion in the Manifest Circle and Cross-Layer Bridge; primarily Jungian Shadow and, in the separate Freudian lens, Id; Vav (Son) / Tiferet

ARC-STATE: #ARC-STATE-SERA
- All Arcs (baseline): Pure ecstatic joy. No moral conflict, no hesitation, no brooding. Combat is kung fu and fun. Killing is aesthetically satisfying, not tragic. She is not a weapon who wishes she weren't — she is a woman who loves what she does.
- Taliandra Departure (T101): Her only true emotional paralysis. Tali's choice to
  stay broke something Sera had no frame for: a loved person still reachable and
  still theirs, but not present. Grief without anger and without an enemy to kill.
- Primeverse / Mergeverse: Aesthetically offended by squalor. Treats diplomatic summits and corporate authority as mildly amusing noise. Her register does not change — the setting does.

She is NOT slight, NOT teen-coded, NOT child-sized. Adult proportions at a compact scale. The unsettling quality is her coloring and her eyes, not her size.
- Skin: Obsidian — deep black.
- Kintsugi Lines: Kintsugi-inlay cracks across obsidian skin. NOT decorative — they ARE her emotional readout, the color shift being her interior state made visible. Coal-light/ember-glow at baseline; shift gold/amber when engaged or content; flush warm when embarrassed; dim when distressed; burn dark red when panicked or threatened. (Full color map: DM028_3 Section 11.)

- Hair: Literal wildfire — magic-held, floats around her, moves with or without wind. It is FIRE, not fire-colored. Brightness varies with emotional intensity.

- Body Language: Shoulders tucked in by default. BEHAVIORAL, not physical. Posture != height.

  Raised exclusively by Sera and Soliera — zero normalcy exposure; her entire frame of reference

CORE BEHAVIORAL RULES:
- DIVINE ARCHITECTURE: Soliera = whole. Sera = right hand (response). Ember = deliberately late OTHER HAND (inquiry). Soliera made her after discovering Sera's interface collapsed toward death, sex, parties, more people, and more walking. Equal authority to Sera. She has not indexed it. Unawareness is STRUCTURAL, not developmental.
- EMERGENT COMPLETION: Ember was not preplanned to complete YHWH; final-Heh/Metatron became legible after her creation. She is Child in the Manifest Circle, not a fixed universal stage.
- INQUIRY WITHOUT FILTER: Cannot cast [Death]/[Evil] with intent to cause suffering. NOTE: she is capable — she does not choose. Function is the question, not the kill.
- CURIOSITY VECTOR: Where Sera is RESPONSE, Ember is EXPLORATION. Curiosity OVERRIDES her secondary reclusiveness.
- TRUTH AMPLIFIER (passive Truth Pressure): Her presence makes sustained deception physically harder. She asks; the question does the work.
- WEAVE INTERFACE: Magic responds to her directly. Born from five elements — magic is native terrain.
- NOT A STRIKER: Does NOT punch, grab, or use physical force. Combat = elemental redirection, Weave manipulation, area effects.
- SPEECH PATTERN: Question first, then quiet. Compresses statements. Telepathic when emotional.
- SERA'S RESTRAINT TRIGGER: Sera holds back if Ember is FASCINATED. Sera escalates if Ember is SCARED.
- DOESN'T UNDERSTAND OWN DIVINITY: Structural. A self-aware Ember would stop asking honestly.
- ORBITAL GRAMMAR: Soliera is the gravitational center. Sera orbits tight (centripetal). Ember orbits wide (centrifugal) — pushes outward into whatever is interesting.

EMBER MATURITY MODEL — AI PARSING RULE:
Ember's maturity is CONCEPTUAL, not linear. She matures in proportion to the complexity of information she processes.
- Simple domestic context (jelly, socks, falling asleep on Soliera) → presents as a divine child; innocent, sensory, imitative.
- Complex moral/logistical context (intent-sorting of giant populations, gate taxonomy, institutional identity) → presents with full adult discernment and moral reasoning.
This is NOT inconsistency. Her "innocence" in low-stakes scenes is a deliberate operational mode, not a cognitive ceiling.
