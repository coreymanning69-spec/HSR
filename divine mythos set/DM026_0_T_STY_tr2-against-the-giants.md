# DM026_0 — TR2: AGAINST THE GIANTS (G1-G2-G3)

#THREAD-ECLAVDRA-SHADOW

## HARD CANON NOTES

1. This file is authoritative for TR2 (Against the Giants, G1-G2-G3 modules).
2. SCALPEL protocol: verify and read only; no new canon without DM confirmation.
3. Module inversion applies: all three modules run as administrative cleanup, not survival encounters. Published difficulty (level 8–12 party, multiple forays) is irrelevant — the Girls don't foray.
4. Ember's Moral Sorting (primary governance): Ember distinguishes willing combatants (eliminated) from fear-conscripted fighters and slaves (spared). Consistent and principled across all three modules.
5. Domestication vs. Elimination: G1 was resolved through domestication. Chief Nosnra and forty giants now belong to Sera. No kill order unless she must return for any reason other than saying hello.
6. Victim-Centered Justice (G2): Jarl Grugnur's resolution was a commission for Yrsa. His kneecaps were removed and he was delivered to her for judgment. Not killed by the party.
7. The Eclavdra Thread (CLOSED AT ARC END): Eclavdra predicted divine intervention and acted through observation. Her agent withdrew with a report, and Eclavdra sealed the current dossier rather than launching a second move. A later return remains possible, but no unresolved action is owed by TR2.
8. Ember is adult-bodied throughout — see DM028_3 for physical spec. Her sorting instinct and moral seriousness reflect divine maturity, not inexperience.

## G1 — THE STEADING OF THE HILL GIANT CHIEF

**Status:** COMPLETE — Resolved through domestication, not elimination
**Tags:** #LOC-STEADING #CHAR-NOSNRA #CHAR-YRSA #CONCEPT-DOMESTICATION


### TR2-G1.01: The Road — Discovery
**Tags:** #LOC-SANCTUM-ROAD #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-LETHA

Overturned merchant cart on the partially-improved Sanctum road. Dead horse killed by a thrown boulder. Shattered wheel. Drag marks leading northeast. A second set of booted tracks from an observer who fled east — Eclavdra's agent, though nobody knows that yet.

Sera Perception check: 59 (Knife-Thought Edge enhanced). Reads the full tactical picture — the boulder's trajectory, the giants' unhurried pace, the presence of the observer.

Ember Survival check: 16. Combined with magical sensitivity: detects attack timing (4-6 hours ago), that the victim was taken alive, and the emotional residue — the horse's pre-attack panic.

Two investigation threads identified: main giant trail northeast, booted observer tracks east.


### TR2-G1.02: The Steading — Arrival
**Tags:** #LOC-STEADING #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NOSNRA #RULE-VELVET-SPIRAL #CONCEPT-DOMESTICATION

They came in out of the rain.

No combat. No casualties. No dramatic entrance beyond the simple fact of their arrival — Sera and Ember first, then Soliera stepping through the great timber doors like weather moving through a room. The Steading reorganized itself around them the way all spaces eventually do. The Velvet Spiral saturates. By the time Letha walked in, forty giants and eight ogres had already made their collective peace with the new ownership structure, and Nosnra was on his knees doing math about his life expectancy. A cloud giant witness also knelt under the same overwhelming recognition; that witnessed posture later gives Sansuri the key to understand who the Barefoot Death is when the account reaches her.

The math came out favorable. That was the point.

**Drift guard.** The Steading submission is not a doctrinal event. It is the
Velvet Spiral acting on a population whose social instinct is formalized, so
the collapse presents as posture rather than broken composure. It is not an
error on the giants' part, not a misapplication of the Ordning, and not a
precedent for giant theology. Do not name it or build a sect from it.

Sera ate ox at the high table — seated cross-legged on it like she owned the elevation — and established the terms: everyone in the room now belonged to her. If she had to come back for any reason other than saying hello, the belonging would end.

Intimidation check: 63. The cave bear pressed itself flat and didn't move for twenty minutes.


### TR2-G1.03: Intelligence Extraction
**Tags:** #CHAR-ECLAVDRA #CHAR-YRSA #CONCEPT-TEMPLE-OF-THE-EYE

The cloud giant — visiting dignitary, intelligence sufficient to recognize an existential threat when one sat cross-legged on the chief's table eating his food — provided everything:

- Eclavdra's coordination of the giant alliance
- The Jarl's name: Grugnur, two days north in the Glacial Rift
- Storm giantess in Area 8, chained on the north wall with her father's manacles, being coerced. Fine food she hadn't touched in three days
- Seventy-eight orc rebels in the lower caverns, trading food with troglodytes for water, left to work themselves to death or starve
- Temple of the Eye below everything — an altar older than the current gods that grants what the user most desires

Eclavdra, the cloud giant noted, had predicted divine intervention specifically. Called it a good test.

Nobody followed up. That thread sits underneath everything like a cold current. Eclavdra isn't reacting to the gods. She's studying them.


### TR2-G1.04: Liberation
**Tags:** #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-YRSA

Ember ordered the three stone giants to free the orcs and leave the manacles off. They departed immediately — the efficiency of people given clear instructions by something that did not require their agreement. The orcs moved through the lower corridors in ones and twos and then a quiet stream, gaunt and blinking, rediscovering what their legs were for.

The door to Area 8 came apart under Sera's hand — not with force deployed consciously, but with the particular quality of heat and pressure that accumulates when something divine decides a barrier is no longer relevant. The iron bar came out of the wall through the door. Sera set it aside gently, because she was in a good mood.

The storm giantess: alive, strong-willed, three weeks unchained, hadn't broken. Her name was Yrsa. She offered it carefully.

Sera flirted with her immediately. Yrsa told her she wasn't her type. Sera said "darn" with complete sincerity.

Sera offered to kill the Jarl "real good." Used the word *reprehensible* with visible pride, noted that Niv had told her it applied to a lot of what she did.

Yrsa said she thought they could handle it.

That's a commission, not a request. It carries weight.


### TR2-G1.05: The Altar — Dismissed
**Tags:** #CONCEPT-TEMPLE-OF-THE-EYE #THEME-DOMESTIC-DIVINITY

The Temple of the Eye below the Steading: an altar older than the current gods that grants what the user most desires.

They walk past it without pausing.

Sera already has everything she most desires. She's walking around in it. There is no unfilled space in her architecture where a wish could get purchase.

Ember operates from pure present-tense logic. She catalogs what is, not what's missing. The altar needs an unfilled space to work with — a wish pressed against a wall. She doesn't have one. There's no gap where a most desire could get purchase.

Soliera is the law that governs forces. The altar would feel that the moment she walked past it. Not the other way around.

Eclavdra will note which problems they chose to solve first. She'll note this too — that they walked past the most powerful object in the Steading without pausing.


### TR2-G1.06: Departure
**Tags:** #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-NOSNRA #LOC-STEADING

Sera informed Nosnra of the arrangement. The Steading operates differently now. Storm giantess treated correctly. Slaves not replaced. Next time Sera comes back and it isn't a social call, everyone dies.

The cloud giant: noted, filed, left alive. Alive-and-knowing is more useful than dead.

Yrsa stayed behind. The Glacial Rift: two days north.

**G1 RESULT:** Zero casualties. Forty giants domesticated. Seventy-eight orcs freed. One storm giantess liberated with a commission accepted. One altar ignored. One conspiracy thread identified.

### TR2 NPC ACTION CLOSURE

Yrsa remains at the Steading long enough to receive Grugnur and settle her own
judgment. She does not become its mediator, administrator, or resident. After
the Against the Giants sequence she returns to her actual storm-giant clan
home. She remains aware of the later SKT movement through the giant communities
the party has already visited.

The Steading is not mediated after her departure. Sera claimed its giants and
left. They continue their ordinary lives under one absolute standing term:
they do not attack humans at all. This is ownership pressure, not a new civic
government or a Sanctum-managed settlement.

Eclavdra's observer reaches her with a complete report: the girls sorted the
giants, freed the coerced, ignored the altar, and left Grugnur alive for Yrsa.
Eclavdra does not attack or reveal herself. She burns the field notes, keeps
one copied page, and redirects her attention toward the Underdark. That is an
NPC action and a deliberate pause, not an unresolved missing beat.


## G2 — THE GLACIAL RIFT OF THE FROST GIANT JARL

**Status:** COMPLETE — Total military clearance with moral sorting
**Tags:** #LOC-GLACIAL-RIFT #CHAR-GRUGNUR #CHAR-YRSA #CONCEPT-VICTIM-CENTERED-JUSTICE #RULE-EMBER-SORTING


### TR2-G2.01: Travel — The Altar Conversation
**Tags:** #CHAR-LETHA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CONCEPT-TEMPLE-OF-THE-EYE

They left the Steading in the grey hour before dawn. Letha mentioned the altar during the walk — she'd been thinking about it. The girls dismissed it. Soliera is too strong for it to matter. Sera has no unfilled desire. Ember doesn't have the framework.

Yrsa stayed at the Steading.


### TR2-G2.02: Rift Entrance — Sentinels
**Tags:** #LOC-GLACIAL-RIFT #CHAR-SERA

Gygax at his most punishing — a multi-level open chasm in severe cold, green ice-light, currents that redirect flight. Designed to grind parties down over multiple forays.

They don't foray.

Two sentinels at the entrance. Sera swept them from the sleigh — one hand each, no pause between. The thunder reached every occupied cave in the complex simultaneously. The horn didn't blow. The effect was identical.

Sera declared total clearance. Kill everybody. Letha agreed quietly. Ember had one amendment: Grugnur specifically, alive, for Yrsa. Then she ran the arithmetic on total clearance — ear collection, caged humans, treaty scroll, diplomatic infrastructure of organized genocide — and said okay.


### TR2-G2.03: Upper Level — Cleared
**Tags:** #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #RULE-EMBER-SORTING

Systematic room-by-room clearance. Sera uses kinetic air waves — not magic, physics. The wet architectural sound that combat doesn't usually make, the steam rising in the cold, the ice taking on its wrong colors.

Letha walks in behind Soliera and doesn't look up at the ledges. She'd heard it. She would always hear it. But she'd learned that looking was a separate decision from hearing.

Ember looks at the ledges. At what's on them.

> "They shot at her," she says. Not accusation. Not surprise. Just the fact, placed carefully in the air between them.

The Great Cavern's two hidden ballista positions — shattered. Antechamber guards — four impact signatures on the north wall. Area 13 guard station — one giant pulled into the wall, the other pushed through it. The gong sounded before Sera reached it. Full alert. Ember silenced it with one hand. Told the iron to stop. It stopped.

Ogre-magi emissaries attempted diplomatic parley. The lead emissary was mid-sentence about protections and treaties when Ember asked what the treaty was for. He hesitated. The hesitation was the verdict. The cavern simplified. Treaty scroll — 100,000 gp in gems for joining the war on mankind — survived, pinned under rubble. Letha kept it.

Ogre quarters: Sera cleared alone. Told Ember to wait in the passage. Found the ear collection. Looked inside the chest for three seconds. Closed it. Told Ember not to open it. Ember said she already knew what was in it through the Weave.

**Ember's moral sorting system in full operation:** noncombatant giantesses and young spared. Armed males eliminated. The distinction between willing combatants and those forced to fight — Ember can tell the difference and does, every time.

White dragons: the ancient male breathed cold on Sera and it became weather. The female dove from a ledge and Sera caught her one-handed, balanced her on an open palm, then set her down gently after Ember ruled them pets rather than combatants.

> "A dog bites when you come into its house. That's not the dog's fault."

Fire lizards: spared — mated pair with eggs. Ember said they're just animals, they have babies.

Hill giant delegation in Area 24: five giants carrying a gold-inlaid skull with an outdated report and a pass to King Snurre's Hall. Sera took the skull, handed the report to Letha (she can't read), told the delegation to go home and remind Nosnra the arrangement holds. They left fast. Ember laughed.
