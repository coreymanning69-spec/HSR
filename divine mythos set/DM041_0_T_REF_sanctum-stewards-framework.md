# SANCTUM STEWARDS — FRAMEWORK

#ITEM-OBSIDIAN-DAGGERS #ITEM-GIANT-CLEAVER #THREAD-SOLIERA-TELEPORT

This dossier contains routed authority. Doran and Wren's supplied
characterization and level-13 launch sheets are locked canon. Both reached
level 14 at the post-frost T530 rest and remained on those sheets through T531.
At T532 Wren's chassis was restructured. At T535 the Ironslag module award
advanced both candidates to 165,000 XP / effective level 15. At T537.0 they were
accepted as full Tier-3 Sanctum field Stewards and divinely raised to level 20.
The active live ledger owns the current edge and values, DM041_A owns
Doran's current source mechanics, and DM041_B owns Wren's current source mechanics.
Unprovided childhood, household, parentage, and intake-scene detail remains
unspecified rather than draft.

## Shared Stewardship Frame

Doran and Wren are not replacements for Velin or Letha. They are the first
named attempt to produce what those two became: people who can remain fully
competent, socially legible, and personally themselves while living beside
divinity.

Niv identified them as viable candidates and Velin signed off on readiness and
briefed them; T537.0 completed their acceptance as field Stewards. Velin may
travel with the party whenever she wants; no structural
ban or one-leg limit applies, but her permission to come does not make her a
required traveler or ordinary rolling character. Korin, Letha, Pell, and Velin trained the pair in the same advanced
cohort for roughly one year, but the two students built their levels through
individual disciplines. Before TR3-SKT neither had completed real field work
alone or as a party.

Their original assignment was not a death test. It was an approximately two-week
field crucible that stretched with the route: could they make decisions, remain useful,
and be good company beside Soliera, Sera, and Ember without hiding inside
formality, losing themselves to first-contact awe, or depending on divine
intervention?

The girls were present for the Nightstone opening, Dripping Caves, the T522
morning gifts/bath, and the Waterdeep South Gate arrival. After that handoff,
Soliera, Sera, and Ember take a Waterdeep/Blackstaff break with Vajra while the
next SKT leg puts Doran and Wren forward as the active playable pair. There is no
literal protective bubble. Doran and Wren walk, climb, fall, bleed, spend spell
slots, become exhausted, and can be knocked unconscious or captured normally.
Sanctum standing and the Waterdeep channel remain background infrastructure,
not automatic field intervention.

## Revival Is Ordinary — [RULED 2026-09-05]

The Sanctum does not treat raising the dead as significant and does not think
about it often. It is a spell that happens near them routinely. Wren and Doran
grew up inside that for roughly eight years, so it is ordinary to them in the way
a working hospital is ordinary to someone raised beside one.

**Do not write revival as a dramatic beat, a moral weight, or a cost.** No
hesitation scene, no price extracted, no debate about whether it should be done.
If a scene needs tension, the tension is somewhere else.

## Teaching Doctrine - Gods Exemplify

The gods do not run drills. They embody an unreachable apex; the mortal
recalibrates upward by chasing it. Technique still comes from mortal masters:
Korin for Doran, Wren's own abjuration foundation for Wren. Divine proximity
moves the ceiling out of sight without replacing the work.

Doran chases speed through interaction. Sera can say "strike me," be gone, and
say "again," always pressing him to his exact limit. His endgame image is a
greatsword moved with short-sword speed.

Wren chases conception through observation. Ember cannot spar spells on the
Weave layer; she is above the mortal school taxonomy. Wren watches impossible
substrate edits through Weave-sight, then reverse-engineers a legal wizardly
architecture that approximates the result. Doran is handed a sparring partner.
Wren is handed a mystery.
