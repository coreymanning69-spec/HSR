## HARD CANON NOTES (continued 2)

Meta: This beat is the official collision of the old political order with the new divine reality. The council's fear and deference, contrasted with Sera's playful sadism and Ember's innocent honesty, create a profound and unsettling atmosphere. The conversation is less about an exchange of information and more about the council's submission to a power they can neither understand nor control. Lady Laeral's mention of Halaster Blackcloak's fear is the ultimate hook, linking the events of Thay to the impending Undermountain arc and confirming that the divine trio's actions have had a cosmic, not just political, impact. Soliera's final, silent act is a perfect representation of her power: a gesture that needs no words to command attention and instill awe.

T418: The Dragon Said It #CHAR-ELIA #LORE-NYMMURH #JUSTIFICATION-CORRECTION

Summary: The council meeting continues with the lords demanding an explanation for the destruction of Thay. Before Sera or Soliera can answer, Elia (the ancient silver dragon Otaaryliakkarnos in human form) arrives and stands beside them. She calmly explains that the Red Wizards murdered an allied brass dragon, Nymmurh, in a cowardly ambush, and that Soliera's actions were not out of "vengeance," but "correction." The room is silenced by the dragon's testimony. Sera then stands and, in a casual and dismissive tone, clarifies that they "didn't destroy the capital" but "corrected it." She reveals that the catalyst for the "correction" was finding out that Thayan slavers had left an aunt of two cousins with only one leg. Sera explains that the people who weren't monsters were saved, and the bad ones were unmade. She ends by pointing at the dragon and claiming, "We rewrote the ending."


T419: This is focused on council responses, useless for our narrative. We have this as a Joke beat in a previous canon source where it's 407; Sera lifting the insane cover off the Yawning Portal's entrance to Undermountain. Sadly we lost that aspect, but the joke is still funny.

Meta: This beat dramatically shifts the narrative from a political interrogation to a divine tribunal. The arrival of Elia, a figure of immense power and moral authority, validates the divine trio's actions and completely deflates the council's attempt to apply mortal law to a divine event. Sera's explanation of the cousins' aunt being mutilated by slavers provides a visceral, emotional core to the divine judgment, making it personal and relatable. This highlights the theme that Soliera's justice is a response to specific, cruel acts, not a grand, impersonal conquest. The final statement, "We rewrote the ending," is a perfect summary of the divine trio's purpose: they don't just punish, they fundamentally reshape reality for the better.

T420: Council Fracture & Divine Authority #CHAR-SOLIERA #POLITICS-END #MAGIC-NULLIFICATION

Tensions rise as Soliera remains seated and silent, letting Sera handle all speaking. The heads of state can’t decide if they’re afraid or entranced.

    “You’re not scared of us,” Sera coos, lounging across her chair, “You’re scared of being seen as small. You already are.”

One of the mages tries a containment ward—Soliera doesn’t move. Time hiccups. The mage falls unconscious, eyes bleeding, not harmed physically but stripped of divine purpose. The room grows silent. The council ends.

T421: Elemensinter, the Living Weave #CHAR-ELEMENSINTER #CHAR-SOLIERA #LOC-STONE-GARDEN #LORE-WEAVE

A mythic meeting occurs in a stone garden that blooms as Soliera approaches. The being known as Elemensinter— a shifting blend of magic, thread, and memory —emerges to offer insight into the layers beneath the world.

Sera twirls through the meeting, treating the ancient Weave-being like a pet. Ember is quiet, unnerved. Soliera doesn’t speak until Elemensinter bends in reverence. They share no words; the pact is felt.

Elemensinter agrees to guide the divine party to the surface entrance of Undermountain’s deeper leyline snarl.


T422: The Tavern, the Lid, and the Start #LOC-YAWNING-PORTAL #ENTRANCE-UNDERMOUNTAIN


Sera drops a reinforced trapdoor like it’s a pot lid, cracking the floor of a busy tavern. This is the disguised access point to Undermountain. Soliera, finishing her bread, stands.

The entire tavern falls into silence. Sera claps once, then points to the stunned crowd.

    “Let’s go.”

They descend—no ritual, no fanfare, just inevitability. The cover is broken, both literally and figuratively. The first divine expedition into Undermountain’s deeper floors begins.

Sera steps off the edge and takes the full drop, roughly 120 feet. She hits hard
enough to crater the ground, announces herself, and is already fanning dust and
making friends when the others arrive. Ember drifts down because she is curious,
not because Sera needs help. Soliera walks down through the air.

T423: The Encounter – Mira, Rena, and Alfia
[ #CHAR-MIRA #CHAR-RENA #CHAR-ALFIA #PARTY-MEETING ]


The party encounters a small trio of surviving adventurers in a hallway built into the outer lip of the dungeon's first floor. These were the same girls met before, however they naturally split as they entered the Tavern.

## ALFIA

Paladin-Cleric of Selûne | Human | Early 20s
Short white hair cropped close, dark brown skin, silver-grey plate that's been repaired enough times the scrollwork doesn't quite match itself anymore. A holy symbol on a chain at her hip — not around her neck, where it would be ceremonial. At her hip, where it's a tool.
She doesn't announce herself. She arrives and the room reorganizes around her without her doing anything. The kind of quiet that isn't absence — it's compression. Something held deliberately still.
She prays constantly and without theater. Not begging, not bargaining. Just open. A channel kept clear. Before Selûne's appearance at T427, whatever was on the other end of that channel was patient and distant. Alfia had learned to be okay with that.
She's young enough that her certainty still reads as faith rather than calcification. She hasn't been disappointed enough times to confuse stubbornness with conviction. That's still coming. For now she just believes, cleanly, the way people do before the world has had time to complicate it.
Watching Sera tell her to stand up — she's in my house, you're a guest too — cracked something open in Alfia that she won't be able to name for a while. She was prepared to prostrate herself before her goddess. She was not prepared to be told she deserved a seat.
Stays at level 8 after T428. Selûne's touch on her crown left something. She's still figuring out what.

## MIRA
[Arcanist/Mage | Human | Mid 20s | Softspoken | Ember likes her a lot |
Pale, blonde hair gone almost white, round copper-framed glasses. Oversized blue scholar's robe belted at the waist because otherwise she trips on it — she's been tripping on it for three years and hasn't hemmed it. Slim fingers. Always has a book open, always has a finger on the page, always looks like she just found the sentence she's been looking for.
She came to Undermountain to study its architecture. Not the monsters, not the treasure. The structure — the way Halaster's logic manifests spatially, how dungeon geometry reflects a fractured mind, what happens to stone when it's been dreamed on for two centuries. She has seventeen notebooks on the subject and opinions about load-bearing hallways that nobody asked for.
Ember found her in a side corridor doing measurements.
That's the whole story, really. Ember crouched next to her to look at what she was doing, and Mira — who flinches at loud noises and has a standing policy of not making eye contact with strangers — just showed her. Pointed at the wall. Explained the stress fracture pattern. Ember said oh in a way that meant she actually understood, and Mira looked at her over the top of her glasses and thought: there you are.
She's the only person in Undermountain who bonded with a divine entity by talking about masonry.
Stays at level 12 after T428. Has enough notes for a book. Probably writes it. It contains three footnotes that reference conversations with Ember without explaining who Ember is.


## RENA
[Rogue | Half-Elf | Late 20s | Worldweary | Wealthy | Extroverted]


Dark hair, dark leathers, pointed ears she doesn't bother hiding. The smile is the first thing and the last thing — easy, practiced, absolutely genuine. She's been running that smile since she was old enough to figure out it worked, and it still works, and she's never felt bad about that.
Half-elf means she's been translating herself her whole life. Too human for the elves, too elf for the humans, fluent in both and belonging to neither. She made peace with that by deciding belonging was overrated and information was better. She knows Undermountain's first fifteen levels the way a river knows its own banks — by running them repeatedly until the shape is just part of her.
She's charismatic the way weather is charismatic. It's not a performance. It's just what she is.
Sera caught her in approximately four seconds. Not the Velvet Spiral specifically — Rena's constitution is better than it looks — but the attention. Sera looked at her and actually saw her, catalogued her, found her interesting, and said so without making it weird. Rena has been charming people her whole life. She had no protocol for being charmed back by something that small and that certain.
The flirtation was real on both ends. Rena knew exactly what Sera was and flirted anyway. Sera respected that enormously.
Stays at level 10 after T428. Still runs the upper levels. Has since added a personal rule: if something makes her feel that seen, she doesn't run from it. She's working on what that means.


T424: The Chilling Room (Divine Antechamber)
[ #LOC-CHILLING-ROOM #INFRASTRUCTURE-OUTPOST #THEME-COMFORT ]


A copy of the Antechamber in the Sanctum, each outpost has to have a "Chilling Room"; which is just that. The place where Soliera and Sera chill and relax, even sleep and make decisions. **The Chilling Room is on Level 1, not deeper in the dungeon.** Kessla set this outpost up already because she knew the Gods would eventually come through; Undermountain is linked in some passages to the Xanathar guild -- which we've taken over.
They find a room already marked with the black bird symbol—a Sanctum outpost. Kessla apparently built it months ago, an advanced team laying groundwork.
 Sera names the chamber the Chilling Room, the divine lounge mandated at every embassy and forward base.

    “Big Room. Soft Bed. Optional cuddling. Mandatory snacks.”

The room is round, tiered, and soft: a throne lounge instead of a throne room. Soliera sits, not above but within, surrounded by her chosen.
Everyone realizes—this isn’t a momentary camp. It’s already theirs.
It's the soft center of what's otherwise a normal Sanctum camp, any Embassy or outpost under Sanctum control must always have a room ready for the Trio. Niv and Velin would be mortified if the girls wandered or teleported somewhere and were met with empty shelves.


T425: Divine Authority in Motion
[ #INFRASTRUCTURE-DECOR #LOGISTICS-SUPPLY ]


Sera spots a man with a dove-cloak—her symbol, imperfectly replicated. She calls him over, flippant and curious. He kneels.
    “Your embassy sanctioned this outpost three months ago, Lady Sera. We’ve been waiting.”
The soldiers stir. Orders ripple outward. Sera waves her hand like she’s stirring cake batter:
    “Grab like… eighty-nine people. Let’s make this whole floor pretty. Torches. Food. Cushions. Wedding vibes.”
No one questions it. The world is being reshaped, not through conquest—but presence.


T426: Mira and Ember
[ #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-MIRA #RELATIONSHIP-BOND #THEME-BELONGING ]


Mira and Ember are developing a weird sense of attraction and understanding of each other, they are the only two introverted personalities in the sea of Gods that are walking around.
Mira watches it unfold, whispering:
    “We’re not just visiting. We’re part of it now.”
Mira has been watching Ember's hair and her skin intently, the Weave itself seemed to be radiating FROM her, not gathering around her. The room was warm and full of pleasant smells. Sera was sprawled across Rena, who had reclined in one of the chairs.
Ember smiles, still unsure of herself, but stronger.
    “You always were. You just didn’t know it yet.”
Sera chimes in playfully, "Awhh, you're both so cute!" As she nuzzles against Rena. Soliera is unfazed, enjoying Her silence and relaxation amongst Her children.
Around the Chilling Room, Hush Agents and Stewards started work on building the place to Sera's specifications. They're accustomed to such demands. The difference, they know, between this and some tyrant is the opulence is for everyone that comes here. Sera just wants it nice for her to visit once a year, everything else is free for the public.


T427: Alfia's Prayer Begins
[ #CHAR-ALFIA #THEME-FAITH #FORESHADOWING-GODS #CHAR-SELUNE]

As the camp builds, Alfia kneels once more. Her prayer is silent. Not begging—just… focused. We don’t resolve what her god sees yet, but the Divine Gaze begins to stir. Something ancient watches. Soliera might one day answer on behalf of that god… or correct it.
She notices some pressure behind her eyes, building. She looks. A small white dot is floating in the corner of the room. Sera looks. Soliera looks.
The dot forms and expands, into a beautiful woman - Selûne, standing in her blue-white moonglow, was in the room. Sera waved, chirped at the Goddess with a pleasant "Hello," as Alfia struggled to her feet.
Selûne returned the wave, bowing slightly towards Soliera. Ember and the others are heading back into the room, Ember coming to stand beside Soliera. Sera, of course, approached Selûne directly.
Sera spoke aloud, "Alfie, this is your person? I can...tell, somehow, that she's linked to you. Hello, again, friend. What do you want?"
Selûne spoke softly, "I come to speak directly to the murderers of my Sister," Sera didn't respond. She looked blankly at her.
        Alfia has stood, now, and came and kneeled directly before Selûne, starting to prostate herself. Sera interjected, "None of that. Get up, Alfie. She's in my house. You're a guest too."
Alfia balked but listened, rising up. Rena and Mira are standing silently near the couches Sera was sitting on before Selûne appeared.
Alfia is worried this would end in conflict, as is Rena and Mira. Everyone knows what Sera is, and to directly come and mention that -
Sera spoke, "Okaaay, so, who's that? Who are you?"
Selûne continued and conveyed that she's actually not upset, the cycle will continue and Shar will return, and mentions Ao (which gets no response from Soliera), and that she is Selûne, Goddess of the Moon, and she's come with peace in mind. Soliera walks closer to her, speaks once.
QUOTE ANCHOR — SOLIERA: "You are different, she was too nihilistic. I have no intention of fighting or controlling any of your gods."
Soliera then stopped speaking, and Sera was clapping and being overjoyed. She loves it when Soliera speaks. A few moments pass, Selûne dips her head slightly and actually touches Alfia on the crown of her head before fading out of the plane. Alfia looked whiter than her dark complexion should allow, pasty almost. Her breathing was quick. Reverent. She has seen her Goddess in person, a rare honor - and saw her Goddess defer to the Beings that are currently laying across the room with her.


T428: The Party Clears the Dungeon
[ #COMBAT-CLEAR #PARTY-SPLIT #LOC-UNDERMOUNTAIN-LEVEL-15 #CHAR-ALFIA #CHAR-HALASTER ]


After a few days of traveling, Sera has easily cleared the Dungeon. Alfia, Mira and Rena stay behind on higher levels after level 15.
Sera enjoys the trip, traveling though the areas easily. It wasn't long before Halaster learned they were there, attempting to scry as they entered level 16, but Sera looked at him somehow, directly, and he thought better of spying on Soliera. Halaster has never seen anyone or anything recognize an external spell without any pre-made countermeasures, he worked on revising his plans in real time.
      Rena is back in Level 1, in the Chilling Room, along with Mira and Alfia. The Sanctum is already working on incorporating them into whatever system they would want to be a part of, especially after being marked with journeying down 15 levels and 2 rests with Sera and Ember.


T429: They Find Halaster and the Mergeverse Portal
[ #CHAR-HALASTER #LOC-LEVEL-20 #EVENT-PORTAL-OPENING #VERSE-TRANSITION ]


At level 20 they find a room of portals, Halaster talks to them, is excited. Ember wanders the room, touches various things. Sera is excited to see Soliera and Ember having a good time. Soliera walks into one of the rooms on Her own, which gets Halaster to quickly follow behind in excitement.
He shows them his newest and greatest idea, Soliera powers it; the Girls walk into the Mergeverse (Primeverse). Soliera has somehow directly fixed his portals and it's not only stable, but it's so flatlined nominal in all his readings and ruminations that it shouldn't be possible.
Halaster stands there in awe, his work completed for him passively, all the wards and experiments and even the quality of the air itself were massively upgraded simply from the passage of the girls. Not long after that, Halaster got a visit from Velin and Niv, whom came bearing constructs and good will.
A base of operations was established on the level with the portal, so the Sanctum could easily track their Gods. Halaster had no objections and understood the need to keep an eye on Beings such as those, "Especially the pretty pale one," he was caught explaining later, "Something about her was just... too perfect. She was too much. I never got a single read from her using any magic or even being on the same...ah, well, whatever." He trailed off, moving on to something else, as the Mad Mage was known to do.
