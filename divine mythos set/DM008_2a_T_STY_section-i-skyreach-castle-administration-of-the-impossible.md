### SECTION I — SKYREACH CASTLE: ADMINISTRATION OF THE IMPOSSIBLE (T161-T169)


T161: Skyreach Approach #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #LOC-SKYREACH
— Skyreach Castle rotates in the clouds above the landscape like a problem someone else was supposed to solve. It is stone and ice and giant engineering and cult logistics stacked into a flying fortress, and it is genuinely impressive if you are a mortal looking at it from the ground. Sera, Soliera, and Drizzt plan the approach from a ridge with unobstructed sightlines. The planning is surgical — not because the castle is dangerous to them, but because a sloppy entry means unnecessary dead who didn't need to die, and collateral damage to infrastructure that will eventually belong to someone the Sanctum has to deal with. Drizzt contributes real tactical analysis. This is one of the beats where having him present is functional rather than ceremonial; he knows fortification patterns from centuries of exactly this kind of problem and the party uses what he knows.

T162: Skyreach Infiltration & Sabotage #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #LOC-SKYREACH-LOWER-HALLS
— Constructs and Hush Agents enter through the lower levels first, moving through the castle's underbelly with the practiced quiet of a system that was designed for exactly this. Cultists stationed in the access corridors are ended before they can register what they're looking at — not combat, not execution as spectacle, just the sound of someone else's plan meeting an obstacle it was not built for. Sera moves through the upper halls at a pace that the constructs can't quite keep up with. The castle's mortal garrison barely has time to understand that the fortress has already been taken before it is. Drizzt works the mid-levels with a clarity of purpose that has nothing to do with heroism — he is removing threats efficiently, and the distinction between that and what Sera is doing is only a question of scale.

T163: Showdown with Rezmir #CHAR-REZMIR #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-DRIZZT #CHAR-RED-WIZARDS #LOC-SKYREACH-THRONE #ITEM-BLACK-DRAGON-MASK
— Rezmir, the black half-dragon Wyrmspeaker, makes her last stand in the castle's uppermost chamber with two Red Wizards flanking her — Thayan arcanists who have survived long enough to believe their survival means something. It does not. Drizzt walks in first and offers the choice plainly: face Sera, or face themselves. The Red Wizards try to calculate their odds and cannot. Rezmir snarls something about power and legacy that no one in the room finds credible given the present circumstances. They all beg for mercy in the end. Sera ends them cleanly, which is itself a mercy, and the Black Dragon Mask is recovered from what remains.

T164: Red Wizards Erased #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-RED-WIZARDS #LOC-SKYREACH
— The two Thayan arcanists die in the specific way of people who spent too long believing that accumulated arcane knowledge constituted leverage. A head flick. Evaporated. Their essence does not persist in any form the Weave can hold. All arcane loot — spell components, research, the systematic documentation of the cult's magical infrastructure — is claimed and catalogued for Maccath to sort through later. The room is very quiet after. The castle, now stripped of its command structure, begins the process of belonging to whoever comes next.

T165: Cloudgazer (The White Dragon Ally) #CHAR-CLOUDGAZER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-BLAGOTHKUS #LOC-SKYREACH
— Cloudgazer is the white dragon who calls Skyreach home — not in the sense of owning it, but in the sense that he has lived here long enough to believe he is a fixture of it. He was Cloudchaser in the module record, but Sera renamed him on a whim — chasing implies work, and work is for everyone else. Here he is Cloudgazer, and the distinction matters because the dragon who submits to Soliera is not the same animal the written module describes. He comes out when he has to — when Soliera's presence makes the physics of the castle feel wrong in ways he cannot articulate — and Sera is already waiting on the parapet at the angle where the wind is loudest. He lands. She looks at him. That is the whole of the negotiation's opening. He submits. Blagothkus, the cloud giant who operated the castle's mechanisms, is deferred and neutralized — he is not an ally, not an enemy, not an obstacle worth dwelling on. Cloudgazer is now an uneasy ally of the Sanctum. He will be uneasy about this for a long time. He does not leave. He takes the younger dragons recruited at the Mere of Deadmen and the Convoy Ambush under his wings, even though they're different races.

T166: Dragon Allies and Prisoners #CHAR-CLOUDGAZER #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-MACCATH #LOC-SKYREACH
— Below the main levels, in a section of the castle that Rezmir used as holding space, the party finds a young black dragon — not wild, not fully cult-broken, but not free either. The abuse is legible in how it moves, the way it holds its wings close, the way it tracks Sera's approach without attacking. Sera recognizes the pattern from the blue dragon on the Greenfields road. The dragon is offered the Sanctum the same way any survivor is offered the Sanctum: not conscripted, not commanded, just pointed at a direction and told it is safe. It goes. Maccath, present now as the castle's ad hoc arcane expert, begins deciphering the fortress's remaining magical controls and marking what's useful for handover.

T167: Great White Death Looms #CHAR-ARAUTHATOR #CHAR-MACCATH #LOC-SEA-OF-MOVING-ICE
— Two threads arrive simultaneously: the first is Maccath herself, who has been held prisoner by Arauthator in his lair somewhere on the Sea of Moving Ice. She is not here yet — this is intelligence about her location. The second is the dragon's continued activity in the North: Arauthator, known in the old accounts as the Great White Death, has been a geographic constant in the region for long enough that the Ice Hunter tribes have structured their entire existence around not drawing his attention. With Skyreach secured, the North is the next vector. The party is already turning in that direction before a formal decision is made.

T168: Skyreach Turned Over #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-NIV-SAIFFAR #LOC-SKYREACH #ITEM-THE-ARK #ITEM-FROSTSKIMMR
— Soliera gives Skyreach Castle to the mortals who have the most structural claim to the region it protects. This is not generosity in the conventional sense — it is infrastructure allocation. The Sanctum does not need a flying fortress; the Sanctum is already more than that. What the northern communities need is a defensible position and a symbol of permanence, and a cloud castle that isn't being used as a cult staging ground fits that function better than anything else currently available. Niv-Saiffar handles the entire handover from a distance: the paperwork, the provisional governance structure, the construct detail left behind to train the incoming stewards. Mortals arrive at the castle and begin the process of making it theirs. They do this carefully, because the walls still remember what was in them. The Frostskimmr and the Ark are already being prepared for the northern voyage.

T169: Supplies Returned, Festival Held #LOC-SKYREACH
— Everything the cult took is returned to the towns and villages it came from — coin, livestock, stolen materials, seized food stores, cultural artifacts that the cult had repurposed as offering props. The constructs handle distribution with logistical precision and absolutely no ceremony, which is why the celebration that erupts around them is so unplanned and therefore so genuine. People are getting back things they had stopped expecting to see again. The story of the "hush" — the silent invasion of a flying fortress that took less time than a town meeting — is already being told in forms that will outlast everyone in the room. Bards are getting details wrong by the third tavern north. None of the inaccuracies make the story smaller.
