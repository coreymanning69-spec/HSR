# DORAN

## Identity

- **Canonical spelling:** Doran
- **Age / gender:** ~21, male
- **Ancestry:** Human; no variant-human biography is implied
- **Class:** Battle Master Fighter 20 (pure; launched TR3-SKT at 13; historical level-15 block retained below)
- **Tier:** 3, full Sanctum field Steward from T537.0
- **Starting XP:** 120,000
- **Background:** Exact background label and intake history unspecified
- **First impression:** Impressive. Korin chose a recruit who looks immediately
  capable of the work.
- **Selection read:** Korin's chosen recruit. The Lone Ranger lineage is visible
  in his pride, forward pressure, and belief that one exceptional operator
  should be able to clear the room.
- **Training lineage:** Korin's forward competence without remaining Korin's
  Lone Ranger.
- **Failure mode:** Propriety as armor. His sex drive runs high inside a
  matriarchal environment full of powerful, attractive, sometimes aggressive
  women, and he manages the pressure by becoming too formal, too polite, and
  emotionally absent.
- **Arc direction:** Solo clearer to member of a pair; brick-head formality to
  actual presence with the women around him. *(spine shorthand: reverence → neighbor — DM002_3 / DM038_L)*
- **Moral reflex:** Immediate help. Sanctum upbringing makes assistance the
  default response rather than a debated heroic choice.
- **Competence read:** He is a thinker and tactician, and in an ordinary mortal
  room he is normally the strongest man present. Neither fact cancels the other.

## Founding-History Boundary

**Confirmed T550.2 continuity.** Korin is alive, semi-retired into teaching, and
leads the Ranger Corps. He is eight days north with a class of thirty and returns
in six. Letha keeps forty of his carvings; Doran has four, beginning with a bad
bear. Doran has called Letha *ma'am* for six years.

DM005_0 establishes the T12 First Exodus and First Village population movement,
but no local source establishes Doran's household, parents, or exact intake
scene. Those details remain unspecified; his age is ~21. His Sanctum upbringing,
Korin training, selection, characterization, and active service are the locked
history available for play.

## Appearance

Doran is 6'4" and built near barbarian-scale: peak-human density, strength, and
maintenance from seven to eight formative years on Sanctum food, clean water,
and constant training. His hair is professionally cut. The first read is not
merely large or dangerous; it is *impressive*: Korin's chosen recruit arriving
with the body, maintenance, and bearing of a professional soldier or mercenary.

In Sera and Ember's full kit he stands exactly seven feet because of the
raised, slab-metal feet and helm spacer. When he sets his weight deliberately, the ground packs
under him. The plate is mirror-white, not luminous: it emits no light of its own
and returns incident light at very high efficiency. The moment lands as wonder
before it lands as combat utility.

Around the girls his posture becomes more formal than the situation requires.
The visible sign that he is beginning to thaw is not disrespect. It is that he
stops presenting every sentence and movement for inspection.

## Voice

Professional soldier/mercenary cadence: very serious, clipped, and terse.
Doran answers directly, uses fewer words than Wren, and defaults to practical
report language. Around the girls he overcorrects into titles, courtesy, and
painfully proper constructions. The stiffness is not lack of desire; it is how
he contains it.

### Canon Voice Exchange

> **Doran:** "You are much more beautiful in person!"
>
> **Sera:** "Duhhh. Probably only better in your dreams! Now c'mon, we've got
> shit to do."

His earnest stiffness bouncing off Sera's bright indifference is the register:
he means the compliment with complete seriousness; she accepts the obvious fact
and moves the scene forward.

## Operational Doctrine

Under pressure Doran pushes forward. His daggers make armor or bone negligible
to his normal decision process; he is fast, decisive, and accustomed to rooms
where nothing more is required of him than entering and winning. He is proud of
that competence and of his ability to clear a room alone.

That pride is pure Korin lineage and the central pair-pressure. Doran's field
test is not whether he can win by himself. It is whether he can remain present
long enough to need Wren, read her call, hand off cleanly, and become one half of
a pair without treating cooperation as a reduction in competence.

His strength context is unusual before the giants ever arrive: he has sparred
his whole life against Korin-tier force, including grip and lift power that most
mortals cannot parse. Giants and gods do not shock him by being strong. The
untested variable is the solo kill without a party, without friends, and without
the girls in the line. That is the crucible.

Against human-scale foes, the dagger-forward combat lock rules. Against
giant-tier bodies, the daggers become thrown pressure and close-finisher tools;
the six-foot greatsword is the main instrument. The lock is not broken. The
target is simply too large for a twelve-inch blade to be the whole answer.

## Social and Off-Duty Read

Doran treats Wren with respect and admiration. Sanctum-raised men default
courteous, and with her he recognizes real competence rather than a person who
needs managing. Around Soliera, Sera, Ember, and the senior women of the Sanctum,
that courtesy becomes excessive: too formal, too polite, a brick-head hiding
inside correct behavior until he thaws.

Off duty he drinks, likes bars, and likes parties. The social appetite is real
even when his field presentation makes him look carved from regulations.

He is permanently on-duty by reflex: he counts exits before his eyes fully open
and almost never lets himself exhale unless the scene has clearly released him.
The T522 stick figure and smiley face on his knee plate matter because of that formality. The man who keeps
presenting himself for inspection finds a stick figure on his armor drawn by a
goddess who cannot write, and the affection lands harder than any blessing.
That is the first soft crack in the thaw.

His desire has no clean automatic landing place. Pell or Letha may become
earnable threads; Velin is a more remote possibility if he genuinely earns it.
None is promised. Ember is not interested and must never be sexualized; Sera
wants women; Soliera is a goddess rather than an available resolution. The
pressure is Doran's to manage, not a claim the women owe him help satisfying.

T522.10 adds a public-facing pressure: on the Trade Way and at Waterdeep's South
Gate, Doran's white armor is read before he speaks. The kit is a banner as much
as protection, and the next leg asks whether he can carry Sanctum visibility
without turning himself into ceremony.

## Costume Inversion and Staged Reveal

Nothing Doran wears is his. His current kit routes to Soliera's hand: artifact
daggers plus her personal remake of the earlier Sera/Ember plate, shield, helm,
and Cleaver. Doctrine still comes from Sera and blessings from the Sanctum.
The only thing on his sheet he owns is what Korin put in his head—a skill, not
an object. The vind rune traced by his own hand is the first thing he makes.

The costume becomes deliberate misdirection once light reaches it. Seven feet
of mirror-white plate returns incident light hard enough to draw the room's aim.
In unlit darkness it emits nothing and gives away no position. Whether Sera
designed the bait function or dressed him as herself and got lucky is not a
live hook. The visible livery works as intended; no private intent or later
reveal is owed.

Against giants, ten inches of dagger edge cannot reach anything vital through a
limb. A future disarm reveal may therefore take the Cleaver away and let an
enemy's accurate taunt expose the borrowed-weapon problem. Doran's answer is
**Read the Seam**: Insight +16, the highest number on either field-Steward sheet and
the weapon Korin actually gave him. Preferred staging is silence while the
read lands during the enemy's gloat. Dagger access itself is already seamless.

The Stewards' blessings grew over time without a ruler-by-ruler accounting.
Helm attribution and white-plate intent are settled-functional history. None are
build blockers, active hooks, or promised later reveals.

The Cleaver's final profile is target-agnostic: ordinary swings use +16 and
fixed 40 damage (+14 and 5-foot reach when choked), while Grand Cleave uses +19
for 120 + 4d20 across a 15-foot, 180° arc. In human-scale construction the
tight-space penalty remains, but struck
walls, fixtures, and structure take the listed damage. The room pays. Current
STR is 27; STR 28 is reserved for a future growth beat.

## T522 Loadout - Doran

Two gifts arrived in two registers. At night, Soliera gave the daggers and fixed
the actual failure from the Dripping Caves. In the morning, Sera and Ember gave
the armor, shield, and greatsword: beautiful, absurd, affectionate things that
are finished before Doran is ready to use them.

### Soliera's Daggers

- Matched black obsidian, divine-made, artifact-level, and the apex of his kit.
  They are his soul and fighting identity.
- Summon, dismiss, and return instantly, including mid-motion; no action,
  draw, drop, stow, or hand-transition cost is required.
- Find the weakness / no-no-sell means damage-type resistance never reduces
  their 1d8+9 line. It does not double damage.
- Find the seam and measured edge remain part of the dagger lock: he reads the
  cut and chooses the depth, including lethal or nonlethal, without penalty.
- The Dripping Caves humiliation cannot recur because the weapon failure was
  the root problem Soliera chose to fix.

### Sera and Ember's Armor

- Magical white half-plate, medium armor, white-and-dark Sanctum livery.
- Dark bird sigil on front and back; the kit is a walking banner of Sanctum
  office before Doran speaks.
- Thick and cumbersome: chainmail-and-leather construction with dragon-leather
  or equivalent joints keeps it functional rather than rigid, but it is not an
  agile suit. While worn, it cancels Mobile's +10-foot speed increase and
  imposes disadvantage on Acrobatics and checks that depend on bodily flexibility.
- Shoulders are articulated with connective leather; not bare, not spiked
  parade armor.
- Opulent and affectionate. Sera and Ember scribbled doodles into it while
  designing it. The knee has a little stick figure; Ember says there is a
  second stick figure on "the other side," location still unrevealed.
- Ember picked the white-and-dark colors.
- This is Soliera's mirror-white divine plate at T537.0, not mithral. It transmits
  one-third piercing, one-half slashing as bludgeoning, and all bludgeoning; muscle
  density absorbs one quarter of each transmitted amount. Net: one-quarter piercing,
  three-eighths slashing, three-quarters bludgeoning.
- Armor, shield, Cleaver, and daggers are undamageable. Doran is not.
- The kit imposes no sound-derived Stealth penalty: it does not ring, scrape, or
  rasp. Its mirror-white surface emits no light of its own, so it gives away no
  position in an unlit tunnel. Incident light returns from it at very high
  efficiency; under illumination the bright-white silhouette is conspicuous.
  Doran may hide using darkness, cover, concealment, terrain, or a broken sightline.
- The plate converts incoming cleaves and boulder-scale force into survivable
  injury; it does not make Doran immune. PLANTED is an active hard-anchor stance
  on a surface that can accept the boot ridges: AC 26, speed 0, and
  displacement-based forced movement or prone fails. Prone effects that do not
  depend on displacement still resolve normally.

### Sera's Shield

- Gauntlet-integrated, forearm-buckled kite/body shield, roughly 3 feet wide
  and 4 to 5 feet long.
- Always on the forearm; the hand under it stays free.
- The shield bonus does not spend a hand, which is the load-bearing magic-item
  perk.
- Quick-release deployment turns it into a freestanding 4-by-3-foot barrier.
- The barrier grants three-quarters cover to one crouching Medium creature and
  half cover to one standing Medium creature.
- At first it gets in his own way. Learning to move with it is part of the arc.
- Dropping the kite for Wren and walking forward is Doran's characteristic
  answer when she needs fixed cover. The beat is available whenever the scene
  earns it; no exact encounter or timing is prewritten. Once deployed, Doran
  loses the shield's full +4 AC contribution until he recovers and rebuckles it.

### Giant Cleaver

- Mithral +2 greatsword, about six feet long and taller than Ember. Its
  208-pound weight is deliberate: Ember compressed/densified a much larger mass
  of the same mithral used for the armor until the weapon was "heavy enough."
  It is not an ordinary-mithral weight calculation.
- Canon name: Giant Cleaver.
- Reach 10 ft. normally, 5 ft. when choked by human-scale construction.
- Ordinary damage is fixed 40; a critical hit is 80.
- Never dulls and needs no whetstone, nick repair, or maintenance.
- Too large for a normal scabbard: stabbed into ground or floor at rest,
  back-carried with a side-release draw and free two-handed shift.
- At issue, Doran could barely lift it; T524 preserves that initial non-live
  state. At the current edge it is fully live with one or two hands at the
  profile in DM041_A.
- Sera's T532 training already established the STR 25 body and one-handed use.
- Ordinary Cleaver-to-dagger transitions are seamless: Doran can keep the
  Cleaver in one hand and summon a dagger into the other.
- Grand Cleave is the deliberate exception. It commits both hands and the whole
  turn, trading movement and bonus action for the formation/carry-through lane;
  Doran's reaction remains available.

Standing table rulings route to DM041_A: the Cleaver uses its full listed
profile with one or two hands; only
human-scale construction and tight ground impose −2 to hit and reduce reach to
5 feet; giant architecture, forge halls, caverns, and open ground do not.
Full armor/boot/helm spacing makes Doran exactly **7'0"** in kit.
Soliera's T537-remade matched helm grants normal sight through magical and
nonmagical darkness to 120 feet and supplies a breathable atmosphere while worn,
including underwater and volcanic environments. It is always on, requires no
attunement, and is not truesight; Doran still requires oxygen and can suffocate
if the helm is absent or its breathing support is unavailable. DM041_A owns the
exact standing rule. Its provenance is settled-functional history and creates no
active inquiry.

### Crucible and Livery

The crucible is the whole set, not only the sword. The daggers are ready because
they are his soul. The rest is perfect and mostly unusable at first: armor too
much, shield in his own way, greatsword barely liftable. His first honest
thought is how he is supposed to do this. That climb is long and suits SKT.

The white-and-dark, the bird sigil, and the absurd opulence are identity
infrastructure. When the field Stewards enter a town, the gear announces Sanctum
before they speak and forces the witness economy to recalibrate.

## Kit and Pair Geometry

The tank silhouette is deliberate misdirection. Seven feet of white plate,
shield, and Cleaver make enemies choose the obvious target. Doran's real method
is the man inside the costume reading the room and holding two knives. The
armor buys him thinking time; it does not turn him into a passive wall.

Every major gift leaves a partner-shaped opening. Ordinary Cleaver/dagger play
is seamless, but Grand Cleave deliberately turns off Doran's movement, bonus
action, and dagger-reaction lanes for one high-output swing. The kite is most
useful when it stands somewhere protecting another person. The mirror-white
plate gives away nothing in unlit darkness, but under illumination it turns
Doran into the obvious reflected-light target. Wren can deliberately create
either field: *darkness* for a helm-sighted screen or *daylight* for RULE-GLARE.
These are pair pressures, not defects to patch away.

The undamageable kit also removes ordinary repair and armorer scenes. That is a
social cost: people who might have participated through hospitality, mending,
or craft cannot help him that way. Use that absence as character texture, never
as proof that ordinary people have nothing else to offer.

Against giants, the plate is shaped protection rather than blanket immunity.
Frost-giant slashing is its easiest matchup; fire riders, cloud-giant
bludgeoning, stone boulders, and hill-giant clubs remain fully consequential.
The matchup table and exact damage rules live in DM041_A.
