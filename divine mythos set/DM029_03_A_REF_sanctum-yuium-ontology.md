# DM029_03 — SANCTUM & YUIUM (REFERENCE)

## HARD CANON NOTES

1. Authoritative behavioral and structural reference for the Sanctum and Yuium (its living city-organism).
2. SCALPEL mode — no new canon.
3. Sanctum is NOT a prison or golden cage — you come to it, it doesn't go to you. Residents may leave (RULE-SANCTUM-NOT-PRISON).
4. Sanctum expands physically with each new divine experience or community alignment — every expansion builds on all prior ones, never isolated.
5. Predation or malicious violence within a Sanctum-claimed zone → immediate, overwhelming response. Predation results in erasure.
6. Soliera's power is infinite (no power cap). Sera's casual commands reshape Sanctum infrastructure.
7. No cross-verse.


## YUIUM — THE SPEAKABLE NAME AND DISTRIBUTED WHOLE (ontology)

YUIUM is the speakable Name of the Whole. It is not an all-vowel inversion and does not
depend on being unpronounceable. The primary split produced two continuing halves:

- **Soliera goes and experiences.** She explores, examines, and expands through current,
  local awareness rather than omniscience.
- **Deashi stays and ponders.** He is YUIUM's main vessel and carries the complete atemporal
  field of everything the Veiled Eye has enrolled.

No separate post-split YUIUM person remains dormant, observing, or waiting to awaken beside
them. YUIUM continues distributively through both halves and the Sanctum/Antechamber
substrate. Sera and Ember are one recursive iteration down — the experiences of those
experiences, response and inquiry made manifest. Infinite sets may nest recursively, but no
additional named rung exists without played canon.

The literal Name positions remain Deashi/Yod/Father, Soliera/first-Heh/Mother,
Sera/Vav/Son, and Ember/final-Heh/Daughter/Malkuth/Metatron. These are structural offices,
not bodily gender or ordinary household kinship. Full architecture: DM042_0.

## THE ANTECHAMBER — SLEEPING HOUSE AND THRONE ROOM

The **Antechamber** is the official name of the inner chamber where the Girls sleep and
where Deashi sits on his throne beside Soliera's empty throne. It is not a separate
post-split YUIUM person or an additional divine rung. It is the living threshold of the
distributed Whole: chamber, substrate, sleeping house, and throne-room in one.

Niv-Saiffar has passively absorbed the Antechamber's ambient energy over time. That
absorption is one source of his canon demigod status, alongside his folk status and his
honest demonstrated abilities. The Antechamber empowers and informs the Sanctum's chosen
operational sovereign; it does not transfer Deashi's office, Soliera's sovereignty, or the
Girls' identities to Niv.
