# STORM KING'S THUNDER — ARC DOCTRINE

## Governing Thesis

Iymrith created a criminal succession crisis: Neri's murder and Hekaton's
removal emptied the ordning's apex and let five giant-lord projects flourish.
The ordning remains legitimate architecture. Returning Hekaton and removing the
manipulation restores it; the Sanctum does not replace giant sovereignty.

Giants are the category being saved. Leadership is sorted, reorganized, allied,
or bound according to its actual choices. Mortal victims define whether a local
resolution succeeds. Divine capacity changes outcomes without turning NPCs,
travel, investigation, or court procedure into disposable scenery.
