arc_summary: >
  The trio sits inside the tavern. Sera and Ember discover that mortal
  food has flavors. Amara Windbreaker holds her stool at their table —
  awe and stubbornness in equal measure. The town's klaxon screams;
  Soliera wills it dead and the brass melts on its post. Outside, the
  Titan still kneels at the gate. Then Clipboard Man arrives on behalf
  of the Company — the first institutional contact Mergeverse will
  make with the Tetrad. The standoff at the tavern threshold sets the
  template for every subsequent Company encounter.
