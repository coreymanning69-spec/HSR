# DM038_N1 — Storm King's Thunder — Played Narrative (T525–T532)

for played turns from T525 through T532. Before it, SKT's played turns lived only in the
state ledger rather than a story log. Three logical layers run in parallel:

## T525 — WATERDEEP: ROARINGHORN

*Played 2026-07-10. Encoded from the session handoff; two dictation recordings were
lost in-session and Corey authorized extrapolation from partial inputs — that
agreement is already reflected here and is not a corruption flag. Dialogue is not
reproduced; events are the record.*

### T525.1 — Blackstaff Morning Calibration

The morning after the T524 rest arrives without ceremony. Everyone wakes whole:
Doran at full fighting state under Ember's healing, Wren with her ward and slots
recovered, Sirac still safe in a guest room because the safest building in Waterdeep
said so and meant it. The day's plan settles over breakfast at Blackstaff: work the
Roaringhorn lead first — the North Ward thread Wren surfaced at T524 — before the
Svardborg pressure forces the pace. Doctrine holds unchanged. Doran and Wren walk in
front, the talk and the light fighting are theirs; Soliera, Sera, and Ember come
behind as the floor under the exam, not the answer.

### T525.2 — High House of Roaringhorn, North Ward

The High House of Roaringhorn receives them. Its lord, **Zelraun Roaringhorn** —
archmage, and quietly a Harper — proves to be exactly the kind of contact the lead
promised. He gives the recruits two things: intelligence, and gifts.

The gifts were rolled live at the table from Magic Item Table B and stand as rolled:
a **scroll of Sending**, carried by **Wren**, and a **cloak of the manta ray**, carried
by **Doran**. The intelligence points the party deeper into the Harper
network — to Ulbrinter Villa.

### T525.3 — Departure for Ulbrinter Villa

Zelraun routes them onward through the Harper network. T525 closes on the decision
to go to Ulbrinter Villa; Remallia's reception and briefing belong to T526.

### T525.5 — Sera walks into it
Sera came back from something and found Kessla and Marrik in the middle of a logistics argument. She walked into it, sat on the edge of a table, and listened for ten minutes. Then she sided with Kessla. Marrik said *of course she did.* Sera said *he's right, I didn't understand the question.* She sided with Marrik. They finished the argument without her, which was probably the point.


## T526 — ULBRINTER VILLA: REMALLIA HAVENTREE

The recruits carried the Cimber problem to Remallia Haventree at Ulbrinter Villa,
and the reception was warm iron — an ally, never an adversary. Remallia delivered
the full intelligence picture and reframed the whole affair. The Ring of Winter is
not a weapon but a doom in search of hands: an awake, ancient will that wants
patiently to bury the world under ice, keeping its bearer young only so the argument
never ends. The blod stone tracks the nearest living Cimber bloodline rather than a
place, which is why hiding or fleeing Sirac is useless — only destroying or
redirecting the stone itself resolves him, and every relative down the line after
him.

She named Nilraun Dhaerlost, a Zhentarim mage entangled in the blod stone and the
giants' scheme, and made plain that neither assassination nor teleportation solves
the problem; the solution space narrows to the disposition of the stone. She handed
over a dossier on Nilraun. Above all she gave the counsel that becomes the arc's
live friction system: at the girls' altitude, no one will ever tell them no —
relationship is the only gate that operates, so words come before anything.
The quest was paid for in advance: the Sanctum does not need to earn the right to
help, only decide how to arrive without making consent meaningless. Remallia still
keeps Artus's chair; the Harper connection is real and personal.


## T527 — THE HANDOFF AND THE JUMP

Acting on Remallia's counsel, the recruits prepared to approach Artus with courtesy
rather than force. Wren received a **silver Harper band** and the exact recognition
phrase *“Remallia Haventree still keeps his chair.”* The band and phrase would prove
that the visit came from a friend.

The scroll of Sending became a talking **BIG-SCROLL-in-a-bottle** addressed to Artus:
plain, gentle notice that they were happy to see him and would be visiting shortly,
so that a man who had spent thirty years unfindable would not be ambushed by his own
solution. Soliera performed the mortal courtesy of raising one hand for the send and
lowering it when the message was away. The reaction rolls stand exactly as played:
**Doran natural 1**, scalding his mouth and spilling the Nilraun dossier; **Wren 7**;
**Remallia 11**.

Then Soliera delete-and-remade the party to Chult — the teleport that is not death
because there is no stop, its courtesy gestures performed entirely for the mortals'
comfort. The five landed in the jungle a few hundred feet from Artus Cimber,
contact not yet made, in a region where the Sanctum's story arrived through
remote and specialized versions rather than Waterdeep-level familiarity.


## T528 — CHULT: FIRST CONTACT

The approach through the green was mortal work: Chult's heat as a wall, Doran
cooking in white plate — his third natural 1 of the arc, routed through
body-discipline, not physiology. A heat collapse gave Ember her opening, and her fix
became the first divine act Artus and Dragonbait ever witnessed. It established the
Literal Removal Rule: the girls remove a concept's personal effect on the people
near them — heat, cold, thirst — without touching the ambient world. The jungle
stays a furnace; the suffering simply stops. Sera introduced the three of them
clumsily and honestly, and the party walked in.

The camp reorganized around who had arrived. Dragonbait fed everyone — theology,
for him — and set out the one good chair for Soliera, bowing over it, making her the
head of the table by the simple fact of her sitting. Ember began learning the
saurial's scent-language grammar, filing declensions like a delighted student.

Over firelit fish, the recruits delivered the truth: Sirac is alive, safe, and
warded at Blackstaff, an honest acolyte of Torm who kept the plain iron ring Artus
left him. The blod stone must stop existing so blood goes back to being blood instead
of a map; nobody at the fire wants the Ring of Winter; and finding Artus was only
ever the courtesy stop — Remallia's words-first — with a standing offer to finish
the stone and leave him a rumor if that was his wish. The Nilraun dossier revealed
Cimber blood archived in Black Network vaults for decades, a Zhentarim scheme to let
the giants hunt and then steal the ring off the winner, and the political wrinkle
that the Zhentarim are Sanctum-blessed — meaning either Nilraun is a rogue cell or
the leash has rot. The ring answered Artus's grief involuntarily, frost webbing the
dossier. He asked what his son was like, and then turned to Soliera directly for the
first time all evening: *why does the Sanctum care what happens to one fisherman's
son in Icewind Dale?*

The answer came through the recruits, so Soliera never had to speak. The Sanctum
does not sort people into worth; caring is the same at every size, and Sirac's only
qualification was being in danger. The errand fell to Doran and Wren because it is
a person's job, not a god's — point a god at Svardborg and there is no Svardborg by
supper and no lesson in it, no world left where a man learns to hold his own wall.
Nothing is owed and nothing is wanted; the stone dies whether or not Artus was ever
found.

Over an unwinding hour — Wren out of her Sanctum robes, Doran out of his plate and
startled to find the worry about dirt had quietly left him days ago (the Sanctum
floor: you can fall, you can't fall out), drink and juice and food that tasted
perfect because it was being noticed — the plan took shape. Sera's blunt option
(kill the giant, home by dinner) met Artus's counter that killing at the door only
teaches that a bigger fist exists, while the *arrangement* working is the lesson
that keeps. Then Ember offered the thing no one in a hundred and thirty years had
proposed: rework the Ring, then show the giants it is different — no one dies.
Dragonbait's scent turned to hope. Artus, bracing loosed at last, said yes: let her
try, show Storvald a dead end, and — quietly — *I want to see my son.*

Morning, Ember entered the Ring itself: an all-white interior plane with the
awake artifact present as a will rather than an object. She exposed its Auril
origin and reassigned its purpose. The Ring of Winter no longer exists to bury
the world in endless cold; it serves as protector of the Spine of the World and
the Sanctum range. Artus accepted the change. His sequence held: settle the
giant problem first, then return for Sirac and Remallia. Sera's argument fixed
that order.

## T529 — SVARDBORG: THE SECOND VISIT

The party arrived openly at Svardborg's front gates in late afternoon. Hraldir,
a frost giant sentry, swung at Sera and slurred her. Sera caught the whale-bone
club; her fingers embedded in it, and a flick vaporized him in a thunderclap,
ice shear, and conical splatter before the club traveled roughly three hundred
feet. Berg disarmed himself. Drufi arrived and publicly enforced the stand-down
she had already promised. Ember held the laughing second sentry in suspended
judgment. Soliera looked at Storvald once and yielded the floor to the recruits.

Wren and Doran led the parley. They established Sanctum jurisdiction before
terms: the Spine was claimed, Auril had already been subordinated, and the Ring
had already been reassigned. They offered food, training, protection, and
partnership. Their insistence that they were "just recruits" made the Sanctum's
ladder legible. Storvald granted guest-right, and Niv arrived in dragon form
before shifting human to complete the landowner-and-tenant framing.

The rest of the day became treaty work and mingling rather than an assault.
Sera joined strength tests; the frost giants learned that the twenty-mile ice
channel was hers. Krigvind's compliment, the giant questions, the feast, and
Storvald's thanks made the new arrangement public. He thanked the girls for
giving Auril new purpose and for leaving his people alive. Ember proposed
construct labor and a merger of groups. Svardborg entered the Sanctum banner
with its jarl retained and Niv remaining to finalize terms.

## T530 — BLACKSTAFF: FATHER AND SON

The full group returned to Blackstaff Tower with Artus and Dragonbait; Doran
and Wren arrived with them rather than waiting upstairs. The mixed
Blackstaff–Sanctum household received the return. Artus and Sirac completed
their father-son reunion, Remallia's connection closed, and the party rested
after the frost arc. No dialogue or full scene prose is reconstructed here.

During the later briefing, Vajra put three pressures on the map: Zalto's
Vonindod excavation and the Triboar fragment, Kayalithica's erasures under a
claimed divine instruction, and Sansuri's mobile Lyn Armaal with Felgolos held
inside it. Ironslag remained the deadline and next route; Lyn Armaal follows,
then Maelstrom, while Kayalithica stays live as a parallel threat. Auril's
portal watch reported calm, cooperative winter after the Ring's reassignment.
The Sea of Moving Ice tunnel crews also reported roughly twenty constructs
missing from their count without wreckage or distress. Umberlee is the leading
suspicion, possibly with giant-pantheon cooperation: the Girls have altered the
Sea of Moving Ice and burned miles of ocean during the Shar/Myrkul conflict.
The silver Harper band's handoff, Ring bearer, and Remallia follow-through are
support-staff work, not burdens carried by the Girls or recruits.

## T531 — WATERDEEP: BANE'S CHOSEN

In Waterdeep, the party encountered and fought a still-unnamed level-20
Conquest paladin chosen by Bane. Scornful Rebuke repeatedly returned psychic
damage when Doran struck him, while radiant smites bypassed the plate's
material and thermal protections. The paladin's raw level and smite pressure
reduced Doran to 0 HP.

Wren placed *wall of force* and hid behind it, then returned to help and was
badly hurt. High-speed Sanctum constructs rushed the field. The paladin
released his active auras and teleported out before containment. Both trainees
then completed a full-rest-equivalent recovery: full HP, Wren's ward, spell
slots, and rest-reset abilities were restored. His personal identity and
mission remain unknown; Bane is the patron and teleportation is the settled
escape. This is a Bane intervention signal, not proof of a coordinated divine
coalition.

## T532 — WATERDEEP: TRAINING WEEK AND REMAKING

Everyone gathered across the integrated Waterdeep household: Blackstaff Tower,
Neverember's estate, and the sprawling Underdeal/Sanctum Embassy. Doran went to
Sera and asked her to push him to his limits so he could begin to hold a candle
to her sun. Praise would always reach Sera, though she would have trained him
anyway. A week of grueling work with Sera and the other Waterdeep masters drove
his body from STR 20 to STR 25 and past human limits. He emerged operationally Large, and able to
wield the six-foot, 208-pound Giant Cleaver one-handed without disadvantage.
Ordinary dagger access is already seamless beside the one-handed Cleaver. Only
Grand Cleave commits both hands and the whole turn.

Wren spent the same week traveling among magi and towers with Ember and Vajra,
talking, learning, and studying the work around her. Ember worked with Wren on
understanding metamagic and then asked Soliera to remake her. Soliera looked at
Wren and spoke once:

> "Mmm. Here, little one."

Sera cheered because she loves when Soliera talks. Wren fainted; Sera caught
her. The remaking was complete when Wren woke as an effective-campaign-level-14
Cleric 12 / Sorcerer 14 stacked chassis. Her Abjuration-shaped
protect-and-extract identity did not change; the force lane moved onto the
transformed Sanctum Black-Bird Sigil, and the divine recovery suite placed a
recovery floor inside the mortal rolling pair.

### T532.5 — The late hour
Sera appeared at Pell's office door at an hour that suggested she'd been wandering. Pell moved a chair. Niv materialized ten minutes later with three cups of tea — no explanation for how he knew, no acknowledgment that he'd timed it. They sat there until Sera said what was on her mind, which took a while. Pell didn't fill the silence. Niv refilled the cups once. Neither of them mentioned it afterward.
