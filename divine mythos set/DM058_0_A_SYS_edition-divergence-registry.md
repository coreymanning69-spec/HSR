# DM058_0 — EDITION DIVERGENCE REGISTRY

## ROW SCHEMA

Each settled row records `subject`, `3.5e`, `5e`, `ruling`, `scope`, and
`settled_at`. A split without a ruling is an open gate, not a settled row.

## UNIVERSAL SETTLED ROWS

| Subject | 3.5e | 5e | Ruling | Scope | Settled |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fortitude save | own save progression | CON save | 5e, CON | universal | pre-play |
| Reflex save | own save progression | DEX save | 5e, DEX | universal | pre-play |
| Will save | own save progression | usually WIS save | 5e WIS default; INT or CHA when named | universal | pre-play |
| Base Attack Bonus | scaling BAB, iterative attacks | proficiency bonus, Extra Attack | 5e; no iterative attacks | universal | pre-play |
| Save DC | 10 + spell level + stat | 8 + proficiency + stat | 5e flat DC per caster | universal | pre-play |

| Touch or flat-footed AC | separate values | no separate value | 5e single AC | universal | pre-play |
| Full-round action | action category | action + bonus action economy | 5e economy; recreate where needed by turn tax | universal | pre-play |
| Concentration | a skill, rolled | one spell held, CON save to keep | 5e | universal | pre-play |
| Attack of opportunity | broad triggers, multiple per round | opportunity attack, one reaction | 5e; War Caster may substitute a spell | universal | pre-play |
| Turn or rebuke undead | turning check and HD table | Channel Divinity | 5e Channel Divinity | universal | pre-play |
| Metamagic | raises effective slot level | costs sorcery points | 5e sorcery points | universal | pre-play |

  5e contested Athletics or Acrobatics.

  per-item charge tables, relevant to the staff and ring.

Open splits are recorded here without a ruling. When selected, append the
decision and move the row into the settled universal or character section.
