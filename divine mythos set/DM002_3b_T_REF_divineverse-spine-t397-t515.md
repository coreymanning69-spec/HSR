# ARC 9: UNDERMOUNTAIN & PORTAL (T396–T429)
#CHAR-HALASTER-BLACKCLOAK #CHAR-ELEMENSINTER #CHAR-MIRA #CHAR-RENA #CHAR-ALFIA #CHAR-EMBER
#LOC-UNDERMOUNTAIN #LOC-YAWNING-PORTAL #LOC-CHILLING-ROOM
#CONCEPT-UNDERMOUNTAIN-OUTPOST #CONCEPT-PORTAL-ACTIVATION #CONCEPT-MERGEVERSE-ENTRY


**Where:** Waterdeep below — Undermountain, Yawning Portal. Then through the portal: Primeverse above Oak City.

**Why:** Council identifies Undermountain rising threat. Trio investigates. Finds something else entirely.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* The Undermountain shifts subtly around her presence — not by Halaster's will but by hers. She steps through the portal at the end because it inhales. She doesn't explain.
- *Sera:* "Let's go." into a trapdoor. The Chilling Room is her design — she names it, furnishes it, frames it with "Optional cuddling. Mandatory snacks."
- *Ember:* Bonds one-on-one with Mira — the only introverts among giants. "You always were part of it. You just didn't know yet." Growing into quiet connection with individual mortals.
- *Halaster:* Curious, unpredictable, not hostile. The only entity in the tour who is simultaneously harmless and genuinely confusing in motivation. He placed objects as tests. He won't know the results.
- *Mira:* Academic mage. Ember's first mortal peer-bond. Not overwhelmed by divinity — fascinated.
- *Supporting cast:* Rena (rogue) gets flirted with by Sera. Alfia (paladin) prays under unseen scrutiny. Something ancient watches. Elemensinter bends to reverence. The dove-cloak agent arrives to confirm the outpost was sanctioned three months ago.

**Sanctum Population:** ~820,000 approaching T450. Infrastructure surge as the Merge portal approaches.

**Ember — Development Beat:**
- First genuine peer-bond with a mortal (Mira). Learns that connection doesn't require overwhelming someone.
- Beginning to fill Sera's size range. Still processing the scale of her own presence.
- Corridor opening toward the portal is the first time she takes Sera's hand without prompting.

→ Elemensinter appears in a blooming stone garden. Soliera's silent presence bends the Weave-being to reverence. A pact forms without words.
→ In a tavern, Sera slams a disguised trapdoor like a lid, cracking the floor. "Let's go." The Trio descends toward Undermountain without fanfare.

→ Soliera sits not above but amidst them; everyone realizes this place was always theirs.
→ A dove-cloak agent approaches, kneeling to explain the embassy sanctioned this outpost three months prior. Sera orders: "Grab like… eighty-nine people. Make this floor pretty. Torches. Cushions. Wedding vibes."
→ Mira and Ember quietly connect, the only introverts among divine giants. Ember tells her: "You always were part of it. You just didn't know yet."
→ Alfia's prayer deepens; something ancient watches. Soliera may one day answer on that god's behalf… or correct it.

**Where:** Baldur's Gate → Candlekeep → Avernus. Zariel's fortress.

**Why:** Elturel pulled into Hell — jurisdictional theft, not apocalypse. The Sanctum classifies it as illegally bound infrastructure.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* Descends not as a hero but as an authority enforcing contract law. The arc's defining moment is the Styx rewrite and its deliberate rollback — she undoes a working fix by choice, proving the change was chosen and not accidental.
- *Sera:* Treats hell's violence as a design flaw. Wants to enter the Abyss and delete every bad actor because she is tired of systems built on cruelty. Asmodeus tells her no — cleanly, as a constraint, refusing to let her reduce the problem to a tantrum with a body count. The restraint is imposed on her, not authored by her; she is out of her depth and turns to Soliera.
- *Ember:* Asks why souls are used as fuel without consent. Devils cannot answer. Destabilizes infernal assumptions through innocent inquiry. Curiosity as her primary engagement mode this arc. Reframes Candlekeep as "a place where questions are allowed to live."
- *Zariel:* Collapses off-screen under the pressure of a higher jurisdiction rewriting the river her war machine depends on. The trio never meets her: no confrontation, conversation, examination, restoration, or boss encounter occurs.
- *Bel:* Attempts dominance framing. Fails immediately. Corrected, not destroyed.
- *Supporting cast:* Asmodeus recognizes Soliera's kernel authority immediately. Hospitality geometry rewrites itself. Sylvira Savikas identifies Zariel's hand in the contract structure. Traxigor restored from curse damage as proof-of-concept mercy.

**Sanctum Population:** ~820,000 at T450.

**Ember — Development Beat:**
- Hell cannot answer her questions. This is the first time Ember recognizes that an entire system has failed — not a person, a system.
- Curiosity as destabilization is her divine function this arc.
- Physically approaching Sera's size and frame by arc end. Different color scheme: obsidian/gold/fire, not Sera's baseline.

**Where:** Barovia — closed-system Domain of Dread. Castle Ravenloft.

**Why:** Stanimir arrives with intelligence, not a plea. Barovia is classified as a jurisdictional anomaly.

**Emotional State:**
- *Soliera:* The arc ends with her most personal spoken line in the entire Divineverse: "Of course, Ember." Trust made audible for the first time.
- *Sera:* Reframes the problem as systemic abuse before anyone else names it. Stays at appropriate scale when the Dark Powers manifest.
- *Ember:* Speaks with moral authority for the first time — "Of course, Ember." is the confirmation. Requests mass resurrection independently. By T515 she is Sera's physical equal in size and presence.
- *Strahd:* Engages diplomatically rather than defensively. Collapses emotionally when dawn breaks — not violently. Survives de-cursed and non-sovereign.
- *Ireena:* Defined as the focal victim — not Strahd. She sets the victory conditions. Her consent architecture governs the resolution.
- *Supporting cast:* Kolyan Indirovich resurrected (breaking a core module assumption). Doru cured of vampirism remotely. Father Donavich's faith crisis resolved through lived mercy. The village stabilizes psychologically within hours.

**Sanctum Population:** ~900,000+ at T515. Pre-Merge surge beginning.

**Ember — Development Beat:**
- First time she speaks with independent moral authority: "These dead deserve to come home."
- Soliera's response ("Of course, Ember") is a public confirmation of trust. This is the moment Ember's growth is acknowledged in-world.
- Physically: Sera's equal in height and presence. Obsidian skin, kintsugi lines (coal-light/ember-glow at rest, heat-spectrum variable), wildfire hair. Distinct personality — curious and soft where Sera is joyful and kinetic, but no longer child-coded in any sense.
