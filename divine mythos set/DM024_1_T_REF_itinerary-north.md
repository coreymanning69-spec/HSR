## How To Use This Document

This file is the canonical reference atlas for the girls' northern travel arc. It is not a module. It is a living DM scaffold: a geographic, political, and cultural atlas of the route as filtered through Sanctum influence and the reputation the divine trio built across `T0-T515`.

Structure per location:

- **[MAP POSITION]** - Physical cardinal placement on the Faerun map
- **[STATUS]** - Visited / First Visit / Extraplanar
- **[TRAVEL ACCESS]** - How the girls get there (teleport node / paved road / Ark / foot)
- **[WORD OF MOUTH]** - What locals have heard about the girls before they arrive
- **[SETTING CHANGES]** - Canon alterations from Sanctum influence
- **[CITY FLAVOR]** - Street-level sensory and cultural detail
- **[EMBASSY STATUS]** - Sanctum diplomatic presence
- **[HOOKS & QUESTS]** - Optional traceable encounters tied to this location
- **[PARALLEL THREADS]** - What Velin, the engineer, Niv, or other Sanctum principals are doing here simultaneously
- **[DM NOTES]** - Meta guidance for running this location with divine characters

## Travel Infrastructure Overview

### The Teleport Network

By `T515`, the Sanctum maintains permanent teleportation circles at the following nodes. These circles are embedded in Sanctum embassy floors and are guarded by divine constructs at all times. Unauthorized use is physically impossible; the circles require Sanctum-keyed attunement.

**Active Nodes (Divineverse `T516+`):**

- **The Sanctum (Spine of the World)** - Master Hub
- **Bryn Shander (Ten-Towns)** - Northern Hub
- **Waterdeep (Embassy in Old Thieves' Guild tunnels)** - Southern Hub
- **Silverymoon** - Eastern Hub
- **Eltabbar (Thay)** - Archived / sealed post-arc
- **The Ark** (mobile node, currently anchored in Waterdeep harbor)

Travel between nodes is instantaneous. Niv-Saiffar coordinates scheduling. Velin handles diplomatic clearance. The girls can appear anywhere on the network with zero travel time if they choose, though this arc is specifically framed as a sightseeing tour, meaning they travel the roads, take the Ark, or use mount / foot for atmospheric effect. Teleport is reserved for emergencies or when Ember gets impatient.

### The Construct Road Network

Sanctum constructs have paved or improved roads connecting:

- **Sanctum -> Ten-Towns** (all seven towns) - full paved road, maintained year-round
- **Sanctum -> Mirabar** - fully paved, wide enough for two wagons abreast, lit at night by construct-placed stone lanterns every fifty feet
- **Mirabar -> Luskan** - partially improved (contested terrain near Luskan's political sphere)
- **Waterdeep -> Triboar** (the Long Road) - jointly maintained with Waterdeep city engineers
- **Waterdeep -> Daggerford -> Baldur's Gate** (the Coast Way) - road improvements underway, not complete

The roads feel different from mundane roads. The stone is seamless: no ruts, no mud, no ice accumulation in winter because the stones emit a faint thermal signature. Merchants who use Sanctum roads report shaved days off travel times. Some cities resent the infrastructure intrusion. Mirabar's merchant council considers it unfair competition.

### The Ark

The divine vessel. Impossibly large. Radiant white hull that does not reflect light so much as generate it. Currently anchored in Waterdeep's deep harbor because no conventional dock could hold it; the constructs built a temporary platform.

The Ark serves as:

- Floating embassy / HQ on sea routes
- Mobile teleportation hub (secondary)
- Visible deterrent - no navy in the Realms challenges a ship crewed by divine constructs and captained by beings who have killed a dragon goddess

For the tour, the Ark will be used for the coastal legs `Waterdeep -> Baldur's Gate`, and for any island / extraplanar transit.

### Word Of Mouth — Local Versions

DM000_4 owns the baseline: the Sanctum and divine trio are household names
throughout the Divineverse and its connected extraplanar routes. Never roll
whether an NPC has heard of them. Local entries in this atlas describe the
version, accuracy, source, familiarity, and likely reaction:

- **Intimate:** direct witnesses and long-standing partner communities know
  specific habits, people, operations, and institutional channels.
- **Regional:** the institution and trio are unmistakable, while details arrive
  through trade, temple, faction, and road networks and may be garbled.
- **Remote or specialized:** awareness is still present, but the story is
  filtered through scholarship, warning, monster rumor, planar report, or
  local cosmology.

Recognition never guarantees deference. It may produce ceremony, calculation,
contempt, testing, avoidance, or a flat confirmation of a known hazard. Doran
and Wren remain individually unknown even when their livery is recognized.
