## PART III: THE MARCH ON ELTABBAR

T377: The Divine Sleigh — Departure for the Capital #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #VEHICLE-SLEIGH
• At Sera’s casual prompt—“Soli, can you make us a sleigh?”—a radiant vehicle pulls itself into existence. Long, smooth, throne-backed, with curved sides like folded ivory petals. Its pullers are two six-legged construct steeds, shimmering with white metal plates veined in gold. They don’t breathe. They simply are. Captain Nerun approaches with a half-bow: “We’ll keep working. More workers should arrive by skiff tonight. This place will be a port before the week’s done.” Sera nods. “We’ll be back when we’ve got Lysa. Or when the city’s on fire. Either way.” The sleigh lifts gently and begins the journey inland—toward Eltabbar, Thay’s capital and the seat of the old order.

T378: Approaching Eltabbar — The View of Evil #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-ELTABBAR
• The sleigh crests the final ridge. The sunlight recoils from the city walls. What should be golden haze becomes oppressive—thick with ash, incense, and blood-caked stone. The view rolls open like a wound. Rows of market stalls sell people, not grain. Slaves are lined up, shackled, eyes downcast. A child is dragged by a man with red tattoos. A priest of Loviatar blesses the act. Chained elves are pushed through the south gate, feet leaving blood. Sera’s jaw tightens. Her lips curl into a smile with no joy behind it. Her hand grips the sleigh hard enough to leave fingerprints in solid wood. Soliera watches with lips pressed tight, eyes squinting. She has never seen this much concentrated evil. Tirren shifts: “This isn’t just bad. It’s wrong. What could we even do here?” Sera’s response is immediate: “We go through the front gate.”

T379: Arrival at the Slave Pits — Planar Lock #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #LOC-SLAVE-PITS #CONCEPT-PLANAR-LOCK
• The divine group approaches the sprawling slave pits—cages stretching for blocks, thousands of broken people pressed into filth. Sera steps off the sleigh. A slaver raises his whip toward her. The leather strikes her skin and shatters like ceramic on stone. She doesn’t even acknowledge it. Soliera remains seated but her presence shifts. Reality locks. The planar boundaries seal. No teleportation. No divine intervention. No escape. Sera announces to the huddled masses: “You’re free now. This is Soliera’s domain.” A Red Wizard commander appears on a balcony and unleashes meteor swarms—not at the gods, but at the slaves. Murder to deny mercy. Ember raises her hand. The spells freeze mid-air, then reverse course. The wizard dies by his own fire. The spells are multiplied; this is a city-wide assault... their own intention returned.

T380: Liberation Begins — Ember’s Mass Healing #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SERA #CONCEPT-DIVINE-WILL-AS-PHYSICS
• Without hesitation, Sera, Soliera, and Ember move through the slave pits, freeing the enslaved and arming them not with weapons but with revitalized health and undeniable divine energy. Guards are swiftly killed. Enemy spells are effortlessly reflected. Ember kneels beside a gaunt woman in a half-open cage. The woman flinches, unsure if this creature of fire is another tormentor. But Ember offers her hand: “You’re free.” Then she raises both arms and a burst of soft golden light surges outward. Mass Lay on Hands. Twice. Burns vanish. Bones realign. Eyes clear. Scars fade. The oldest man begins to sob. Children who hadn’t stood in days rise slowly. A mother realizes her child’s fever is gone. The pits are no longer silent. They’re filled with people standing. With weeping. With whispered awe.

T381: City-Wide Uprising — Walking Conquest #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #LOC-ELTABBAR
• As the triad moves through the city, others spontaneously begin to fight and flee on their own volition. The Red Wizards’ control structure shatters under divine pressure. Sera walks ahead, flicking her hand at any guard who approaches—they split clean in half. Ember intercepts spells mid-cast, reversing them with added force. Soliera walks between them, presence cracking windows and bending light. Where she steps, chains fall apart. Diseases vanish. The city’s leaders are in frenzy—temple bells clanging, war horns sounding, scrying pools flickering with static. Messages fly tower to tower. No one can leave. The planar lock holds.

T382: Temple of Shar — “Mercy Not Found” #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-TEMPLE-OF-SHAR
• They reach the Temple of Shar—black-stone columns and shadow-swallowed arches oozing despair. A priest stumbles out, robe ash-covered: “You don’t understand what you’ve done! This temple has stood for centuries! It guides the lost!” Sera’s voice goes flat: “You fucking what? You take care of the weak?” She steps forward. “Out there, in the pits, there’s a woman missing her leg. Boys too small to cry anymore. And you sat here. In robes. In silence.” She tells the cousins to step back. Then she slashes horizontally—from one corner of the temple to the next. The entire structure splits. First a crack. Then a rumble. One half drops backward. The other keels forward into the street. A giant dust cloud rises. T382 is renamed: MERCY NOT FOUND.

T383: Temple of Bane — Righteous Fury #CHAR-SERA #LOC-TEMPLE-OF-BANE
• They see the Temple of Bane next—fortress-like, militant, banners of tyranny. Sera walks directly toward it. Priests try to bargain. She doesn’t slow. Warriors form defensive lines. They last three seconds. By the time she reaches the altar, there’s nothing left standing. She claps her hands once. The temple implodes—stone folding inward, the sound like thunder compressed into a single heartbeat. “That’s two,” she says cheerfully.

T384: Temple of Myrkul — The Bone Lord’s Shrine #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #LOC-TEMPLE-OF-MYRKUL
• The Temple of Myrkul is gothic horror incarnate—bone pillars, skull motifs, the air thick with necrotic residue. Undead guards rise to defend it. Ember steps forward. Her arms ignite with the five-element breath again. The undead are unmade not by destruction but by purification. Sera doesn’t even participate—she just watches approvingly as her sister burns away centuries of death worship. The temple doesn’t collapse. It evaporates. “That’s three,” Ember says quietly, then looks surprised at her own voice.

T385: Finding Aunt Lysa — The Search Complete #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #CHAR-AUNT-LYSA #CHAR-EMBER
• While the temples fall, Tirren and Daviya search desperately through liberated areas. Door after door. Cell after cell. Daviya calls out: “Auntie! Lysa!” Nothing. Then—in a crumbling structure near the lower market—they find her. Emaciated. Scarred. Missing fingers. But alive. Daviya collapses beside her, sobbing. Tirren stands guard, tears streaming. Ember approaches and heals her completely—flesh restored, trauma soothed. Lysa wakes in her niece’s arms, confused and overwhelmed. “How?” she whispers. “God came,” Daviya says simply.

T386: Approach to Tarneth’s Castle — Divine Advance #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-EMBER #LOC-TARNETH-CASTLE
• With the aunt secured on the sleigh, the triad advances toward Tarneth Sazher’s castle—the Zulkir of Necromancy’s seat of power. His elite forces fail instantly. Ember nullifies spells mid-cast. Sera flicks men in half without breaking stride. Citizens flee. Slaves revolt. Mob justice reigns in the streets behind them. Tarneth watches from his balcony, furious at his isolation. He screams insults into the wind. They’re not listening.

T387: The Castle Breach — Sera Opens the Door #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #LOC-TARNETH-CASTLE
• They arrive at Tarneth’s formidable castle. Sera pauses at the main door—not from inability, but consideration. These cousins will need to rule from somewhere. Preserving the structure matters. Ember steps forward and rests one obsidian hand against the magically sealed door. Her kintsugi lines brighten as she reads the wards — necromantic, layered, ancient. She tilts her head slightly. *Why does this hold itself shut?* The question is honest. The wards do not answer her, which is its own answer. She steps back; this is not the kind of door that opens for a question. Sera, with a single effortless hand, pushes it wide open. The hinges scream. The wards shatter — pulled apart, not by force, but by Sera moving faster than the Weave they were anchored in. They’re inside.

T388: Confrontation with Tarneth — Divine Inevitability #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TARNETH-SAZHER #CHAR-EMBER #THEME-DIVINE-INEVITABILITY
• They calmly approach Tarneth, the Zulkir of Necromancy. Before his ultimate demise, they engage him in conversation—systematic, chilling, forcing him to comprehend the full scope of his doom. Tarneth rages: “You’re not God. You’re a tantrum with skin. A butcher wrapped in velvet.” He looks past Sera to Soliera: “You could have ruled with us. There was a path. But you came to end us.” Sera laughs. Ember laughs. Soliera grins slightly and walks between the two cousins, placing a hand on each of their backs. Sera says: “We came here for their aunt. This was just the city she was in.”

T389: Ember’s Declaration — “These Two Will Rule” #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #THEME-RAGS-TO-RULERS
• At the crumbling threshold, Ember speaks unexpectedly—the first time she’s asserted herself in public: “These two will rule this land now.” She doesn’t know why she said it. But it resonates through the hall like divine decree. Soliera, ever silent, stands flanking the cousins. Her stillness isn’t passive; it’s profound endorsement. Truth made manifest in her presence. Tarneth’s face twists with incomprehension. Two peasants? Ruling his domain? But he sees Soliera’s hand on their backs. He understands: this isn’t negotiation. This is succession.

T390: Sera’s Question — “This Is the Man Who Hurt You” #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #CHAR-TARNETH-SAZHER
• Tarneth, still clinging to illusion, desperately begs for Shar—failing to comprehend that divine intervention has been blocked. Sera turns her gaze to Tirren and Daviya: “This is the man who hurt you.” Not a question. A statement. A gift. Before the full horror unfolds, Ember guides the cousins away from the scene. They don’t need to see what happens next.

T391: Tarneth’s Demise — Methodical Destruction #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TARNETH-SAZHER #EXECUTION-BRUTAL #THEME-PUNISHMENT
• Sera steps on Tarneth’s ankle and crushes it along with the stone below. “Ah, we’ve done this part before…” she purrs. He screams. “You thought I forgot what you said about Soli?” Another step. His femur shatters. The screams double. Each flick is an apostolic act. Each scream is a confession. Each break is a prayer. She’s doing what Soliera doesn’t—becoming the enforcer, the will given flesh. In the hallway, the cousins hear the screams restart. Then pause. A loud snap. Then screams again, hollow now, as if from bloody throats. Then a single *pop*. And a sing-song note: “That’s better!” They blanch. Ember touches them both: “I know. But he said horrible things about Soliera. That’s how it has to go.”

— The castle hallway fills with the sickening sounds of mutilation—snapping bones, hollow screams that echo, then a profound, unsettling silence. Away from the carnage, Ember gently consoles the still-shaking cousins, explaining softly, "This had to happen." Soliera, walking calmly behind Sera as she emerges from the chamber, remains completely unbothered, her serene expression a clear sign of absolute approval. Her gaze upon the scene is not judgment, but satisfied resolution.

— Meta: This beat frames the horror as a form of divine mercy. Soliera permits and even approves of the brutality not out of cruelty, but because it was necessary for systemic correction. This isn't chaos; it's divine pedagogy through consequence, a brutal lesson delivered directly to the source of corruption, ensuring the complete eradication of the old, oppressive order.


T392: Sera Emerges — “Yay!” #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #THEME-CASUAL-POWER
• The grand door creaks open. Sera emerges—grinning, humming, absolutely delighted. Her bare feet are stained a little red. Behind her walks Soliera. Still. Silent. Unfazed. Perfect. “Yay!” Sera cheers. “We found your aunt and stopped the bad guy!” Ember smiles too. The cousins look around, still processing. Sera is already talking while walking: “Let’s get back to her. Then to the Ark! And the village! Oh, we’ve got a lot to talk about. I can already hear Niv back at the Sanctum…”


## PART IV: AFTERMATH AND ESTABLISHMENT

T393: Return to the Village — Infrastructure Blooms #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-CAPTAIN-NERUN #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE
• They return to the Ark at the bay. The area is vastly transformed. Initial base has grown into a sprawling hub of divine infrastructure. Docks complete. Houses built. Fields ready. Constructs and missionaries work alongside original villagers in harmonious community. Ships arrive on the horizon. Teleportation circles pulse on the stone. The fleet has arrived. The port grows. The village heals.

T394: The Captain’s Report — Divine Logistics #CHAR-CAPTAIN-NERUN #CHAR-VELIN #INFRASTRUCTURE-BUILD
• Inside the Ark, they gather at the long table. Captain Nerun reports: “Teleportation circles are forming. More ships visible on the horizon. Reinforcements arrived thanks to pre-ordered Sanctum logistics.” The aunt, whole and strong again, rests nearby surrounded by caretakers. Sera talks nonstop about the city, the temples, the pop! noise Tarneth made. Velin is already organizing paperwork. Supplies flow by the hour.

T395: Divine Client State — The Cousins’ New Role #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #THEME-RAGS-TO-RULERS
• Sera tells the cousins: “The Sanctum will handle the clean-up. You’re rulers now—divinely chosen.” They’re overwhelmed but obedient—echoing Exodus or Daniel’s ascension tales. Ember backs her up, explaining they’ll be trained. The transition happens like divine fables: “You were nothing. You are now everything. Because God said so.” The tone is authoritarian but maternal. Tirren and Daviya don’t fully understand yet. But they’re starting to.

T396: Sunset Over Ruin — Arc Closure #CHAR-SOLIERA #THEME-DIVINE-INEVITABILITY
• The Ark sails again. The capital burns behind. Construct and divine aid settle the coast. Neighboring regions will respond to this event—some with fear, others with embassies. The geopolitical landscape has been irrevocably altered. And Soliera—quiet, poised—stands by the far window. She looks up at the sky. Not to escape. Not to leave. But to acknowledge. It is peaceful. For now. She smiles up at the sky—subtle, radiant. The arc is closed. The balance is restored.

NPC RECAP — END OF ARC

SOLIERA: The silent, approving, ever-watching core of the divine. Her subtle smile is the campaign’s metronome, signifying ultimate approval and the unfolding of divine will. She did not need to act throughout the entire Thay arc—her presence was sufficient. The world adjusted to her preference.

SERA: The embodiment of divine fury, now tempered by focus. Still undeniably brutal, but learning to channel her power with strategic patience when Soliera’s deeper truths are involved. Her joy-first, violence-second nature was on full display—she clapped and cheered while temples collapsed. Her love for Soliera remains the ultimate motivation for all action.

EMBER: The inquiry function maturing into active intervention without losing its native vector. She did not become a "weapon" — she has never been one. What changed in this arc is that her questions began to land as decree (the cousins-will-rule moment), and her Weave Interface scaled from passive to mass-effect (intercepting and reversing city-wide spell assaults; mass Lay on Hands across the slave pits). She still does not strike. She redirects, reads, heals, and asks. Her first unprompted *public* speech — declaring the cousins would rule — was Soliera's questioning function landing in the room with full divine weight. Ember did not know why she said it. The room did. She now finds her own voice between Sera’s fierce justice and Soliera’s profound stillness, but the voice is a question that has learned it is sometimes also an answer.

TIRREN & DAVIYA: The impoverished cousins, now unexpectedly crowned rulers. They remain appropriately overwhelmed by their divine ascension, representing the awe and shock of chosen mortals. Their journey from starving villagers to divine-backed rulers embodies the theme of mercy as infrastructure.

AUNT LYSA: Rescued, healed, and restored. She represents the personal stakes that drove the entire mission—the divine chose to reshape a nation because two peasants wanted their family back.

CAPTAIN NERUN: Key logistical figure managing the transition. He represents the efficient, mechanical aspect of divinity in action—a man who learned quickly not to question God.

NIV-SAIFFAR, VELIN, MARRIK: Still off-screen but critical, coordinating the global infrastructure of the Sanctum. They are the unseen gears that allow mythic power to manifest in practical, nation-shaping ways.

TARNETH SAZHER: The Zulkir of Necromancy, now paste. His arrogance and blasphemy against Soliera earned him extended suffering before death. He serves as example: power without wisdom dies screaming.

RED WIZARDS: Scattered and broken. Their power structure in Thay has been utterly shattered, their influence decimated.
