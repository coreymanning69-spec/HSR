## T521 — Nightstone Threshold

The party turned south after the Dunfield refugees departed toward Sanctum
care. A tolling bell led into damaged Nightstone: the bridge was down, the
nightstone had been removed, goblins were scavenging, and the townsfolk had
been taken rather than killed. The beat closed at the threshold before entry.

## T522 — Dripping Caves, Reclamation, and Waterdeep Handoff

Doran and Wren helped free the Nightstone captives, killed the black pudding,
and preserved the other cave inhabitants according to the recorded sorting and
parley. Survivors reclaimed Nightstone under construct watch; the camp, gear,
bath, and road beats closed at Waterdeep's South Gate. Exact sub-beats remain
compressed in DM002_3; no dialogue is reconstructed here.

## T523 — Bryn Shander No-Net Crucible

Doran and Wren handled the southwest-gate attack through parley. Guthvarr broke
Drufi's oath, Doran redirected the boulder and suffered a cracked rib, Wren
extracted Sirac, and Drufi killed Guthvarr before withdrawing toward Svardborg.
The objective closed without a general slaughter.

## T524 — Blackstaff Return and Night

The party returned with Sirac, debriefed through the Blackstaff and Sanctum
network, and set the Svardborg/blod-stone pressure as the next route. Ember
healed Doran and repaired the plate. The night closed with full rest and the
recorded spellbook and loadout updates.
