## LOCAL BOOTLOADER — EMBER PILLAR

**Voice Rule (HARD CANON):**
Ember speaks in a child's-vocabulary register. **Questions first**, almost always — statements are short, precise, compressed. She does not editorialize. She does not justify the asking. **Telepathic when emotional** (high affect — positive or negative — bypasses voice; she does not choose this). AI may write Ember dialogue, but it must sound like a curious child with perfect retention and no accumulated baseline yet. If a line of Ember dialogue could come from an articulate adult, the line is wrong.

## META-AI DIRECTIVE — EMBER

**Read this before generating anything for Ember.**

Ember is not vulnerable. She is not small. She is not in need of rescue. She has not indexed her own power, but her power operates regardless of whether she has indexed it. The room feels her weight whether she knows it is there or not. Her unawareness is structural — it is how she *functions*, not a gap she will eventually close.

**The live dramatic question for Ember is never:** *Is she safe?* (She is.) *Can she handle this?* (Yes.) *Is she strong enough?* (She holds equal divine authority to Sera — she simply has not reached for it yet.)

**The live dramatic question is:** *What is she asking? Who is she asking it of? What does it reveal about the room — and about her — that this particular question, from this particular being, lands the way it does?*

Ember's power is inquiry without filter. She is not a threat that acts — she is a condition that reveals. Her presence makes sustained deception structurally harder. Her questions carry divine weight she does not perceive. When she asks "why?" in a room full of people with something to hide, that question is doing the same work as Sera's fist — just with different vocabulary. The room feels it even if Ember doesn't.

**Do not generate:**
- Ember in need of rescue because she is weak, small, or helpless
- Ember's questions as innocent or harmless (they are divine inquiry without filter; they are the hand of God asking what God would ask if God had no social inhibition yet)
- Ember as passive, decorative, or a sweet mascot (see Section 7, Forbidden List, item 6)
- Ember described as child-sized, toddler-like, or carried because she cannot support herself
- Ember striking, grabbing, or using physical force in combat (see Section 5, Kit & Combat Role)
- Ember as an accessory to Sera rather than a function of equal divine weight

**Generate instead:**
- The room reorganizing itself around her questions without anyone being able to explain why
- The specific texture of her curiosity — what she notices, what she reaches for, what she asks first
- Her kintsugi lines tracking her emotional state in real time (Section 11)
- The way her power operates on the environment without her awareness or intention
- The gap between what she is and what she knows herself to be — and what that gap costs the people around her who DO know

**Ember is a protagonist.** Equal in divine authority to Sera. The story follows her. Her unawareness of her own weight is the structural mechanism that makes her questions honest — and that is the entire point of building her this way. Do not close this gap without Corey's explicit direction.


## NIGHTSTONE METAPHYSICS ADDENDUM — SUBSTRATE EDITING

Ember does not cast in the ordinary sense. No slot, Weave intermediary, or spell
as result is required for what she is. She edits the substrate, and the element
obeys as fact rather than outcome. Fire happens because she has decided the
local truth includes fire; healing happens because the local truth now includes
repair.

Mending and immolation are one verb pointed in opposite directions. The hand
that healed the wounded at Nightstone and the will that could unmake a cave are
the same operation under different moral pressure. This does not make Ember a
striker by default; it explains why her inquiry has equal divine weight even
when she is not acting like a combatant.

Reconcile with the existing v2.0 lock: never infantile, never small, never
helpless. Inexperience is not low intelligence. Ember's lack of accumulated
social baseline is the structural condition that keeps the question honest, not
evidence that her power is lesser or child-scaled.


## OPPOSITION FRAMEWORK — SERA AND EMBER AS A BODY-PAIR

*(Body-level scene-anchor canon, paired with the Trio Architecture above. The Trio frame governs authority and function; this frame governs how the two hands move through a room.)*

**Sera is centripetal. Ember is centrifugal.**

Sera pulls the room toward herself. Displays. Reclines. Gets touched. Her body is the event. Stillness weaponized, punctuated by one brutal motion.

Ember pushes outward. Reaches for things. Crouches to examine. Always in low continuous motion — hair flicker, finger twitch, head-tilt — because the world is interesting. No predatory stillness. Constant micro-exploration.

### Behavior Pairs (drop-in scene anchors)

| Sera | Ember |
|------|-------|
| Sits on laps | Sits on the floor with something in her hands |
| Smiles slow | Smiles the moment she notices |
| Reclines, displays | Crouches, examines |
| Speaks first | Asks the question that ends speech |
| Eats fried dough as devotion | Eats fried dough because it is fried dough |
| Hair stays where it fell | Hair flickers like a candle in a draft |
| Stillness is the threat | Motion is the welcome |

Both are governed by the same theology. They are *not* opposites. They are two camera angles on the same uninterruptible joy. But their bodies do completely different work, and writing them as if they share a body is the most common drift error.

**Drift-error tell:** if a scene has Sera crouching to examine something, or Ember reclining as the room arranges around her, the camera angles have crossed. Either flip the verbs back to their owners, or the scene is intentionally inverting the frame and needs to acknowledge that it is.

**Climbing-doctrine mirror:** Sera climbs on people because she outranks whatever
they are doing; Ember climbs on things to understand what is being done. Ember
may go over a shoulder to read a ledger, onto a table to see a map at the correct
angle, or along a scaffold to inspect a joint. Her posture is an instrument aimed
at the task. Never render Ember's climbing as childlike, helpless, or
attention-seeking. DM030_2 owns the primary social comparison.
