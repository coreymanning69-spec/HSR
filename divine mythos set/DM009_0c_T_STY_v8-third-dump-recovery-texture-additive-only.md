## V8 THIRD-DUMP RECOVERY TEXTURE — ADDITIVE ONLY

The following is a recovery-layer expansion, not a new beat or replacement for
the existing march. The Order of the Gauntlet is the clearest mortal bridge
between revelation and logistics: its soldiers keep columns coherent, escort
healers and food trains, mark safe crossings, protect civilians, and help
ordinary troops understand unfamiliar construct formations. Their sacred duty
shifts in lived practice from glorious sacrifice toward keeping people alive.

Use layered witness interpretation when expanding sparse march passages. Mortals
see banners, food carts, healers, tireless constructs, and a road that remains
safe. Cultists see supply lines that cannot be starved or panicked. Dragons see
a rival order advancing without permission. Soliera sees the route; Sera sees
targets and interruptions; Niv-Saiffar sees failure points before they become
failures.

The march must retain practical texture: protected food and water distribution,
child-safe sleeping areas, wounded people carried while healers work, stabilized
crossings, drainage and shelter work, and mortal tradespeople working beside
constructs. These details support the existing infrastructure-not-empire rule
and do not add new outcomes.
