## PART II: ARRIVAL IN THAY — THE FISHING VILLAGE

T365: First Sight of Thay — The White Speck #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE #ITEM-THE-ARK
• Perspective shift: Two young adults stand on a bluff overlooking a shallow bay near a suffering coastal village. Tirren (male, seventeen, lean and serious) and his cousin Daviya (female, dark curls hacked short as punishment). They see a white speck on the horizon—not gold like Thayan skimmers, not black like merchant barges, not red like anything from Thay. White. Like bone. Like frost. Like the sky before a storm. It approaches without turning, without drifting. It has already decided where to go. The villagers behind them know something is different. Wrong like new. The cousins watch the shape grow into a vessel with sails like cathedral towers. Children play on the upper deck. “Gods,” Daviya whispers. “No,” Tirren says. “I don’t think they’re gods.” He pauses. “…You sure?” “No.”

T366: Arrival at the Bay — The Captain’s Realization #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-CAPTAIN-NERUN #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE #ITEM-THE-ARK
• Captain Nerun stares at the pathetic dock—half-rotted timbers, tilted at a slant, held together by habit more than nails. “My Ladies,” he says, “This ship won’t dock there. It’s too big.” Sera waves dismissively: “Oh, well, we’ll have to fix that.” The captain opens his mouth to ask how, then remembers who he’s speaking to. “Ah, yes, ma’am.” Why ask God? Just say yes. The Ark glides in anyway. Somehow. The dock holds. Somehow. Reality adjusts to Soliera’s preference. Local Captains are aghast, claiming the Ark can't fit on the shallow docks. Sera, however, casually waves off his concerns, and the Captain, remembering who he's with, relents, his skepticism melting away.

T367: Establishing the Forward Base #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-CAPTAIN-NERUN #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE #ITEM-THE-ARK
• The Captain quickly engages with villagers. With Sera’s blessing, he sends word back to the main Sanctum base requesting more personnel and supplies. The Ark serves as nucleus for a burgeoning forward operating base. Constructs immediately begin work: raising docks, fixing roofs on shanties, erecting protective fences. Constructed workers and liberated villagers labor together, transforming the area. This is Soliera’s infrastructure doctrine in action—mercy manifested as competence, love as logistics.

T368: First Confrontation — The Titan Rises #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-THAYAN-LORD #CHAR-TITAN-CONSTRUCT #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE
• A local Thayan Lord arrives with a heavily armed contingent, attempting to assert authority over the unexpected divine presence. He doesn’t know what he’s looking at. From the depths of the bay, a colossal 70-foot-tall Titan Construct rises with thunderous emergence. It was laid there as a dormant guardian, now summoned by the intrusion. The Titan swiftly and ruthlessly crushes most of the attacking forces—armored men swept aside like toys. The lord barely survives the initial clash.

T369: Sera’s Swift Justice — The Red Wizard Interrogation #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-RED-WIZARDS #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE #ITEM-THE-ARK
• Sera sees the aggression and moves with impossible speed, almost teleporting across the distance. She reaches the Red Wizard leading the attack and casually flicks off both his feet with a nonchalant gesture. She drags the screaming wizard back by his arm. His other hand flails desperately, but he fails to free himself. She asks if he knows the whereabouts of Tirren and Daviya’s aunt. When he admits ignorance, her interest vanishes. With a final decisive flick, she kills him. His body slumps lifelessly. Sera returns to the Ark as if she’d merely gone for a walk.

T370: Finding the Cousins — “Our Chosen” #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE #ITEM-THE-ARK
• The first wave of children pours from the Ark—laughing, chasing each other, clean and fed and safe. Behind them, constructs move in seamless coordination. Crates slide down into perfect rows. Entire academies’ worth of supplies stack in view. Tirren and Daviya approach cautiously. They’ve never seen children like this—dressed in silks, eyes bright. Sera spins on the boards, barefoot and barely contained. She points directly at them and declares: “Ohhhkay! I’ve found our chosen from this country!” She twirls and beams at Soliera, radiant with pride. Sera picked them. She doesn’t know why yet. Neither do they. But the moment has just rethreaded the fate-web of Thay. Of course - Soliera had seen them from the horizon.

T371: Local Tension — The Lord’s Mistake #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-THAYAN-LORD #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE
• A rumble of hooves breaks the shoreline peace. Three carriages. Ten guards. The local lord’s vassal arrives—not with curiosity, but correction. A villager panics and tries to flee. Sera calls out: “You’re safe now. Why run from these people on horses?” The man freezes. Her voice rewrote something. The lord steps down, cloak fanning behind him, eyes on the women atop the dock. “You there, whores. Wha—” He never finishes. Sera is already there, one finger pressed against his chin. “Did you mean me? Or Soliera? Or Ember?” He panics. Reaches for her arm. Bad choice. She slaps his hand and it detonates—a splash of red and shredded bone painting the dirt behind him. A horn sounds from the ship. The Titan’s halberd rises. Intent is clear: move, and it wipes the group. The lord collapses in a puddle of his own piss, grasping his stump.

T372: The Cousins Learn the Mission — Their Aunt #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #CHAR-AUNT-LYSA #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE
• Daviya’s voice is steady but thin: “My mom died when I was eleven. His dad left for the mines and never came back. So we got each other. And an aunt. Somewhere.” Tirren adds: “She was taken. Slavers. The unofficial kind. Someone always pays them off.” Sera walks forward, grin completely gone. She brushes Daviya’s shoulder, then Tirren’s chest. “You didn’t tell us that part before.” Tirren shakes his head: “You didn’t ask.” Sera whispers: “We’ll go find her.” Daviya protests: “That’s not—you don’t have to—” “No,” Sera says. “I want to.” She turns and walks back toward Soliera. “If they still have your aunt, then they’re not just slavers. They’re already dead.”

T373: Divine Restoration — The Cousins Healed #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-THAY-COASTAL-BASE
• Daviya faints from exhaustion, heat, and lack of nutrition. Tirren catches her but stumbles—he’s equally frail. Sera calls softly: “Em? Can you come try to help them?” Ember approaches shyly, kneeling beside them. She looks to Soliera—not for permission, but for belief. Soliera watches. That’s everything. Ember lowers her hands. The glow from her palms isn’t fire—it’s warmth without burn, light without glare. Divine will without judgment. Under her touch, everything re-forms. Tirren’s cheek rounds out first. His left eye, clouded since childhood, flutters open clear. Daviya’s scar across her belly glows gold, then vanishes—not hidden, but healed. Her organs unwind from the cauterized mess left by slavers who didn’t care. She can breathe all the way in for the first time since the raid. They’re heavier now—fleshier, stronger. Returned to the versions of themselves they never got to be.

T374: The Bathhouse — Divine Domesticity #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-THE-ARK-BATHHOUSE #ITEM-THE-ARK
• The Ark contains luxuries beyond comprehension: heated stone floors, perfectly filtered crystal water, heat and air control, oils and shampoos in colors neither cousin has ever seen. Sera leads them to the bathhouse—a $300,000 room by real-world standards, just for bathing. Tirren stands neck-deep, overwhelmed, as Sera teases him relentlessly. Ember struggles to undress without staring at anyone. Daviya discovers what shampoo is and nearly cries. Then Soliera enters. She disrobes without ceremony—her body is that of a mother. Full breasts that hang naturally, a little pubic hair dark and kept, thick hips shaped by intent rather than labor. She steps into the water. It doesn’t ripple. It yields. The cousins are breathless. They’ve seen so many body types today they never thought they’d see. And they realize this isn’t seduction. This is comfort. This is being seen for the first time.

T375: Night on the Gazebo — Stars and Stories #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-THE-ARK-GAZEBO #ITEM-THE-ARK
• The evening air is perfectly warm. The gazebo’s cushions are the size of beds. The fire burns low and aromatic. Soliera is seated, robe loose. Sera is nestled beside her, half-draped over her lap. Ember watches the flame flicker across her own skin. The cousins hover at the edge, unsure where to sit. “Anywhere’s good,” Ember says quietly. They lower themselves onto cushions that sink like hugs. Tirren groans involuntarily. Daviya just stares—at sky, at fire, at Soliera. Sera asks, mouth full of honey fruit: “So? Did y’all have a good day?” Tirren laughs weakly: “I fainted. Then I cried. Then I almost died of embarrassment.” Daviya wipes her eye: “I think I did all of that and learned what shampoo was.” For the first time in their lives, they aren’t surviving anymore. They’re alive.

T376: Planning the Liberation — The Aunt’s Location #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-TIRREN #CHAR-DAVIYA #LOC-ELTABBAR #ITEM-THE-ARK
• Sera stretches languidly, one arm over Soliera’s thigh. “So what about your aunt? Where would she be?” Tirren’s posture tenses. “Aunt Lysa. She was taken last year. Slavers from the settlement west of us. The big one.” Daviya murmurs: “We thought she was dead. Or worse.” Ember asks: “Would she be happy for you?” Daviya pauses. “I don’t know. She might say we don’t deserve this.” Sera rolls to her knees, suddenly smiling. “Then tomorrow, we go get her.” Ember’s eyes glint. “Will they fight us?” Sera turns to look at Soliera, then back. “They can try.”
