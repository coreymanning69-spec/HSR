## TIER 2: INNER CIRCLE


#CHAR-VELIN

IDENTITY: Velin | (VAYLEN, VAYLIN - dictation)
VERSE: T/M
FACTION: Sanctum (Protocol/Stewardship)
ROLE: Diplomatic Steward | Embassy Coordinator | Sanctum Voice
STATUS: Retired Founder
INTRO: T6 | LAST: T431+ / M183+ active (M171-M175 debrief DM021_1; M183.1 Kiyuru letter routing)

ORIGIN:
- Human woman from the First Village, the same founding community that produced
  Reva; her presentation is elven-inspired without changing her human ancestry
- One of the original party from the First Village
- Already "mature young adult" when she joined — aware of her body and its influence
- Notably had trimmed her pubic hair before arriving, which taught Soliera about how people primp for others
- This early lesson about human presentation shaped Soliera's understanding of self-image

APPEARANCE:
- Female human; tall, elegant, and deliberately elven-inspired
- Diplomatic bearing; carries herself with practiced grace
- Often in formal Sanctum attire

ROLE IN SANCTUM:
- Primary diplomatic steward alongside Marrik
- Manages embassy affairs and external communications
- Cooks for Soliera when she's at the Sanctum (rotates with Marrik)
- Handles protocol — knows when to release captives, when to apologize for Sera
- "Experienced, long-suffering" — accepts Sera's volatility with patient hope

KEY MOMENTS:
- T46: In the kitchen cooking breakfast while Niv checks in
- T47: Releases Tali from the antechamber, gently apologizes for Sera's treatment
- T115: Established as steward at Waterdeep Embassy
- T431: Briefs the girls at the teleportation arrays before Avernus

ARC-STATE: #ARC-STATE-VELIN
- Early Sanctum (T6–T100): Professional, long-suffering calm. Trusts that Sera will eventually gain wisdom. Smooths the wake constantly. She is not anxious — she is patient in the way that infrastructure is patient.
- Post-Tiamat / Mergeverse: Productive anxiety as a lifestyle. When the divine trio is elsewhere, she bakes bread at 4:30 AM and builds logistical empires in silence while everyone around her panics. Her calm is the frame the household hangs on.
- Sera's Friend: Sera routinely pulls Velin and other friends or Sanctum staff
  into errands, meals, outings, and spontaneous changes of plan, sometimes
  literally carrying or dragging them along. M174 is this ordinary friendship
  grammar, not an unresolved secret mission.

#CHAR-MARRIK

IDENTITY: Marrik
VERSE: T
FACTION: Sanctum (Logistics/Stewardship)
ROLE: Chief Steward | Caravan Logistics | Trade/Context Founder | Fixer
STATUS: Active
INTRO: T5 | LAST: T110

ORIGIN:
- Traveling from Mirabar toward Luskan when the First Festival drew his attention
- The festival formed off-season because Soliera and Deashi's local presence made the region prosperous: more food, more pregnancies, more health, and early worship momentum
- Met Soliera and Deashi at the First Festival / First Village harvest gathering (T5)
- His mind and body were sharpened by Soliera's blessing during the early remaking
- Wife and children live in the Sanctum
- Represents the first generation of mortals directly elevated by divine contact

APPEARANCE:
- Male human
- Practical, capable bearing; reads rooms, roads, accounts, and people with the same clean precision
- Often working — hands never idle

ROLE IN SANCTUM:
- Founding chief steward; retired from daily Sanctum operations
- "Marrik can fix anything, Soliera decides" — DM01-T95
- Manages caravan logistics, supplies, practical operations, maps, trade routes, and languages
- Brought in the cloth, material context, workers, social habits, and mortal-side domestic infrastructure that let the non-construct Sanctum begin actual life
- Cooks for Soliera when she's home (rotates with Velin)
- Heals those injured by Sera's "lessons" (quietly, without complaint)
- Retires with more wealth than anyone on earth and the keys to the Sanctum
  treasury as part of the retirement. His budget is functionally unlimited: if he
  wants to buy a country, the country knows the Sanctum is good for it. This is
  post-currency dignity, not ordinary riches.

KEY MOMENTS:
- T5: Met at the First Festival while traveling Mirabar-to-Luskan; drawn into the first mortal orbit
- T7-T12: Organizes food, rooms, travel logistics, supply runs, and the first practical trade links
- T10: Remade into a trade/map/language/concept genius whose first instinct is generational wealth and infrastructure
- T46: Heals Tali's bruised rib after Sera's treatment
- T49: Again heals Tali with "gentle practicality"

ARC-STATE: #ARC-STATE-MARRIK
- Early Sanctum (T5-T50): Guile and logistics at mortal scale. He sees the impossible and immediately starts building a supply chain around it. Not a prophet, not a warrior-king; the man who makes the chairs, cloth, food, trade routes, and social expectations arrive before anyone realizes they are missing.
- Retired Founder: Obscenely wealthy, permanently so, and finished with full
  dignity. The T10 remaking converted a capable logistical mind into the greatest
  mercantile and supply-chain intellect of his era. Treasury keys make the payout
  literal; his family dynasty and infrastructure continue without requiring his
  labor.

#CHAR-HOTHGAR

IDENTITY: Hothgar | Hothgar the Great
VERSE: T
FACTION: Sanctum-adjacent Founder | Tundra Tribes
ROLE: Barbarian King | Founder of Hothgar's Hoard | Early Escort
STATUS: Active / Retired Founder
INTRO: T5 | LAST: T100+

ORIGIN:
- Large tundra barbarian who met Soliera, Deashi, Reva, and Marrik at the First Festival
- Honest, helpful, and socially direct; immediately useful because he does the obvious hard thing without dramatizing it
- Enjoyed Velin's company on the early Sanctum trip; adult intimacy with Velin, Thorin, and Cale is part of the T8 camp grammar
- Carried Reva for stretches during the first pilgrimage when her body could not keep pace

APPEARANCE:
- Remade at T10 into a 7'2" human warlord frame: enormous, charismatic, and physically overwhelming
- Barbarian-king presence by every practical metric; less a courtly ruler than a man people naturally follow

ROLE IN SANCTUM:
- Muscle, protection, morale, and tribal legitimacy during the first pilgrimage
- Built Hothgar's Hoard: thousands-strong tribal army/migration network backed by Sanctum supplies
- United local and then regional tribes of many folk and ilk under prosperity rather than conquest
- By T50, much of the village and herd migration into Sanctum underground holdings was driven by Hothgar the Great and his Hoard

KEY MOMENTS:
- T5: First Festival ally during the earliest worship formation
- T6-T7: Joins the escort to the Sanctum; carries Reva when needed and holds the group's physical center
- T10: Remade into the 7'2" charismatic barbarian-king body that becomes his public myth
- T11-T50: Converts Sanctum abundance into regional migration, alliance, and prosperity

ARC-STATE: #ARC-STATE-HOTHGAR
- Early Sanctum (T5-T50): Honest force made useful. He understands Soliera's orbit as opportunity and duty, not abstraction. He does not need theology to recognize food, warmth, protection, and a better future.
- Retired Founder: Barbarian King and uniter of the north. Hothgar's final achievement was not the Hoard but what it enabled — the unification of disparate northern tribes, peoples, and regions under a single coherent structure built on prosperity rather than conquest. He is the man who brought the north together, and it holds. Retired from Soliera's direct orbit, his presence is geopolitical rather than personal; the north answers to the structure he built.
- He leaves to rule the barbarians of the tundras as his own man. The Hoard was
  apprenticeship for a throne, not a permanent obligation to remain in the girls'
  active party.

## FOUNDING-GENERATION AGE AND RETIREMENT RULE

All founding helpers retire with dignity unless they choose to remain active.
Velin and Niv stay because they choose to; silence around the others after T50 is
conclusion, not reduction. Hothgar and Marrik entered as grown men in roughly their
twenties through forties, with prior lives and finite arcs. The Letha cohort entered
young, around eighteen, and grew inside the divine household. The older founders
relate as completed colleagues; the younger cohort attaches as family. The same
architecture recurs with Wren and Doran as stewards-in-training.
