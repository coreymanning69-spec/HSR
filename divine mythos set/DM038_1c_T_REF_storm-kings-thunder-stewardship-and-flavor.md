## Stewardship Crucible — Doran & Wren

**Current status (T537.0+):** Doran and Wren are full Tier-3 Sanctum field
Stewards. Recruit and trainee language below describes their T522–T536
crucible history; it is not their current rank. For T537.0-forward true-giant
mechanics, retrieve #RULE-SKT-GIANT-SCALE rather than restating it here.

**Selection and launch:** Niv identifies Doran and Wren as viable candidates.
Velin approves their readiness and gives the mission brief. She may travel with
the party whenever she wants; there is no structural travel ban, one-leg limit,
or never-travels rule. Her permission to come does not make her a required
traveler or an ordinary rolling character. The active live ledger owns the
continuation edge, current values, and what changed.

**Mechanical party:** Doran and Wren are the only ordinary rolling adventurers.
They make checks, roll initiative, spend hit points and limited resources,
investigate, negotiate, navigate, and decide which mortal-scale problems to
engage. Where they fumble mortal interaction, the test is surfacing; no senior
steward appears to patch the gap.

Their competence is a floor, not a substitute personality. Do not reduce Wren
to an Investigation bonus or Doran to Athletics and attack lines. Let each
observe, reason, speak, and choose before deciding whether genuine uncertainty
requires a roll.

**One mode, not two (presence doctrine):** ONE MODE, NOT TWO. Do not split the
  arc into a field-Steward-rollable surface thread and a girls-only deep thread. The
single authoritative protection statement is: **"Soliera won't allow anyone
She's personally following or watching to die or suffer needlessly."**
"Needlessly" is load-bearing. The crucible can still cost injury, capture,
failed objectives, spent resources, tactical defeat, retreat, and suffering
  that belongs to the test rather than pointless harm. The field Stewards make their
own checks, spend resources, fight, and eat real failure on the things they
choose to engage. Big-scale antagonists (Iymrith, Kraken Society, any
  deity-level presence) are never keyed as field-Steward-faceable and are never
rebalanced downward. Preserve them at full source weight; the girls resolve
them when encountered.

**Intervention, not shielding.** The Crucible is not a bubble. Dice ride as they
fall. Field Stewards can be hit, injured, captured, wrong, or forced to retreat. Divine
care intervenes at the needless-death-and-suffering boundary; it does not erase the
field state required to reach that boundary. Equipment is the standing care
channel — direct rhyme from Korin/Letha at T32 to Doran/Wren's T522–T525 gifts.

**Care is on-notice, not ambient.** Attention is the divine act: what God notices
exists at full resolution. This is why Doran's natural 1s route through judgment,
social choice, or body discipline instead of preventable physiology the girls have
already noticed and removed.

**Literal Removal guard.** The girls remove concepts such as heat, cold, thirst,
or pain from their *personal effect on nearby people*. They do not rewrite the
ambient environment merely by relieving someone. Chult remains a furnace while the
person stops suffering it. Mortal dialogue may misread this as weather alteration;
do not promote that imprecision into mechanism.

**Failure doctrine:** Failure means lost objectives, spent resources, injury,
capture, mishandled people, overlooked evidence, tactical defeat, retreat, or
demonstrated social unreadiness. It does not require permanent death to matter.
The field Stewards must be able to lose, not forced to lose. Dice stay live, bluffs
can be called, and worst-case outcomes remain real even when unlikely. Do not
narrate them into wins because they are good; roll the meaningful pressure
points. Bryn Shander's T523 boulder redirect is the canonical example: the
brace held on live dice, the redirect worked, and the cracked rib was the
receipt.

**Backstop distinction:** Routing intelligence to Velin, Vajra, the Sanctum, or
another legitimate channel is tradecraft, not a flinch. Asking the girls to
come handle the problem is the flinch. Sera remains a Chekhov's gun the
field Stewards are reluctant to touch; invoking her should feel like failing the
exam. The T524 plan threads this correctly: Doran and Wren in front for talk
and light fighting, gods behind as the floor under the exam, not the answer.

**Advancement (T537.0+):** XP is frozen at 165,000 for both field Stewards and
is no longer an advancement basis. The active live ledger owns the current value; DM035_0e
receives only later reviewed adaptation, injury, equipment, or status receipts.

**Camera:** Field-Steward POV from T537.0 forward; trainee POV is the historical
T522–T536 label. Soliera, Sera, and Ember render as weather without
becoming passive scenery. Sera remains within approximately fifteen feet of
Soliera and intervenes by whim, never as a dependable party resource. Ember is
an adult woman and active inquiry presence. Never voice Soliera or Deashi.

**Character authority:** DM041_0 owns characterization, draft biography, launch
sheets, and historical mechanics. DM041_A owns Doran's source combat mechanics;
DM041_B owns Wren's source spellcasting and combat mechanics; DM044_0 and
DM044_1 are the paired encounter runtime. DM004_2 owns Tier 3
roster metadata. The active live ledger owns current levels and resources; DM035_0e owns
adaptation and advancement receipts.

**Gear as character:** Treat tools as extensions of who the field Stewards are, not
only attack lines. Doran planting the Giant Cleaver can be an honor play; the
constraint that he cannot yet swing it is part of the scene. Brace-and-redirect
is discipline, not a damage trick. Wren's glasses are a POV and exposition
device; Projected Ward and dimension door express protect-and-extract doctrine.

**Glasses give data, not meaning:** Wren's Weave-sight reads magical structure.
It can tell her that Sirac's iron ring has no magical structure; it cannot tell
her whether the ring matters as decoy, key, keepsake, or grief. The day the
glasses answer human meaning, investigation dies.

**Antagonists have internal life:** Keep giving opposition conflicts that do
not revolve around the heroes. Drufi killing Guthvarr to preserve her sworn
word is the model: honor, succession pressure, fear, ambition, and doctrine can
move sideways to the PCs. This is how the module becomes a living Forgotten
Realms problem instead of a sequence of rooms.


## Flavor Doctrine and Texture Notes

These notes supply ambient texture for wilderness travel, giant-territory scenes, and the general register of a world under ordning pressure. They are not plot events and not foreshadowing in the mechanical sense — they are the background frequency against which plot events occur. Pull what a given moment calls for; none of it is obligatory.

### Giant-Kin Pressure: Feral Register

Ogres, ettins, and trolls operated for generations under invisible ordning pressure — not the full ordning, but a subordinate tier that derived behavioral structure from the hierarchy above them. With the ordning collapsed, that structure is gone and the effects are visible in the field.

Ettins' two heads have always disagreed. Under the ordning they disagreed in a managed way — a contained argument with a functional outcome. Now the arguments are escalating toward splitting decisions, and both choices tend to be bad. Ogre packs are forming in unusual configurations, without the coherent hierarchy that normally prevents them from fighting each other more than anything else. Trolls are ranging into territory they have avoided for decades — not because they are coordinating with anything, but because the invisible boundary signals are gone. They are not aware of being out of bounds. There are no more bounds.

Use as background texture in any wilderness encounter involving these creatures. Not a plot development, not a signal about an encounter's outcome — the ambient consequence of a cosmological structure losing its keystone. The feral behavior predates the girls' arrival and does not resolve until the ordning is restored.

### Ordning Defector Graffiti

In wilderness areas under ordning pressure — mountain passes, boulder fields, the margins of giant-traffic corridors through the North — crude giant-script is carved into rock faces and boulders. Not signposts. Not warnings to mortals. A cultural argument being conducted in stone by beings too separated to meet.

Some carvings assert rank the old way: name, lineage, the deed that justifies the claim. These are traditional ordning declarations, carved into cliff faces because there is no court left to declare before. Some renounce rank entirely — abbreviated, blunt, using the script for finality. A few carvings overwrite earlier inscriptions; in giant culture this is an act of specific contempt, equivalent to speaking over someone in a formal assembly. The argument is ongoing and has no venue except stone.

For mortals, this is illegible without giant-script literacy. For those who can read it: encountering these carvings is a diagnostic of the ordning collapse from the inside, in voices that predate the girls' arrival. Not a puzzle, not a clue toward any specific outcome. Background frequency — the giant world's internal argument made physical and durable, visible to anyone who knows where to look. Presence of new carvings near older ones indicates recent giant traffic through an area even when no giant is visible.

Use as texture on overland travel through giant-territory margins, particularly Ch. 3 Savage Frontier legs and any passage through the Spine of the World approach.

### Animals as Early Signal

The natural world registers giant activity before mortals do. Use as a diagnostic layer for travel scenes, particularly Ch. 3 Savage Frontier overland legs and any wilderness passage in giant-territory.

Deer won't cross certain mountain passes — not because giants are there now, but because something large has been using those passes regularly enough that the deer's behavioral geography has rerouted around them. Lakes near stone giant territory have seen fishing decline; the vibration-work on the lake bed has shifted bottom sediment enough to displace the deepwater fish. A shepherd who mentions that his flock has refused a particular valley for six weeks is giving an indirect seismic report.

This is not mystical. It is the predictable consequence of very large things moving through an ecology with regularity. The party can read the natural world as a diagnostic for giant activity before anyone sees a giant — ask the right NPC the right question. The shepherd, the trapper, the fisherwoman keeping the wrong kind of records about a catch that used to be reliable: all of these are early signal. The shape of where giants have been becomes visible through where animals are not.
