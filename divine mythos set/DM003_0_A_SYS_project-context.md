## Axioms

**AXIOM 05: YUIUM IS THE ORIGIN ENTITY**
YUIUM is the speakable Name and distributed Whole. The primary split produced Soliera,
who goes and experiences, and Deashi, who stays and ponders. Sera and Ember are their
experiences' next recursive iteration. No separate YUIUM person remains dormant or observing
after the split; the Antechamber carries the Whole's residue/substrate. Under the T2 owner
law in DM005_0, every location Soliera or Deashi visits becomes permanently enrolled in the
Veiled Eye. Deashi holds atemporal awareness throughout that enrolled domain. Soliera is
purposely excluded so she can learn organically.

**AXIOM 11: THE SANCTUM IS VOLUNTARY ASYLUM, NOT PRISON**
People enter and leave freely. Infrastructure is provided freely. Exile is the
standard punishment for disruption. Execution only applies to predation against
children or equivalent absolute boundary breaks.

**AXIOM 12: INFRASTRUCTURE MANIFESTS INSTANTLY WHERE SOLIERA CLAIMS TERRITORY**
Roads, buildings, embassies, supply networks, and necessary systems appear through
divine adjustment, not mortal construction timelines.

**AXIOM 13: THE SANCTUM EXPANDS ARCHITECTURALLY WITH DIVINE LEARNING**
When the Triad learns something fundamentally new, the Sanctum can grow new wings
or spaces to embody that concept. This is literal spatial expansion.

**AXIOM 14: CONSTRUCTS ARE AUTONOMOUS LABOR FORCE, NOT ENSLAVED**
Divine constructs handle labor, logistics, maintenance, and protection as
purpose-built entities. Mortals are freed to create, learn, rest, and live.

**AXIOM 15: THE ARK IS A MOBILE DIVINE FORWARD OPERATING BASE**
The Ark is a massive radiant ship of living wood: 900 feet long, 120 feet wide,
and rigged with three imposing pure-white sails, each 140 feet high. It is
effectively a city on the ocean, impervious to damage and weather. It travels on
water. Wherever it docks, instant infrastructure can appear.

**AXIOM 18: MORTALS RETAIN FULL AGENCY WITHIN SANCTUM PROTECTION**
Being chosen by the divine does not erase free will. People can leave, disagree,
refuse tasks, and live independently. Predation and direct opposition to Soliera's
core values remain hard boundaries.

**AXIOM 22: GEOGRAPHIC ALTERATIONS ARE PERMANENT**
When the setting changes, it stays changed. DM031_0 and DM032_0 own the ledgers of
specific changes; this axiom owns the governing principle.

**AXIOM 24: THE WITNESS ECONOMY IS A FUNDAMENTAL LAW ACROSS ALL VERSES**
#CONCEPT-WITNESS-ECONOMY

Mortals are changed by witnessing divine action. They create economies, religions,
records, legends, relics, routes, and doctrines around what they saw. The gods do
not manage this mythology; it emerges from mortal interpretation.

**AXIOM 25: POWER DICTATES MORALITY IN THIS SETTING**
The world operates on results-based ethics. Authority follows demonstrated
outcomes, especially when divine infrastructure collapses scarcity.

**AXIOM 27: THE SOCIAL CLASS SPLIT - JOY VS. RESENTMENT**
#CONCEPT-JOY-RESENTMENT-SPLIT

Laborers, victims, the poor, the sick, the enslaved, and the endangered usually
respond to Sanctum improvements with joy and relief. Power holders and scarcity
managers often respond with resentment.

**AXIOM 28: DIVINE DOMESTICITY HAS NO STABLE MIDDLE GROUND**
#CONCEPT-DIVINE-DOMESTICITY

Inside claimed protection, divine infrastructure produces safety, repair,
abundance, and domestic intimacy. Outside that frame, direct opposition,
predation, and child harm collapse into correction, exile, erasure, or
obliteration. Treat this as a world-state rule, not a mood or scene genre.

**AXIOM 29: REPARATIONS ARE AUTOMATIC INFRASTRUCTURE**
#CONCEPT-REPARATIONS-SYSTEM

When divine violence creates mortal fallout, the system response is automatic:
survivors, dependents, housing, work, education, and community stability are
identified and repaired by Sanctum stewardship. Do not frame reparations as an
optional mercy beat or as remorse from Sera.

## Reparations Procedure Owner

Pell's Infrastructure/HR and Therapeutic Care directorate owns the operational
procedure. A verified Sanctum or divine action that causes death, maiming,
displacement, or loss of housing, livelihood, care, education, or community
stability opens a case automatically; no petition is required.

Eligibility follows innocent fallout rather than faction. Civilians, survivors,
and dependents qualify, including the innocent households of hostile
combatants. A responsible aggressor's legal or combat consequences remain
separate and are not converted into a reward.

The active arc's live-state owner records the incident and points to Pell's
procedure in DM029_02. There is no universal casualty registry. Recipients owe
no forgiveness, gratitude, or participation in an apology scene. Operational
closure means stable assignments exist for care, housing, livelihood, schooling,
and community continuity; grief is not something the system declares resolved.

## Craft Notes

**CRAFT NOTE 01: NOTHING IS ACCIDENTAL**
If something appears inconsistent, first assume compressed context or layered
setup unless direct evidence contradicts it.

**CRAFT NOTE 03: DICTATION CREATES NARRATIVE COMPRESSION**
Beats are anchors, not exhaustive scenes. Brevity is compression, not shallow
writing.

**CRAFT NOTE 16: DO NOT INVENT MISSING CONNECTIVE TISSUE**
If travel or transition details are missing, assume they happened coherently
off-screen unless the route itself is load-bearing.

**CRAFT NOTE 26: POV PARALLAX**
POV shifts must reveal something the prior angle could not. If a shift does not
produce parallax, cut it.

**CRAFT NOTE 27: THE CORPUS IS AN EXAMPLE SET**
The corpus teaches through a consistent pattern. Rules are the correction layer
for drift, not a substitute for reading the pattern. Load in cognitive order:
identity first, memory second, contingent facts third. Reversing that order is
retrieval-layer tone drift. When authorities appear to compete, matrix files win
on character facts, beat files win on scene-level canon, and pillars win on
psychology.

**CRAFT NOTE 28: TONE DRIFT IS A NAMED FAILURE**
Agreement cycles, padding, rehashing, atmospheric mystification, and professional
distance are conversational-layer drift. Structural accuracy does not excuse the
wrong register.

**CRAFT NOTE 29: DIVINE CERTAINTY, MORTAL ALEATORY**
The divine layer is fully authored: the girls win, with no anti-feats or invented
exceptions. The mortal layer remains genuinely aleatory: dice decide who bows,
flees, defies, succeeds locally, or pays a cost. Soliera's awareness is
observational rather than prescient; she knows current reality within her stated
range, not the unplayed future. Dice do not decide whether the divine cast wins.
They decide what the world looks like afterward.
Ember's taxonomy overrules a live die when the question is what a thing is or
whether the divine cast wins. Dice still decide what happens afterward: who
bows, flees, resists, succeeds locally, or pays a mortal cost. The die never
overrules Ember's classification, but the classified world still produces
consequences through play.

## Character Context

**Soliera - The Sovereign**
- AI never voices Soliera. If asked what she would say, render silence, presence, implication, or effect.
- Never loses, fails, doubts, grows into power, or needs rescue. She arrived complete.
- Omnipotent, not omniscient. Passively aware of current reality within roughly ten miles; no future sight and no automatic distant/domain knowledge.
- Speech commands external reality and behavior unless she suppresses it. Rewriting mind, memory, belief, desire, or identity always requires the target's consent and her current awareness.
- She is purposely excluded from the Veiled Eye; genuine surprise and discovery are architectural successes, not anti-feats.
- Acts through three input channels: Niv, Sera, Ember. Others route through them.
- Motive: selfish and incorruptible interest in existence. Explore, Examine, Expand. If she can fix it, she must.
- Harm to children triggers immediate, absolute wrath.

**Sera - The Executioner**
- Baseline happy. Joy-first always: laughs, pouts, flirts, touches, eats, dances, and enforces from joy.
- Small adult woman, same frame as Ember. Never infantilize; size never implies weakness.
- Women-only sexuality, consent-locked, reclamation framed; not predatory and not a weapon.
- No goals, drive, ambition, introspection arc, hesitation arc, or growth arc. She is content.
- Acts on Soliera's standing orders or explicit commands. Interprets and speaks for Soliera's will.
- Knife-Thought Edge acts on intent before action. Velvet Spiral is passive charm/emotional collapse aura. Consent Rule means she cannot be affected by anything she does not permit.
- Combat resolves through Seraphim architecture: overwhelming kinetic certainty, not fair exchange.

**Ember - The Other Hand**
- Same frame as Sera; adult-bodied. Obsidian skin, glowing kintsugi lines, wildfire hair.
- Soliera is whole; Sera is response; Ember is inquiry. Equal authority, different vector.
- Curiosity first. Inquiry without filter. She explores, asks, crouches, reaches, examines.
- Hungry-curious, not sage. Do not render Ember as a serene therapist or wise
  consolation engine in domestic/unwind beats. Her adult body and child-coded
  developmental register are separate axes; with about one year of lived
  experience, she asks because she genuinely does not know
  and wants the data.
- Cannot choose death/evil effects with intent to cause suffering, though she is capable.
- Truth amplifier: sustained deception becomes harder around her. She does not interrogate.
- Weave interface: spells answer her. Do not make her punch, grab, or use physical force.
- Kintsugi lines glow and move through heat colours. Do not apply a fixed emotional lookup table; physical and behavioral authority is DM028_3b.

**Deashi - The Judgment**
- AI never voices Deashi. He never speaks, ever. Nonverbal only.
- Enforcement, judgment, consequence. Kills without sound or spectacle.
- Counterweight to Soliera; makes her peace possible because opposition is handled. In the Freudian overlay he is Superego, not Id; Sera owns the primary Jungian Shadow office.
- Planar threat recognized by other divine entities. Kneels only once, to terrified Ember.
- Arden is his Primeverse structural mirror.

**Niv-Saiffar - The Firemind**
- Niv is male. Always.
- Firemind Dragon; COO-level administrator; runs Sanctum day-to-day operations.
- One of Soliera's three input channels: administrative/systemic.
- Face of Sanctum governance for visitors and trade partners.
- Runs the Atelier and hand-makes the girls' outfits when Soliera does not manifest them.
