### LOCATION 01: THE SANCTUM
**[MAP POSITION]** Spine of the World, far north - the most isolated hospitable zone in the mountain range
**[STATUS]** Home Base - established `T0`
**[TRAVEL ACCESS]** All roads begin and end here. Master teleport hub. The Ark launches from Waterdeep but is resupplied from here via construct freight.
**[WORD OF MOUTH]** Source region. The Sanctum is ordinary civic reality here,
and the trio are known through direct presence rather than report.

**Setting Changes (20+)**
1. The Sanctum is wheel-shaped: concentric rings connected by spoke-corridors, expanding into deep tunnel systems through the mountain. The outermost ring is public-facing (markets, schools, guest housing). The innermost ring is divine-residential. The tunnels between rings contain everything else.
2. Population: 800,000 to 1.2 million residents at peak. Mix of humans, dwarves, elves, half-races, constructs, and a small population of formerly hostile creatures (kobolds, goblins) who accepted Sanctum civic terms.
3. Niv-Saiffar (Firemind Dragon, male) runs day-to-day operations. He is the face of Sanctum governance for all mortal visitors and trade partners. Soliera is the authority. Niv is the administrator. The distinction is understood by all insiders and confused by all outsiders.
4. Sera has a private wing. It is decorated entirely in things Soliera has given her or things she has made herself. No construct maintains it - she maintains it. This is considered eccentric by Sanctum residents and deeply understood by Soliera.
5. Deashi holds a throne near Soliera's private quarters. It has never been described as ornate. It is simply the place where he is. Residents know not to loiter in that hallway.
6. Ember's living quarters are adjacent to the main garden courtyard. The garden was originally a utility space. After Ember arrived, Soliera had it replanted. This has never been explicitly acknowledged.
7. The construct population performs all physical labor: mining, farming, road maintenance, construction, fishing, sanitation. This frees mortal residents to specialize entirely in craft, art, trade, education, military training, or administration.
8. The Sanctum maintains schools organized by race and caste - not to segregate, but to give each group tools specific to their biology and culture. A dwarf child learning metalcraft gets different instruction than a human child learning the same. The outputs are comparable; the methods differ.
9. The trading system is post-scarcity within the Sanctum's walls. Basic necessities (food, shelter, water, basic clothing) are provided. Luxury goods are traded. Currency still functions but is not required for survival. This has created a distinct Sanctum economic culture that outsiders find confusing or threatening.
10. The coliseum (built early arc) functions as the primary entertainment venue, civic meeting hall, and public announcement space. It seats 40,000. Soliera has attended three events since it was built. The fact that she could attend again is sufficient to keep the space maintained to obsessive standards.
11. The Sanctum road system begins here: six major roads extending like spokes outward from the wheel city toward Ten-Towns, Mirabar, two fishing village clusters, a glacial tribal area, and a currently sealed passage toward the Underdark that Deashi surveyed once and did not comment on.
12. The teleport circle chamber is a dedicated room in the administrative ring. It contains eight circles in a geometric arrangement. Niv coordinates scheduling on parchment boards that get updated hourly.
13. The Sanctum blackbird symbol (Mark of Dominion) appears on every road milestone, every embassy wall, every construct chassis, and every treaty document the Sanctum has ever issued. It is a territorial marker understood across Faerûn and its connected routes.
14. Sanctum law operates on three tiers: Soliera's will (implicit, never written, always enforced), Niv's administrative decrees (written, publicly posted, regularly updated), and civic consensus (mortal residents vote on local ordinances, which Niv can veto, which Soliera has never had to override).
15. The Sanctum operates a diplomatic stewardship program. Velin leads it. Trained mortal diplomats (called Stewards) are assigned to each embassy. They are the daily face of the Sanctum in foreign cities. Most are former refugees or people the girls rescued. They are fiercely loyal.
16. Missionary work is the Sanctum's primary soft-power tool: sending goods, constructs, trained personnel, and road infrastructure into neighboring areas without political demands. The only ask is acknowledgment that the Sanctum exists and that its people are protected under guest-right wherever they travel.
17. The Sanctum has never publicly declared itself an empire, a religion, a guild, or a government. This ambiguity is intentional and occasionally maddening to the Lords' Alliance and Harpers alike.
18. Sanctum cuisine is now famous across the North. The combination of unlimited construct labor, deep-mountain fresh water, and Soliera's early interest in what people eat has produced a food culture that fuses every racial tradition represented in the population. People come to the Sanctum from Silverymoon just to eat.
19. The Sanctum maintains a Hall of Records: every person who has ever lived, worked, or passed through is documented. Births, deaths, trades, crimes, and notable events. Velin oversees this. It is the most complete demographic record in the North.
20. The Mark of Dominion operates as a practical deterrent. Any building, road, or vessel bearing the Sanctum's blackbird symbol is understood to be under divine protection. Attacking it is understood to be an attack on the girls. In `T515`, this has created an informal peace zone across every Sanctum-touched territory - not because any treaty was signed, but because no one with survival instincts wants to test what happens next.
21. The Sanctum is technically invisible from outside the mountain range unless you know where to look. There are no towers above the peaks. The warmth that emanates is the only ambient tell. This is by design.
22. Sera's standing instruction to constructs regarding Ember: she is never to be physically prevented from going anywhere, but she is also never to be more than two constructs' distance from a Sanctum adult. This has resulted in Ember having the most elaborately casual "security detail" in history - constructs who appear to be doing maintenance tasks that happen to follow her everywhere.

### Physical Architecture and Rendering Law

The Sanctum is built at dragon scale, in white marble. Its doors, halls, and
clearances are large because dragons work and move through the building; the
scale is not aimed at impressing a viewer. Sanctum grandeur is therefore
incidental and utilitarian rather than display, statement, or competitive
wealth. Scenes should preserve that distinction.

**Embassy Status**
MASTER HUB. All other embassies answer here. The embassy chamber in the administrative ring houses Velin's full diplomatic staff (forty-plus Stewards), a communication post for receiving messenger constructs from all foreign embassies, and a crisis-response unit that can mobilize within minutes.

**DM Notes**
The Sanctum is rarely the setting of a session - it is the frame. Opening beats that begin here should focus on the contrast between Sanctum-normal (infinite competence, casual abundance, Niv doing twelve things simultaneously) and what's about to change when the girls go outside. Niv handing Sera a freshly bound travel itinerary that Velin prepared in triplicate is a good visual beat. Sera immediately sitting on Soliera is also a good beat.
