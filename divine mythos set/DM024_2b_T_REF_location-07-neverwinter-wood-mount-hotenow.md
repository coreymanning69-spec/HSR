### LOCATION 07: NEVERWINTER WOOD / MOUNT HOTENOW
**[MAP POSITION]** East of Neverwinter, surrounding the city on its inland side. Mount Hotenow rises from the center of the forest.
**[STATUS]** FIRST VISIT
**[TRAVEL ACCESS]** Overland from Neverwinter’s eastern gate. No roads past the forest edge.
**[WORD OF MOUTH]** Remote/local-cosmology version. The wood’s inhabitants—fey, wood elves, and creatures of the old forest—know the Sanctum through their own information networks. Their version is closer to a named weather pattern than a political entity.

**Setting Changes (20+)**
1. Neverwinter Wood predates the city it surrounds by thousands of years. The wood elves who live in its interior have watched Neverwinter be built, burn, be rebuilt, explode, and begin rebuilding again. Their perspective on mortal cities is one of patient detachment.
2. Mount Hotenow’s eruption in 1451 DR created a permanent scar through the southeastern portion of the forest: a two-mile-wide lava flow corridor, now cooled to black rock, where nothing grows. The fire elementals below consider this corridor their territory. Wood elves avoid it. City engineers have been examining whether the cooled lava can be quarried.
3. The lava corridor has created an inadvertent road — the cooled rock is flat and relatively smooth. Someone has been using it as a path at night. The tracks are not humanoid.
4. The forest has a layered Fey presence — a Summer Court satellite court maintains a small reflecting-pool glade approximately four miles northeast of the city. The gate to the corresponding Feywild location opens on midsummer nights and closes within the hour. The wood elves know about it. The city does not.
5. The Fey court that uses this gate is a minor noble’s traveling party — not politically significant in Feywild terms, but culturally rich. They have been using this gate for approximately three hundred years and will be annoyed if the girls’ visit disrupts the schedule.
6. There is a green dragon’s old lair in the deep wood — abandoned since before the eruption. The dragon (Chuth-adjacent lineage, not Chuth himself) relocated when the eruption changed the chemical composition of the groundwater. The lair still contains residual poison gas pockets. Wood elves mark the area with white stones. Travelers who miss the white stones have not returned.
7. A significant population of Spriggans (fey-adjacent trickster creatures) lives in the root systems of the oldest trees in the wood’s interior. They steal things. They return things. The math of what gets stolen vs what gets returned has never balanced in a way anyone can explain.
8. The wood’s inner canopy is dense enough that midday light reaches the forest floor as green-filtered shadow. Sound behaves differently in there — voices carry at strange angles, echoes that don’t make geometric sense.
9. There is a stream in the forest that runs uphill for approximately seventy feet before leveling out and running normally again. This has been studied by three separate Neverwinter mages. None of them have found an explanation. The water is normal water. The stream bed is normal rock. The water simply goes uphill.
10. The wood elves who inhabit the forest are not politically affiliated with any external authority. They acknowledge Neverwinter’s existence. They do not acknowledge Neverwinter’s authority over the forest. This is a quiet ongoing tension that Neverember has wisely chosen not to escalate.
11. The eruption killed approximately sixty wood elves who were in the forest’s southeastern section when the lava flows hit. This is remembered. The wood elf elder council has not officially blamed Neverwinter (the eruption is a natural/elemental event, not the city’s fault) but the proximity of the tragedy to mortal settlement is noted. Some younger elves are less measured about this.
12. Mount Hotenow has a physical path to its summit — originally carved by the fire elemental cult that used to operate here before the eruption. The path is mostly intact. The summit is a caldera of black rock and smoking vents. The fire elementals do not welcome surface visitors, but they have not actively prevented the path from being used.
13. There is a creature at the summit — not an elemental — that has been there since before recorded history. It does not interact with visitors. It watches. It is approximately the size of a house cat. Nothing about this is normal and the fire elementals refuse to discuss it.
14. The forest floor in the northern section contains a root network that connects most of the oldest trees. This root network transmits chemical signals — essentially the forest has a nervous system. The wood elves are aware of this and use it as a crude alarm system. When something significant enters the forest, the trees know before the elves do.
15. The Spriggans have a specific interest in clockwork objects. Since Neverwinter clockmakers started operating near the forest edge, Spriggan theft has been almost entirely focused on small clock components. Several workshop owners have a standing arrangement: leave three clock-gears on the forest edge on Tuesday, get three other objects returned (always objects they haven’t seen in years, always in good condition) on Wednesday.
16. There is a section of the forest where all the trees grow in a perfect circle approximately a hundred feet in diameter. No tree grows inside the circle. The grass inside is always the same height regardless of season. The wood elves call this place a name that translates roughly to “where the old argument ended.” They do not explain further.
17. Something large has been moving through the forest at night for the past two months, moving southwest. It leaves no tracks. The trees’ root network has been registering what the wood elves describe as “a very old thing that is walking carefully.” They have not investigated. The Fey court that uses the midsummer gate canceled their last visit without explanation.
18. A specific type of mushroom grows only in the lava corridor and nowhere else in the known world. It is a deep red-black color. It has been studied by four alchemists. Three died during the study. The fourth concluded that the mushroom is “definitely alive and possibly aware” and relocated to Waterdeep.
19. The wood elves have a standing protocol for encounters with powerful outsiders: they observe for three days before making contact. If the outsiders are still in the forest on day four, an elder will appear and ask three questions. The questions are never the same twice. The answers determine what happens next.
20. Mount Hotenow’s fire elementals are aware that Deashi exists. They became aware during the period of his Icewind Dale activity. Their response to this awareness is the elemental equivalent of extreme caution: they have been significantly reducing their surface-adjacent activity since learning about him. This is why the eruption activity has been at a stable low level for the past decade. The connection has never been publicly identified.

**Hooks & Quests (TR-17 through TR-19)**
* **TR-17:** The large thing moving southwest through the forest toward Neverwinter. This connects to the Kraken sighting — or does it?They may be unrelated. Or the forest thing may be a creature fleeing whatever the Kraken is fleeing from.
* **TR-18:** The wood elf elder’s three questions. If the girls are in the forest on day four, the elder appears. The questions are tailored to the specific visitors. The elder has been preparing questions for beings of this nature for forty years, on the theory that someday they would come. The questions are not what anyone expects.
* **TR-19:** The circle where the old argument ended. Ember wants to sit in it. Soliera will allow this. What happens when Ember sits in the circle is the question. The wood elves have been watching this location for three thousand years waiting for someone appropriate to try it.
