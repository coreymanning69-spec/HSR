YUIUM = distributed Whole (no separate post-split command node)
Deashi <——> Soliera [equal primary halves: staying/pondering <——> going/experiencing]
Soliera ——> Sera [response]
Soliera ——> Ember [inquiry]
Sera <——> Ember [equal manifest authority; different functions, no sibling rank]

                       individually and together; explicit household status]
Niv-Saiffar [administrative command] <——> Velin <——> Letha <——> Korin <——> Pell ——> Kessla
Kessla <——> Named Generals / Visiting Dignitaries / Allied Regional Powers

Below Kessla: all external liaisons, allied generals, diplomatic attachés, and
regional governors who operate under Sanctum mandate but maintain their own
internal structures. This tier is fluid by design — it expands as the Sanctum's
footprint grows.

CONSTRUCTS — THE BASELINE LAYER (see bottom of this section):
Countless. Distributed throughout every tier. They respond to Sanctum authority
and execute directed tasks. Not a chain-of-command layer — a force multiplier
for every layer above them.


## NIV-SAIFFAR — ADMINISTRATIVE COMMAND, LOGISTICS & MACRO-ECONOMY
## Role: Administrative Head | He/Him | Firemind Dragon; Ancient Red Dragon chassis, humanoid form preferred for desk work

Niv has always been in charge of Sanctum administration. His INT 35 judgment is
canonically exact: he does not make mistakes. Soliera remains sovereign purpose
and Deashi remains threat authority; Niv turns that frame into operations.

Functional teams under Niv:

- Route and waystation operations coordinate convoy timing, resupply, drivers,
  porters, and active overland or extraplanar corridors.
- Harbor and coastal operations manage ports, vessel logs, cargo inspection,
  fishing fleets, dock labor, and naval support.
- Stockpile operations track grain, ore, gems, metals, textiles, and general
  surplus across depots and warehouse districts.
- Internal distribution operations assign food and materials through kitchens,
  residential distribution points, and inventory systems.
- Construct-logistics teams schedule construct labor across every supply lane
  and connect Niv's plans to the baseline construct workforce.

These are functional reporting structures, not a fixed roster. Team size,
regional partitioning, and mortal staffing expand or contract with need.

## PELL — HR, THERAPEUTIC CARE & INTEGRATION
## Role: Infrastructure and HR Director | She/Her | Halfling, elevated by Soliera

### Reparations Procedure

**Owner:** Pell's Infrastructure/HR and Therapeutic Care directorate owns every
case opened under Axiom 29. No new office or universal casualty registry exists.
The active arc's live-state ledger records the incident and points here; Pell
owns the case and its cross-directorate assignments.

**Automatic intake trigger:** A case opens when verified Sanctum or divine
action causes death, maiming, displacement, or loss of housing, livelihood,
care, education, or community stability. The nearest Steward or field lead may
record the incident, but no petition, consent form, or recipient request is
required before stabilization begins.

**Eligibility:** Civilians, survivors, and innocent dependents qualify,
including innocent households of hostile combatants. Eligibility follows
fallout, not faction. A responsible aggressor's legal or combat consequences
remain separate; the procedure does not reward the responsible aggressor.

**Assignment lanes:**

- Care teams own arrival care, medical support, and vulnerable-person stabilization.
- Labor teams own work placement, income continuity, and labor repair.
- Settlement teams own community integration.
- Mediation teams own disputes involving the procedure or Sanctum policy.
- Child-services teams own orphan and unaccompanied-child care.
- Velin's residential teams own housing continuity.
- Letha's education teams own schooling and education continuity.

**Recipient posture:** Help is infrastructure, not an apology performance.
Recipients owe no forgiveness, gratitude, affection, or declaration that the
harm is repaired. They may know it as reparations or simply as the Sanctum
fulfilling its obligation; the label never gates service.

**Operational closure:** Pell closes the case only after stable assignments
exist for all applicable care, housing, livelihood, schooling, and community
continuity lanes. Grief, anger, and relationship consequences are not declared
resolved by administrative closure.

Functional teams under Pell:

- Care and domestic-service teams support vulnerable arrivals, medical triage,
  long-term welfare, and household needs.
- Labor-relations teams handle work placement, shifts, income continuity, and
  grievances across mortal work sectors.
- Settlement teams integrate displaced, rural, urban, Underdark-adjacent,
  non-human, and trauma-affected arrivals into community life.
- Conflict-resolution teams mediate residential, workplace, intercultural,
  staff, and policy disputes before or after escalation as appropriate.
- Child-services teams coordinate intake, placement, schooling, house support,
  and overnight construct assistance for unaccompanied children.

Pell assigns each reparations case across these teams and the linked Velin and
Letha functions. Administrative closure remains governed by the procedure
above, never by a uniform staffing model.

Kessla controls access, compartmentation, and reporting. Cell sizes, station
counts, identities, and geographic partitions remain operational variables.


## CONSTRUCTS — THE BASELINE LAYER

Countless. Not metaphorical — the Sanctum has never formally counted them because
there is no ceiling on production and no operational reason to set one.

They are not a chain-of-command layer. They respond to Sanctum authority at any
level and execute directed tasks with exact competence and zero ambition. A
single construct can hold a building upright during repairs, guard a corridor for
six months without fatigue, carry enough grain to provision a town, or operate in
complete darkness in the Underdark-adjacent tunnels without assistance.

What they can do: carry, build, guard, farm, mine, clean, maintain, courier,
hold structural load during construction, serve as mobile barriers, operate
vehicles, work rock faces, relay messages, and stand in a blizzard without
complaint for as long as required. They can also cook — functionally. They do
not season.

What they cannot do: exercise the Hush Agents' judgment, replicate Amara's
warmth, or substitute for Niv's strategic intuition. They have precision,
patience, and inexhaustibility. That is what they are for.

Every mortal staff member in every department operates better with construct
support than without it. The constructs make the scale possible. The mortals
make the scale mean something.
