### Ring-haste overlay

The table above is Doran's un-hasted baseline because Wren may choose a
different long-rest load. While her Ring of Concentration holds *haste* on him:

- **AC does not change.** The AC LOCK retires haste's +2; his armor class is 25
  hasted or un-hasted;
- speed becomes **60 ft in kit / 80 ft without it**;
- he has advantage on Dexterity saving throws;
- he gains haste's restricted extra action: Attack (one weapon attack), Dash,
  Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object; and
- the vind-rune still converts no more than 20 feet of movement to flight.

The ring cannot be broken by damage to Wren. DM044_0 must display the active
speed, save advantage, and extra attack rather than silently folding them into
the baseline. It must not display a hasted AC, because there is no longer one.

## ACTION ECONOMY — ONE TURN

| Slot | Options |
|---|---|
| **Action** | Attack (**4 attacks**) · Action Surge (**2/SR**) for a second and third full action · Dodge · Grapple · Shove |
| **Bonus action** | **Read the Seam** · **Quick Toss** (dagger + d12) · two-weapon dagger attack · Second Wind (**30 HP**, 1/SR) · Ring of Regeneration (16 HP, requires at least 1 HP) |
| **Reaction** | **Deft Answer** · **Riposte** · opportunity attack |
| **Movement rider** | **Bait and Switch** while moving; no action |
| **Free** | summon or dismiss either obsidian dagger instantly, including mid-motion; one ordinary object interaction remains available |
| **Per long rest** | **Indomitable (3/LR)** — reroll one failed save per use |

**Superiority dice:** **16d12**, save DC 22; all expended dice return on a short
or long rest. One expended die returns when Doran scores a critical hit,
**maximum once per round**, up to the sixteen-die maximum. Improved Combat
Superiority at Fighter 18 raised the die from d10 to d12; the pool size did not
change, because the printed Battle Master pool does not grow after level 15.

**Relentless (Fighter 15):** If Doran rolls initiative with no superiority dice
remaining, he regains one. Record the feature even though short-rest recovery
and critical-hit recovery make the trigger unlikely.

**Current peak burst:** two Action Surges + Quick Toss = **13 attacks in one
turn**, or **14 while ring-hasted**. A dagger can appear in Doran's open hand at
the instant Quick Toss resolves; there is no draw, drop, stow, or transition
cost. Deft Answer can add a reaction dagger attack during the round if its miss
trigger fires, except during a Grand Cleave turn.

> **Reaction asymmetry — intentional.** Doran's action economy nearly doubled
> from Fighter 15 to 20 while his reaction economy stayed at one. Opportunity
> attack, Deft Answer, and Riposte all compete for a single reaction, and the
> latter two share the same on-miss trigger. This is a known, deliberate gap: it
> is the one place where the solo clearer cannot be everywhere. Do not grant a
> second reaction to smooth it.

## SIGNATURE FEATURES

### READ THE SEAM

Bonus action: make an Insight check against the target's AC. On success, Doran's
dagger critical range against that target becomes **16–20** and remains until
that target hits Doran. A miss does not erase the read, and the read has no
round or scene timeout: once he sees the seam, he does not forget it. A hit on
him proves the exchange changed and he must read that target again.

### DEFT ANSWER

When a creature misses Doran, he may use his reaction to make one free dagger
attack against it. No superiority die is required. Riposte remains available as
the die-spending, damage-boosted alternative; one reaction means he chooses.

## ATTACK ROUTINES

### A. Obsidian daggers — his instrument

**+16 to hit** · **1d8+10** · thrown 30/70 · summon/dismiss instantly, no action

- 4 attacks plus either Quick Toss or the Two-Weapon Fighting bonus attack =
  **5 dagger attacks per turn**, or **6 while ring-hasted**, with no draw,
  reload, or ammunition. Two Action Surges carry that to **13 in a single turn**.
  Quick Toss adds a d12; the ordinary off-hand attack spends no superiority die.
- **Resistance bypass:** damage-type resistance never reduces a dagger hit.
  "Applies real vulnerability" does not double damage; the line remains 1d8+10.
- Either dagger can appear or vanish at will, including mid-motion. Doran can
  hold the Cleaver one-handed, fill the open hand with a dagger in the same
  instant, or turn an apparently empty-hand strike into a stab with no tell.
- **Measured edge:** Doran chooses cut depth, lethal or nonlethal, without penalty.
- **Ward bypass, thrown:** a single thrown dagger slides cleanly through force
  constructs — *wall of force*, *forcecage*, *resilient sphere*, *blade
  barrier*, Otiluke's — with no attack penalty and no damage lost to the
  barrier. This does not extend to general abjuration (*glyph of warding*,
  *alarm*, *forbiddance*, and kin are unaffected).
- **Ward bypass, dual-held:** the two daggers used together can physically cut
  into a force wall. This is a different mechanic from the thrown
  slide-through, not a scaling of it.
- **Indestructible:** the obsidian is Soliera's material and cannot be broken,
  sundered, or destroyed. *(Derived property, pending author confirmation: a
  dagger sunk into a surface and then dismissed releases cleanly, leaving no
  evidence behind. Treat as derived, not ruled.)*
- **Authorship protection (doctrine, not mechanics):** the daggers are
  Soliera's work, and they will not function against her chosen — not as a
  rules interaction, not as a ruling to adjudicate. She simply will not permit
  it. Even if someone could control Doran (they cannot), she stops it at the
  source. This is not a property on the weapon; it is a property of who made
  it.
- Called shots are not a separate subsystem. Maneuvers are the called-shot system.
- The daggers remain the camp-clearing, enclosed-space, and native-fighting configuration.

### B. Giant Cleaver — the campaign weapon

**Mithral +2 greatsword · 6 ft · 208 lb · reach 10 ft · one- or two-handed**

**+16 to hit** · **40 fixed damage** · **critical hit 80** against every target

- The 208-pound weight is intentional divine fabrication. Ember used the same
  mithral as the armor but compressed and densified a far larger mass into the
  six-foot weapon until it was "heavy enough." Ordinary mithral lightness does
  not reduce this purpose-built mass.
- The mastery clock has closed the old giant-only gate: the ordinary Cleaver
  deals **40 fixed damage** against every target.
- Low variance is intentional: the Cleaver delivers rather than spikes.
- Never dulls and requires no maintenance.
- Back-carried with a side-release draw and free shift to either grip.
- **Live now:** Doran uses the full profile with one or two hands and no mastery
  disadvantage.

### C. Grand Cleave — unified technique

**+19 to hit** · **120+4d20 damage** · 180° arc · reach 15 ft

Grand Cleave consumes Doran's whole turn. He commits both hands to the haft and
gives up movement and bonus action. His reaction remains available, but no
dagger, Quick Toss, or other bonus-action line can occur that turn. Action Surge
does not create a second Grand Cleave. The arc does not distinguish allies from
enemies.

- Make one attack roll and apply it to each body in the arc in order. A natural
  20 doubles the **final** `120+4d20` total against every body reached.
- The arc holds seven Medium-unit spaces. A Medium body costs one; a Large body
  costs two. A hit automatically parts each Medium or Large body reached until
  the capacity is exhausted.
- The first Huge-or-larger body takes the damage and stops the blade.
- A parted body is destroyed. *Revivify* still functions inside its one-minute
  window; *raise dead* does not; *resurrection* or better is required afterward.

This is a mode swap, not a linear upgrade. The dagger routine retains precision,
range, resistance bypass, force-construct access, and reaction economy; the
universal Cleaver rider changes the throughput comparison. The old "daggers are
the boss lane; Cleaver is bait" conclusion is retired until the pair is
re-simulated against the current sheets. Grand Cleave remains the formation read
and natural-20 lottery. Using it on mortal bodies is a permanent doctrinal
decision.

### D. Carry-through, maim, and skid

An ordinary Cleaver swing can carry through bodies along its adjudicated line.
Each target at or below the attack's actual damage is parted and the blade
continues; the first survivor takes the damage and stops it.

Any Cleaver hit maims the struck limb when the attack deals at least one-quarter
of the target's maximum HP, or when the attack roll is a natural 20. Approach
and position determine the limb. Grand Cleave's automatic parting of Medium and
Large bodies resolves before this lesser maim result.

**DORAN LIMB LOCK — [RULED 2026-09-05].** The trigger above stays live in
gameplay exactly as written. What Doran does not get is a *called-strike system*:
no declared-limb table, no aiming die, no face chart. He applies two hundred
pounds of edge five times in six seconds; precision targeting is not the problem
being modelled. Which limb goes is narrated from approach and position, and
narrative gating covers the descriptive feats — bracing, levering, lifting a
door off its frame — not the combat result. Kiyuru is the opposite case and is
unchanged: `DM043_D1` keeps the declared-number d8 called strikes and the
cannot-remove / can-impair rule against Doran.

A surviving target skids `5 × floor(actual damage / 50)` feet. Gargantuan
targets halve that distance, rounded down to the nearest 5 feet. Damage below
50 produces no skid.

### E. Configuration competence gate — corrected

The former drop/stow transition cost is retired. Ordinary one-handed Cleaver
use and instant dagger summoning coexist without tempo cost. Grand Cleave keeps
both hands on the haft during its whole-turn resolution and bars bonus-action
dagger use, but it does not consume Doran's reaction; normal reaction lanes,
including Deft Answer when triggered after the turn, remain available.

## STANDING RULINGS

### RULE-CLEAVER-HAND-GATE

Doran may carry, draw, and wield the Cleaver normally with **one or two hands**.
Its listed attack, damage, and reach are fully live and take no mastery penalty.
The body gate closed at T532 when Sera's grueling Waterdeep training carried him
to STR 25 and taught his Large frame to own the 208-pound weapon.

The satisfied body gate also permits seamless ordinary Cleaver-to-dagger play:
the Cleaver can remain in one hand while a dagger appears in the other. Grand
Cleave remains distinct because its technique, not the weapon's weight, commits
both hands and the whole turn. The final refinement belongs to this existing
T532 Waterdeep week with Sera; it adds no later training beat and was not an
unlock during T549 play.
