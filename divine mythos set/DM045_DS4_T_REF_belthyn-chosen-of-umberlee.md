# DM045_DS4 — Belthyn — Chosen of Umberlee

#CHAR-UMBERLEE #THREAD-SEA-CONSTRUCTS #THREAD-MARITIME-CRIME-SURGE

> **SUPERSESSION NOTE — 2026-08-15.** Belthyn's name, drowned-triton ancestry,
> Tempest Cleric chassis, construct-collateral objective, and runtime are now
> settled. Earlier build-open wording records the pre-ruling state only.

## WHY UMBERLEE CARES

Umberlee's relationship with mortals at sea was never worship. It was tribute.
Sailors did not love the Bitch Queen; they paid her, and the payment bought
passage. That is a protection racket with a divine counterparty, and it worked
for as long as no one could offer a better rate.

The Sanctum offered a better rate. Sanctum protection of shipping did not
attack Umberlee, argue with her, or diminish her — it made her irrelevant to
the transaction. A captain who is already safe has nothing to buy. This is the
same structural injury the Sanctum keeps inflicting without aiming at anyone:
infrastructure, extended far enough, dissolves the leverage of whoever was
previously selling safety.

She cannot contest it directly. The Sanctum's maritime protection is not a
fleet to sink or a shrine to topple; it is a standing condition. So she does
what a displaced operator does when the front door closes. She sends someone to
take the lane back another way.

## THE DISPLACEMENT CHAIN

This is the settled causal spine. It is the reason the Chosen exists and the
reason the maritime crime surge and the missing constructs are the same event.

1. Sanctum ship protection becomes reliable across the relevant lanes.
2. Tribute collapses, because safety is no longer scarce.
3. Umberlee's leverage over maritime traffic collapses with it.
4. A Chosen is deployed to restore leverage by means other than tribute.
5. Roughly twenty Sea of Moving Ice tunnel constructs go missing at T530 —
   no wreckage, no distress, no debris field.
6. A maritime crime surge follows.

Steps 1 through 4 are locked. Step 5 is a recorded fact in DM038_L; the
2026-08-15 Belthyn ruling closes the former leading-suspicion gate: she took the
twenty intact as collateral. Step 6 is locked as a pattern; its specific
incidents remain unwritten and available.

The absence of wreckage is the tell worth holding. Twenty constructs did not
fail, break, or fight — they stopped being there. Whatever took them did not
need to destroy them, which is a different and more uncomfortable class of
capability than sinking would have been.

## THE INVERSION — SETTLED

The Ordinant (DM045_DS1) opens by domination: Bane's chosen arrives and applies
force downward. The proposal on record is that Umberlee's chosen opens the
opposite way — she arrives already **owed** something, and the first encounter
is a debt being called rather than a threat being made.

Why this is attractive: it is the only approach that matches the displacement
logic. Tribute was a transaction. A collector is the correct successor to a
racket, where a raider is not. It also gives the pair something they cannot
punch, which is where Doran and Wren are least practiced.

The debt's counterparty is the Sanctum's maritime infrastructure. Belthyn holds
the twenty intact tunnel constructs below as collateral and makes the party
choose between an immediate rescue and the wider lane that depends on those
constructs. The debt is not a legal claim; it is Umberlee's enforced price for
displacement.

Belthyn is a drowned triton. Her human form is a worn drowned body rather than
a disguise, which is why a ground-facing collector does not require a dramatic
sea-shape reveal.

Before the endgame she traveled through Waterdeep in one of those bodies. At a
distance it read as a beautiful woman. Up close the eyes were dead and cold,
the visible fact of the body she wore rather than a failing disguise. She kept
her identity hidden and observed the city and Sanctum-facing lanes without an
on-camera confrontation.

## HOW SHE READS ON THE GROUND

Only what the locks support. Everything here follows from human form plus
collection, and nothing here is a stat.

She is in the room before anyone identifies her as a problem. Human form is not
a disguise she maintains under strain; it is how she works, so there is no
moment where it slips and reveals something. The reveal, when it comes, is
informational rather than visual — what she knows and what she is owed, not
what she turns into.

A collector does not need to win a fight. She needs the ledger to close. That
makes retreat cheap for her and makes killing her a poor answer to her, in the
same structural way the exit lock protects Eclavdra. She is not written as
unkillable; she is written as someone for whom the fight is rarely the point.

## PLACEMENT — SLARKRETHEL AND THE ENDGAME

She stands with Slarkrethel at the endgame. Hold three things about that:

- **It is placement, not partnership.** Nothing states she serves the kraken or
  that he directs her. Two maritime powers with a shared obstacle end up in the
  same room; the source layer in DM038_6a is unchanged by her presence.
- **It does not backdate.** Slarkrethel's Hekaton operation is his own, engineered
  for his own reasons, and predates the displacement chain above. Do not
  retrofit her into Ch. 11.
- **The Morkoth chart stays his.** DM038_6a records a navigational chart that
  predates the campaign and points at something Slarkrethel wants and the
  Society does not understand. That mystery belongs to him. Do not resolve it
  by handing it to her.

## OPERATIVE RUNTIME BLOCK — T537

**Build:** Drowned Triton Tempest Cleric 20, with Fathomless-style Chosen grafts.
The class is provenance; the block below is what runs at the table.

| Saves | STR +12, CON +13, WIS +14, CHA +12 |
| Initiative | +2 |
| Spell save DC / attack | 22 / +14 |
| Legendary resistance | 3/day |
| Senses | darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 22 |

### Fixed encounter suite

- **Control:** *control water, maelstrom, wall of water,* and *destructive wave*.
- **Command:** *mass suggestion, command,* and *hold monster*.
- **Sustain:** *heal, greater restoration,* and *freedom of movement*.
- **Encounter-selected:** any other legal Cleric spell. The file does not carry
  a full prepared list.

### Actions, grafts, and counterplay

- **Multiattack.** Two Tidehook attacks, +14 to hit, 2d8+6 bludgeoning plus
  2d8 lightning each. A creature hit twice in the same turn is grappled
  (escape DC 22) by the water itself.
- **Debt Call (Recharge 5–6).** Up to three creatures Belthyn can see make a
  DC 22 WIS save. On a failure, each is pulled up to 30 feet toward a chosen
  flooded or water-bordered space and cannot willingly move farther from it
  until the end of its next turn.
- **The Undertow Ledger.** While a creature is grappled, restrained, or inside
  her water terrain, Belthyn can choose to hold it rather than damage it. Her
  objective is custody, not a kill count.
- **Legendary actions (3).** Move up to half swim speed without provoking;
  Tidehook attack; or spend 2 actions to cast *command* without a slot.
- **Reaction — Worn Body.** When hit, Belthyn may reduce the damage by 30 and
  move up to 15 feet through water or a flooded space. If no such space exists,
  the reaction fails. Dry, enclosed terrain is intentional counterplay.
- **Chosen terrain — Collection Tide.** A 40-foot radius of seawater she
  creates is difficult terrain for enemies, does not impede her, and ends if
  she is incapacitated. It is not an avatar manifestation.

## ENCOUNTER POSTURE

Belthyn is a **commander/controller and prison warden**, not a duelist. Her
room should include captives, bounded exits, water routes, and a choice whose
cost cannot be solved by simply defeating her. The party can win the fight;
the real pressure is whether they can leave with the people and constructs they
need while her Kraken Society numbers close the routes.

**Counterplay:** remove or deny water, force her into dry enclosed spaces,
break grapples, and split her from the prepared prison geometry. Do not use her
to rewrite Slarkrethel's prior operations or the Morkoth chart.
