# THE ORDINANT

#CHAR-THE-ORDINANT #CHAR-BANE #ITEM-BLACK-HAND-OF-BANE #ITEM-SCEPTER-OF-THE-TYRANTS-EYE

## WHY BANE CARES

The Sanctum runs his portfolio and gets devotion for it. At T75 Sera renamed a
seven-House drow city without learning its original name and the civilization
reorganized around the gesture. That is dominion. It was achieved without fear,
and it was *loved*.

Bane is not facing a rival. He is facing a refutation. Every year the Sanctum
stands is a theological argument he is losing in public.

## THE MIRROR

DM041_A's read on Doran: nothing he wears is his. Soliera personally remade the
current white kit from its earlier Sera/Ember forms; doctrine still comes from
Sera.

The Ordinant is the same and worse. Bane's gauntlet, Fzoul's scepter, the
church's plate. Two borrowed men in borrowed kit — one dressed with love, one
**issued equipment**.

## APPEARANCE

- **Plate:** Matte black-iron. Unornamented. No spikes, skulls, or trophies.
  Immaculate and *correct*. Bane's church runs on rank and hierarchy; tyranny is
  bureaucratic. He arrives like a man enforcing a regulation, and the regulation
  is you.
- **No shield.** Both hands are full and neither is defense. His AC reads low for
  his tier. That is a real tell, findable in play: he has never needed to block.
- **No helm, by choice.** He carries one and removes it. Domination requires an
  audience.
- Against seven feet of mirror-white plate returning incident light, he is the
  man absorbing it.

## THE KIT

### The Black Hand of Bane — worn left

Bane's own gauntlet. Visibly **too large for him** — a god's hand on a man's arm
— and it always will be. Frost crawls up the plate when he closes it.

The left hand does not swing. It **reaches**. He takes it off the maul to claim
you, which is contemptuous and deliberate.

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| Supplied attack adjustment | +3 to Black Hand attack rolls only; damage still adds STR +7. The Black Hand itself supplies this deliberate Divineverse homebrew adjustment. |
| Unarmed strike | 1d10+7 cold + 1d8 radiant; counts as magical |
| Unholy | +2d6 necrotic against good-aligned creatures |
| Axiomatic | +2d6 psychic against creatures not sworn to a lawful hierarchy |
| Mighty Cleaving | On reducing a creature to 0 HP, immediately make one additional attack |
| **Dominion** | See below. Routes through the Hand, not the weapon. |
| Attunement | Chosen of Bane only |

**Icy-touch note:** Cold is canon iconography and pure theater against Doran,
who is thermally immune. The Hand's threat was never damage.

### The Scepter of the Tyrant's Eye — right hand

Sentient minor artifact, forged by Teldorn Darkhope and Fzoul Chembryl. Corey
ruling: run it at **maul** profile rather than the printed heavy mace.

**Bludgeoning is Doran's live lane.** Divine plate halves slashing and denies
piercing. Every hit from this weapon lands in full, and the Improved Divine
Smite rider is radiant, which the plate also does not stop.

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| Weapon | +3 maul, 2d6 bludgeoning, two-handed |
| Sentience | LE; INT 12, WIS 17, CHA 14; Common and Infernal; hearing and darkvision 120 ft; **it has opinions about him** |
| Soul trap | A non-Banite touching it loses 1d4 STR per round. At STR 0, the creature dies and its soul is absorbed. |
| Displacement | Bonus action: attacks against him have disadvantage until the start of his next turn |
| Rod of Rulership | 1/day, 30 minutes; humanoids within 120 ft; CHA save DC 23 or obey |
| Hold Monster | 4/day, DC 23 |
| Circle of Doom | 1/day, necrotic burst, DC 23 |
| True Strike | 3/day, advantage on next attack |
| Beholder immunity | Immune to all beholder rays except antimagic |

The beholder-ray immunity and the Scepter's Fzoul provenance are retained as
historical kit texture only. Neither creates a current Waterdeep, Xanathar,
Moonsea, or Nihiloor hook unless a mortal brings it into play.

## RUNTIME BLOCK

### Attacks

- **Scepter (maul):** +16, 2d6+10 bludgeoning + 1d8 radiant (Improved Divine Smite).
- **Black Hand:** +16 from STR +7, proficiency +6, and a **Divineverse
  homebrew +3 to-hit-only adjustment**; 1d10+7 cold + existing riders + 1d8
  radiant. It is not a 5e +3 weapon and therefore never receives +3 damage.
- **Extra Attack:** two attacks; **three under Invincible Conqueror**.
- **Divine Smite:** 15 slots (4/3/3/3/2), up to 6d8 radiant per smite.

### Auras — 30 ft; 60 ft with the canonical Standard planted

- **Aura of Protection:** +9 to all saves, self and allies.
- **Aura of Conquest:** Frightened creatures in the aura have **speed 0** and
  take **10 psychic** at the start of each turn.
- **Aura of Courage.**

### Features

- **Scornful Rebuke.** A creature that hits him takes **9 psychic**, ignoring
  armor. Doran's four-attack action self-inflicts 36; his thirteen-attack peak
  can self-inflict 117. This is what dropped him at T531. His combat identity is
  the trap.
- **Invincible Conqueror.** One minute: resistance to **all** damage, a third
  attack, and critical hits on 19–20. Chosen graft: recharges on 5–6 instead of
  once per long rest.
- **Conquering Presence.** Channel Divinity: each creature within 30 ft makes a
  WIS save DC 23 or is frightened for one minute.
- **Guided Strike.** Channel Divinity: +10 to one attack roll.

### Legendary and reaction package

- **Legendary Resistance (3/day).** If the Ordinant fails a saving throw, he may
  choose to succeed instead.
- **Legendary actions (3 points).** He regains all points at the start of his
  turn and may spend one option at the end of another creature's turn:
  - **Scepter Strike (1):** Make one Scepter attack.
  - **Tyrant's Advance (1):** Move up to half speed without provoking
    opportunity attacks.
  - **Black Hand Claim (2):** Make one Black Hand attack.
- **Preemptive Displacement.** Before the first attack roll targeting him while
  Displacement is inactive, the Ordinant may use his reaction to activate it.
  Attacks against him have disadvantage until the start of his next turn.

### Chosen grafts

1. **Dominion.** A creature the Ordinant reduces to 0 HP does not fall
   unconscious. It stabilizes, conscious, and belongs to Bane until removed from
   the aura for one full minute or freed by Liberation-grade magic.
2. **Terror precedes the blade.** His attacks have advantage against creatures
   frightened of him.
3. **Bane's Recall.** No action, uncounterable, and unpreventable. He leaves when
   he decides to.
4. **The Standard — canonical deployment equipment.** A black iron gauntlet-standard driven
   into the ground. While planted, Aura of Conquest reaches 60 ft. He cannot move
   it once set. This is the intended findable seam for Doran's Read the Seam.

## DESIGN SEAMS — GM SIDE

**Against Doran (AC 25, 370 HP, initiative +21, WIS +11, INT +10, CHA +4;
thirteen-attack peak):** Bludgeoning and radiant bypass the plate. DC 23 fear
lands on a natural 1–11 against WIS +11; once frightened, speed 0 kills the
Mobile kite, Doran swings at disadvantage, and the Ordinant swings at
advantage. Preemptive Displacement blunts the opening target and every hit Doran
lands costs him 9. The universal Cleaver rider changes the arithmetic: thirteen
ordinary Cleaver hits average roughly 572 damage before superiority dice or
crits. The 900-HP package makes raw survival the floor, not the encounter.
**Pushing
forward is how he loses**, precisely his solo-pride failure mode.

**Against Wren (220 HP, ward 75 with overflow nullification, DC 22):**
His WIS +18 and CHA +24 close the obvious control lanes. Her live saves are his
**DEX/INT +10**, while attack-roll spells must contend with AC 21 and the
first-target Displacement reaction.
His fear does not reach her at all: Wren's permanent mind lock (DM041_B1,
DM044_1) makes her immune to the frightened condition, so no save is rolled and
neither Aura of Conquest rider attaches. Its speed-0 control and 10-per-turn
psychic damage affect only frightened creatures; the former ward-grind line was
invalid and is retired. **Liberation — Indomitable Spirit** is therefore the
counter for Doran, not for her.

**The intended lesson:** They cannot out-attack him and cannot out-save him.
They win by reading the Standard, breaking line of sight, and operating as two
people. That is the correction target on both sheets.

**AC 21 is deliberate.** He is easy to hit. Being hit is how he wins. The final
point is the Black Hand's empty-shield-arm cover; the Hand is therefore both a
defensive seam and the source of its expressly homebrew attack adjustment.

## T531 — RETROACTIVE READ

He was never trying to kill Doran. **He was taking him.** Dominion had landed and
the claim was incomplete when high-speed Sanctum constructs hit the field,
forcing a choice: hold it or leave. He left.

Nobody in-world knows this: not Doran, Wren, or the Sanctum. It surfaces the
second time it happens.

## MISSION — PROPOSED

The Hand of Bane was destroyed in the Astral and its fragments scattered across
the planes. Waterdeep holds Blackstaff Tower and a planar-capable
Underdeal/Sanctum Embassy.

He was not in Waterdeep for Doran. Doran got in the way.

## RETINUE — AVAILABLE, UNUSED

**Holy Hands of Bane.** Six-foot levitating obsidian temple sentinels. Any magic
item or weapon touching one is absorbed; damaging spells heal them; *dispel
magic* slows one for a minute.

**Collision flagged:** Doran's daggers are Soliera's artifact obsidian. Obsidian
sentinels that eat magic weapons against divine obsidian blades leaves an
undetermined question: which obsidian wins. Keep it unresolved until rolled.

## BANKED, NOT BUILT

**Niv-Saiffar arrival beat.** Base 35 per DM004_1. The scale reminder is not that
help arrives; it is that Bane sends his single greatest mortal instrument and
what answers is the **logistics master**. The man whose hoard is the Sword
Coast's paperwork, who burned the sky at T297. Waterdeep is Niv's infrastructure;
he would notice a Banite operation the way one notices a missing invoice.

Staged for later. Not scheduled.
