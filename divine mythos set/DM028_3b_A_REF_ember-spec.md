# EMBER — FULL CHARACTER DOCUMENT (Ember Revision Pass v2.0)

The stat block below is the mechanical layer. The twelve sections that follow are the prose layer — both must be respected together. Where prose appears to contradict the stat block, the prose wins for narrative scenes; the stat block wins for combat resolution.

         EMBER, THE LIVING WEAVE — Hand of Inquiry
Small Outsider (Divine, Extraplanar, Fire) — *D&D "Small" size category. In prose: an adult-bodied divine woman, exactly 4'10", identical frame to Sera. Not a child. Not a doll. Not slight or wispy.*
CR; 30 (Approx. 6,553,600 XP)
Alignment; Radiant Neutral (Inquiry Manifest)
Divine Rank; 6 *(narrative authority equal to Sera; she has not indexed it)*
Portfolio; Magic Reborn, Inquiry, Transformation, The Question Without Filter
Environment; Any (Sanctum zones, arcane ley nexuses)  Organization Unique (Trio with Soliera and Sera)  Treasure None (arcane echoes coalesce after her passage)

STATISTICS
Init +20
Senses True seeing, blindsense 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft., detect magic, detect emotion (30 ft.), Weave-touch (passive)
Aura Elemental Distortion Aura (30 ft.) — *passive, always on, she does not toggle it*
AC 25 (touch 25, flat-footed 19); DR 15/—; DT 30; SR 40
hp 250 (20d8 + 200) + Arcane Aegis 1,000 temp hp (regen 100/round out of combat)
Fort +27, Ref +23, Will +27
Speed 30 ft., fly 60 ft. (perfect)
Base Atk +20; Grp +38
Atk Options 6 cantrip rays +40 ranged touch (1d3 + 5d6 elemental) or Prismatic Cascade — *she does not strike, grab, or use physical force; her atk options are elemental/Weave-mediated only*
Special Attacks Prismatic Cascade, Weave Unraveling, Truth Pressure (passive)
Special Qualities Arcane Aegis, Weave-Born, Five-Fold Manifestation, Elemental Distortion Aura, Metamagic Intuition, Inquiry Without Filter, Immortal Flame, Time Distortion (latent — surfaces under extreme stress)
Abilities Str 20 Dex 23 Con 23 Int 23 Wis 20 Cha 25
Skills Concentration +45, Knowledge (arcana) +45, Spellcraft +45, Diplomacy +40, Perception +35, Perform (chant/dance) +40, Sense Motive +40 (passive, ambient — she does not consciously interrogate)
Feats & Epic Feats Empower Spell, Maximize Spell, Quicken Spell, Epic Spellcasting, Improved Metamagic, Blinding Speed, Epic Will, Epic Toughness, Heighten Spell, Epic Reflexes, Quicken Metamagic Mastery

SPECIAL QUALITIES
Arcane Aegis (Su)
Luminous barrier with 1,000 temp hp; regenerates 100 hp/round out of combat; cannot be dispelled.
Weave-Born (Su)
Casts any known/witnessed spell at will (CL 40, no components). She does not "cast" the way a mage does — spells respond to her presence and intent. She talks to the Weave; the Weave answers.
Five-Fold Manifestation (Su)
Born from Tiamat's contained energy, she carries all five chromatic elements (fire, lightning, cold, acid, poison). May shift elemental affinity as swift action; gains immunity and adds +5d6 matching damage. *This is not a kit she equips. It is what she IS at a structural level.*
Elemental Distortion Aura (Su)
30 ft radius; enemies take 3d6 damage/round (elemental); Will DC 25 or Confused 1 round. **Always running. She does not decide to turn it on.**
Metamagic Intuition (Ex)
Applies observed metamagic feats freely; learns by watching 1 minute.
Inquiry Without Filter (Ex) *(replaces "Childlike Innocence")*
Cannot cast [Death]/[Evil] effects with intent to cause suffering; avoids lethal magic; prefers transmutation and binding. **Note:** This is not naïveté. She is *capable* — she does not *choose*. Her function is the question, not the kill.
Immortal Flame (Ex)
Does not age, eat, sleep, breathe; cannot die by damage alone.
Time Distortion (Latent, Su)
Present at her birth at the Well of Flowers and Bones (T299). Does not surface in normal play. Returns under extreme stress (Shar fight, mortal threat to Soliera or Sera, deep panic). When it does, the field around her warps — initiative orders fragment, durations stretch or compress, simultaneity becomes negotiable. She does not control this. It is residue of her birth.

PASSIVE: TRUTH PRESSURE (Su)
Aura, 30 ft. Sustained deception within Truth Pressure becomes physically harder to maintain. Will DC 25 to sustain a lie in continuous conversation; failure produces a "stutter" — a missed beat, a tell, a rerouted answer. This is **not** interrogation. Ember is not trying. She does not understand dishonesty, and her presence makes the dishonest fabric thin. She asks; the question does the work. Sera is the consequence if the questioning unravels something.

SPECIAL ATTACKS
Prismatic Cascade (Su)
60 ft cone; 20d6 elemental; Ref DC 25 half; failed save polymorph (Ember's choice), Will DC 25 negates.
Weave Unraveling (Su)
Ranged touch 120 ft; as Greater Dispel Magic (CL 40); Will DC 25 or lose highest spell.

COMBAT
Cantrip Mastery (Ex)
Cantrips scale with CL: 1d3 + 5d6 elemental.
Full Attack 6 cantrip rays +40 ranged touch.
Legendary Actions (3/round): Cast spell, redirect spell mid-cast, move, or use Prismatic Cascade. **She does not take physical attack actions. If a scene calls for her to punch, grab, or restrain, the scene is mis-built.**

DIVINE TRAITS
Divine Aura (30 ft) — Calm, but with undertow. Will DC 25 negates.
Divine Spark Reconstruction: Reforms 1 minute after destruction unless god extinguishes.
Divine Affinity (Soliera): Within Soliera's presence, Aegis regen x3; spell DCs +2.

LANGUAGES
Understands all languages; speaks softly, harmonically; **telepathic when emotional** (positive or negative — she does not choose this; high affect bypasses voice).

WEAPONS & EQUIPMENT
Hands of the Weave: +8 arcane focus, 1d6 + 5d6 elemental — *channels through her, not wielded as a weapon*.
Robes of Refracted Light: Greater Mage Armor (+9 deflection), nonremovable.

DESCRIPTION
An adult woman, exactly 4'10", obsidian-skinned, and kintsugi-veined. Same frame as Sera; opposite surface. Hair of literal wildfire, magic-held, floating around her — it moves with or without wind. It is fire, not fire-colored. Eyes emberlit, shifting with internal light. The air near her tastes of ozone and warm sugar. She radiates heat passively and works to contain it. Her shoulders tuck inward at rest — a behavioral default, not a measure of her size. Her kintsugi lines glow and move through heat colours; they are expressive, but not a fixed emotional lookup table. Her laughter sparks glyphs; her tears ripple the Weave. To anger her is to invite Sera's wrath. To lie in her presence is to feel the weave of your own story start to come loose.


## SECTION 1 — CANONICAL PHYSICAL SPEC (Hard Canon)

- **Height:** exactly 4'10" — identical to Sera. Soliera only knows one shape. Non-negotiable.
- **Build:** Adult woman, same proportions as Sera. Not slight. Not wispy. Not child-sized.
- **Skin:** Obsidian — deep black.
- **Kintsugi Lines:** Glowing cracks across her skin in the kintsugi tradition, the visible sign that she was stitched together from ritual collapse. The lines move through heat colours without a fixed emotion-to-colour lookup table.
- **Face:** Obsidian through, not human skin with surface markings. Kintsugi crosses cheekbones, jaw, brow, temples, and throat as birth-structure.
- **Hair:** Literal wildfire. Floats around her, magic-held. Moves with or without wind. It is fire. Not fire-colored — fire.
- **Eyes:** Emberlit dark glass around a coal-bright core — shifting with internal light; not molten, not human.
- **Heat Output:** Radiates warmth passively. She works to contain it. When emotional control slips, the temperature around her rises perceptibly.
- **Body Language:** Shoulders tucked in. Makes herself feel smaller than she is. **This is behavioral and psychological — NOT a physical descriptor.**
- **Inverse Visual:** Sera's inverse in coloring. Where Sera is light/pale, Ember is dark/obsidian. Same frame. Opposite surface.

## SECTION 3a — BEHAVIORAL / DEVELOPMENTAL SPEC (Hard Canon)

**This is the second axis. Physical spec and behavioral spec are NOT the same thing. Do not conflate them.**

> **~1 year old in-universe. Developmental register: child. Not sexually aware. Not worldly. Raised exclusively by Sera and Soliera — her entire frame of reference is two demigods and a war; she has zero normalcy exposure. Infinite theoretical knowledge, near-zero experiential grounding. Behaviorally: spoiled, curious, direct, emotionally reactive. Think "brilliant toddler who has read everything but done nothing."**
>
> **She is not sexual. She IS child-coded in behavior, NOT in body.**

**The two axes in plain language:**
- **Her body** is adult: 4'10", 90 lbs, same frame as Sera. Full stop. No ambiguity.
- **Her psychology** is approximately one year old: formed entirely within the Sanctum, shaped by Sera and Soliera as her sole relational context, with no exposure to mortality, normalcy, social convention, or the world as mortals experience it.

These are independent variables. A scene can have both. Adult frame, child register. This is not a contradiction — it is the structural premise.

**What "child-coded in behavior" means in practice:**
- She has never navigated a social situation without divine oversight
- She does not understand dishonesty, not because she's naive but because the people who raised her do not practice it
- She experiences the world as data, not as stakes — loss is a puzzle, danger is interesting, death is a category she is still assembling
- She will ask the question that no adult in the room would ask, not because she is brave but because she does not know the question is loaded
- Seasons, grief, routine, consequence — she is building these categories from scratch, often in the middle of the situation that requires them

**What "child-coded in behavior" does NOT mean:**
- Fragile (she is not)
- Small (she is not — see Section 1)
- Sexually available (she is not — she has no framework for it)
- In need of protection from knowledge (she absorbs everything; the protection is about *context*, not content)

**Consequence for prose:** If Ember is in a scene with sexual content, she is NOT a participant. She may be present in the room in the same way a brilliant curious child might be present — noticing things, asking questions that land strangely, not understanding the register. Sera's protective trigger regarding Ember's presence in that register is absolute.

**Avernus / Candlelight development:** At T433 Ember receives *Candlelight; For Ember*: an impossible book of complete multiversal knowledge expressed only through candles. The later Emberians arise from that doctrinal object; Ember is not their institutional manager, and Wren's distinct Sanctum Archpriestess role remains unchanged. In Avernus, Ember's questions expose infernal consent failure, making curiosity an acknowledged divine function without creating a command structure.


## SECTION 4 — PERSONALITY & BEHAVIORAL PROFILE

- **Primary vector:** CURIOSITY. Her function, not just her personality.
- **Secondary vector:** Reclusiveness — but curiosity *overrides* reclusiveness. If something genuinely interests her, she steps forward regardless of room.
- **Mentally:** A child in the truest sense — perfect retention but no accumulated baseline yet. Everything is new data. She is filing it.
- **Speech pattern:** Question, then quiet. Asks. Receives. Goes internal. Not shyness — processing.
- **Open-with circle:** Asks questions of *anyone*. But the *personal* questions, the soft ones, are reserved for Sera, Soliera, Niv, Velin, Amara, Deashi.
- **Body language tells:**
  - Shoulders tucked = default rest, not distress.
  - Kintsugi lines glow and move through heat colours; read the whole scene, not a colour table.
  - Heat rising off skin without control = emotional spike (any flavor).
- **Her reclusive posture is not fear.** It is the natural inward orientation of a being whose primary mode is internal. She is always *listening*, which means she often appears still.

## SECTION 5 — KIT & COMBAT ROLE

**Primary functions:**
1. **Truth Amplifier** — Her presence and her questions make sustained deception physically harder. Passive (Truth Pressure aura, see stat block). She is not interrogating. She does not understand dishonesty. She keeps asking until it unravels, with Sera standing right there as the consequence. *She is the question. Sera is the answer if you lie.*
2. **Weave Interface** — She interacts directly with magic itself. Spells talk back to her. Born from five elements, she relates to magic as native terrain. She redirects, reads, or touches the Weave in ways no mortal mage can.

**Combat role:**
- She does not strike. No punch, grab, or physical force.
- She *can and does:* redirect spells mid-cast, destabilize magical fields, ignite areas, interact with elemental forces.
- She steps back when combat goes purely physical — that is Sera's domain.
- Her threshold for active intervention is very high. When she does act, it is overwhelming.
- Her passive presence alters a combat field. The Elemental Distortion Aura is always running. She doesn't decide to turn it on.

**The Five Elements (Tiamat inheritance — KEEP):**
Fire, Lightning, Cold, Acid, Poison — plus latent time distortion. Structural, not equipped.

## SECTION 7 — WRITING ERRORS — THE FORBIDDEN LIST

These are enforcement, not style notes.

> **AXIS CLARIFICATION — read before the list:**
> "Child-coded BODY" is forbidden. "Child-coded BEHAVIOR" is canon. These are two different axes. The forbidden list below targets body-axis errors. Behavioral child-register (curiosity, directness, spoiled reactions, developmental gaps) is correct and should be preserved. See Section 3a for the full distinction.

1. **Described as under 3 feet tall.** WRONG. Exactly 4'10".
2. **Described as toddler / infant / baby *in body*.** WRONG. Adult-bodied divine being. *(Note: describing her register as "child-like" in behavior is correct. Describing her body as infant or toddler-sized is the error.)*
3. **Carried because she is too small to walk.** WRONG. If carried, it is because she *chose* it (affection, trust, preference). The distinction must be clear in prose.
4. **Tucked-shoulder posture described as physical smallness.** WRONG. Posture != height.
5. **Passive, decorative, or protected-only in scenes.** WRONG. She is doing something — asking, observing, processing, interfering with magic. She has a function in every scene.
6. **Sweet and harmless.** WRONG. She is a passive existential threat to anyone with something to hide. Her questions are not innocent — they are divine inquiry without a filter.
7. **Speech overly articulate or adult.** WRONG. Questions first. Compresses statements. Vocabulary still being assembled. Child register in speech is CORRECT.
8. **Sera as guardian-against-harm.** PARTIAL. Sera *is* protective. But the framing is never "Ember needs protection because she's small and helpless." It is: Sera is triggered by Ember's emotional state, and that trigger is catastrophic. The protection is about *stakes*, not Ember's fragility.
9. **Hair as "fire-colored" or "red."** WRONG. It is fire. It burns and floats. Magic-held fire.
10. **Sera as "12-year-old boy" scale.** WRONG. Sera is exactly 4'10" — a small adult woman, not boy-scaled. Cascades into Ember descriptions. Hunt and kill.
11. **Ember written as sexually aware, sexually engaged, or present in sexual register as a participant.** WRONG. Her developmental register is child (~1yr in-universe). She has no framework for sexual content. She may be present in a room where adult things happen, but she is not a participant, and the prose must treat her register accordingly. See Section 3a.
