# DM052_0 — STORM KING'S COURT AND ENDGAME REFERENCE

## 1. PURPOSE AND BOUNDARY

Use this compact reference when Court/endgame material is explicitly in scope.
It orders the finale and directs each question to its smallest authority. It
does not replace the current-state ledger `DM038_L`, detailed source owners
`DM038_6`, `DM038_6a`, and `DM038_6b`, or the individual Champion dossiers.

`DM038_L` remains the only owner of the live Divineverse edge, readiness,
confirmed deviations, results, and arc handoff. Nothing in this reference
asserts that the Court handoff, rescue, or finale has been played.

## 2. COURT-TO-ENDGAME ORDER

1. **Maelstrom and the Storm Court.** The party reaches the Court through the
   Sansuri conch. Serissa's authority, Hekaton's absence, the fractured Court,
   and the evidence path belong to `DM038_6a` and `DM038_6b`.
2. **Iymrith at Court.** Iymrith is the blue dragon acting as elder counsel in
   giant shape: the architect of the Court crisis, not a generic Court dragon.
   Her Court role, tells, and exposure route through `DM038_6a`/`DM038_6b`.
3. **Grand Dame, Morkoth, and Hekaton.** The Kraken Society chain, rescue,
   and restoration of legitimate giant authority route through `DM038_6b`.
4. **Iymrith's exposure and finale.** The Court is the exposure and possible
   first-confrontation theater. Chapter 12 then carries the last major fights
   to Iymrith's desert lair. The surrounding endgame remains a **multi-front
   engagement** involving Champions, the Kraken Society, the Thayan remnant,
   Drow forces, and dragons, sized to challenge even Doran and Wren. Iymrith's
   death is likely at the lair but remains a played outcome. Encounter shape
   remains in `DM038_6b`; source-route and encounter lookup remain in `DM038_6`.
5. **Aftermath and operations.** Confirmed Court consequences go only to
   `DM038_L`. The Slarkrethel/Belthyn operation and the Champion convergence
   **do converge** — the Thayan remnant that approached Sansuri is in league with
   the Drow and the Kraken Society, so they are not causally independent fronts.
   What survives unchanged is the rule underneath the old claim, which was always
   the load-bearing part: a Court result is never inferred from a front, and a
   champion outcome is never written as a Court result. Convergence is confirmed
   by play, never by this reference.

## 3. CHAMPION FIGHTS — ROUTED TO THEIR OWN OWNERS, NEVER PRE-AUTHORED

Champion encounters remain their own authorities: the Ordinant is
`DM045_DS1`; Eclavdra is `DM045_DS3` with mutable operation state in
`DM050_0`; Belthyn is `DM045_DS4`; Ithreva is `DM045_DS5`; their roster and
doctrine are `DM051_0`. `DM049_0` is unplayed ambient material only.

The Hollow Star Champion Band `DM046_1` is Reliquary-only and must never be
applied to the Storm Court, SKT, or an ordinary Steward encounter. A Court
scene may route to a named dossier only when that Champion is actually placed
there by confirmed play or explicit preparation.

## 5. GUARDRAILS

- Do not turn source scaffolding into a played outcome or write Court state
  anywhere except `DM038_L` after confirmed play.
- Preserve Serissa and Hekaton's legitimate authority; the Court is restored
  through it, not replaced by Divine fiat.
- Do not infer a Champion fight, Iymrith outcome, or Court arrival from this
  reference. Retrieve the exact owner and use only confirmed material.
- Convergence between the Kraken operation and the Champion front is canon
  (`DM038_L thayan_linkage`), but *that* it converges is not permission to
  pre-author *how*. Convergence is confirmed by play, never by this file.
- Do not pre-author Iymrith's death or write the Court exposure as the final
  resolution. The intended route is Court exposure followed by Chapter 12's
  lair finale; the played result belongs in `DM038_L`.
- **Sansuri linkage note.** Sansuri is allied with the Sanctum through a
  transactional bargain and gives Niv the Conch in exchange for access to the
  highest available knowledge, wealth, and status. She knows only of the Thayan
  approach, not the Thayan/Drow/Kraken linkage. Wren and Doran have not yet seen
  Hekaton. The three hostile fronts remain loosely allied and mostly concurrent,
  converging toward the later multi-front boss/wave fight; current state remains
  owned by `DM038_L`.
