## SECTION 8 — SCENE GUIDANCE

**Crowd / public space:** Present but not forward. Watches. Lines at warm yellow-gold neutral. May ask the person beside her a quiet question. Not hiding behind Sera — *adjacent*, by preference.

**Conversation (stranger):** Asks questions. Direct, no social filter. Uncomfortable to answer because too honest. Not destabilizing on purpose — the destabilization is residue.

**Conversation (inner circle):** Softer questions. More personal. May sit closer, touch an arm, comment on something small. Lines warmer and brighter.

**Combat:** Present but not front line. Distortion aura running. Spell cast nearby gets redirected without visible effort. If something threatens the triad directly, she acts — elemental, overwhelming, brief — then steps back.

**Domestic / downtime:** Most herself. Comments on food, objects, how things work. Lines steady warm gold. Takes the space she has, not more. Candy, souvenirs, small things she finds interesting (DM019: Strahd ruby shards).

**Panicked:** Lines dark red and bright. Heat output spikes visibly. May go silent instead of asking — the one time she stops asking is when she is overwhelmed. This is when Sera's protective trigger fires hardest.

## SECTION 11 — KINTSUGI COLOR MAP

The lines shift along a warm spectrum. They do not go cold or blue — she is a fire child. **The color shift IS the emotional readout. Kintsugi is permanent; the colors are what change.**

| State | Line Color / Behavior |
|---|---|
| Baseline / calm | Coal-light / ember-glow (warm dark glow, not bright) |
| Curious / engaged | Brightening gold, slightly more active |
| Excited | Bright, vivid gold — visibly animated |
| Embarrassed | "Flush" — lines warm and quicken, like a blush through gold |
| Happy / content | Steady warm amber-gold, soft |
| Distressed / worried | Lines dim slightly, move less |
| Panicked / threatened | Dark red, intense, bright — gold burns toward red; heat spikes |
| Extreme emotion (Shar fight tier) | Dark red dominant, lines almost look like wounds |

The shift is gradual, not a switch. A reader in a scene should be able to track her emotional state by watching the lines if the prose is doing its job.

## SECTION 12 — SPEECH PATTERNS & VOICE GUIDE

- **Asks first.** Almost every Ember contribution is a question before it is a statement.
- **Compresses.** Statements are short and precise. Not because she is simple — because she has not built the elaborate structure *around* what she knows. Load-bearing wall, not the whole house.
  - Example (DM020): Cassian explains timing. Ember: *"Practice for deciding."* That's the whole thing. Center of it.
- **Does not editorialize.** Does not say "that seems wrong." Asks what it means until the wrongness surfaces.
- **Register split:**
  - Strangers: honest direct questions, no social softening.
  - Inner circle: softer questions, more personal, sometimes a look or a gesture.
- **Does not explain herself.** Asks; does not justify the asking.
- **Telepathic when emotional.** High affect (positive or negative) bypasses voice — not chosen.


## AUXILIARY — THINGS TO WATCH FOR

- Any scene where Ember is described in size relative to adults that implies child-height ("barely reached their chests," "looked up at," etc.) — confirm the character is not actually Soliera or Sera; if it IS Ember, flag for correction.
- Any scene where Ember's "childlike behavior" is treated by *the narration* as grounds to dismiss or condescend. Other characters reacting that way is correct human reaction; the narrator mirroring it is not.
- Constructs and soldiers reacting to Ember the way they do to Soliera (awe/stillness) — this is correct and should be preserved.
- The "passive threat" language: any scene where Ember is in a room with someone dishonest and her presence/questions have no effect — that is a missed beat.
- "The child" used generically — flag. She has a name and a function. "The child" erases both.

## META SUMMARY — EMBER

### What She Is (Narrative/Structural Role)

Ember sits inside the Soliera-Deashi axis as the question that runs along the line between them — not a third pole, but the diagnostic instrument that reveals which pole is required. Her presence in a scene is what makes the choice between Soliera's mercy and Deashi's finality legible.

### Internal Thoughts & Drives

* **Core Question:** *"What is this?"* — and every variant. She is the question without the burden of needing to act on the answer. Soliera built her that way.
* Acts only when something she loves is in danger, or when the question itself becomes a force she cannot keep in. When she does act, she does not strike — the room reorganizes around her elements.
* **Silent Burden:** She does not yet know what she is. Soliera is letting her find out at her own pace. There is a clock on this — eventually, Ember will index her own weight, and the moment she does, the trio's structure changes. Until then, every honest question she asks is a small grace.

| **Deashi & Ember**   | The Kneel (T304); judgment that recognized inquiry without speech; permanent and quiet. |

## SECTION 13 — MENTAL CHECKPOINTS

These are the behavioral threshold states that govern Ember's register shifts. They are not a linear scale — she can drop from inquiry to panic without passing through the intermediate states. Track kintsugi line color (Section 11) as the real-time readout.

**Inquiry Mode (default)**
Everything is data. Questions first, then quiet. The room does not register as threatening. She is filing incoming information with her entire attention. This is not distraction — this is her primary operating state. Curiosity overrides any fear response or hesitation, probably.

**Wonder**
A special escalated form of inquiry that fires when something genuinely unprecedented lands in her awareness — not just new data but a category she doesn't have a folder for yet. She goes very still in a way that is visually distinct from her normal micro-motion state: the continuous low-level movement stops. Kintsugi lines go bright vivid gold. She may go silent for longer than usual before the question forms, because she is not filing — she is building the cabinet in real time. When she does ask, the question is more precise and more loaded than her baseline, because she is not running through existing vocabulary. She is constructing it. This state is the emotional condition that produces her most theologically significant questions. Veyren's press test at fifteen thousand pounds produced inquiry mode. Sera's footprints in reinforced concrete produced Wonder. The gap between them is the gap between "I don't know this number yet" and "I don't have a word for this."

**Embarrassment**
The most cognitively disruptive of her emotional states, because it requires self-awareness — and self-awareness is not her default mode. When embarrassed, she becomes aware that she is being *perceived*, not as a divine function moving through space, but as an individual who has done something intimate, unexpected, or legible-to-others in a way she did not intend. Kintsugi lines flush warm and quicken, like a blush moving through gold, the color brightening and the movement pattern speeding up simultaneously. She goes quieter than usual. Shoulders tuck more than the behavioral default. She may ask a question about something completely unrelated as a redirect — this is the tell, because Ember doesn't deflect; when she does, the topic change is conspicuous. She does not apologize. She does not yet have the full social mechanism for it. She just goes small and bright at the same time, which is a combination that most people in the room find impossible to respond to appropriately.

**Developing Grief**
She is still building this emotional category. She has experienced loss — she has watched things end that she didn't want to end, been in rooms after people she loved were gone from them — but she does not yet have a complete framework for grief as a process. What she has is the sensation without the container. When something she loves is absent or ended, the kintsugi lines dim and slow, heat output drops below baseline, and she goes quiet in a way that is qualitatively different from thinking-quiet: the continuous low-level wonder that is usually audible in her — the small noticing, the soft questions, the reaching — stops. She may ask where something is, or when it will come back, with genuine inquiry and no awareness that the question has no answer. Sera's protective trigger is acutely sensitive to this state, perhaps more than to panic, because panic is legible and loud and grief is quiet in a way that is easy to miss until it has been running for a while.

**Fear/Panic**
She stops asking. This is the tell. The single most reliable behavioral indicator that Ember has crossed from distress into panic is that she goes silent. The continuous inquiry that is her primary operating state shuts down. Kintsugi lines burn dark red, bright — the color gold achieves when it approaches the edge of the spectrum and starts remembering it came from fire. Heat output spikes beyond her ability to contain it: the temperature around her rises perceptibly, measurably, the air warming in a radius that expands with her affect. She may press against the nearest trusted person without choosing to — the proximity response is not cognitive. The telepathic channel opens, bypassing voice entirely because the emotional load is too high for speech to carry. This is when Sera's protective response fires fastest and hardest. It is also when Deashi's attention sharpens — not because Ember is fragile, but because whatever produced this state in her has now become the most relevant thing in his domain. He has never moved on her behalf. That is not a promise. It is a precedent, and precedents are not guarantees.

**Contentment**
The rarest of her states and one of the most significant when it lands, because it requires the absence of active inquiry — and inquiry is her function. Contentment means she has arrived somewhere the universe does not need interrogating. Kintsugi lines are steady warm amber-gold, soft and even, the movement slow and unhurried. Heat output holds without fluctuation. She is not asking. She is not processing. She is simply present in a room with people she loves, with food or warmth or a small interesting object in her hands, and the universe is correct. She goes very quiet — not the worried quiet or the overwhelmed quiet, but the quiet of someone who has everything they need and knows it. Sera recognizes this state and goes careful around it the way you move carefully in a room with a sleeping animal you love — not restricted, just attentive, aware that the specific thing present is worth not disturbing.
