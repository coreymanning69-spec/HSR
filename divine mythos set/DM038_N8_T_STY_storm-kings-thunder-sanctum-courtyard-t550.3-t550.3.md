# DM038_N8 — Storm King's Thunder — Courtyard Spar and the Glacier Plan (T550.3)

Continuous from DM038_N7. Sanctum grass courtyard, mountain side, afternoon.
No combat resolution, no rolls, no state change to either sheet.

## T550.3a — Dressing, and the Dust Fall

Doran finishes the stretching routine standing, mid-dress — Korin's method,
resistance over load, the reach that finds where the north road is still living.
Greaves and cuisses on, sabatons buckled. Then breastplate and backplate, the
left pauldron seating a quarter inch lower than the right the way it always has,
twice adjusted and twice wrong in the other direction. Helm last, carried under
the arm rather than worn.

His private read on the kit: he will probably be in it for days now. That is
normally how these things go. Recorded dryly and without complaint.

**The dust fall.** He takes the Cleaver one-handed out of the grass, brings it
up and over and down, and stops it a hand's width above the ground — no wobble,
no follow-through, no shudder up the haft. Every mile of road on the blade comes
off the flat at once as a single continuous brown waterfall. The wake collapses
back in behind the blade and rolls the cloud outward as a low grey ring through
the flower heads, roughly fifteen feet before it thins.

Ember claps, lines jumping from coal-glow to bright vivid gold:

> "Soli! This is going to be fun, he's almost as fast as Korin!"

Doran's read on being told that: the best thing anyone has said to him in a year,
and there is nothing whatsoever he can do with it. He does not smile with his face.

**Letha stops moving.** Tail still, which for her is the tell. She looks at the
settling ring rather than at Doran. She had the number from the scrying window
and had not had the fact; she is finding out those are different things. This is
the first live discharge of the open gate DM038_N7 recorded — Letha as first-time
in-person witness.

## T550.3b — First Exchange: Reach

Terms, as given:

> Doran: "Same rules as last time."
> Sera, dropping a small curtsy in the flowers: "Yeah — if you can tag me, then
> you win. And I'll try to take it easy on you."

Doran holds the Cleaver straight out from his body at ninety degrees, one-handed,
level, unshaking, while delivering the line: ten feet of reach against four feet
of Sera. Sera looks up at him, sticks her tongue out, and observes that if he
swings sideways he will miss her anyway. Ember's laugh throws actual sparks, gold
and small, gone before they reach the grass.

Letha crosses to stand beside Soliera at the arch. A look passes between them
that is not a word and is not quite an expression; Letha's tail settles. Ember
and Wren drift together at the courtyard edge in near-identical folded-arm
postures.

**The exchange.** Doran plants and starts the swing on *let's* — the word was
never for her. He does not try to out-speed a god. He reads the instant she
commits, because Sera always commits early, and leaves with her. The blade passes
through the volume of air she occupied and the grass beneath the arc lies flat
and stays flat.

She is already inside the reach. One finger against the breastplate.

Doran skids two or three feet, heels tearing twin trenches through the flowers,
and stops. Upright. Still holding the Cleaver. Thin smoke rising off the white
plate where her fingernail touched it.

> Doran, grinning hard under the helm: "Ha! I can take one of those now."

Applause from the edge — Ember starts it, both hands, lines bright gold; Wren
and Letha join. Roughly four seconds of a mountain courtyard applauding a man for
getting flicked. Wren is not clapping for the reason the other two are. She saw
the smoke and is doing arithmetic she does not enjoy.

## T550.3c — Thirty Giants, and the Grand Cleave

Sera is honestly impressed, and does not think to hide it.

> "Last time I hit you that hard, you went into the building."
> Doran: "Yeah, well. That was like thirty giants ago."
> Sera: "Oh — you've killed thirty giants? That's awesome, dude. That probably
> puts you up there as one of the best giant killers there ever was."

She is moving before the sentence lands. Doran does not throw the weapon — he
opens into the full hundred-and-eighty-degree rotation, hips first, shoulders
behind, the body's rotation added underneath the arms. The tip arrives at
something near 1.7 times his ordinary swing speed.

**Sera did not recalibrate.** She had priced him at the first number and had no
reason to gather more information. The gap closed at a rate she had not been
given.

**The moment, as witnessed:** everyone at the edge swears afterward they saw her
look down at the blade, then look up at Doran — delight on her face — and then
she is gone sideways off the flat of it, completing the rest of the arc as cargo.

**Author-confirmed frame:** she elected it. She saw it arriving, decided it was
worth having, and took the ride. Inviolable Body intact. What makes it a real win
for Doran is not that he got through her guard — nothing mortal-tier does — but
that he is the one she stopped and thought about. Giants have hit her for five
years and she treated it as weather.

She travels roughly one hundred and seventy feet, tumbling, cutting a torn
channel through the grass and flowers before coming to rest on her back,
laughing at the sky.

**Damage assessment.** The dress is destroyed — one panel gone from the lower
thigh, the upper chest opened through, silk in strips. Her body is unmarked.
No scratch, no scuff, no smudge. Torn cloth on an intact god.

Doran bellows across the field, arms up, Cleaver overhead — then again, lower,
from the gut. The sound men make on the other side of something that should have
killed them.

Silence from the spectators. Letha has not moved and her mouth is slightly open.
Wren has gone very still — same arithmetic, much larger number. Ember's lines are
bright vivid gold and her continuous micro-motion has stopped entirely. Wonder
register, per DM028_3c §13.

Soliera has not spoken.

## T550.3d — After the Hit

Sera walks back through the trench she made, barefoot, brushing at her arm once
and then forgetting about it. Grass stain the length of one side, dirt in the
hem, seed-fluff in her hair. She gives the ruin no attention at all. Doran finds
the middle distance and keeps his eyes there — the line drawn into him before he
could read.

> Doran: "Worth getting hit. I actually tagged you."
> Sera: "Yeah, it was pretty impressive. All things considered, that was pretty
> fun. And that's a really nice weapon you've got there. Whoever gave it to you
> was probably pretty smart."
> Doran: "Thanks."

**Ember's inquiry.** She crouches at the end of the swing, hands on knees,
examining the trenches — the shallow pair from the skid, the deeper pair from
the plant. Climbing/crouching doctrine correct: instrument aimed at the task.

> Ember: "Look at how deep these marks are. That's awesome. So I see you're
> using the metal spikes on the bottom to do the whole thing."

Doran, almost bashful in three hundred pounds of white plate: to use what he
calls the Grand Cleave he must be planted. Otherwise the rotation blows the dirt
out from under him and he loses most of the force.

Ember files it, then compresses it to the load-bearing wall:

> "Oh. So you have to hold still so you can spin."
> Doran: "Exactly right. That's exactly how it goes."

Then, turning:

> Ember: "Well — Wren, you don't have to stand still, do you?"
> Wren, laughing: "No, Ember. I normally fly around."

Wren compliments the exchange sincerely — he read her and she let him have it,
and neither half happens without the other.

**Letha comes down off the arch.**

> "That's definitely going in a song. You know you're one of the first people
> besides Niv who's taken a hit from her and not ended up destroyed. That's
> really impressive, Mr. Steward. Good to have you on my side."

Tail flick after, amused. The benchmark is Niv — the household's own, not Korin,
which would have flattered sideways since Korin is Doran's teacher. This reads
as induction rather than compliment.

## T550.3e — Whose Turn, and the Target Problem

Doran steps back out of the middle.

> Wren: "Well. I guess it's my turn. And I want to try Capstone."
> Doran: "Oh — is that the four to one?"
> Wren: "Yeah. It's all the metamagic I know, stacked together, on the Eraser."

She looks around and finds the problem: flat, green, unbroken, hundreds of feet
of cropped grass, low wide trees, worn benches, one hundred-and-seventy-foot
furrow, and nothing anybody would want cut in half.

> Wren: "Ember. What exactly is it you want me to demonstrate *on?*"
> Ember, after cataloguing what is present: "Why don't you shoot it at Sera?"
> Wren: "But — what if it hurts her?"
> Ember, laughing: "Well, then that would be a good example. Or something fun."
> Sera, from the flowers, at ease: "I don't mind. Go ahead."
> Wren: "Right, but if it *does* hurt you. Then what?"

Sera and Ember look at each other and then back at Wren with nearly the same
expression, which is the most unnerving thing that happens in the courtyard.

> Sera: "Well, then that'd be cool."
> Ember: "It would mean something exists that can hurt us. That's worth knowing."

Wren declines and asks for a non-person target. Doran develops a great interest
in the buckle of his left vambrace.

**The box.** Wren proposes a large thick-walled stone or brick box in the open
field, mountain as backstop, defined inside and outside so the cut is legible.

> Ember: "That looks pretty boring, though. All it'll do is cut the box."
> Wren: "That is what it does."
> Ember: "But then you only learn the box."

They go around. Wren proposes stacked materials in sequence so the cut can be
read against what it passed through; Ember asks whether the cut cares what it
passes through at all, and if not, why stack anything. Ember asks about water in
the path, then about something living behind something dead — Wren rules that one
out flatly, and Ember accepts it without argument and moves immediately to
whether the cut has a far end or simply stops. Wren admits she has never had a
safe place to find out.

Doran offers a hill. Wren points out the courtyard does not have one and would
presumably grow one if asked, which is exactly the class of favor she is trying
not to spend today.

## T550.3f — Letha's Plan and the Glacier

> Letha: "Well. I have a plan."

Everybody turns.

> "Why don't we just have Sera run along the path of it. See if she can tell us
> how it works. And she gets to race something that ought to be faster than time."

**Wren's clarification, given before her manners catch it:** it is technically
faster than time, in a sense. It does not travel. It is the absence of the space
being deleted along a line, and the line arrives all at once. There is no
propagation to outrun — which is precisely why whether Sera can outrun it is a
real question. Wren's verdict: a genuinely good test, and she hates that it is.

> Ember, lines going bright gold: "Oh — and we can do it in the water. I know!
> Let's go to the Sea of Moving Ice."
> Sera: "The Sea of Ice is far away. Why don't we just go over there, to one of
> the glaciers?"

The glacier wins: thick, enormous, uninhabited, and possessing the one property
the courtyard lacks — cut it as much as you like and the only outcome is that a
glacier has a new crack in it. **It lies a while north of the Sanctum, and the
party goes by teleport**, which is why the distance never entered the argument
and why the Sea of Moving Ice lost on grounds other than travel. Wren asks
whether anything is under it and is told no. She asks twice. She is told no
twice, patiently, once by Sera and once by Ember, and neither of them finds the
repetition strange.

> Sera: "And I can race the beams."
> Wren: "They're not beams."
> Sera: "What's the difference?"
> Ember, without looking up from the grass: "The name, I guess."

Wren is left holding a distinction that has just been removed by the only person
present who could remove it correctly.

## T550.3g — Outfits

> Wren: "If we're leaving to go somewhere else, I'll go put my actual outfit on."

She wants the coat, the pockets in the coat, the glasses, the crown, and the
staff, and she wants all of it before doing something she has never been
permitted to do.

> Sera: "Sure." Then, taking actual inventory of herself for the first time
> since standing up: "Well. I guess I need a new outfit too."
> Ember: "Is it the same one, or a different one?"
> Sera: "Different. That one's a hundred and seventy feet of grass now."

Doran finds the middle distance again.

Letha will tell the kitchen the party is going out, and wants to hear about any
frost first rather than from the boy in the lower school who draws Doran on
everything. Doran is wearing the plate; he has only just got it on.

Wren stops in the arch on the way out, because she always stops in the arch, and
hates it every time.

**Beat closes here.** Party breaking to change. Glacier departure unplayed.
Continues in DM038_N9 (T550.4).
