### Turn tax

| Metamagics on one spell | Cost |
|---|---|
| **One** | sorcery points only |
| **Two** | sorcery points + bonus action |
| **Three or more** | sorcery points + entire turn: no movement, bonus action, or reaction |

At three metamagics Wren plants. This is a readable tell. *Sanctuary* and
Doran's protection are the answers.

### Quicken and concentration

Wren's house Quicken rule permits one bonus-action leveled spell and one
action leveled spell in the same turn. It does not change concentration:
Wren personally holds one spell while the Ring of Concentration may
independently hold its long-rest-loaded spell.

Representative legal stacks:

| Stack | Cost | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Twin + Maximize + Enlarge, 7th-level *magic missile* | 8, full turn | 18 darts, 90 force, no attack or save, 240 ft |
| Twin + Maximize, *disintegrate* | 7, bonus action | 100 damage twice |
| Widen, *reverse gravity* | 3, sorcery points only | 100-ft radius, up 100 ft, then down; Twin and Maximize do not apply |
| Quicken *synaptic static* + *spirit guardians* | 4, one turn | immediate non-concentration burst plus one maintained concentration field |
| Any compulsion with Silent/Still elected | 0 | no verbal or somatic tell; ring elections do not count toward turn tax |

## SPELLS

**Free** means the full cleric list is always live without preparation.
**Known** means one of Wren's original 38 sorcerer-exclusive selections through
7th tier, plus *fireball*, which moved from the retired wand into Wren at
T537.0. The current 1st–7th total is therefore 39. At 8th tier and above,
Corey's settled grants add spells without swaps. **Domain** means castable from
the extra domain-only slot. Choices not listed below remain author-gated even
when a per-tier ceiling has unused room.

### Cantrips — 11

All use maximum character-level scaling: *guidance, thaumaturgy, toll the dead,
sacred flame, spare the dying, light, fire bolt, mind sliver, prestidigitation,
minor illusion,* and *mage hand*.

The 8th tier opened at T535 with 10 general slots and two domain-only slots.
The 3.5 imports resolve through the Edition Translation table below: Will →
WIS +18, Fortitude → CON +12, Reflex → DEX +10, spell resistance becomes
advantage, and every save uses Wren's flat DC 22.

Wren knows every normal published Cleric and Sorcerer/Divine Soul spell. Only
*wish*, Karsus-origin magic, and individually NPC-exclusive spells are excluded;
slot counts do not create a finite known-spell pool.

### SIMULACRUM — AUTONOMOUS EXTRACTION CASCADE

*Simulacrum* is a current 7th-level spell and takes Wren's **entire turn** to
cast: no movement, bonus action, or reaction remains. It is usable once per
long rest by Wren and by each copy. Each body may sustain one personally cast
copy, and no more than four copies may be active globally. The result is literal and autonomous, not a
familiar, puppet, illusion, or remote sense: it has its own mind, thoughts,
judgment, initiative, and reaction economy. Wren knows it exists and what she
wanted it to do, but does not perceive through it or know what it is doing or
feeling.

The copy is exact Wren except that **HP, spell slots by tier, and sorcery points
halve and round down each generation**. Everything else copies unchanged at
creation: ward 75, Channel Divinity 3, *Portal Shear* readiness, working Robe
of the Archmagi, working Staff of the Magi with current charges, Crown motes,
robe stars, and all other current capability. If its caster is flying, it is
born flying. It may make the next copy while the global cap allows it.

| Generation | HP | Total slots | Sorcery points | 7th-level general slots |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Wren | 220 | 150 | 50 | 10 |
| 1 | 110 | 75 | 25 | 5 |
| 2 | 55 | 32 | 12 | 2 |
| 3 | 27 | 14 | 6 | 1 |
| 4 | 13 | 5 | 3 | 0 |

Halve each general and domain slot tier independently, then total the result.
The cascade stops at generation 4 both because G4 has no 7th-level slot and
because four active copies is the global ceiling. Replacement casting is legal
only when fewer than four copies are active. The copy is extraction-first and combat-second:
its independent *Dimensional Shift* and reactions can hold an exit while Wren
and Doran separate.

Copies persist through long rests and survive the death of their parent. Original
Wren feels the death of every copy in her descendant chain as a non-sensory loss,
but receives no last thoughts, moments, pain, replay, cause, sensory transfer,
or forensic information.

**Iron-flask divination ruling:** While Maegera is contained in an iron flask,
the occupant is in an extradimensional prison, occupies no plane, and has no
coordinates. *Discern location* and every other location-divination therefore
return null rather than meeting a ward or block. The physical flask remains an
ordinary trackable object. This is a containment-state rule, not a limitation
of Wren's spellcasting.

### Niv's Descent — 5th-level signature

Named by Ember, this spell turns Wren's deliberate reveal from above into one
committed control event.

- **Trigger and commit:** Wren intentionally dives at least 20 ft and channels
  through the fall. She takes no falling damage; her wings remain manifested.
  Dive, channel, and landing consume her entire turn: no movement, bonus action,
  or reaction remains. She can fly again on her next turn. Elective ring-held
  Silent/Still remains free and excluded from turn tax.
- **Shockwave, 0–20 ft, DEX DC 22:** on a failure, a creature has disadvantage
  on its next attack and crosses rough terrain; on success, only rough terrain
  applies. It may remain a sphere, or at cast time Wren freely sculpts it into a wedge
  excluding one creature's square, a 5-ft terrain-only wall lingering 1–2
  rounds, or—when using air/thunder—a knockback push instead of stumble.
- **Elemental wave, 0–30 ft, CON DC 22:** an unreshapeable sphere deals **8d6**
  of the Energy-Substituted element to enemies, full on failure and half on
  success. Allies do not save and instead heal for the same roll. Doran heals
  the maximum **48** through his own Blessing of Wound Closure; every other ally
  receives the rolled 8d6.
- The free baked-in Sculpt does not count against the spell's ceiling of up to
  three additional metamagics. Twin, Enlarge, and Quicken are dead on this
  spell. Widen and Extend affect control geometry/duration; Maximize, Empower,
  and Repeat are live on enemy-facing damage only, with Empower's ×1.5 applied
  after Maximize. Heavy stacks are an aspirational fluency discovered
  in play, not assumed combat mileage.

### Sigil force lane

One cast per spell per long rest, independently tracked: *Portal Shear, wall of force, Bigby's
hand, Otiluke's resilient sphere, Mordenkainen's private sanctum, forcecage,*
and *Otiluke's freezing sphere*. Sealed capstone *imprisonment* remains
1/long rest. The lane therefore supplies eight one-time instruments across a
dungeon, not a repeatable casting lane.

### Weave-sight resolution

The absence of *see invisibility* is not a functional combat hole. Invisible
creatures read as targetable green shapes under the glasses. Identity, illusions,
shapechangers, and the Ethereal remain social or identification problems to be
encountered in gameplay.

## THE STARS

### ROBE OF STARS AND CROWN OF STARS

The two star systems occupy different halves of the turn:

- **Robe star — action:** *magic missile* at 5th level, seven automatic-hit
  1d4+1 force darts; six per day.
- **Crown mote — bonus action:** ranged spell attack for 4d12 radiant; seven
  motes, expended on hit or miss.

*Crown of stars* is a 7th-level, one-hour, non-concentration spell. Seven motes
orbit Wren's head. At four or more they shed bright light for 30 feet and dim
light for 30 more; at three or fewer only dim light remains. Her illumination
is the ammunition counter.

Wren may Quicken the initial casting for the sheet's normal **4 SP**, freeing
the first action. Each later full pattern uses a robe star as the action and a
crown mote as the bonus action for about 50 damage before accuracy, with no
additional slot, SP, or concentration cost. Six full rounds exhaust the robe;
the crown carries a seventh mote alone.

The Ring of Concentration can hold one spell, Wren can personally concentrate
on a second, and *crown of stars* can run as a third non-concentration effect.
Metamagic reaches only the crown's initial casting; hurling a mote and pulling a
robe star are not spell casts and cannot be modified.

Force and radiant remain live against fire giants. Both ammunition pools are
visible, so an intelligent enemy can count Wren's remaining resources. The
banner literally dims as she spends it.

## SPELL-LIST DOCTRINE

Wren treats magic as narrative rather than slot-based because of the source
through which she channels it. She chooses the spell the scene requires; her
150-slot ledger records that freedom rather than serving as her primary limit.
Her real constraints remain one personal concentration spell plus the
long-rest-loaded Ring of Concentration, one turn against the three-metamagic
tax, and her body.

Her AC 18 / 23 under *shield* and open STR save are deliberate. The 75-point ward
nullifies the damage instance that breaks it, so a single hit does not kill
her; sustained disciplined attention does. Encounters should ask whether the
enemy can look past Doran and commit to Wren. Drow houses are specifically
built to make that choice.

A focused mob check against ten level-5 attackers exhausted the invisible ward
through repeated small hits and killed her in roughly four rounds. Treat that as
a pressure finding, not a universal time-to-kill constant: many small instances
are dangerous precisely because none is large enough to fire overflow
nullification. Her softness remains load-bearing.

### LIGHT DOCTRINE

- *Daylight* supplies 60 feet bright and 60 dim and dispels overlapping darkness
  of 3rd level or lower. Drow *darkness* is 2nd level, so *daylight* wins that
  exchange. The spell lasts one hour and requires no concentration.
- *Daylight* is not sunlight and does not itself trigger Sunlight Sensitivity.
  When Wren places it on Doran or his kit, DM041_A **RULE-GLARE** applies as an
  explicit house ruling: the mirror-white plate's reflected light forces the
  DC 22 Constitution save, with sunlight-sensitive creatures at disadvantage
  on that save because of the glare rather than the spell.
- *Dawn* is actual sunlight, deals 4d10 radiant, and moves 60 feet as a bonus
  action.
- *Continual flame* is permanent heatless light: what stewards leave behind.
- *Hallow* is permanent consecration.
- *Crown of stars* renders a Sanctum priest literally.

### KNOWN DUDS

*Dominate person* targets humanoids and is dead against giant-type creatures.
The fire lane also fails against fire giants, but Energy Substitution converts
Wren's own casts. Staff-of-the-Magi item casts are the deliberate exception.
