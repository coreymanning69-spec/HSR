RELATIONSHIP WITH SERA/SANCTUM:
- "Game recognizes game." Mutual professional respect — both extraordinarily capable and operate through personality as much as force.
- NOT on speaking terms with the Girls during the Travel Arc (DM025_0). Jarlaxle
  did not make himself known in Luskan because the Girls were continuously
  surrounded by high-profile witnesses. He does not walk Luskan's streets in
  daylight when he can run the city from the background.
- Sera KNOWS he's there. She doesn't push it. That is the whole relationship.
- He and Velin corresponded while he watched the Girls alter Luskan. His message
  was a joke, not an unmade request; he sends communications through Velin, not
  Sera directly.
- AI NOTE: Do not write Jarlaxle as avoiding the Girls out of terror or waiting
  on a missing approach beat. He stayed in his normal background lane while too
  many visible powers and witnesses occupied the foreground.
