# DM040_0 — Doran and Wren — Compact Character Capsules

#CHAR-DORAN

## Doran — Compact Character Capsule

**Identity:** Doran. Human man; he/him. Tier-3 Sanctum field Steward and one half of
the mortal rolling pair. Dictation variants such as Dorian, Doreen, Duran, and
Dorran resolve to Doran.

**First read:** Impressive. He has a near-barbarian peak-human build,
professionally cut hair, and the bearing of a professional soldier or
mercenary. His armor silhouette is enormous, but he is a thinker and tactician,
not merely the strongest person in an ordinary mortal room.

**Current T537 mechanics:** Battle Master Fighter 20; 370/370 HP; AC a flat 25;
16d12 superiority dice at DC 22; four attacks with two Action Surges; Relentless;
ordinary universal-rider Cleaver/dagger access, Grand Cleave whole-turn mode,
Read the Seam, and
Deft Answer are current. Raised by divine uplift at T537.0, not XP. Exact
resolution remains in DM041_A and derived DM044_0.

**Dress:** all white. Soliera personally remade the current white kit from its
earlier Sera/Ember forms. The mirror plate's lineage is unchanged, but he carries the
Sanctum's blessing and the Divine Trio's direct stamp visibly. Strangers read
him as sponsored before he says anything.

**Sense:** Soliera's T537 remake is always on and needs no attunement. It lets
him see normally through magical and nonmagical darkness to 120 feet and filters
air, water, and poison to provide breathable air while worn, including underwater
and volcanic environments; it is not truesight. Doran still requires oxygen and
can suffocate without the helm or its support. His mirror-white armor emits no
light; reflected glare is the separate `RULE-GLARE` house rule, and Wren's
*daylight* is not sunlight.

**Voice:** Serious, clipped, terse, and less verbose than Wren. He speaks as
someone permanently on duty. Around Soliera, Sera, Ember, and the Sanctum's
matriarchal gravity, he becomes excessively formal and polite.

**Relationships and pressure:**

- Korin selected and trained him directly; Niv identified him as a viable
  stewardship candidate, Velin approved and briefed him, and T537.0 completed
  his acceptance as a field Steward.
- He trained beside Wren under Korin, Letha, Pell, and Velin.
- He respects and admires Wren but begins with the assumption that his own speed
  can clear the room before a paired method becomes necessary.
- The stick figure and smiley face on his T522 knee plate begin his thaw because
  they register as affection inside all that formality.
- Possible Pell, Letha, or more remote Velin threads must be earned. Desire
  creates pressure, never entitlement or a promised outcome.

**Behavioral engine:** Immediate help, forward pressure, tactical room-reading,
and an ingrained readiness to take the dangerous physical lane. Off duty he
drinks, likes bars, and likes parties.

**Week-ahead personhood gates:** food opinions, one owned worthless object, and
one socially harmless incompetence he keeps attempting remain unauthored.

**Failure mode:** Solo pride plus propriety-as-armor. He can remain correct while
becoming emotionally absent.

**Correction target:** Solo clearer to member of a pair; brick-head formality to
actual presence with the women and people around him.

**Mechanical route:** Current values and readiness are in the active live ledger. Doran's
compact structured mechanics are in DM041_A, with expanded rules and equipment
in DM041_A1; encounter resolution is DM044_0; appearance, history, and loadout
fiction are in DM041_C.

#CHAR-WREN

## Wren — Compact Character Capsule

**Identity:** Wren. Half-elf woman; she/her. Tier-3 Sanctum field Steward, head of
people, and one half of the mortal rolling pair. Dictation variants such as Ren,
Wrenn, Rin, and When resolve to Wren. Exact age and biographical boundaries are
owned by DM041_D.

**First read:** Broad competence. She looks and acts like a party in one:
precisely equipped, formally spoken, visibly educated by the Sanctum, and shaped
by her Elven inheritance. Red hair, freckles, soft beauty, and exceptional
equipment sit over a proven mortal leader.

**Divine damage:** every spell and every attack she makes carries DIVINE
alongside its printed type, never replacing it. Cleric source is Ember's
ability; her Divine Soul source is Soliera Herself. Mechanics owner: DM041_B.

**Current T537 mechanics:** Cleric 20 / Sorcerer 20 stacked; 220/220 HP; ward
75/75; 50/50 sorcery points; 132 general plus 18 Portal/Liberation domain-only
slots = 150 total; DC 22. Portal Shear and Niv's Descent remain her signatures,
now joined by the Portal Toll, the Open Door anchor, and the Aura of the Unbound;
her categorical mind-altering immunity remains permanent. Raised by divine uplift
at T537.0, not XP. Exact resolution remains in DM041_B and derived DM044_1.

**Dress:** a Robe of the Archmagi in brilliant white and gold, with a permanent
crown of stars over her head and an indestructible white-wood staff topped by a
black bird on a metal perch. She and Doran now match instead of inverting, and
neither of them can be mistaken for unsponsored. Soliera personally remade the
current kit, with each object chained from its earlier form; Ember is not the
current maker.

**Voice:** Precise and formal without being bloodless. She watches, follows
implications, and often supplies the useful structural read before anyone asks.
Portal theory is where useful precision becomes one definition and edge case too
many.

**Relationships and pressure:**

- Niv identified her as a viable candidate; Velin approved and briefed her; and
  T537.0 completed her acceptance as a field Steward.
- She trained beside Doran under Korin, Letha, Pell, and Velin.
- TR3-SKT is her first close field contact with Soliera, Sera, and Ember.
- She admires Doran's athletic intensity without beginning from a standing
  crush or relationship commitment.
- She is openly bisexual and woman-leaning. Sera may flirt freely; Wren's
  response is written moment to moment and never forced.

**Behavioral engine:** She performs the math on relaxed divine altitude and gets
vertigo. Under threat she shields, repositions, preserves an exit, and insists
that leaving immediately is usually wiser than waiting for danger to worsen.
The near-leg-loss bite scar is the lived source of that doctrine.

**Failure mode:** First-contact awe can crack the mortal poise she has already
earned, especially when Sera focuses on her.

**Correction target:** Proven leader among people to a steady operator beside
gods—useful, observant, and fully herself without distance as a shield.

**Week-ahead leadership gates:** name a dependent, establish one prior failure,
and show who performs her old role badly in her absence. These are prompts, not
backfilled facts.

**Mechanical route:** Current values and readiness are in the active live ledger. Wren's
compact structured mechanics are in DM041_B, with expanded spells and casting
detail in DM041_B1; encounter resolution is DM044_1; appearance, history, and
exact identity facts are in DM041_D.

## Pair Note

- Doran and Wren begin as classmates, not siblings, an established adventuring
  party, or a couple.
- From T537.0 they are full Tier-3 field Stewards, still below Velin and Letha's
  settled Tier-2 inner-circle standing.
- Since T537.0 the pair geometry is arithmetic, not theme. Within 30 feet of
  Wren, Doran has permanent *freedom of movement* and advantage on every saving
  throw. Separating them is now the correct enemy play, and Wren's exits are the
  only answer to Doran's standing inability to leave a room.
- Both are thinking, observant, highly competent people before they are sheets.
  Roll only when the outcome is genuinely uncertain.
- Doran's starting instinct is to clear; Wren's is to control, shield, and find
  the exit. Their growth is learning when each read completes the other.
- Their divine mentors know they will survive and ultimately succeed. Permanent
  death and party wipe are excluded; judgment, growth, injury, capture, retreat,
  resources, and local success or failure remain live.
- Attraction may emerge during play but is never assumed, owed, or advanced
  automatically.
