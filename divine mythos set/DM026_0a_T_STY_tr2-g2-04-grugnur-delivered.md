### TR2-G2.04: Grugnur — Delivered
**Tags:** #CHAR-GRUGNUR #CHAR-YRSA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CONCEPT-VICTIM-CENTERED-JUSTICE

The Jarl was standing in his chamber with his wife. Three minutes of silence since the gong — every room above and around him going quiet in sequence. His weapon was in his hand because putting it down felt worse than holding it.

Sera told him Yrsa says hi. Told him the woman he chained had spent three weeks deciding whether to send someone like Sera. She decided. Told his wife she could leave — the women and children upstairs were alive, she could join them or go elsewhere.

Soliera closed the distance between the Rift and the Steading — not teleportation, just the space between two points ceasing to be two days of travel — and Grugnur was standing in Yrsa's hall.

Sera flicked off both his kneecaps. The sound was wet and structural.

> "This is the position you need to be in."

He scrambled. Hands on stone, dragging himself by his arms. The frost giant Jarl who had commanded a military alliance and chained a woman reduced to something that moved along the floor by pulling.

Yrsa watched from her chair. Three weeks of compressed decision-making.

Ember looked at Yrsa. Held it for two seconds. Then looked away deliberately and walked out. The room belonged to someone else now.

The door closed behind them. Resolution is Yrsa's, offscreen.


### TR2-G2.05: Interlude — Hot Spring
**Tags:** #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-LETHA #THEME-DOMESTIC-DIVINITY

Soliera looked at the ground in a valley south of the Rift and a hot spring appeared — not dramatically, just a pool of steaming water in a stone basin that had apparently always been there except it hadn't been thirty seconds ago.

Sera was undressed before Letha finished unsaddling. Ember tested the edge, then was in up to her shoulders — the water around her taking on a faint gold luminescence from the kintsugi lines. Letha got in with the efficiency of someone who'd learned not to be self-conscious around divine beings. Soliera sat on the stone rim, feet in the water.

They discussed G3 intelligence while bathing. Letha briefed from the report and the cloud giant's testimony. The chain: Nosnra at the bottom, Grugnur in the middle, Snurre at the top. But Snurre's not the top — he's taking orders from Eclavdra. Drow.

The Hall of the Fire Giant King: volcanic slag hill, three hundred feet high, obsidian gates, polished floors, iron doors. Two upper levels torchlit, one lower level dark and natural cavern. Snurre on the throne with bodyguards, hell hounds, flaming sword. Queen Frupy in her own chambers — smarter than him, the actual coordinator.

> "Frupy," Sera says.
>
> "Frupy."
>
> "That's her *name*?"

Ember giggled. "Queen Frupy."

The critical intelligence: every female and young fights on Snurre's orders. They fight with hopeless abandon because his fear is greater than their fear of intruders.

Sera went still. The pool's surface was very still around her.

> "He makes the women and kids fight?"

> "Ember. You're going to have to sort again."

> "I know. If the women fight because they're scared of him, that's different than if they fight because they want to. I can tell the difference."

> "Yeah. You can."

Ember, floating on her back, fire-hair spread like a corona:

> "If she predicted us, then she wants us to come."

> "Good," Sera said. "Then she won't be disappointed."

**G2 LOOT:** Treaty scroll (alliance intelligence), pass to King Snurre's Hall, crossbow of speed (Letha took it off the trophy wall), alliance report in gold-inlaid skull with troop numbers, raiding schedules, tribute calculations.

**G2 RESULT:** Yrsa's commission delivered. Grugnur's kneecaps removed, delivered to Yrsa's hall for her judgment. All combatant males, ogres, ogre-magi, and guard positions eliminated. All noncombatant giantesses and young alive. White dragons, fire lizards, ice toads spared. Hill giant delegation sent home. Rift functionally empty of military capacity.

### TR2-G3.01: Approach and Entry
**Tags:** #LOC-FIRE-GIANT-HALL #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER

Camp night under extravagant furs. Sera stole the spot closest to Soliera. Ember cuddled up to Letha, giving Sera her chance for soft intimacy with Soli. Dawn. Letha started breakfast. Casual conversation — Letha briefing the final module details while the girls ate.

The volcanic hellscape opened around them on approach: a three-hundred-foot slag hill, smoking vents, lava flows, the air like hot metal. Ember was pleased. Fire was her native element in ways that went deeper than preference.

Obsidian gates: twenty-nine feet tall, ten wide, three feet thick. Designed for fire giant strength or a knock spell to open.

Sera opened them with one hand.

Ember waved at a hidden guard. The alarm horn sounded. Two ettins in the Grand Hall.


### TR2-G3.02: The Hall — Clearance
**Tags:** #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #RULE-EMBER-SORTING #CONCEPT-MORAL-SORTING

The sorting principle from G1 and G2 held through G3. Ember ran the same arithmetic: systematic oppression triggers elimination. Fear-conscripted fighters — giantesses ordered into combat by Snurre's terror — were sorted by Ember's read of their intent. Those who fought because they were ordered to and were more afraid of Snurre than the intruders: spared where possible. Those who fought with genuine aggression: not spared.

Sera cleared rooms with the same casual physics. Kinetic air waves, the wet architectural sounds, combat resolved before the environment finished registering it had started. The volcanic stone absorbed impact differently than ice — hotter, sharper, the resonance lower.

Ember enjoyed the scenery genuinely. The lava flows, the gas jets, the obsidian floors catching light. She walked through fire giant territory the way she walked through everything — with curiosity and the particular attention of someone cataloguing a new kind of beautiful.

The rakshasas in the guest room — three of them, barred because even Snurre didn't trust them — were noted and dealt with. Gnoll thralls were sorted: slaves who fought because they had no choice received the same consideration as conscripted giantesses. Those who fought for pleasure did not.


### TR2-G3.03: Snurre and Frupy
**Tags:** #CHAR-SNURRE #CHAR-FRUPY #CHAR-SERA

King Snurre Iron Belly on his throne. Thirteen feet tall, black iron armor, flaming two-handed sword. Four elite bodyguards in chain shirts. Two hell hounds at his feet. Storm-giant-equivalent combat stats. HP 92.

He'd known they were coming. The chain was broken — Steading transferred, Rift emptied — and his information network had delivered the shape of what was moving south toward him. He mobilized everything he had. It was what he was designed to do.

It didn't matter.

Queen Frupy — the brains, smarter than Snurre, coordinating from her own chambers with eight attendants — fought harder and more intelligently than her husband. The module calls her a sly and cunning horror. She earned it.

It didn't matter either.

Everyone who fought died. Snurre. Frupy. The bodyguards. The hell hounds. The organized resistance that was supposed to adapt between raids and set ambushes and fall back in layers toward the lower levels — all of it resolved in the time it takes to describe it.

The girls don't retreat. The giants adapted to nothing.


### TR2-G3.04: The Lower Level — Eclavdra's Trail
**Tags:** #CHAR-ECLAVDRA #LOC-FIRE-GIANT-HALL

Level three. Natural cavern. Dark. No torches, no gas jets — just rock and whatever light the lava provided. The Drow connection.

Eclavdra's scroll: instructions ordering Snurre to gather every giant type plus ogres, ogre-magi, cloud giants, anything he could raise, for a full invasion of the eastern provinces. Signed by her.

She wasn't there. She'd left before they arrived — or she was watching from somewhere the module doesn't show. The cloud giant in G1 said she predicted divine intervention. Called it a good test. Whatever test she was running, it produced exactly the data she wanted: timing, methodology, sorting criteria, what the girls prioritize, what they ignore.

The scroll was kept. The intelligence was filed. The trail points deeper — into the Underdark, toward whatever Eclavdra is actually building.

The girls chose not to follow. Not today. This was an Itinerary side-stop, not a campaign pivot. Eclavdra's thread moves to dormant future possibility after her observer files the report.


### TR2-G3.05: Aftermath — Claiming the Hall
**Tags:** #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #THEME-DOMESTIC-DIVINITY

With everyone who fought dead and everyone who didn't sorted and released, the Hall of the Fire Giant King belonged to no one.

Then it belonged to Soliera, the way everything the girls walked through eventually did. Not through declaration — through the passive physics of divine presence occupying a space long enough for the space to notice.

The volcanic hall. The obsidian floors. The lava flows and gas jets. The iron doors and vaulted passageways. Ember liked it. She walked the empty corridors after the fighting was done and the stone was still warm and the geometry of a place built for fire giants had a particular beauty that she was the right person to notice.

They stayed long enough to appreciate the scenery. Sera found it interesting. Ember found it beautiful. Soliera found it. That was enough.

Then they left. Back to the Itinerary. Back to the road. The Hall sits empty behind them — claimed, not occupied, the way everything in Soliera's wake is claimed. The giants who survived are scattered to the geography that produced them. The conspiracy thread points into the Underdark toward Eclavdra and stays there, unresolved, until someone decides it matters enough to follow.

**G3 LOOT:** Eclavdra's invasion scroll (intelligence), various fire giant treasures (catalogued by Letha), the Hall itself as a claimed territory.

**G3 RESULT:** King Snurre dead. Queen Frupy dead. All combatant forces eliminated. Noncombatant giantesses and young sorted and spared per Ember's system. Eclavdra's conspiracy confirmed but not pursued. The three-module chain is closed: Steading domesticated, Rift emptied, Hall cleared.


## ARC SUMMARY — AGAINST THE GIANTS (COMPLETE)

**Three modules. One consistent principle: the module's power assumptions are inverted.**

G1 was designed as an elimination problem. Sera turned it into a domestication problem. Zero casualties. Forty giants now belong to her.

G2 was designed to grind parties down over multiple forays. The girls don't foray. Total clearance. Victim-centered justice delivered — Yrsa's commission fulfilled exactly as requested.

G3 was designed as the toughest fight in the chain. The toughest fight was still a fight, and fights against Sera end the same way regardless of the opponent's preparation level.

**The consistent thread:** Ember's moral sorting system. In every module, she distinguished between willing combatants and fear-conscripted fighters. This is the governance mechanism — not Sera's violence, but Ember's discernment directing it. The sorting was consistent, principled, and never overruled.

**Yrsa after TR2:** She received Grugnur at the Steading and resolved his
judgment offscreen, then returned to her actual clan home. She does not remain
at or mediate the Steading. She keeps informed about later SKT events through
the giant groups already encountered.

**The Steading after TR2:** Sera claimed the giants and left. They continue
their normal lives without Sanctum administration, with the permanent rule
that they do not attack humans at all.

**The Eclavdra thread:** Dormant by choice. She predicted divine intervention, called it a good test, and receives the observer's report. Whatever data she gathered, she keeps it. The Underdark connection may return later, but no active mystery or response is owed here.

**The Deposition Principle (from TR1):** The girls didn't gain resources from these modules. They deposited consequences. The giant alliance is dismantled. The orcs are free. Yrsa has her justice. The Hall is claimed. The conspiracy is identified. The girls got: good food, a hot spring, nice scenery, and a laugh about Queen Frupy's name.
