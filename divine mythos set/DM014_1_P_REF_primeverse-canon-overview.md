SOLIERA PRIME – CANON OVERVIEW
"She was raised by the land and loved by a golem that kills anything that hurts her. And still, she offers flowers first."

Soliera Prime – Narrative Core
Origin:
Born in a post-Gate, humanity-dominant world where metaphysics and technology coexist. Raised in a village known as Cradle under the quiet protection of Evan and Arden. Her mother's identity is mythic and unclear, but Soliera was left with purpose.

Personality:
Compassionate, happy, not jaded. Soliera Prime is innocence maintained by divine violence — her peace is absolute because those who would hurt her simply don't survive.

Mythos Role:
A girl who never struggled, never lacked. She is joy as a character — sun-warmed skin, dirt-streaked knees, and enough magic to rewrite the world if she ever thought to do so.

Arden – Divine Guardian
Nature:
An unkillable, multi-form entity of paternal judgment and execution. He is perfect control beneath impossible strength — and he only exists because she exists.

Behavior:

Does not speak aloud.

Kills without sound or spectacle.

Appears only when intent turns hostile.

Exists in three modes: City Mode, Travel Mode, and War Mode.

Core Rule:
If you wish Soliera harm, you die. Sometimes in the next heartbeat. Sometimes in the next breath. Sometimes in the past.

Lore Systems & Worldbuilding
1. Intent-Based Reality:
The world subtly shapes itself to Soliera's will — doors open when she's curious, animals stop when she watches. Her surroundings respond, not in flash or command, but in cooperation. The Rule of Three / Third Attempt Rule is the clearest repeatable edge: on the third attempt at a distinct thing, reality adjusts whether or not she knows she is attempting it.

2. Tier System (PROM Variant):

Tier 1: Civilians

Tier 2: Elite soldiers

Tier 3: Unique operatives (Veris, early Cassian)

Tier 4: Mythic field-clearers (Varn, Veyren, late Cassian)

Tier 5: Singulars (Arden, Deashi, Gods)
Soliera exists outside this system. Arden overrides it.

3. Itemization and Monster Design:

Enemies and artifacts have modular tags (Prefix/Suffix system).

Example: Cursed Iron Fang (Deals bleed damage, +1 shock resistance).

Enemies include folklore horrors, glitch-beasts, stealthed monstrosities, conceptual terrors.

Locations
1. Cradle (Village):
Soliera's origin. Peaceful, fertile, subtly impossible. Prosperity bloomed from love, not conquest.
2. Outpost Seven:
Her chosen location. A company megafortress disguised as a base. Instantly retrofitted into a divine embassy. Now a zone of myth and safety.
3. The Compound (Cassian Arc):
Beneath Outpost Seven. Multi-floor horror-stack with mimetic warping, undead Company dead, and corrupted space.
4. The Field Zone:
A 20-mile defense radius, heavily layered checkpoints, monitored globally. Arden's presence assumed at all times.

Key Characters (Quick View)
For full character profiles see DM014_3.

Veris (Project DIVIDE / Countersign):

She was the result of not being able to directly interact with Soliera Prime: the Company, parts of the Church, and independent operators fused science, miracle attempts, folk magic, and myth theory into one program. Countersign Divide is the recovered institutional synonym for Project DIVIDE. The program wanted a divine counterweight; Veris is the surviving result, a Tier 3.5 enhanced human.

Rational, enhanced, brutally trained from childhood. White-blonde, freckled, compact, and physically tuned cooler than baseline human for muscle usage; to her, that regulation is normal.

Now thawing emotionally after contact with Prime.


Cassian:

Tier 4.5. Cold, methodical, plain-clothes threat math. His bosses call his room-reading method the Gravemaker Doctrine.

One-man army. Perfect reflexes. Guardian of peace.

Respects Soliera. Sees himself in Veris.

Mythic among mortals — feared and respected worldwide.

5'9" and 220lbs. Brown hair, wears dark combat slacks normally.

Veyren:

Beast-man strong. Primal energy. Fights first, laughs second.

Spars with Varn. Respects Soliera. Wants to protect her, too.
6'6 and 350 lbs. Built like a lions coils around a body builders frame, he's a reflex/attack hunter. Uses his hands as claws, slices or ribs chunks, bites and tears. Etc. Has incredible regeneration abilities.

Varn:

Orc forge master. Came seeking Arden. Stayed for Soliera.

Shocked to learn Arden serves a young woman. Respects her without question now. 7'2 Orc, big and burly. Enjoys crafting weapons and armor, it's a right and a privilege to make high quality stuff in his society

Crossworld Potential
Divine Soliera & Sera Arrival:.

World learns: there are others — higher ones.

Divine Soliera = the perfected future of Prime.

Sera = incomprehensible force of physicality.

Arden recognizes himself in Deashi. They might nod. That's enough. Structural mirror: Arden : Soliera Prime :: Deashi : Divine Soliera.

Narrative Constants
Rule of Three / Third Attempt Rule – Reality adjusts itself on her third try.

Peace Through Protection – Her life is peaceful because Arden exists.

Power Without Ego – She's as strong as gods, but she doesn't lead. She lives.

The Room Rule – She walks through a place, and the best possible version of that place becomes real beside her.

The Closet – Infinite, context-aware outfits appear. Beauty through belonging.

b) She's got control over the space/time/area/concepts around her, very similar to Soliera Divine,
