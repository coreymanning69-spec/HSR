## COMPANY INTERVENTION AND SETTLEMENT — PART 2: SETTLEMENT CLOSE AND AMARA WITNESS RECORD

At the table, Ember slid down from the leather seat, brushing crumbs from her knees. She looked down at the blue-and-white jacket Amara had dressed her in, sticky stains smeared across the front, and huffed.
"Oh. I'm back to being messy again." With childlike resolve, she began peeling it off, leaving Amara half-reached as if she might intervene, cheeks coloring again.

And then Soliera stood.

It was nothing more than the quiet motion of a woman rising — yet it rippled like a commandment through the room. Amara rose instantly, half a gasp caught in her throat. Steven straightened with her, rag still in hand, as though some inner voice had whispered: stand or be struck down.

Soliera said nothing. She didn't need to.

Steven busied himself immediately, bending into the safety of his role, scooping plates and cups with frantic order. Cleaning, polishing, restoring. He knew his place.

The tavern doorframe filled with a new shadow.

Captain Commander Bucky stepped inside, unhurried. He remembered the melted klaxons. He remembered the girl's distaste for noise. So he did not knock. He did not raise his voice. He walked in as though entering a predator's den, movements measured, respectful.

He stopped short of the table, salt-and-pepper head slightly bowed, eyes never rising. His words were steady, careful, each one balanced on a razor edge.

"Hello, ladies. I am Commander Bucky."

No eye contact. Not with the doll crouched by the bleeding man, not with the obsidian girl discarding her jacket, not with the goddess standing silent at the center. He kept his gaze low, treating them like one treats wild, sacred beasts — with reverence, with caution, with the fear that one flicker of the eye could mean death.

The room held still, waiting for Sera's judgment.

M52 — Instincts and Attire

When Soliera stood and began to move around the bulk of the leather loveseat, the motion pulled the air with her. Silent, inexorable, an orbit forming.

Bucky felt the vector of her path, the inevitability that she would pass right beside him. And instinct — not training, not protocol, but something older — told him what to do. He dropped immediately, one knee to the boards, head bowed.

The tavern caught its breath.

Sera's lips curved into a smile, sharp and approving. "Ah. Good instincts. I like you."

Because to her, this was the natural order: people bowed when gods passed. Anything less was arrogance. And arrogance meant correction.

At the far side of the room, Ember wriggled out of her stained jacket, letting Amara fuss over her. This time, Amara had found a plain shirt — orange with black and yellow stripes — a simple thing, but against Ember's ember-fire hair it looked almost ceremonial, a harmony of flame colors.

It hung long on her, brushing her thighs. But below, there was still only the pure-white linen undergarments, silky and stark. Amara bit her lip, fussing with the hem, and looked to Steven.

"Do we have any pants?" she asked softly, as though bargaining with the barkeep could balance the impossibility of the scene.

Steven swallowed, nodding quickly, already turning toward the back with the desperate focus of a man who knew: better to keep cleaning, better to keep providing, than to be still and noticed.


M53 — Names and Gifts

While Bucky stayed kneeling at the threshold, head low and unmoving, Veyren pushed himself upright. His frame was whole again, the regen knitting him back into the bruiser's carved physique. Half his armor was still sheared away, raw flesh beneath the plating, but he stood tall regardless.

Sera circled him like a cat, fingertips trailing idly across bare steel and skin, brushing along the gouges where her flick had stripped him. She wasn't assessing, not really — just playing, curious, disinterested and intent all at once.

"You're awfully big," she mused, eyes half-lidded.

"Yes," Veyren rumbled, voice steady now. "That's one of my gifts. I'm Veyren."

She tasted the name aloud, turning it like a sweet in her mouth.

"Hmm. Veyren. I like that."

Her finger tapped against the ridge of his chestplate. "You can regenerate."

Veyren chuckled, a dry sound, and shrugged the ruined half of his armor. "Yeah. That's kind of my whole thing. They send me in when we might need to get splattered."

Sera's laughter chimed, sharp and bright. She flicked the edge of his shoulder as if testing its strength again. "Good. I'm glad you lived."

For the first time since stepping through the gate, the statement hung like a genuine mercy — though from Sera's lips, even mercy was edged with threat.


M54 — The "King"

Bucky shifted, not rising, but easing his kneel into something less punishing, more formal. His spine stayed straight, his eyes low. When he spoke, his voice carried the weight of command held in check.

"My name is Commander Bucky. I lead this entire area, as far as the Company is concerned. The Company is the government here." His eyes flicked to the doll in silk. "So, Lady Sera… I would be the king you were looking for."

Sera perked up, sapphire-blue eyes sparking. She clapped her hands once, delighted.

"Oh! Well, I wanted to ask you why y'all came through here and destroyed the door while we were eating. That was awfully rude. And then you didn't even come the first time. I almost forgot we were waiting on you."

She walked toward him now, light steps carrying the same inevitability as a hammerfall. Bucky's chest tightened — every nerve said flight — but he stayed bowed. His breath caught when she drew close, silk brushing the edge of his vision.

But she did not strike.

She tilted her head, voice soft but edged. "Well, we're done eating now, so I guess it's fine. But next time, don't bother us."

Bucky bent lower, forehead nearly to the boards. "Yes, ma'am. I mean—yes, Lady Sera."

Sera grinned at him.

M55 — Oak City Settlement Close (Timeline Condensation)

The following is a condensation of beats covering the remainder of the Oak City interlude before DM016_1.

The girls exit the tavern and wander. Ember climbs onto an APC; the turrets autolock onto her. Sera bisects the machine without ceremony. People scatter as it slides apart, clearly split along a line of absolute force. No explosion — just division.

Veyren becomes a de facto tour guide, keeping pace with the Trio through streets that empty ahead of them and fill behind. They enter a hospital. Soliera moves through it in silence. Everything around her improves: the ill stabilize, water runs cleaner, soldiers nearby lose the will to impede her. Not by command — by proximity.

By the end of the day, Oak City has welcomed its new owners with open arms. They are absurdly powerful, yes. Unpredictable by most measures. But they heal. They clean. They laugh. God is a woman. The world must learn what that means.

The Trio spends several weeks in the Oak City area. This period includes: watching kung fu movies with Veyren and Bucky's people, and Sera's spar with Cassian, Veyren, and Niv.

## APPENDIX — AMARA WITNESS RECORD

Amara's perspective on this arc was explored in depth. She has her own framework for processing what she witnessed, her own reactions that didn't surface to the group, and an internal life the narrative visited. Her presence here is not passive — she was a lens.


### WITNESS FRAME — PHASE 2 (M43–M55)

*Anchor beat: M41 — Ember's skin*

Amara is at the table now. She did not choose this. She has been folded into the orbit of three beings she cannot categorize and she is doing the only thing her training supports in a total information failure: she is watching, precisely, and she is noting what her body does in response to what she sees.

Sera propositions her openly. The heat in Amara's face is noted and filed. Ember takes food seriously and spills it and is wrapped in a borrowed jacket. Amara reaches forward to dress her — and her fingertips brush obsidian skin that is warm when it should be cold. The barkeep appears beside her with a rag because people do small acts of service when they have no other way to participate.

The clipboard man arrives. Sera threatens casually. The clipboard man leaves everything he came in with. Amara watches and notes: the threat was delivered in the same register as the food order. No mode switch. No performance. The same girl who wanted juice told a man that she would kill him and both statements had identical weight.

By the end of Phase 2 she understands the category she has been wrong about. It is not *dangerous.* It is *danger as a property of the air,* the way pressure is a property of depth.

### AMARA'S SENSORY VOCABULARY — DIVINE CONTACT

*Amara has no theological vocabulary. She builds hers from physical sensation.*

**Soliera's presence:** Pressure from all directions simultaneously. Not weight — compression. The feeling of a room becoming more real. She has felt variations of this from powerful practitioners; Soliera's version is to that feeling what an ocean is to a cup.

**Sera's field (Velvet Spiral):** Warmth that doesn't come from a source. The specific disorientation of having a threat-read produce no actionable data. Something between a pheromone response and a wrong step on a staircase — the body expecting one thing and receiving another.

**Ember's presence:** The uncanny. Things that should be cold being warm. Light that comes from inside a surface rather than bouncing off it. Ember is the most disorienting of the three for Amara because Ember looks the closest to something that follows known rules and then doesn't.


### AMARA'S OPERATING RULES

- **NO THEOLOGIZING.** Amara does not reach for spiritual language. She thinks in terms of force, category, threat-read, and body. If she needs a word for what Soliera is, she will build one from physical sensation before she borrows one from any tradition.
- **PHYSICAL INTERIORITY ONLY.** Her internal experience is expressed through body. Shoulders dropping. Throat tightening. The pace of her breath. Hands that move toward old habits and stop.
- **STAFF TIMING — CRITICAL.** The bo staff leans against the table at M0. She does not grip it, reach for it, or make contact with it until M18. The reach in M18 is the demotion beat. It cannot be spent earlier.
- **NO DIVINE VOCABULARY.** Amara does not use words like "goddess," "divine," "holy," or "sacred" in her interiority. She builds her own vocabulary from the data her body gives her — pressure, temperature, force, weight.
- **REFRACTION, NOT REWRITE.** Amara's camera does not change what occurs. It changes what the camera looks at during those events.
