# THE PAIR

## Current Arc Doctrine

Shared pair doctrine lives here once. DM041_A and DM041_B own character-specific
source mechanics and do not mirror this material. The doctrine is:

Exposure symmetry: Doran's failure modes are `DM041_A3`; Wren's are `DM041_B4`.
The pair is durable because the two lists do not overlap; an encounter that
reads only one of them is not pressuring the pair.

- Do not reduce field-Steward capability for balance. Enemies adapt or fail.
- Where a Steward capability has multiple supported strengths, use the stronger reading; make opposition survive through action denial, positioning, and encounter structure rather than silently weakening the kit.
- Resolve effects on the grid first, then describe their footprint in prose; description never adds targets or resolutions beyond the grid result.
- Their full sheets are mechanically live, but tactical fluency is learned one
  beat at a time. The sheet is the ceiling, not the opening position.
- Matched white plate and white-and-gold robe are Sanctum heraldry. There is no anonymous version
  of the pair, and prepared enemies prepare for the Sanctum.
- Their literal-demigod mentors answer upward to gods and overgods and send them
  with informed confidence that the mission will ultimately succeed. Death and
  permanent party wipe are not the examination.
- Defeat, capture, injury, unconsciousness, humiliation, retreat, resource loss,
  and failed local objectives remain live. The point is what Doran and Wren
  notice, choose, learn, and become while paying those costs.
- The crucible does not require a loss. It requires that defeat remain available:
  dice stay live, bluffs stay callable, and the worst case stays real even when
  unlikely. Ride the dice; do not narrate the pair into wins.
- Guthvarr's boulder is the canonical stakes example: attack 22 against Doran's
  braced AC 25, redirect 26, and a cracked rib from the residual force of the
  point-blank redirect. The brace held. The cost was still real.
- The completed frost-giant, Ring of Winter, and Artus leg already granted major
  bonuses and abilities. That growth is the precedent, not a balance mistake.
- The Sanctum has no procurement shortage. Components and optional ritual props
  are free and effectively unlimited at home, while field slots, item charges,
  action costs, and per-fight state still matter.
- A champion/legend band around level 20 exists between the field Stewards and the
  girls. The T531 Conquest paladin establishes that escalation rung.
- They inherited Sera's announcing doctrine without her invulnerability. Sera's
  casual T75 renaming of a seven-House drow city is the scale contrast:
  Ironslag will not reorganize itself around Doran and Wren.

## Starting Relationship

Doran and Wren know each other from approximately one year in the same advanced
training cohort. They are not childhood siblings, a pre-formed adventuring
party, or an established couple. They know one another's classroom habits and
training tells but have not learned what either person becomes when tired,
injured, embarrassed, frightened for someone else, or responsible for a bad
decision in the field.

Doran respects Wren's competence but begins with the Lone Ranger assumption that
his own speed and daggers will usually settle the room before a paired method is
necessary. Wren sees the impressive athlete clearly and also sees the operational
problem: a man who can clear almost anything alone has not yet learned when
winning alone is the wrong shape of success.

Wren is openly bisexual, leans toward women, and does not begin with a defined
crush on Doran. Attraction remains a live possibility rather than a prewritten
outcome.

Sera flirts with Wren constantly and freely. Sera's side is Corey's to author;
Wren's side is not steered. Wren may come to like it or she may not. Her answer
must be written honestly, moment to moment: awe, fluster, deflection, warmth, or
disinterest. Sera's interest does not decide Wren's answer.

Doran's possible attraction toward Pell, Letha, or Velin is likewise earnable,
never guaranteed. His high drive and the surrounding matriarchy are examination
pressure, not a romance schedule.

## Opposite-Gendered Mirror

Their fumbles share a root and take opposite shapes. Wren cracks most visibly
around Sera: beauty, flirtation, instability, and first-contact awe make her
verbose control slip. Doran cracks around the women collectively along the
desire/propriety axis: the more he wants and the less acceptable any easy outlet
feels, the more perfectly formal and less personally present he becomes.

Use the Steward-POV camera to cut between them reacting to the same divine
moment. Wren over-explains. Doran becomes a field report in human form. Neither
reaction should be flattened into generic awe.

## Pair Tactical Doctrine

The sheet is the ceiling, not the opening position. Do not patch perceived gaps
before play tests whether the field Stewards remember the tools already available.

- Twinned *hold monster* costs 4 SP and can paralyze two giants. Attacks within
  5 feet then crit automatically; Doran converts the removed turn into five
  dagger attacks, or eight with Action Surge, under the default ring-held
  *haste*. Without that ring load, the totals are four and seven.
- *Synaptic static* is the stronger module-wide area answer against giants: 8d6
  psychic against weak INT saves plus its minute-long penalty die.
- *Heroes' feast* closes poison and gas exposure for twelve hours; *tongues*
  solves Giant-language access; *teleport* and *teleportation circle* cut
  overland delay; *word of recall* is Doran's extraction if Wren remains able to
  cast; and *arcane gate* supplies battlefield movement.
- *Commune* and *augury* let the second generation ask upward without surrendering
  the decision. *Freedom of movement* closes Doran's restraint lane.
- *Warding bond* gives Doran +1 AC, +1 saves, and resistance while routing equal
  damage into Wren. At AC 18 it may kill her; retain that inversion.
- *Sanctuary* is Wren's answer after a three-metamagic full-turn commitment.
- *Simulacrum* is Wren's extraction asset first and combat asset second. Its own
  initiative, reaction, and Dimensional Shift make the field shape two soft
  bodies and one hard body without shifting Doran's burden onto either Wren.

No spell requires a material component or cost. Optional ritual props are free
through the Sanctum; field slots, turns, concentration, and consequences remain
the real limits.
