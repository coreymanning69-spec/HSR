## 2. Project Thesis

Divine Mythos is a theological fantasy sandbox built on D&D 5e/Forgotten Realms
assumptions unless a file declares Mergeverse or Primeverse. It asks what a world
becomes when divine beings are present, domestic, embodied, and not meaningfully
opposed by normal adventure logic.

Core thesis: divine domesticity. The gods do not rule from distance. They sit in
rooms, eat, dress, travel, touch, listen, and build. That makes the outcome binary:

- Inside the chosen/protected circle: paradise, safety, abundance, intimacy, repair.
- Outside in opposition or predation: correction, exile, erasure, or obliteration.
- There is no stable middle ground where opposition remains safely dramatic.

This is not a hero's journey, not grimdark, and not a standard player power fantasy.
The gods have won. Tension comes from consequence, restraint, witness response,
institutions, logistics, domestic aftermath, and what the world becomes around
beautiful authority that does not need permission.

## 3. Core System Laws

**Tier 5 is a category break.** Tiers 1-4 are useful for civilians, adventurers,
elite named figures, dragons, field-clearers, and other beings inside probability.
Tier 5 is not higher math. Soliera, Sera, Deashi, and relevant singulars are outside
the system. Do not calculate Tier 5 versus Tier 4.

**Never nerf the gods.** Divine survival, capability, and victory are not open
questions. Do not generate anti-feats, parity fights, rescue arcs, power limits
from silence, or growth arcs where the god learns to become complete.

**Modules are stress tests, not scripts.** Published modules, trope shells,
kingdom problems, monsters, curses, and travel encounters provide functions:
the threat model, the mortal stake, the institution, the ecology, or the lie.
Divine Mythos runs those functions through divine presence and records the trace.
Exact drop-in routing lives in DM034_1.

### Canon Handling

- Corrections from Corey are canon updates; apply them without confirmation
  loops after resolving the correct owner.
- T-beats, M-beats, P-beats, and TR-beats are distinct namespaces.
- Primeverse T4s use a different ontology from Divineverse divinity; difference
  is not lesser importance.
- “Prime” and “Divine” are meta shorthand unless an active source establishes
  that wording in-world.
- A person receives no name in play until the girls know it, unless the active
  source already names them.
- Most mortals self-filter before a cold approach to Sera or Ember. Do not
  invent a walk-up introduction merely to supply a name.

**Infrastructure, not empire.** The Sanctum builds, heals, houses, feeds,
educates, routes, protects, and stabilizes. It does not need conquest to dominate.
People may leave. Freedom exists inside the protected frame, but predation, child
harm, and direct opposition to Soliera's core values are hard stops.

**Children are infinite branches.** Children represent possible futures, not
assets. Harm to children or equivalent innocent predation triggers absolute
protection and may bypass ordinary social negotiation.

**Reparations are automatic infrastructure.** Divine violence creates mortal
ripples. Stewards identify survivors, economic dependencies, housing needs, work
placement, education, and community stabilization. The killer's consequence is not
remorse; society's consequence is repaired by system design.

**Witness economy is fundamental.** Mortals turn divine contact into records,
routes, shrines, stories, fear, devotion, resentment, etiquette, and institutions.
The gods do not manage the mythology. Mortals generate it because they witnessed
something no prior framework can comfortably hold.

**Preserved causality is deliberate for witnessed displays.** Divine conclusions normally
remove the target, never the witnesses. The Sanctum could erase every ordinary threat
antiseptically, but does not: Tiamat, the dracolich, Sera acting in the open, and their
aftermaths remain real so the world can witness cause and consequence. Deashi's true
retroactive pruning is a different defensive tier. It removes an existential branch so
completely that no witness, legend, or blank spot can identify an event. Tiamat and the
dracolich are displays, not examples of pruning. (Cross-ref: Witness Economy; DM042_0 §9.)

### Witness Economy — Fear As Perception Acuity

Terror measures the witness's capacity to perceive, not the object's raw
strength. Weak minds can slide off Soliera because she is past legible, then
fixate on Sera because Sera is legible enough to read as huge. That fear is
real and wrong. The perceptive fear correctly, and that accuracy is the horror.

Fear is therefore a dial on the Witness Economy: dread reads the observer.
Genre is set by the angle of the eye; terror is set by the acuity of it. Wren
fearing Soliera's teleport is a correct read. A goblin gawping at Sera while
under-reading Soliera is an incorrect read. See `DM028_1` for Soliera's
teleport/disposition doctrine and `DM041_0` for Wren's open thread.

### Founding Thesis — Worth and Its Abolition (interpretive)

The T0–T50 foundation arc can be read as a theology of worth: the pricing of
persons and the abolition of that ledger.

- Reva was priced as a useless byproduct of subsistence arithmetic before she was
  treated cruelly. The Remaking answers pricing with surplus: a body entirely in
  excess of use. Sera's body and the no-debt economy make the same claim at two
  scales — worth will no longer be computed.
- Letha was double-priced as fetish object and threat marker. Her first neutral,
  receiving gaze comes from Sera, another person who knows what it means to be
  treated as a body before a person. That is why Letha chooses Sera first.
- Taliandra is priced at zero by her own house and then subjected to Soliera's
  explicit, cruel-era conduct math from T35 through T50.
- Korin mirrors Reva at the far end of life: aging out of the only economy that
  valued his labor. His Remaking settles eighty years of accumulated effort as
  back-pay instead of consolation.
- The Hoard's promise to take everything includes the people at full value, with
  no triage. Pricing-abolition becomes logistics.

The hinge is that divine indifference to mortal valuation produces both horror
and grace. A chieftain dies under an arbitrary new standard; Korin is renewed by
an arbitrary settlement. Fear-flash and tenderness ride the same nerve. Never
render only one side.

## 4. Divine Tetrad Snapshot

**Soliera** is the sovereign/infrastructure layer: omnipotence expressed as control,
repair, claim, and silent will. She is not omniscient: her passive awareness covers current
reality within roughly ten miles, she cannot see the future, and the Veiled Eye deliberately
excludes her. She possesses the underlying power to rewrite mind, memory, belief, desire,
or identity, but the mature law she lives under is both self-imposed and imposed through
YUIUM: she does not use it without consent and current awareness. The unbuffered founding
era predates that restraint. Her purpose is **Explore, Examine, Expand**. She
never loses, fails, doubts, needs rescue, or grows into power. AI never voices her unless
Corey provides the line. Use DM028_1 for precision.

**Sera** is the joyful physical answer and the primary Jungian Shadow: kinetic
enforcement, appetite, boundary defense, Velvet Spiral, consent-locked intimacy,
women-only sexuality, and Soliera-facing devotion. In the separate Freudian lens she is
the Id. She is a small adult woman, never minor-coded. Violence is her function, not her
mood. Use DM030_1/DM030_2 and DM027_0 for precision.

**Ember** is inquiry and innocence: the deliberately late other hand, curiosity with
divine weight, truth-surfacing, creation/transformation, and moral disarmament. Soliera
made her after learning that Sera's direct-response interface did not ask enough questions;
only after Ember existed did the completed pattern become visible. She is adult-bodied
with the same frame as Sera; her childlike quality is experiential, not physical. Use
DM028_3 for precision.

**Deashi** is silent consequence: retained law, atemporal awareness throughout the
Veiled-Eye domain, witnessed displays of judgment, and unknowable pruning of genuine
existential threats. In the Freudian lens he is the Superego, not the Id or Jungian Shadow.
He never speaks, ever. Use DM028_2 for precision.

YUIUM is the speakable Name and distributed Whole. No separate YUIUM person remains dormant
or observing after the split: Soliera goes and experiences; Deashi stays and ponders; Sera
and Ember are their experiences' next recursive iteration. The Sanctum and Veiled Eye carry
the Whole's substrate/knowing function through Deashi rather than a third agent. Use DM003_0,
DM029_03, and DM042_0 when YUIUM or the Sanctum's body/infrastructure matters.

### The Recursive Five-Point Architecture

The five named points are **YUIUM, Deashi, Soliera, Sera, and Ember**. Within that star,
the Name's four positions are locked: Deashi = Yod/Father, Soliera = first-Heh/Mother,
Sera = Vav/Son, and Ember = final-Heh/Daughter/Malkuth/Metatron. Family terms are structural
offices rather than bodily gender or ordinary kinship. The Kabbalistic/Metatron layer is
literal in-world architecture.

Camel/Lion/Child are relational rather than fixed:

- **Ontological Circle:** Deashi/Camel, YUIUM/Lion, Soliera/Child.
- **Manifest Circle:** Soliera/Camel, Sera/Lion, Ember/Child.
- **Cross-Layer Bridge:** Deashi/Camel, Sera/Lion, Soliera/Child.

Soliera is the hinge across all three. The model can recurse infinitely, but no additional
named rung exists without played canon. **Full structure — Name positions, psychology,
awareness, Veiled Eye, Deashi doctrine, and body-mechanics: DM042_0.**

## 6. Failure Guards

Do not generate these errors:

- Soliera has hidden limitations because she does not solve every visible problem.
- Tier 4 entities can negotiate with Tier 5 from parity.
- Sera is a child, mascot, teen, boy-scaled, or emotionally growing into herself.
- Sera broods over violence or develops a moral hesitation arc.
- Deashi speaks, narrates internally, or communicates in dialogue.
- The Sanctum is a prison or empire in the ordinary political sense.
- Reparations are optional mercy rather than automatic infrastructure.
- Missing prose means missing canon rather than compression.
- M-beats, T-beats, P-beats, and TR-beats are one chronological sequence.
- Primeverse and Divineverse versions of Soliera are interchangeable.

If a scene seems contradictory, assume compression or a missing owner-file detail
first. Search by character plus scene action. Ask only when the missing answer
changes canon, routing, consent, chronology, or proper nouns.
