# DM038_N6 — Storm King's Thunder — Goldenfields Close and Sanctum Departure (T549.3–T550.1)

This file is closed prose through T550.0 plus a recorded T550.1 departure
boundary. The later bare Sanctum position and current route → DM038_L; refreshed
readiness → DM038_L.

The spectral dragon appeared with Ithreva and died in Doran's opening charge.
Its presence and destruction are canonical, not a staged dossier proposal.

## T549.3 — The Fourth Action

G2 spent the four *time stop* turns on setup and blinked the last of the distance
on the fourth, arriving at Ithreva's shoulder. Two castings of *feeblemind*
followed—one quickened under Wren's house rules and one on the action.

Ithreva saved both, 30 and 26 against DC 22. No legendary resistance was spent.
*Time stop* broke on the first casting.

## T549.4 — Grand Cleave

Doran planted, committed both hands, and swept the arc through Ithreva, the
Named Rogue, and G2. The Rogue and G2 were destroyed; Ithreva took 171. Doran
knew G2 was in the arc before he swung. The technique did not distinguish ally
from enemy. The two deaths fed Ithreva twenty points of Harvest.

This was the played result under the then-current Grand Cleave ruling. The final
Cleaver rules are owned by DM041_A/A1/A2 and projected by DM044_0; the old dice
formula has no forward authority.

## T549.5 — The Ledger

G1 used its last sorcery point on Careful and fired Portal Shear through Doran,
Ithreva, and the Ledger of Attendance. Ithreva saved. The Ledger did not: 83
force/teleportation damage destroyed its 80-point body. Its 141 written names
came apart into grey confetti and went out before reaching the ground.

Re-entry ended permanently. Attendance was stripped. The Named Rogue remained
dead. Doran was untouched by the line.

## T549.6 — The Wrong Wren

Ithreva put five Blood Darts into G1 for 88 necrotic damage and destroyed it.
She believed she had killed Wren. Truesight gave her no correction because a
simulacrum is a creature, not an illusion or shapechange; Mind Lock denied
*detect thoughts*, and snow left no corpse for *speak with dead*.

The direct simulacrum connection ended immediately and carried no pain, replay,
cause, sensory transfer, or forensic information.

## T549.7 — The North Door

Doran struck twice more across two rounds. Ithreva spent 30 Harvest to refuse
154 and another 30 to refuse 196 through Not Yet.

A door opened on her north side and the real Wren stepped through. Ithreva's
Blood Darts put 67 into Wren's ward and zero into her HP, leaving the ward at
8/75.

## T549.8 — Four Lines

Wren used the day's Portal Shear with Split Ray, Twin, Maximize, Distant, and
Careful. Four 100-foot lines opened across the north road. Ithreva saved against
three and failed one, taking 160. This four-line cast is a historical played
result; it is not the later level-10 merged Eraser capstone.

## T549.9 — The Body Ends

Ithreva turned toward the cellar ramp. Doran struck for 179 and she spent the
last Harvest she could afford to refuse it. Wren landed ten maximized, empowered
*scorching ray* attacks at 18 each. Doran's following strike left the body with
38 HP, and Ithreva deliberately released it rather than waiting for the final
cut. The last Named Dead fell with nothing left to attend.

Ithreva's Goldenfields body was destroyed. Her grey bone staff teleported away.
Her phylactery remains intact in Thay as GM-only truth; no character learned its
location here. The threat survives historically, but the phylactery hunt is not
an active SKT hook.

## T550.0 — Goldenfields Released

Goldenfields was released. The Ledger, G1, and G2 were destroyed. The temple of
Chauntea remained sealed and free; Miros Xelbrin remained alive. The cellar ramp
was not converted into a new playable branch.

The complete Sanctum relief deployment recovered all 131 evacuees and both grain
workers alive. No recovery debt, missing-worker search, or local resupply hook
remains.

### State at close

- **Doran:** 361/370 HP; AC 25; Action Surge 0/2; superiority 13/16.
- **Wren:** 220/220 HP; ward 8/75; 34/50 SP; Portal Shear spent for the day.
- **Simulacra:** G1 and G2 destroyed; no copy active.
- **Ithreva:** Goldenfields body destroyed; staff teleported away; phylactery
  intact in Thay as GM-only truth.
- **Field:** Ledger destroyed; Re-entry and Attendance ended; both Named Dead
  permanently down; Goldenfields released.

## T550.1 — Recorded Departure Toward the Sanctum

Doran and Wren depart toward the Sanctum. This is a recorded boundary only. The
later bare Sanctum position and refresh are live state in DM038_L;
their arrival, report, and any scene involving Soliera, Sera, Deashi, or Ember
have not been played or authored here.
