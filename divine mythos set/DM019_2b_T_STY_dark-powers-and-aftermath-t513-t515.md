## DARK POWERS MANIFESTATION (T513)


### T513: Deicide by Fistfight

_Tags: #LOC-BAROVIA-VILLAGE-SQUARE #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-EMBER #CONCEPT-KAIJU-SCALE-DIVINE-VIOLENCE #CONCEPT-DEICIDE-BY-FISTFIGHT_
- Soliera walked outside last. Reached the village square and stopped. She looked up.
- Sera followed her gaze after a moment. Wondering what She sees.
- A crack appeared in the sky. The remaining mist — dissipating since the curse broke — forced itself together, compressing into a sphere, splitting the air around it into a dark, jagged tear.
- Sera: nonplussed, other than a sharpness to her grin that made local mortals nervous.
- Ember glowed brightly, stepped beside Soliera. Soliera didn't react.
- The Dark Powers threw their tantrum. Not a negotiation. Not a probe. Full-scale cosmic rejection of everything that just happened — the entities that fed on this domain for four hundred years had their entire investment portfolio deleted and they were furious.
- A giant hand formed from the black, murky portal in the sky. The arm expanded, the portal with it. Another formed above it. An abomination of tentacles and mist and what looked like gore for the sake of being terrifying attempted to reach toward the village.
- Kaiju scale. Mountain-range sized. The gem-eye — deep arterial red, the size of a city block — pulsed with the concentrated malice of four centuries of harvested tragedy.
- Soliera did nothing. Ember was going to act, but Sera moved.
- Sera burst from her spot — cratering the cobblestones — directly into the chest of the manifestation. She was a speck against it. A pale mote against churning black fog the size of a mountain.
- The Dark Power regarded her with curious indifference. The way something enormous regards something small before deciding whether to notice it.
- An ear-splitting noise. Half the torso — gone. Not damaged. Not wounded. Vaporized. A chunk the size of a city block simply ceasing to exist, cauterized by velocity, dark mist unable to flow back into spaces where Sera had decided fog was no longer welcome.
- Then another noise. And another. She was striking and using airwave pressure to cause devastation at a scale that matched the target.
- From the castle overlook, Strahd, Sergei, and Tatyana watched. Strahd explained: "That is Sera. She is their enforcement."
- Sergei: "She's fighting it? Alone?"
- Strahd: "You misunderstand. She is not fighting. She is cleaning."
- The Dark Power tried to grab at the village. Soli's passive protection ensured no collateral reached anyone — windows cracked but people were fine. Reality quietly adjusted shockwaves around innocents.
- The manifestation screamed at a frequency that made Strahd's dead bones ache. It was dying and it knew it.
- Sera was laughing. Not cruelty. Joy. The kind of clean, honest enjoyment of someone doing what they were made to do at the scale they were made to do it at.
- The gem-eye tracked her. Sera launched herself at it. Impact. The gem shattered. Ruby fragments scattered through the atmosphere as the manifestation began its terminal collapse.
- Sera fell. Not flew — fell. Terminal velocity through clear Barovian sky, trailing ash and fire, grinning the entire way down.
- She cratered through the church steeple. The one with the ugly flag on it.
- From the rubble: "Told you I'd change that flag, Ember. Waiting until we talked to the man was just a time killer."
- She emerged from the church, ash-covered, silk destroyed, barefoot on warm cobblestones, grinning so wide it made her eyes crescent.
- "Check this out." Extended her closed fist toward Ember.
- Ruby fragments tumbled into Ember's palms. Pieces of the gem. Shards of a Dark Power's eye — broken from the setting by the force of Sera's punch, carried back through free fall and atmospheric fire and a church roof because Sera had thought, in the fraction of a second between punching a god and falling from the sky, Ember would like these.
- They were warm. Pulsed faintly — not with malice anymore. Just pretty stones now. Rocks that used to be a prison.
- Ember's glow brightened. Fire-hair flared gold and white. "For me?"
- "For you. Souvenirs. From the scary sky thing."
- "Sera, these are beautiful."
- "Yeah, well. Thought they'd match your whole thing. The gold lines and stuff."
- Ember clutched the fragments to her chest, cradling them against obsidian skin where the gold kintsugi lines caught the rubies' light and multiplied it.
- "Thank you. This is the best souvenir ever."
- "Better than the Thayan skull?"
- "Way better than the Thayan skull."
- Sera ruffled Ember's fire-hair — which should have burned her hand and absolutely didn't.
- The square was full. Every living person in Barovia stood in open air, in sunlight, looking at the sky where the Dark Power had been and finding only blue.
- Ember held up the ruby fragments: "We won!"
- And the village of Barovia, for the first time in four hundred years, cheered.


## AFTERMATH AND ESTABLISHMENT (T514–T515)


### T514: The Village Breathes

_Tags: #LOC-BAROVIA-VILLAGE-SQUARE #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-ISMARK-KOLYANOVICH #CHAR-KOLYAN-INDIROVICH #CHAR-IREENA-KOLYANA #CHAR-DONAVICH #CHAR-STANIMIR #THEME-INFRASTRUCTURE-AS-FREEDOM_
- The cheers faded the way all cheers do when reality reasserts itself. Not abruptly, but in stages. Volume dropped, smiles stayed, eyes changed — from euphoria to something more complicated. Relief. Confusion. The dawning understanding that the thing they'd been afraid of was gone and now they had to figure out what came next.
- Ember had already moved on. She stood near the fountain, turning in slow circles with hands on hips, surveying the square with the critical eye of someone planning a renovation.
- "The portal should go here. Because it's close to the water and the middle of everything, but it's not blocking the road. Niv says portals near water sources are good because the resonance helps with — something. I forget the word. But Niv said it and Niv's always right about building stuff."
- She moved three steps left. "Or here. Because then people coming through would see the fountain first, and that's pretty, and first impressions matter."
- Sera, from the fountain's edge: "Em. Pick one. Niv will move it if it's wrong. He always does."
- "But I want it to be right the first time!"
- "It will be. You're good at this."
- Ember chose the eastern spot — first instinct, best instinct — and began pacing out dimensions.
- Ismark approached Sera: "What happens now? For us. For Barovia."
- Sera, kicking her feet against fountain stone: "In just a little bit, the Sanctum's gonna be here. Once Em gets the portal up, Niv will send people through — organizers, builders, people who are really, really good at figuring out what a place needs. Supplies, food, blankets, medicine. They've done this before. They're good at it."
- Ismark: "Done this before?"
- Sera: "Yeah. We've kind of adopted a few places. Waterdeep, parts of the coast, some stuff overseas. The Sanctum takes care of people. That's the whole point of it."
- Ismark: "Will you stay?"
- Sera: "Nah. We'll visit. But staying isn't really our thing. We go where we're needed, and pretty soon you won't need us. You'll need Niv. He's better at the long-term stuff anyway. We're more of a... catalyst. We show up, break the thing that's broken, set up the pipeline, and then our people run it."
- Kolyan, who had joined: "You speak of this Sanctum as though it were a nation."
- Sera: "It is. Kind of. Million-plus people, infrastructure for days, constructs that don't sleep. No one goes hungry, no one goes cold. It's a good place. And it's about to be your neighbor."
- Ireena, arms still wrapped around herself (habit, not cold): "And the people who come through the portal... they'll actually help? Not just... claim things?"
- Sera: "They'll help. If any of them don't, tell Niv. He'll correct it. He's a dragon. He takes logistics personally."
- Ireena almost smiled. Not quite. But close.
- Donavich emerged from his church — steeple-less now, Sera's crater still smoking. He stood in sunlight touching his own face.
- Stanimir: "You think this was the Morninglord's doing?"
- Donavich considered. Looked at Sera on the fountain. Looked at Soliera standing motionless. Looked at the hole in his church.
- "I think that the Morninglord would be embarrassed by comparison."
- Stanimir wrote that down.


### T515: Barovia Becomes Sanctum Protectorate

_Tags: #LOC-BAROVIA-VILLAGE-SQUARE #LOC-SANCTUM #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-STANIMIR #CONCEPT-SANCTUM-PROTECTORATE #CONCEPT-DOMAIN-RESTRUCTURING_
- NPC reaction montage across the village:
  - The old woman from the crypt (who'd asked Sera if she was an angel) found her great-granddaughter. They sat on a bench outside the tavern holding hands, not talking. The great-granddaughter kept touching the old woman's arm. Confirming she was real.
  - The woodcutter's apprentice — dead for a century, still twenty years old — stood in a field at the village edge. Face tilted up. Eyes closed. Standing in sunlight because that was the only thing that made sense right now.
  - Two children — one living, one resurrected — played near the fountain. The living child teaching a clapping game. The resurrected child kept getting it wrong because the game hadn't existed when she was alive. Both laughing.
  - Sera helped an old man whose hands shook too badly to work his door latch. Flicked it open with one finger. "Thank you." "You're welcome." Simple. Warm. Moved on.
- Sera and Ember on the fountain, processing:
  - Sera: "Did you see how big that gem was? That thing was probably bigger than the whole town. And it was just the eye."
  - Ember, patting her knee: "Yes, Sera. You did awesome. That was a really good adventure."
  - Sera: "Right? Like, I hit it and the whole sky just — fwoom. Gone."
  - Ember: "The fire was very cool. And you brought me souvenirs."
  - Sera: "I brought you souvenirs! From inside the thing! While it was exploding!"
  - Ember, firmly: "You did a really good job and I'm very proud of you and can we please talk about where the portal goes now."
- Portal establishment: Soliera authorized, Ember designed the placement. Sanctum constructs and personnel incoming through Niv-Saiffar's logistics chain.
- Sera addressed the village on next steps: the Sanctum will always be right behind them. They can take care of all the questions the villagers have. Warm blankets. Food. Whatever is needed.
- Ember: "And they're gonna help us plan the party! Because with everybody back alive, we need to go ahead and start the celebrations."
- Sera clapped: "Of course. This is such a great time."
- The party — the biggest party Barovia has ever seen — is the last declared event of the arc.
- Stanimir's journal: full, hand cramping, documenting everything for Lady Voda. The historical record of the day three gods walked into Barovia and, in approximately two days, dissolved a four-century curse, resurrected sixty-plus dead, killed an abstract cosmic entity with bare fists, and established a permanent portal to a divine infrastructure network.
- Barovia is no longer a Domain of Dread. It is a Sanctum protectorate. The mists are gone. The sun is real. The dead are alive. The vampire is still a vampire, but he's crying in a castle talking to the woman he loved four hundred years ago, and nobody is suffering for it anymore.
