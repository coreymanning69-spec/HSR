### M156 — The Landing
#LOC-OAK-CITY-LANDING-ZONE #CHAR-VEYREN #CHAR-CASSIAN #CHAR-EXECUTIVE-F #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-AXEL #CHAR-JEFFERSON
Veyren steps off first. Six foot six, three eighty, tank top, shorts, new beard and mustache, hair grown long
from Afghanistan deployment — he decided to keep it after Ember said she liked it. Helicopter rises on its
struts after his weight leaves it. Cassian follows — five nine, two twenty, suit, the precision of someone who
treats clothing as a variable. Executive F last — five eleven, lean, charcoal suit, the quiet satisfaction of a
projection confirmed. Recognition moves through the group. Sera has already jumped down from wherever she was.
Arms out, reaching up toward Veyren, grinning. He picks her up over his shoulder with one arm. Goes briefly
still when he registers she's not wearing underwear. She grabs his hair — it's almost as long as hers, she
approves completely. He laughs. Ember gets Veyren's other shoulder shortly after, delighted at altitude.
Jefferson and Axel watch the dynamic from the edge — the girls noticeably warmer and more open with Veyren than
anyone, which produces a feeling Jefferson identifies as jealousy and Axel confirms as correct. Cassian explains
quietly — Veyren is her favorite because he didn't die. He was split in half early on for using a nickname for
Soliera. Sera split him longitudinally on the spot. Axel says there's no way. Cassian says yes. Yes. The group
moves toward the fifteen-story glass building at the district's functional center — built in a year,
Sanctum-adjacent construction, clean vertical lines catching ambient light. They stop outside it. The titan at
the gate still kneels. The building is the sentence the world wrote in response to the girls having walked
through the gate two years ago.

### M157 — The Lobby And The Class
#LOC-OAK-CITY-HQ #LOC-TESTING-FACILITY #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-VEYREN #CHAR-CASSIAN #CHAR-MARA #CHAR-SABLE #CHAR-DARIO #CHAR-TERRAN #CONCEPT-TIER-TAXONOMY
Automatic doors delight Sera and Ember. Sentries go rigid at Executive F, Cassian, Veyren — then recalibrate
discovering all three are deferential to a barefoot four-foot-ten woman. Secretaries find adjacent focal points.
The group moves through the lobby with Veyren managing proximity, Cassian and Jefferson running social
interference. Cassian mentions the testing facility — they have equipment for special ability assessment. Sera's
ears perk immediately. Tests. Praise. She'll pass whatever they want. The room laughs genuinely. Cassian
confirms she can have praise anytime. Sera refines — earned acknowledgment and unconditional praise are
different currencies and she wants both but they're different. They enter the testing corridor — walls shifting
from corporate to honest industrial, ceiling climbing incrementally. The testing room is two-fifty meters
across, fifty feet ceiling peak at center, forty feet of composite rebar wall on all sides. Half a city in a
room. Floor gridded with modular anchor lines. Eleven cadets mid-drill — cover-to-cover rolls with projectile
release at the apex, 0.4 second window for trained baseline humans. Mara — compact, late twenties, spatial
processor, doesn't look at targets, immediately forward with Sera, charismatic, the one who meets Sera's
directness with her own — is the standout. Dario — young, careful, marks in the padding when he stops being
careful. Terran — broad-shouldered, asked Veyren about the roll. Instructor Sable — eight months running the
class, two degrees of eye narrowing when she clocks the incoming group. Executive F's face causes visible social
vertigo in at least one cadet. Cassian says they'll observe. The drill resumes. The room's social temperature
shifts from professional distance to genuine warmth because a barefoot woman in almost-red is laughing and the
thing that goes bump in the dark feels very far away. Ember asks what the point of the roll is. Cassian delivers
the precise explanation — knowing isn't timing, timing is when knowledge becomes action. Ember compresses it:
practice for deciding. Cassian says exactly. Veyren tells Terran about expanding the window three feet for his
frame. Room laughs. Cassian removes jacket. The suit was containing something. The room recalibrates. Veyren
puts his arm next to Cassian's. Mine works better, Cassian says mildly. Veyren's grin is enormous.

M158 — "7324"

#LOC-TESTING-FACILITY #CHAR-VEYREN #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-CASSIAN #CHAR-JEFFERSON #CHAR-AXEL #CHAR-EXECUTIVE-F #CHAR-MARA #CHAR-DARIO #CHAR-TERRAN #CONCEPT-TIER-CEILING

Veyren sets Ember down. Requests the thousand-pound punch test. The class has never seen this machine — it's
tier three and four assessment only. The hatch opens, rails extend, machine slides out and locks into floor
anchors. Hydraulic piston, force distribution plate, bullseye cushion, sixteen-point sensor array. Display board
calibrates to zero. Ember cheers him on — go Veyren go Veyren. He dedicates the strike to her. This one's for
you Em. The punch is a complete kinetic chain — rear leg driving up, hip rotating past where most bodies stop,
shoulder continuing into the follow-through, the cat agility feeding the bruiser force, every joule delivered to
the point. The machine rocks back. Anchors slide upward. Cushion opens at the strike point. Display reads 7,324.
He's never gotten a seven thousand. Wants to go again. Contextualizing begins — average civilian punch 150-200,
trained fighter 500, elite combat athlete pushing 900, documented professional ceiling around 1000. Seven times
that. Cassian notes the math flatly. Axel notes the probability of that exact number first try — expected,
moving on. Veyren flexes. Textbook pose, enormous grin, pure joy. Room erupts genuine. Sera claps the real way.
Asks who else wants to go. The room goes quiet with the specific silence of honest self-assessment — nine
hundred doesn't follow seven thousand, not because it's wrong but because the frame has permanently changed.
Veyren reads the room correctly and fills the silence with warmth instead of letting it curdle. The number stays
on the board. The cushion stays open. The machine waits.

M159 — The Strike, The Wall, The Gauntlet

#LOC-TESTING-FACILITY #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-VEYREN #CHAR-CASSIAN #CHAR-EXECUTIVE-F #CHAR-JEFFERSON #CHAR-AXEL #CHAR-MARA #CHAR-DARIO #CHAR-TERRAN #CHAR-SABLE #CONCEPT-VACUUM-WAVE #CONCEPT-BUBBLE-GEOMETRY #CONCEPT-DISPLAY-CHARACTER #CONCEPT-SPIGOT #EVENT-NORTH-WALL

Cassian sends the memo — clear the east wing, full evacuation north of the training room boundary wall,
immediately. Building pings light up. Executive F steps in — don't worry, the evacuation isn't for us. Looks at
Sera. Grins honestly. What we're about to see is over the top. We need to be prepared. Sera is already doing the
thing — one arm behind her back, other at her side, weight rocking side to side, every inch the nervous
schoolgirl and everything but. Asks Veyren how much of it he wants left. He asks how much of what. How much of
the machine. He laughs — whatever you want Sera. Takes two casual steps left. The pressure in the room shifts.
Cadets' necks crinkle. The whole time they were standing between an invisible wall and an ocean of violence and
nobody understood the ocean had a spigot. Sera waves at the room. Well hey y'all, it's just me. One cadet
stutters — how are you putting out this much pressure. She says oops, didn't think she was. Ember walks past her
to the machine. Examines it. Looks back. Sera — how are you going to mess it up if it's already messed up. Sera
says she didn't think it would matter. Ember shakes her head — no, it has to be right. Looks at Soliera.
Soliera, can you fix it? Soliera nods once. The machine restores — cushion closing, anchors resettling, display
cycling to zero — in the time between one moment and the next. No light, no sound, no visible effort. Things
returning to correct. Executive F looks away. His throat moves. A child made of fire asked the mother who is God
to fix something and the mother obliged and the fixing was so simple and domestic and complete that the
simplicity is the overwhelming thing. Sera says yeah that does look way better. Steps up to the machine. Display
reads zero. Nobody sees it happen. One moment she stands there. Same moment the top half of the machine is
through the north wall. The vacuum wave expands — should end the room, should end everyone in it. Soliera's
hands come up. The bubble geometry becomes legible in fracture lines — perfect sphere, everything outside
cracked, everything inside untouched, the ground beneath them unharmed, the boundary drawn with the precision of
something that was always there and just became visible. North wall: surviving lower four to five feet. Above
that: rubble radius. Beyond that: gone, condensed, somewhere in the structural aftermath. Display board still on
its arm inside the bubble. Attempting to display a number. The number has too many digits for the available
columns. Leftmost position shows a character outside the standard numeric set. The system reporting: I cannot
tell you how much. Only: more. Ember cheers, sparks coming off her hair — yes, woo-hoo Sera, I knew you could do
it. Everyone else stands silent. One cadet makes an autonomous biological decision entirely independent of his
training and personal dignity. He stands very straight afterward and stares at the wall. Dario and Terran make a
silent accord — we don't mention it. Alarms trigger. Building security arrives in a stack and stops when they
see the hole. Engineers arrive with tablets and lower them. Medical arrives and finds everyone physically fine.
Administrator arrives with clipboard and looks at Executive F for institutional guidance. He says: training
incident. Axel offers the vocabulary — condensed. Mara confronts Sera directly — you were doing that the whole
time, the whole day, the café, all of it, that's just what there feels like around you. Sera confirms. Mara: and
the reason nobody was screaming. Sera: would've been annoying. Mara: you could have warned someone. Sera: would
that have helped. Mara: no. Probably not. Veyren tells Terran — nothing gets past, that's the operational
reality, we're not guards, we're family. Ember finishes the sentence from the side without looking up from her
sparks. Yeah. That. The display still reads more. The north wing is evacuating. Sera looks at the hole and
suggests they should probably fix that. Looks at Soliera. The administrator takes a step back.


M160 — THE RESTORATION

#LOC-TESTING-FACILITY #CHAR-SOLIERA #CHAR-SERA #CHAR-EMBER #CHAR-CASSIAN #CHAR-TERRAN #CONCEPT-INFRASTRUCTURE-AS-RESOLUTION #CONCEPT-AWE-VS-FEAR #EVENT-NORTH-WALL

Sera looks at the hole, then at Soliera. Soliera lifts one hand — not effort, just a small movement of the fingers — and the north wall returns. Rubble un-falls. The condensed mass uncondenses. The bubble's fracture lines close. No light, no sound. Ember claps, delighted. Sera puffs up — partnership joy, her family fixing the world. Terran's nervous system quits; he goes down, the first mortal to lose consciousness from divine proximity. Cassian quietly resets the room. Fear, hope, and awe arrive at once and don't resolve — the mix just sits there.
