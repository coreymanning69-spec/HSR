## Suzu Titan Doctrine, Scene Log, and Open Threads

Suzu / Plum Titan Layer: Japan's modern protector myth did not replace the shrine
layer; it grew beside it. Kiyuru and the imperial treasures express the old sacred
continuity. Suzu and the plum titan express the post-gate, post-media certainty that
the small can protect the many through a machine made personal. The Japan hub should
read both as native expressions of belief-intent, not as a contradiction between
tradition and technology.
Strategic Doctrine: Suzu's titan functions as national deterrent in the way nuclear
weapons do elsewhere, but culturally inverted: not annihilation, protection. The visible
apparatus around her is immense because the country is not hiding its answer to fear.
The machine is a public monument, a logistics problem, and a prayer with feet.

SECTION 6: SCENE LOG (M180+)
M180 — A Shift of Perspective: Executive J’s briefing room. Gate Leviathan
monitoring stations go dark simultaneously. Kiyuru feels the closure through the planet
— “the world is quieter than it was.” J requests the Oak City feed. Connects
“condensed” notation to prior testing facility reports. Cassian’s margin note: “There is
no ceiling.”

M180.1 — The Call: F and J on the personal channel. F describes the girls — “the
closest thing to an omnipotent being I’ve ever encountered.” Expresses hope for the first
time in thirty years. Cassian confirms Soliera closed Leviathan from the briefing room.
“She did not stand up. She did not leave the room.” J: “I would like to meet them.”
M180.2 — Oak City Arrival: J and Kiyuru arrive by helicopter. Cassian meets them.
Kiyuru feels the city before seeing it — trees wrong (too healthy), air better, sparrow
holds eye contact. “The land is spoken for. Someone decided this place would be well.”
Sera intercepts them on the walk because she felt the artifacts. Kusanagi goes quiet in
her hand. Mirror says hi back. J offered cake and a chair. The grass where Kiyuru bowed
stays greener for years.

M183 / TR-JP Handoff: Kiyuru's invitation is routed through Velin and surfaced
at breakfast after M182. Cassian is already briefed; Veyren has not been told
and stays behind; Letha enters Kiyuru's orbit. M183.3 is the formal bridge into
the regional TR-JP namespace.

TR-JP Closed Arc / Open Suzu Introduction: Japan-side travel record established the
second Japanese T4 lane. Suzu is not a Company executive's personal weapon; she is a
national protector whose apparatus includes ministry schedules, engineered roads,
carrier helicopters, and a standing titan treated as monument and deterrent. Core scene
images to preserve: the model kit in the chest cockpit; either the foot/ankle
airlock with internal elevator or the independent hand pickup, never both in one
entry; the plum titan bowing to the girls without instruction; and
Sera reading Suzu as a mortal in her own weight class. TR-JP-01 through TR-JP-05
are compiled and the exit is locked. The café sketch is hypothetical and unplayed;
Suzu and the Girls have not met. Do not continue the Japan arc unless Corey explicitly
routes back; DM043_0 owns the still-unplayed direct introduction.

DC Incident (Hypothetical/Loose Canon): Girls teleport to Washington DC. National
Mall. Hot dog cart. Gable responds. Punches Sera — hand shatters. Heat vision — no
effect. Vacuum — Sera finishes hot dog. Ember reframes: “Keep people safe by making
sure nobody does anything stupid near her.” Hot dog vendor puts extra mustard on both.
Testing Facility Sparring (Loose Canon): 2v2: Gable + Veyren vs. Cassian + Kiyuru.
Power team vs. precision team. Kiyuru cuts Gable (first blade wound from a person).
Cassian exploits Veyren’s neural sensitivity with a resonant frequency pen. Gable
catches Kusanagi bare-handed — two belief systems in direct contest. Sera enters: four
T4s attack simultaneously. Sera is simply elsewhere. Pickpockets Cassian’s EMP mid-
attack. Kusanagi goes quiet in her hand. “Y’all are fun. Same time tomorrow?”
