## Northern Court Diplomacy: Part 3

VELIN — THE DOMESTIC AXIS:
Velin walks the lower halls on her nightly round. Communal kitchens — stocked, warm, fires banked correctly. The children's dormitories — settled. The intake corridor where Hothgar's latest migration column landed three days ago — four families, now housed, now oriented. She records names. Arranges quarters. Keeps the newly arrived away from corridors with complex politics until they've had a chance to breathe.

She has been doing this since T12. She does not think of it as duty. She thinks of it as the actual job.

MARRIK — ROUTES AND PROJECTIONS:
Marrik has a map spread across a full dining table, ink markers at every active outpost. Luskan. Bryn Shander. Waterdeep. The new Silverymoon circle. He draws a new line south — Greenest will need a supply node once the Dragon Cult business is done. He circles it twice, adds a margin note in the shorthand only he and Velin can read: *Hold until after.*

He has learned, over the years, not to build infrastructure before the Girls finish their work. The aftermath is always larger than the plan. Waste is never resources; it's timing.

HOTHGAR'S HOARD — THE MIGRATION NETWORK:
Somewhere northeast, Hothgar moves with the latest column — six hundred souls from three settlements that chose integration over a winter they were not going to survive. He runs a column the way he always has: scouts forward, food distributed at intervals, the sick in the center where the strong can keep pace around them. He does not think of himself as an army. He thinks of himself as a very large man who knows how to keep people moving in the same direction.

The Sanctum has never asked him to do this. He started at T12 and has not stopped.

KORIN — THE VETERAN'S WATCH:
Korin sits in the training yard off the second spoke, teaching four young constructs the proper angle to set a deadfall snare. The constructs do not eat. They will never need snares. He is teaching them anyway, because the young fighters who train beside the constructs will need to know, and the constructs are better pupils — they retain it perfectly, can demonstrate it without complaint, and do not get bored.

His obsidian knife turns in his fingers. The original. He still expects his leg to ache when the cold comes off the mountain. It never does. He has been waiting for it for thirty years.

SANCTUM STATUS (T140 CONCURRENT):
Population: ~430,000. Trade routes: active across the Sword Coast north, Waterdeep, and now Silverymoon. The Walking God church spreads without direction from the Sanctum — it does not need any. The machine does not miss Soliera. It was built to run without her present.

That is the point.
