# Mergeverse & Primeverse Setting Changes

This file tracks all physical, political, and institutional changes to the Mergeverse and Primeverse caused by the Divine Trio's presence. The **Deposition Principle** applies: the Girls do not extract resources or leverage — they deposit infrastructure, safety, and impossibly casual miracles into the modern world.

Soliera's passive healing field cured systemic slum diseases as she walked. Jointly managed by Sanctum logistics (Velin) and Company liaisons. |

The Badlands arc resolved not through force but through the simple reality that no faction structure survives contact with Soliera's institutional gravity. |
| M-05 | **Neon City (Neo-Moscow) — Border Wall** | **ALTERED** | Sera effortlessly deleted a massive, 40-meter militarized border wall blocking the slums — turning urban oppression into open infrastructure in seconds. The wall's absence is now a landmark. |
| M-06 | **Gate Leviathan (East Sea, 312m depth)** | **ALTERED (CLOSED)** | Former SSS-class world-ending threat. Spontaneously CLOSED because Soliera willed it so — without leaving her chair. The trapped bodies of the failed human insertion teams were deleted from the collapsed, time-frozen environment so they could not remain as rotting matter in an inaccessible hell. Their souls were not erased. |
| M-07 | **The Slum Feast Site** | **ALTERED** | Site of the M126–M150 communal feast arc. Became a recurring gathering point. The neighborhood around it has reorganized around the memory of the event. |

### Undermountain Portal Lock

There is one established Undermountain cross-verse portal: the Level-20 portal
the Girls walked through from the Divineverse into the Mergeverse. The former
Level-23 Primeverse portal references are retired as obsolete continuity. The
portal remains active while Undermountain expands into a near-city and develops
slow trade and conversation with Oak City.

The live possibilities are two distinct portals or one portal with a stale
level reference. Cross-verse architecture remains unresolved until an
authoritative ruling is made.
