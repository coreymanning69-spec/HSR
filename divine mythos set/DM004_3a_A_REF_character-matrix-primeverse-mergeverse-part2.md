- Civilian Life: Real government job. Files expense reports. Reviews containment budgets. Sits in chairs slightly too small for him. Loosens his tie in the stairwell and goes through the roof.
- Key Loose Canon Beats:
    - DC Incident (National Mall): Punched Sera — hand shattered against her cheekbone. Heat vision stopped on contact. Vacuum — Sera finished her hot dog inside the 20ft radius; her voice carried without a medium. Every escalation was his. She was static. She was eating.

Gable caught Kusanagi bare-handed on the second exchange. Two belief systems in direct contest. Palm bled. He threw Kiyuru across the room — first tactical action he had taken in the fight, and it was the right one.
- Reframe (Ember): "You're scared. She's not going to hurt you. Keep people safe by making sure nobody does anything stupid near her." Mission re-architected from "fight the threat" to "manage the perimeter."
- Cultural Effect: Broken-hand story is spreading through the T4 network faster than any official report — recalibration vector for global T4 posture.
- Personality: Boy Scout. Genuine. Identity built on being the protector. Meeting Sera broke his framework because she radiates genocide-scale lethality while giggling about frosting, and his prayer-lineage says protect people from things like that — but the people around her do not need protecting.
