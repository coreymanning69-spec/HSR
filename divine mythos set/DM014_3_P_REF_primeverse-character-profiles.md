This file is the deep character-profile pass for the Primeverse cast around Soliera Prime: Evan (mentor / civilian father), Arden (conceptual guardian), Veris (Project DIVIDE / Countersign), Cassian (Tier 4 operator; Gravemaker Doctrine), Veyren (feral bruiser), and Varn (orc forge master). For the canon overview and Soliera/Arden's narrative core see DM014_1; for the institutional/magic systems they operate in see DM014_2; for the wider Mergeverse interactions see DM014_4 and DM022_0.


Evan
Archetype: Father figure / mentor / quiet backbone
Tier: 1 (Human civilian)
Role in the World:
• Raised Soliera Prime like a daughter after her mysterious birth
• Given the pendant by Soliera's mother before she vanished
• Villager, expert in farming, irrigation, and systems
• One of the first to realize that Arden wasn't just a guardian — but a divine response

Tone & Behavior:
• Warm, reliable, often silent in a strong way
• Wary of the Church and the Company — not vocal, but deeply watchful
• Treats Soliera with casual awe — he knows she's not normal, but never worships her
• Works with his hands, prefers action over lectures

Narrative Function:
• Represents "the world before" — what's worth saving
• Bridges innocence and wisdom
• His existence reminds Soliera that the world can still be good without power
• Church contact kept him away for a time; Soliera later decided she had been
  without her father long enough and brought him to Outpost Seven, where he is
  an ordinary man with effectively unlimited security clearance

Arden
Archetype: Conceptual guardian / divine deterrent / father-as-weapon
Tier: Undefined (operates outside the system)
Role in the World:
• Manifested from Soliera's fear in an SSS-ranked dungeon when she was a child
• Has protected her since infancy, killing anything that poses a true threat
• Morphs depending on context: city mode, travel mode, war mode
• Invisible executioner — he's the reason Soliera is untouched by the world

Tone & Behavior:
• Never speaks aloud
• Only responds to Soliera's needs, not her words
• Moves when needed, disappears when not
• Worshiped by some; feared by most; recognized by the Company and Church as untouchable

Narrative Function:
• He is the unconscious violence of the world — hers, but hidden
• Forces societies to obey without ever declaring himself
• A myth walking inside the plot

Veris (Project DIVIDE / Countersign)
Archetype: Engineered equal / trauma-forged mirror / tragic anti-goddess
Tier: 3.5 (Enhanced human / experimental divine-counterbalance intent; enhanced-human result)
Role in the World:
• The only successful prototype created to "neutralize" Soliera
• Countersign Divide is the recovered institutional synonym for Project DIVIDE
• Grew up under constant surveillance, surgery, and chemical manipulation
• Trained from infancy with reinforced bones, modified tendons, cybernetic organs
• White-blonde, freckled, compact, and physically tuned cooler than baseline human for better muscle usage
• Sent to Outpost Seven under "civilian" disguise — but quickly unraveled by proximity to Soliera

Tone & Behavior:
• Awkward, stiff, overly formal — doesn't understand comfort or spontaneity
• Deeply damaged but noble in intent
• Learns quickly — has instinctual strength, rare self-repair capability
• Treats her regulated body baseline as normal; the horror is administrative, not performative
• Bonding with Soliera through swimming, daily life, and shared vulnerability

Narrative Function:
• She's what the world wanted Soliera to be — controllable, predictable, and wrong about its own premise
• Her healing begins where Soliera's love meets her broken pieces
• She and Soliera will eventually fall in love — restoring agency to both

Cassian
Archetype: World-weary expert / John Wick archetype / peaceful killer
Tier: 4 (Human peak operative)
Role in the World:
• Veteran Company operator, famous across continents
• Trains others, but no one wants to be compared to him
• Seen as the man who can solo dungeons, assassinate monsters, or clean a room of 12 armed men in 6 seconds
• Works at the Outpost now — a bridge between the old guard and Soliera's world

Tone & Behavior:
• Soft-spoken, dry humor
• Precise, elegant killer — every move intentional
• His bosses call this the Gravemaker Doctrine: an average-looking man in plain clothes planning every angle in the room before anyone knows a fight exists
• Sees Veris as a younger version of himself — protective toward her
• Treats Soliera not as a weapon, but as someone to be grateful for

Narrative Function:
• He anchors power into mortal consequence
• Shows what strength without divinity looks like
• Might become Veris's protector in his own way

Veyren
Archetype: Feral strength / comic bruiser / animalistic friend
Tier: 4 (Company tier-four melee unit)
Role in the World:
• Giant brawler type — half-feral, all instinct
• Speaks plainly, reacts physically
• Has become attached to Soliera and her peace
• Sparred with Veris; respected her strength; flirts via combat

Tone & Behavior:
• Sniffs out strength with his fists
• Respectful of Arden, Cassian, and Soliera in different ways
• Acts as friendly chaos to balance Veris's stiffness
• Doesn't scheme — he shows up and does shit

Narrative Function:
• Counterbalance to the thinkers
• Shows joy in strength — fights are fun, not war
• Could become one of Veris's earliest friends

Varn
Archetype: Orc builder / divine-forged follower / silent observer
Tier: 3.8–4
Role in the World:
• Received visions of Arden through his Forge God
• Came to the Outpost expecting to meet a god—met Soliera instead
• Confused, reverent, awed, but not fanatical
• Spars with Veyren, interacts with Veris with curiosity

Tone & Behavior:
• Measured, observant, old-soul energy
• Powerful blacksmith and brawler
• Watches Soliera closely—he doesn't pray, he waits
• May build her tools, weapons, or forge spaces sacred to her

Narrative Function:
• Introduces themes of divine misinterpretation
• Reinforces the "blacksmith" myth around Soliera and Arden
• Might become her item-forger, if she asks

The Many (Outpost Seven Librarian)
Archetype: Folklore entity / deep observer / unknown origin
Tier: Unclassifiable (not a Company or Church designation — simply not applicable)
Role in the World:
• Operates at Outpost Seven under cover as the human librarian
• The only non-human presence at the Outpost in the sense that matters — everyone else is what they claim to be
• Origin is folklore or myth — something that emerged from collective belief or old-world story in a way that resists clean classification; nature deliberately vague and not yet fully defined even at the narrative level
• Came specifically because of Soliera Prime — her existence is the reason The Many is here

Tone & Behavior:
• Presents as unremarkably human; the cover holds because The Many is good at it and nobody is looking
• Deeply, quietly fascinated by Soliera — not predatory or agenda-driven, but with the focused attention of something that recognizes significance when it encounters it
• Observes without interfering; witnesses without reporting to any institutional body
• Was present when Soliera reshaped a metaphysical vow and erased the concept behind it — Intent Magic development milestone

Narrative Function:
• Represents the aspect of the world that watches Soliera from outside all institutional frameworks
• Not Company, not Church, not Gate-entity — something older and more ambient
• What The Many is building toward, or what they want from observing her, is an open thread
• Its ontology remains folklore-vague by design. The Divineverse Girls, Niv,
  and Velin register as peer-class beings; meeting any of them will break its
  practiced observer composure

Note: Cross-reference DM029_03 for The Many's Divineverse context. The Primeverse librarian persona is a separate operational layer.

Gold (Summoned Companion)
Archetype: First companion / emotional anchor / the choice that wasn't need
Tier: N/A — not a combat classification; not a guardian construct
Role in the World:
• Summoned by Soliera Prime almost immediately after completing her first formal magical education at Outpost Seven (all six schools covered at basic level)
• The teacher present was visibly impressed — the summoning was not expected, not assigned, and happened before any assessment was complete
• Dog-like in form and emotional register: protective, warm, present in the way constructs are not
• Has a flight mode
• Not a guardian — Arden holds that function absolutely. Gold is company. Gold is what Soliera wanted, distinct from what she needed.

Tone & Behavior:
• Responds to Soliera's emotional state more than explicit commands
• Present at Outpost Seven; no institutional classification or threat assessment filed
• Arden has not reacted to Gold's presence in any documented way

Narrative Function:
• The gap between Arden (protection, necessity) and Gold (companionship, choice) is the entire point of Gold's existence in the narrative
• Gold is the first thing Soliera Prime manifested that she reached for by want rather than need
• Gold remains around the Outpost school and has become its unofficial mascot

Father Arren (Miracle Magic Teacher)
Archetype: True believer / quiet witness / faith without agenda
Tier: 2–3 (Church-trained; access to minor miracle work)
Role in the World:
• One of the teaching staff at Outpost Seven's expanded educational wing
• Teaches Miracle Magic — the Church's structured access to divine-adjacent spellwork
• Most of the Outpost's teachers are human; a few are not. Arren is human, ordained, unremarkable by institutional measure.

Tone & Behavior:
• His read of Soliera Prime is what distinguishes him: not the Church's official posture of cautious surveillance, but genuine and joyful devotion — he looked at what was in front of him and decided it was a sign
• Teaches without agenda, without the sideways attention of someone building a report
• Does not discuss his private position openly; he simply teaches
• Found a small carved wooden sunflower at the chapel threshold one morning — placed by Evan, witnessed by a child. Arren held it during evening prayer and reached no institutional framework for it. His private conclusion: "That man does not ask for miracles. He becomes them, quietly." He kept the carving.

Narrative Function:
• Represents institutional faith that survives contact with the real thing without collapsing or weaponizing
• His warmth toward Soliera is one of the reasons she allows him to teach her — he doesn't overwhelm her with reverence
• Arc thread: his position within the Church's formal monitoring structure versus his actual feelings about it
