### RULE-ENCLOSED-SPACE

The Cleaver penalty applies only in **human-scale construction and tight ground**:
rooms, halls, alleys, ships, longhouses, tents, corridors, stairwells, and
Svardborg interiors. It does **not** apply in giant architecture, forge halls,
caverns, or open ground. Test: if Doran can stand and spin with the blade out
without striking anything, the space is open.

- **−2 to hit** with the Cleaver;
- reach drops from 10 ft to 5 ft;
- every struck wall, fixture, or structural element along the adjudicated swing
  arc takes the listed Cleaver damage — **the room pays**; and
- daggers are unaffected and remain the clean indoor option.

This is also the cost of Doran's Large size. Do not add a second squeezing tax.

### RULE-THERMAL-EXEMPTION

Ember removed the heat-exhaustion condition at its root after Doran overheated
and collapsed in Artus Cimber's camp. Dragonbait was present with Ember for the
incident. The completed T532 body work closes the whole thermal axis, not only
environmental exposure; this is Ember's direct intervention, not Wren's active
channeling of Ember and Soliera.

- Extreme heat, extreme cold, ambient forge air, glacier air, hypothermia, and
  thermal exhaustion do not affect him.
- Heat, fire, cold, and freezing effects deal no damage and cannot numb him.
  Ordinary carried material may still burn away. Ice may freeze or restrain his
  body physically, but the cold itself does not hurt him.
- Volcanic or forge heat cannot harm him, but Doran still requires oxygen. Without
  the helm, toxic air, particulate, absent oxygen, and suffocation remain live
  hazards. With Soliera's helm worn, it supplies a breathable atmosphere in
  environments such as underwater or volcanic conditions; this is helmet support,
  not bodily oxygen independence.

### RULE-LARGE-SIZE

Doran is **Large**, occupies and controls a 10-foot square, and can legally
grapple or shove Huge creatures. Effects limited to Medium-or-smaller targets
do not affect him. His carrying ceiling is doubled.

Trip Attack and Pushing Attack scale with him: if Doran can legally grapple a
creature, he can legally trip or push it. Their printed Large-or-smaller cap is
superseded for him.

### RULE-DIVINE-PLATE

- **Provenance:** this is mirror-white divine plate personally remade by Soliera at T537.0, not mithral half-plate.
- **Stage one:** transmit one-third piercing; transmit one-half slashing as bludgeoning; transmit all bludgeoning.
- **Stage two:** giant-strength muscle density absorbs one quarter of all transmitted damage, including bludgeoning.
- **Net physical damage:** one-quarter piercing, three-eighths slashing, three-quarters bludgeoning.
- Armor, Cleaver, shield, and artifact daggers are undamageable. Doran is not.
- This is survivability, not immunity: the T523 boulder cracked his rib through
  intact plate.

### RULE-PLANTED — hard anchor

Set the boot ridges into a surface that can accept them and receive the hit:

- **no AC change** — the former +2 is retired under the AC LOCK; he stands at 25
  planted or mobile;
- speed 0;
- displacement-based forced movement and displacement-based prone fail;
- prone effects that do not depend on displacement still resolve normally;
- PLANTED cannot be entered on a surface that cannot accept the ridges.

PLANTED is now purely an anchoring decision rather than a defensive one. Its
value is that nothing moves him: no shove, no gust, no telekinetic yank, no
knockdown that works by displacement. Trading all movement for that immunity is
the real cost, and it is a sharper choice without an AC sweetener attached.

### RULE-DEPLOYABLE-KITE

Dropping the forearm kite is a quick-release deployment. It stands on its own
as an undamageable 4-foot-high by 3-foot-wide barrier: three-quarters cover
(+5 AC and DEX saves) for one crouching Medium creature, or half cover (+2 AC
and DEX saves) for one standing Medium creature. Dropping it for Wren and
walking forward is Doran's characteristic protection style; no specific scene
is prewritten.

Deployment uses Doran's ordinary object interaction. While the kite is standing
elsewhere, its +4 total shield contribution leaves Doran at **AC 21** in every
configuration. Recovering and rebuckling it requires proximity and an ordinary
object interaction.

This is the only surviving AC variance on the sheet, and it is deliberate. Every
upward rider was retired at level 20; the one remaining movement is downward,
and it fires when he gives the kite to Wren. Protecting her costs him four
points of armor class against a tier-4 threat band that already hits AC 25
roughly half the time. Keep that price visible at the table.

### RULE-STEALTH

The divine plate imposes **no sound-derived Stealth penalty**: it does not ring,
scrape, or rasp. The mirror-white surface emits no light of its own, so an unlit
tunnel does not make Doran a beacon or give away his position. When incident
light strikes the plate, it returns that light at very high efficiency and may
be conspicuous. He still may hide using darkness, cover, concealment, terrain,
or a broken sightline as the scene permits.

The white kit is cumbersome. It imposes disadvantage on Acrobatics and checks
that depend on bodily flexibility. Mobile raises Doran's unarmored speed to 40
feet; the white kit cancels that extra 10 feet, leaving him at speed 30 while worn.

### RULE-NO-ATTUNEMENT

Attunement does not apply to Doran's gear. It was divinely made for him; nothing
he carries occupies a slot, and none of it can be attuned away by anyone else.

### RULE-REGENERATION-AND-ZERO

The Sanctum-pattern Ring of Regeneration restores **4d4 HP as a bonus action**,
with no daily limit or charge pool. Doran activates it one-handed by pressing an
adjacent finger to the ring or touching it with his other hand.

The partial Blessing of Wound Closure maximizes all healing he receives: the
ring therefore restores **16 HP**, Second Wind restores **30 HP**, and Wren's
healing spells land at maximum on him. The self-stabilization portion is not
granted.

Second Wind's maximized value is `1d10 + Fighter level`, so it reads 30 at
Fighter 20. The former 24 was stale drift carried from Fighter 14 and was
corrected during the level-20 pass.

The ring requires at least 1 HP and does nothing at 0 HP because an unconscious
Doran cannot take the bonus action. Death saves are real and unmodified.

### RULE-VISION

Soliera's helm grants Doran the ability to **see normally through magical and
nonmagical darkness to 120 feet**.

- The grant is always on: no activation, action, concentration, slot, or
  attunement.
- It answers darkness only. It is **not truesight** and does not pierce
  invisibility, illusions, shapechanging, transmutation disguises, or the
  Ethereal Plane.
- Beyond 120 feet, ordinary darkness rules resume.
- Wren may now use *darkness* as a screen Doran can fight inside. Drow and other
  creatures without a separate means of seeing through magical darkness remain
  unable to see through their own *darkness*.
- The grant does not answer *blindness, fog cloud, hallucinatory terrain,* or
  any obstruction that is not darkness.

### RULE-GLARE

***Daylight* is not sunlight.** It does not trigger Sunlight Sensitivity or any
other sunlight-keyed effect. It does dispel overlapping darkness spells of 3rd
level or lower; drow *darkness* is 2nd level.

The glare is an explicit **house ruling** on Doran's plate, not a property that
the *daylight* spell has by itself. When Wren casts *daylight* on Doran or his
kit, the mirror-white plate returns the spell's 60-foot bright radius from a
moving seven-foot silhouette for one hour.

A creature that attacks Doran from within the bright radius, or starts its turn
within the bright radius with line of sight to him, makes a **DC 22 Constitution
save**. Resolve an attack-triggered save before the attack.

| Result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Save | No effect. |
| Fail | Disadvantage on attack rolls against Doran until the start of the creature's next turn. |
| Fail by 5 or more | Blinded until the start of the creature's next turn. |

Sunlight-sensitive creatures, including drow, have disadvantage on this save
because of the concentrated reflected glare. This does **not** make *daylight*
sunlight. Doran, Wren, and any creature not looking at Doran are unaffected.

The spell costs one 3rd-level slot, lasts one hour, and requires no
concentration. The helm and *daylight* therefore stack with Wren's ward and the
Ring of Concentration load. *Dispel magic* removes the spell, *counterspell*
denies the cast, and a creature beyond 60 feet is outside the glare.

### STANDING PAIR PRACTICE

Wren can cover paralysis and restraint with the Liberation-domain *freedom of
movement*: no concentration, one hour, and effective against nonmagical grapples
and restraints. She casts it before engagements as standing practice **if she
remembers**. This solution is already on the pair's sheet and does not justify
another item.

## MANEUVERS — COMPLETE CATALOGUE, 16d12, DC 22

Korin taught Doran the complete curriculum directly. Doran may spend his pool
on any Battle Master maneuver published in the 2014 PHB or Tasha's; the ordinary
maneuver-known limit does not apply.

|---|---|
| **Ambush** | add d12 to a DEX (Stealth) check or initiative roll |
| **Bait and Switch** | swap with a willing adjacent creature while moving; add d12 AC to one participant until the start of Doran's next turn |
| **Brace** | reaction melee attack when a creature enters reach; add d12 damage |
| **Commander's Strike** | forgo one attack and use the bonus action so an ally can make a reaction weapon attack with +d12 damage |
| **Commanding Presence** | add d12 to Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion |
| **Disarming Attack** | add d12 damage; STR save DC 22 or drop one held object |
| **Distracting Strike** | add d12 damage; next attack by another attacker has advantage before Doran's next turn |
| **Evasive Footwork** | add d12 to AC while moving, lasting until Doran stops |
| **Feinting Attack** | bonus action for advantage on the next attack against an adjacent target this turn; add d12 damage on hit |
| **Goading Attack** | add d12 damage; WIS save DC 22 or disadvantage attacking anyone else |
| **Grappling Strike** | after a hit, bonus-action grapple and add d12 to the Athletics check |
| **Lunging Attack** | add 5 ft. to a melee attack's reach; add d12 damage on hit |
| **Maneuvering Attack** | add d12 damage; an ally uses its reaction to move half speed without provoking from the target |
| **Menacing Attack** | add d12 damage; WIS save DC 22 or frightened until the end of Doran's next turn |

Quick Toss, Maneuvering Attack, Goading Attack, and Bait and Switch remain the
fastest expression of the strategist package, but they are defaults rather than
the edges of his vocabulary. Doran decides where allies stand, where enemies
stand, and who enemies are looking at.

**Dual Wielder:** the feat's +1 AC is retired under the AC LOCK. Doran's other

## PHYSICALITY

**6'4" barefoot → 6'8" in the lift boots → 7'0" in full helm/spacer.**
240 lb, peak-human density.

| Carried | Weight |
|---|---|
| Giant Cleaver | 208 lb |
| Divine plate | ~40 lb |
| Shield, daggers, kit | ~15 lb |
| **Total** | **~263 lb** |

At STR 27 his house carrying capacity is 675 lb; Large size doubles it to
**1,350 lb**. He carries roughly 263 lb. Encumbrance is not a live constraint,
and any planned beat that depends on a lower carry ceiling is void.

STR 28 remains deliberately banked as a future growth beat. Do not pre-spend it
in derived numbers, carrying capacity, attack bonus, or damage.

The physical read remains a man functioning normally beneath a load that would
pin another mortal to the floor. Mobile makes him speed 40 without the armor;
the white kit brings him back to 30 and applies its agility cost.

## LOCKED BUILD, SATISFIED HAND GATE, AND MODE SPLIT

1. **Fighting Style:** Two-Weapon Fighting.
2. **Feats through Fighter 20:** Resilient (WIS), Skill Expert (CON / Insight),
   Alert, Dual Wielder, Mobile, Sentinel, and **Resilient (INT)** at level 16.
3. **Level 19 uplift instead of a feat:** the eighth slot was spent as an
   across-the-board **+2 to all six ability scores** rather than a feat or a
   conventional ASI. This is the divine-uplift package, not a purchased option,
   and it is why every derived number on the sheet moved at once.
4. **Giant Cleaver single-hand gate:** satisfied at T532. Sera's week of
   Waterdeep training raised Doran to STR 25 and one-handed mastery; the uplift
   later carried him to STR 27.
5. **Configuration competence gate:** the former ordinary drop/stow cost is
   retired. Only a Grand Cleave turn excludes dagger access and the
   dagger-dependent Quick Toss/Deft Answer lanes.
6. **Final refinement:** the fixed-damage, capacity, maim, skid, and
   non-discriminating arc rules are part of the existing T532 Waterdeep week;
   no later acquisition beat is added.

The maneuver catalogue, superiority pool, style, feats, live one-/two-handed Cleaver
profile, dagger arithmetic, initiative basis, plate rules, and full-kit height
are locked.

The legendary *Potion of Giant Size* is the in-module temporary version: Huge,
STR 25, doubled HP maximum, +5 melee reach, and triple weapon dice. It may appear
as loot without permanently rewriting Doran.
