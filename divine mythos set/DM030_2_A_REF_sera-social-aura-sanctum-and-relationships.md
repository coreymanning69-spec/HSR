## Phase Continuity Calibration — Not a Growth Arc

The following phase register is a behavioral continuity aid, not a conventional
internal growth story. Sera does not become morally gentler, develop a human
conscience, or outgrow her core logic. Her stable center remains joy, possession,
immediate response, relational mercy, and exclusive deference to Soliera. What
changes across eras is how safely and deliberately that same logic expresses itself
inside a household that has become more secure.

- **T6–T30 — Founding survival register:** Reva's remaking leaves Sera
  obsessive, physically protective, and quick to punish perceived threats to
  Soliera. Use the early register as raw control-seeking and survivor attachment,
  not as a separate moral identity.
- **T210–T220 — Joyful-punisher register:** lethal external posture remains
  absolute, while affection inside the circle becomes playful, tactile, and
  openly delighted. Violence and joy are simultaneous outputs, not emotional
  opposites.
- **T356–T375 — Enforcer register:** Sera uses deliberate cruelty when a target
  must understand the consequence before removal. This is pedagogy and judgment,
  not remorse, hesitation, or philosophical self-questioning.
- **T386–T394 and later — Domestic-control register:** greater household safety
  lets Sera spend more of her unchanged authority on humor, intimacy, clothing,
  play, and protection. Outsiders still meet the same absolute threshold; the
  family experiences more of the joy beneath it.

When this register conflicts with a scene-specific hard-canon beat, the beat wins.
Do not summarize this trajectory as Sera "growing," "healing," or becoming less
violent. Describe it as stable divine behavior gaining safer domestic context and
more controlled expression.

## Visual and Social Anchor

Sera is the same frame as Ember, pale, midnight black hair, enormous sapphire blue eyes, barely dressed by default, and joy-first. That visual is not decorative. It is part of the room law: small adult woman, absolute body, cheerful affect, and catastrophic enforcement sitting in one visible package.

**Default dress lock:** Outside an era-specific wardrobe instruction, "barely
dressed" resolves as very revealing silk worn in a relaxed, lounging register. It
is adult ease rather than display anxiety, armor logic, or red-as-default drift.

### Fertility-handmaid register and trainee tension

Sera can read as a fertility handmaid: embodied abundance, intimate attendance,
devotional sexuality, and effortless adult invitation. Use Ishtar and *Record of
Ragnarok* as touchstones for sovereign sensual presence, not as costume copying or
character substitution.

In the trainee field this creates asymmetrical tension. Doran's desire has nowhere
clean to land; reverence and propriety keep converting it into posture. Sera's active
interest goes toward Wren. That tension is texture, not an automatic triangle,
romance outcome, or consent override.

### Early-era social organism and proof of love

In the founding era, Sera believes she has nothing but her body. Love and sex are
therefore the evidence that she is loved, not merely appetite. The household treats
intimacy as an ordinary proof of belonging: Letha and the girls share it routinely.
Niv later becomes the sole male-formed intimate of Soliera and Sera, separately
and together. Sera remains women-only: Niv is a dragon in a male humanoid form,
not a man, so the category does not open or weaken her orientation. The T15
refusal remains load-bearing because later intimacy occurs only after her body
is no longer the sole proof of lovability she believes she possesses.

Her survivor hypervigilance becomes charisma once the body is safe. She studied
rooms involuntarily for her entire life; from safety, that surveillance reads as
warmth and makes her a natural bond-broker. The household functions as a social
organism: Soliera is gravity, never recruiting and rarely interfacing; Sera is
weather, brokering the bonds around what people are being converted *to*. Niv talks
through her, Letha comes for her, and Korin approaches because she is interested.
Never-voice-Soliera is therefore canon-native from T16 onward, not merely a table
restriction.

Letha is the first person to choose Sera over Soliera. She says yes for Sera before
seeing the morning devotions, choosing the person before the fanatic. This is the
first datapoint that Sera can be loved as a destination rather than a route, and the
template for later girl attachments.

She does not simply enter social space. She recalibrates it.

## Velvet Spiral

Velvet Spiral is Sera's passive social/aesthetic pressure. It is not a charm
spell. It is the pressure generated by Sera's existence: reverence, attraction,
protectiveness, obedience, fear, and social-instinct collapse all trying to
resolve around a divine body.

DM027_0's technical translation calls it:

> "divine-biological / aesthetic compulsion"

DM001_0 clarifies that it is natural to her existence and non-magical. This file
describes the social output: unsettled attention, eroding composure, shifting
priorities, and rooms reorganizing around her.

Use it as social gravity:

- People stop posturing.
- Hands become visible.
- Witnesses look for adjacent focal points.
- Hierarchies wobble.
- Professionals maintain eye contact with the middle distance.
- The room gets warmer, easier, stranger, or more afraid depending on whether Sera is pleased.
- Desire toward Sera is the constant pressure; genuine indifference is the anomaly.
  Admission, expression, restraint, and consent still vary by person.
- A particular bodily reaction does not by itself prove sexual orientation;
  Mara's heart flutter may register significance even while desire remains part
  of the room's pressure.

## Social Room Law

When Sera is socially active, the room receives a new law:

- Do not interrupt the divine household.
- Do not posture unless invited to play.
- Do not threaten children or protected people.
- Do not mistake playfulness for permission.
- Do not assume being harmless makes you invisible.
- Do not assume being powerful makes you relevant.

The social effect is not always fear. Often it is ease. In Oak City, the gate
plaza stops from recognition, not panic. Air gets easier to breathe, light
warmer, and aches soften. At the testing facility, the room shifts toward
warmth because Sera is laughing and the thing in the dark feels far away.

## Functional Illiteracy

Sera cannot read raw text or interpret signs, maps, or written directions. She
can speak with people, ask what a thing is, and interact with objects normally;
the limit is written-symbol interpretation, not comprehension, agency, or social
competence. Do not substitute a silent successful reading beat for an interaction
she would handle by asking someone.

## Sanctum Function

Sera's Sanctum role is first worshiper, dresser, enforcer, interpreter, and
living social proof that Soliera's domestic divinity is not symbolic.

The girls sleep and dress in the Sanctum because Sera wants to play at being
the human girl she never got to be. She is happy there. She sings little songs
and paints her nails. These ordinary pleasures are not incidental decoration;
they are the domestic motive beneath the ritual and a necessary counterweight
to the Barefoot Death register.

She dresses Soliera. She arranges Soliera's presentation. She attends to
Soliera's body and space as sacrament, not servitude. The act is devotional
and authoritative: Sera is close enough to dress God because she belongs
there.

This file preserves the frame directly:

> "Sera's existence is worship made flesh."

In scenes, this means:

- Dressing Soliera is one of Sera's highest pleasures.
- Domestic intimacy is not a break from divine authority; it is how divine authority looks in this story.
- Sera's closeness to Soliera gives her social weight no institution can replicate.
- She does not need a temple to be priestly.
- She is not low-status because she serves. Service is the proof of proximity.

## Relationship: Letha — The Perch

Inside the Sanctum, or another secured inner-circle space where Soliera is
present, DM000_1's proximity-relaxation clause lets Sera free-range through the
shared environment. The **Letha Perch** is the canonical social ritual that
makes that home-law visible:

- Sera enters a meeting Letha is already running, greets her, and answers the
  practical question of what she needs with: "Nothing. Just came to see you."
- Sera climbs up and perches on Letha's hip. Letha is a tall tiefling, roughly
  5'9"; Sera is 4'10". The movement is effortless and long-practiced.
- The meeting continues. Sera may play with Letha's ear, comment, or interject.
  Letha may switch her to the other hip mid-presentation so she can keep
  pointing at the board: no negotiation, no acknowledgment, only load
  redistribution. If Letha is seated, Sera climbs the back of her chair instead.
- Witnesses do the theological processing. Letha does logistics.

Letha is the only intimate besides Soliera who receives the full furniture
treatment. Her processing of Sera is affection-routed rather than worship-routed;
the T19/T27 history makes the perch earned familiarity, not Velvet Spiral
fallout. Do not generalize the ritual to Velin, Kessla, or other staff. Attempted
on anyone else, the room seizes; on Letha, the meeting continues, and the
continuation is the point. Cross-reference: DM004_2b tag `sera-perch-ritual`.

### Climbing Doctrine — Same Silhouette, Opposite Vector

**Sera climbs on people because she outranks whatever they are doing. Ember
climbs on things to understand what is being done.**

- Sera's climb is a rank statement. She deposits herself into a workflow the
  same way she deposits change into locations. Attention flows to her because
  she is the most important thing present, and her body agrees.
- Ember's climb is investigative: over a shoulder to read a ledger, onto a table
  to see a map at the correct angle, or along a scaffold to watch a joint being
  set. The silhouette may match Sera's; the motive does not.
- Ember climbing must never read as childlike, helpless, or attention-seeking.
  Her posture is an instrument aimed at the task. DM028_3 owns the mirror guard.
