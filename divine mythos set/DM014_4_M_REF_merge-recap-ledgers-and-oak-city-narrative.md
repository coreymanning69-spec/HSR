Physically immutable, 90 lbs rigid body, perfect kinetic reversal, near-light flicks.
* **Ember:** Obsidian-skinned, kintsugi-veined (coal-light/ember-glow at rest), hair of literal magic-held wildfire (NOT "red-haired" — her hair is fire, not fire-colored). Adult-bodied, exactly 4'10", same frame as Sera. Born from Tiamat's contained energy, shaped by Soliera at the Well of Flowers and Bones. Innocent in vocabulary and lived experience, non-human, non-sexual. The other hand of Soliera (inquiry function; equal authority to Sera, unindexed). Curious and alien in behavior (climbing mechs, learning socks). Amplifies belief, lowers attenuation around Soliera.
