# Divineverse Setting Changes — Faerûn, Underdark & Extraplanar Atlas

This file tracks the physical, political, and spiritual geography of Faerûn, the planes, and the Underdark following the interventions of the Divine Trio. All changes are governed by the **Deposition Principle**: the Trio does not extract loot or resources — they deposit infrastructure, safety, and terrifyingly casual miracles.

## GPT PROJECT STANDALONE ROLE

In compact or file-capped load, DM031_0 carries the Divineverse location memory that
would otherwise be scattered across DM005_0-DM019_2, DM025_0, and DM026_0. Use it to
answer what changed physically, politically, spiritually, and logistically
after the Girls passed through a place.

For itinerary play, pair DM031_0 with DM023_0 and the relevant DM024 atlas. For
exact scene prose, route to the owning beat file; for ordinary worldstate,
DM031_0 is authoritative. Read the regional entry as a location-state snapshot:
completed changes are established, while a stated future direction or recovery
gap is not a completed intervention. The active domain ledger owns current
readiness and the played edge; this atlas does not advance either.


## Recognition and Named-Figure Removal Law

#RULE-SANCTUM-FAME-CONSTANT #RULE-NAMED-FIGURE-REMOVAL-CONSEQUENCE

Throughout the Divineverse and its connected extraplanar routes, the Sanctum
and the divine trio are household names. Thay is a Divine Client State ruled by
Tirren and Daviya after the slave revolt, liberation, and destruction of the
Red Wizard order; change at that scale has already traveled through merchants,
temples, courts, roads, and rumor networks.

- Never roll whether an NPC has heard of the Sanctum or the trio. Roll only for
  version, accuracy, source, and emotional interpretation.
- Sword Coast populations know the Well of Dragons, gods walking, and the
  Waterdeep relationship. Nobles, Harpers, Zhents, guilds, and temples may know
  specific operations and staff. Isolated, monstrous, and Underdark populations
  still know a rumor, warning, or local version.
- Doran and Wren are the unknown quantity. Their institution and equipment are
  famous; their individual reputations develop through play.
- Recognition is not deference. It may produce contempt, testing, refusal,
  opportunism, or tired confirmation of a known hazard.
- This law does not automatically transfer awareness into independent
  Primeverse or Mergeverse populations.

When the trio kills, maims, deposes, or otherwise removes a named figure with
standing, the removal leaves a shaped absence. Carry five questions:

1. Who knew the figure, depended on them, or was made safer by them?
2. Who benefits, and how does that change behavior?
3. What version of the removal circulates rather than merely what happened?
4. Who wants revenge, and who can afford to act?
5. Which owning domain ledger records the beat, actor, confirmed witnesses,
   current absence, and unresolved reactions?

Prep or simulation evidence never becomes a campaign-wide casualty register.


## I. THE NORTH & SAVAGE FRONTIER
*The Core & Home Base*

### ALTERED LOCATIONS

* **The Sanctum (Spine of the World) — Divine Capital:** A massive, wheel-shaped divine city hollowed into the Spine of the World, with a recorded population of approximately 2.1 million souls. Run by the CR 37 Firemind Dragon Niv-Saiffar, it features multiple layers of wheel-spoke style circles connecting to grand, well-lit caverns. It represents absolute safety. Crime does not exist. Orphans are protected, pregnancy is revered, and infinite divine constructs ensure perfect logistics. Features a permanent, open portal to Barovia in its Antechamber.

* **Icewind Dale / Ten-Towns:** The eternal winter was shattered. Deashi systematically cleansed Auril's shrines, moderating the weather and securing the region's long-term survival. The area is now a thriving Sanctum protectorate with a heavily fortified embassy in Bryn Shander, seamlessly connecting all ten towns through divine logistics and teleportation nodes.

* **The Sea of Ice:** Permanently scarred by divine transit. A 20-mile-wide stretch of solid pack ice was obliterated and displaced when Sera jumped and shattered it to clear a perfect cruising lane for the Ark. It remains a permanently open, radiant sea lane — a landmark channel on charts made after the crossing.

* **Svardborg / Frost Giants:** Allied vassal-tenants under the Sanctum banner after the T529 parley. Storvald retains the jarlship; guest-right is honored; food, training, protection, and construct labor are extended. At the T529 settlement Drufi served as liaison and Niv was stationed at Svardborg to finalize terms; consult the active ledger for later assignments. The old Everlasting Ice project is obsolete. Tags: #giant-treaty #no-battle

* **Ironslag / Fire Giants:** Allied Sanctum protectorate after the bloodless T535 resolution, with the ore-room salamander as the site's only casualty. Duke Zalto and fifty fire giants accepted written binding governance; four hundred captives are free. Velin authors the binding protectorate settlement and Niv executes it. Brimskarda and Cinderhild retain private allied family life. Ember relit the 140-year-cold adamantine forge by invitation and without Maegera. Soliera enlarged the assembly hall from a 50-foot to a 120-foot ceiling without seams or lost chalk marks, confirming that Ostorian construction was divine-tier fiat rather than a lost mortal technique. Four missing fragments became present, and the completed 110-foot Vonindod is sentient, cooperative, silent, unnamed, and retains self-determined personhood and purpose. Eclavdra later removed Maegera and the intact flask; neither is a party objective. Tags: #giant-treaty #no-battle #ostorian-fiat #vonindod

* **Giant-category direction after T535:** The giants are the category being preserved. Svardborg and Ironslag prove the governing pattern: giant leadership is morally sorted, reorganized, allied, or placed under binding terms rather than exterminated as a people. At the T535 settlement, the fire giants belonged to the Sanctum in all but the pending paperwork; later administrative status belongs in the active ledger. The earlier *Against the Giants* treatment in DM026_0 is precedent; the missing conversation-specific reasoning from *Princes of the Apocalypse* remains an open recovery gap and must not be reconstructed by inference.

* **Black Network / Kraken Society lethal lane:** Corey has declared these two organizations—not giant-kind—the intended elimination lane for the coming arcs. This is future direction, not a claim that either organization has already been destroyed. Jasper, Hendram Solt, and Nilraun route into the Black Network side; Lyn Armaal's telescope/archive trail routes toward the Kraken Society.

* **Ring of Winter:** Reassigned by Ember from world-freezing purpose to guardian and protector of the Spine of the World and Sanctum range. It was not recovered by Storvald and is no longer a contested frost-giant objective. Tags: #artifact-repurposed #auril-lineage

* **Spine of the World:** Under direct Sanctum control. Svardborg's arrangement is framed as tenancy and alliance inside that jurisdiction, not a separate sovereign claim against it.

* **Auril — artifact propagation precedent:** The T121–T123 restructuring of Auril propagates to artifacts sourced from her. Old rulings bind new cases; the Ring's T528 reassignment is the confirmed precedent in play.

* **Luskan (The City of Sails):** Visited during the Travel Arc (TR1). The notoriously treacherous eastern harbor currents were permanently corrected and normalized simply by Soliera's passive presence. Ember accidentally released the sealed 4th-floor room of the Host Tower of the Arcane. House Harker experienced a massive political windfall simply due to their proximity to the Girls during their stay.

* **The Giant Steadings (Hill, Frost, Fire):** The *Against the Giants* module was entirely inverted.
  * *Hill Giant Steading:* The steading was transferred intact, not destroyed. Chief Nosnra and forty giants belong to Sera. They continue their ordinary lives without Sanctum mediation under one absolute term: they do not attack humans at all. The visiting cloud giant dignitary departed with intelligence, the orcs were freed, and Yrsa received Grugnur for judgment before returning to her actual clan home.
  * *Glacial Rift:* Cleared via Ember's moral sorting. Jarl Grugnur had his kneecaps removed by Sera and was delivered to the storm giantess Yrsa for absolute justice.
  * *Fire Giant Hall:* Subjected to total military clearance by Sera. King Snurre and Queen Frupy were neutralized. The volcanic hall is now claimed Sanctum territory.

* **Silverymoon (Gem of the North):** Center of the Silver Marches and a vital Sanctum ally. A confrontation with Shar and a conversation with Mystra occurred here. Sera and Soliera are friends with Lady Alustriel Silverhand. They also met Drizzt Do'Urden here — Sera likes him a lot. Teleportation circles link it directly to the Sanctum.
  * *Moonflower Garden:* During their first stay, Sera and Soliera visited a crescent terrace of cultivated moonflowers and Alustriel rejoined them there. The named garden is a Divineverse-specific location built from Silverymoon's established cultivation of moonflowers, not a claim that the proper noun belongs to baseline Forgotten Realms geography.

### BACKGROUND AND INDIRECTLY AFFECTED LOCATIONS

* **Neverwinter (City of Skilled Hands):** A beautiful, rebuilding coastal city. Locals have heard rumors of the "Walking Gods" to the north, sparking both theological awe and political anxiety.
* **Neverwinter Wood / Mithral Hall / Citadel Adbar / Mirabar / The High Forest / Port Llast:** Baseline canon. No Trio intervention. All aware, at varying degrees of distance, that something divine has changed the north.


## II. THE SWORD COAST & WESTERN HEARTLANDS
*The Most Politically Reshaped Region*

### ALTERED LOCATIONS

* **Waterdeep (City of Splendors) — Primary Embassy Hub:** Now effectively a divine-aligned mega-city. Lord Dagult Neverember was healed and blessed. The city's population has nearly doubled due to pilgrims and the absolute safety guaranteed by the Sanctum Embassy. Functions as the diplomatic bridge between mortal politics and divine reality.
  * *Revisit (Avernus Prep):* The Blackstaff Tower (Vajra Safahr) was utilized as a legal consultation room prior to the Trio's descent into Avernus.

* **The Upper Ward (Waterdeep) — Divine Embassy District:** The entire Upper Ward district serves as the main Sanctum embassy and logistics hub. Merchants operate freely, trade flows openly. Educated people share knowledge for pay — tutors and specialists supporting the wider city's function.

* **Undermountain — Sanctum-Linked City:** The ultimate dungeon has been pacified along the Trio's path and has grown far beyond an outpost. Under and around the Yawning Portal, multiple levels and Underdark veins now function as a busy near-city of Sanctum housing, trade, workshops, embassies, and conversation. The Chilling Room remains near the entrance as an exact replica of the Sanctum's Antechamber. The atlas records one established cross-verse portal, on Level 20, where Halaster demonstrated it and the Girls walked into the Mergeverse. Their trade and dialogue began slowly; consult the relevant domain ledgers for later progress.

**Language rule:** Soliera, Sera, Ember, and Niv-Saiffar understand languages instantly and use **Earth Common** as their default shared speech. Most people do not possess that capacity. Only the strongest or most intelligent Sanctum operators can reliably communicate beyond Earth Common without having learned the language; everyone else uses Earth Common, interpreters, or a language they actually know.

* **Greenest (The Greenfields):** Rescued from the Cult of the Dragon, rebuilt, and divinely shielded. The defense was inverted into a flawless rescue operation by Sera.

* **Mere of Dead Men / Castle Naerytar:** Cult stronghold cleared. A Young Black Dragon was brought into Sanctum ranks. Named cult agents killed.

* **Skyreach Castle:** Captured during the Tyranny of Dragons arc. Soliera raised her hand and spiked atmospheric pressure to force the flying fortress out of the sky. Sera shattered Cloudgazer's resistance through sheer attention. The castle was donated to local mortal governance — the Girls had no interest in keeping it.

* **Elturel (The City on the River):** Formerly dragged into Avernus. Sera decided she didn't like the theft, flipped a "jurisdiction flag," and with Soliera's silent approval, the entire city was effortlessly returned intact to the Material Plane. Its souls were not downgraded or retained, and no memory wipe occurred. This was a correction of coerced ownership, not a chain-breaking battle.

* **Candlekeep:** Visited for a planar consultation regarding Avernus. Soliera gave it *Candlelight; For Ember*, an impossible meta-book containing complete multiversal knowledge solely as candle-information. The book later gives rise to the Emberians, a formal religious order whose people, rites, hierarchy, property, and date remain unspecified. Sylvira is the consultation's infernal translator; Traxigor chose restoration beyond his otter form. Candlekeep remains neutral institutional territory, not a Sanctum dependency.

### BACKGROUND AND INDIRECTLY AFFECTED LOCATIONS

* **Baldur's Gate:** Skipped over by the Triad to pursue cult wagons. Massive, sprawling city-state; deeply aware the world's axis has shifted north.
* **Moonshae Isles / Carnath Roadhouse / Boareskyr Bridge:** Baseline canon. No Trio intervention.


## III. THE UNDERDARK
*Subterranean Realities*

### ALTERED LOCATIONS

* **The Cleaved Circle (Underdark Fissure):** A massive geographical anomaly created when Deashi swung his sword to protect Soliera. The strike cleaved a perfect circle through miles of the Underdark, instantly erasing a CR 30 Dracolich threat and altering the subterranean map permanently.

* **Soliere-Dzan (Former Drow City):** Before contact, this was a small, modest city of roughly seven Houses, minor by Underdark standards. Sera never learned its actual name; while walking its streets she substituted "Soliere" for the original drow word, which is therefore unrecorded. Everything that followed — its Soliera-centered worship, Sanctum roads and tunnels, Divine Constructs, House of Soliere rule, restructuring, and rise into a dominant Underdark metropolis — grew downstream of that casual renaming. Soliera finds the resulting worship mildly amusing and treats visiting as a normal Tuesday. Taliandra Zauviir's original place in the 5th House fits the seven-House hierarchy; that house became functionally supreme in the recorded settlement.

* **Eclavdra / House Zauviir divergence:** Published Eclavdra is House Eilservs; Divineverse Eclavdra is House Zauviir. She is the last Zauviir who mattered after the remainder of the line folded into Sanctum orbit after T100. An unnamed non-Xorlarrin Menzoberranzan house hosts her force with walls, forges, and bodies; her authority over its drow, priests, and spiders comes directly from Lolth. The Proto-Forge and Taliandra's transformed but recoverable mother feed the final operation, while Taliandra herself is absent. `DM050_0` owns that operation state; `DM045_DS3` owns Eclavdra's operative runtime.

* **T536 Deep Road outcome:** Doran and Wren followed the Xorlarrin trail without visiting Soliere-Dzan. In a Northdark bolt-hole they found Draac, Taal, and six elite warriors dead simultaneously, without wounds, disturbance, or magical signature. Draac's belt loop was closed and intact but empty. GM truth now closes the hidden cause: Eclavdra killed the eight and removed both Maegera and the intact iron flask. Neither was a party objective after that revelation. The characters did not learn that truth at T536. Menzoberranzan's seal is historical context, not a route.

### BACKGROUND AND INDIRECTLY AFFECTED LOCATIONS

* **Menzoberranzan / Gracklstugh / Blingdenstone / Lluir-Dhûr:** Baseline canon. The Drow city is aware of Soliere-Dzan's new supremacy and is managing its feelings about it.


## IV. THE EASTERN REALMS & HEARTLANDS
*The Site of Divine Retribution*

### ALTERED LOCATIONS

* **Thay (The Broken Nation):** The nation of necromancy was systematically dismantled.
  * *Eltabbar:* The capital was completely annihilated by divine force in retribution for the assassination of Nymmurh (revealed at T334; reckoning executed across T356-T396). Nymmurh is **bronze in fact**; "brass dragon" survives only as in-world reference voice because untrained eyes commonly read his coloring as brass. The temples of Shar, Bane, and Myrkul were deleted.
  * *Faith hemorrhage:* Public divine defeats change the religious map. Shar and Myrkul are faith-damaged after being beaten publicly twice; Bane is aggrieved and starving after Eltabbar and the Sanctum blessing of the Zhentarim. Evil-god power loss through redirected or collapsed faith is a core saga mechanic, not background flavor. Shar's destabilization and Myrkul's broken avatar are what permit Selûne to manifest directly; her presence clarifies rather than dazzles. This consequence is promoted here from DM014_4:468, which is archive-only-superseded and carries no live authority.
  * *Ao / slow-time return:* Ao's intervention and the slow-time return remain an open divine thread. Do not close or explain them by inference.
  * *Thayan Coastal Village:* Site where the impossibly vast and radiant Ark arrived, establishing a forward operating base with instant infrastructure.
  * *The Red Wizard Enclaves:* Power structure utterly shattered; influence and infrastructure decimated.
  * *Established outcome:* Thay is a Divine Client State. Slaves revolted and were liberated. Ruled by the cousins Tirren and Daviya — a pure rags-to-rulers ascension, divinely sanctioned.
  * *Recognition consequence:* A country on the map changed hands and changed kind. This setting fact underlies the campaign-wide fame constant in the Recognition and Named-Figure Removal Law above; local files vary only the version and reaction.

### BACKGROUND AND INDIRECTLY AFFECTED LOCATIONS

* **Cormyr / Suzail:** Baseline canon. Chivalric monarchy watching the north very carefully.
* **The Dalelands / Shadowdale:** Elminster has met the divine Trio. He knows to bow his head and not be his normal rough-around-the-edges self.
* **Zhentil Keep (The Moonsea):** The Zhentarim were blessed at the Mustering Fields with trinkets carrying divine imbued effects. They know who their gods are.
* **Mulmaster / Aglarond / Rashemen:** Baseline canon. No Trio intervention.


## V. THE LANDS OF INTRIGUE & SOUTH
*The Wealthy and Wild Southern Regions*

### BACKGROUND AND INDIRECTLY AFFECTED LOCATIONS

* **Amn / Athkatla:** The gold-obsessed merchant republic remains stable but deeply paranoid about the economic implications of a city (the Sanctum) that requires no money to function perfectly.
* **Tethyr / Calimshan / Calimport:** Vast desert nations; maintaining baseline structure and a wary, respectful distance from the Northern Sword Coast's new deities.
* **Chult / Port Nyanzaru:** Disease-ridden jungles and dinosaur ports. Physically unaffected, but trade routes have shifted as the world's gravity pulls toward Waterdeep and the Spine of the World.
* **Halruaa / The Shaar / The Great Rift / Lantan:** Baseline canon. No Trio intervention.


## VI. WILDERNESS & KEY ADVENTURING SITES

### ALTERED LOCATIONS

* **Misty Forest / Chuth's Lair (T214–T220):** Survivors rescued; Chuth was killed shortly after being found. The area was cured of its pervasive poison. Soliera simply cleared it — leading into Sera jumping around singing praises as everyone was healed and staring in awe.

* **Well of Dragons — Garden of the Flame (T291–T326):** The cult's ritual site for Tiamat's rise. The dragon masks were destroyed save the Obsidian — which made Ember. Soliera, Sera, Deashi, and an army wiped the area. Deashi erased Tiamat after a 5-fold Disintegrate temporarily displaced Sera. Mortals felt Soliera's unshielded presence as "an ocean above the sky." Deashi bowed to newborn Ember so as not to scare her. The Well is now a garden.

### BACKGROUND AND INDIRECTLY AFFECTED LOCATIONS

* **Evermeet / The High Moor / The Hordelands / Kara-Tur / Maztica:** Baseline canon. No Trio intervention. The further reaches of the world are aware something seismic has happened in the North.


## VII. EXTRAPLANAR & DOMAINS OF DREAD
*Where Metaphysics Bow to Divine Domesticity*

### ALTERED LOCATIONS

* **Barovia (Sanctum Protectorate):** Formerly a sealed Domain of Dread. The gothic horror module was dismantled by invasive divine domesticity. The mists bowed to Soliera and were erased. True sunlight was permanently restored. Sera shattered a mountain-sized manifestation of the Dark Powers with her bare fists.
  * *Established outcome:* Strahd von Zarovich is alive, entirely de-cursed, and peacefully reunited with a resurrected Tatyana and Sergei. He rules quietly, knowing he is utterly outmatched. A permanent, open portal to the Sanctum sits in the village square, turning the nightmare realm into a pleasant, divinely guarded valley.

* **Avernus (The Nine Hells) — Administrative Audit:** Treated not as a warzone, but as an administrative violation. The descent was a legal audit.
  * *The River Styx:* Reframed by Soliera as a broken public utility. Soliera placed small suns above it, demonstrating a clear harmless paste and alternate power source, then deliberately rolled the change back to prove scope and reversibility.
  * *Zariel's Fortress:* The Archdevil Zariel fainted from the sheer ontological pressure of the Trio's plane-level authority.
  * *Asmodeus:* Recognized Soliera's kernel authority immediately. The parley read as negotiation from the outside, but functioned as jurisdictional containment: hospitality, seating, and space reorganized around Soliera's authority. Sera offered a favor; Asmodeus used it for clarification, forcing Sera to turn to Soliera rather than introspect beyond her action-function. Soliera deliberately removed the Styx suns, returned Elturel, and healed Asmodeus's true form of its eternal wounds. Asmodeus survived by compliance, not victory, and was neither deposed nor made a Sanctum subordinate.
  * *Other Lords of the Nine:* No separate on-page interventions are logged in the current corpus. Treat them as downstream of the Avernus audit and Hell's new boundary condition: consent is now enforced, infernal contract mechanisms are constraints rather than laws, and Soliera's jurisdiction outranks the system when coercion is exposed.
