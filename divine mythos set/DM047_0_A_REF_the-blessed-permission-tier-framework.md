# DM047_0 — The Blessed — Permission Tier Framework

## PURPOSE

The corpus has no concept of an item tier above 5e's artifact rarity. A
corpus-wide sweep for above-artifact, artifact-tier, supra-artifact,
god-forged, and divine-tier-item language returns zero hits across all DM
files. The category does not exist on disk in any form.

It exists in play, and has for a long time. It is scattered across individual
item entries in four owner files with no shared vocabulary, no membership
criterion, and no owner. That is the gap this file closes.

**This is not a power increase.** Nothing gets stronger. This is a naming and
consolidation pass over objects that already behave this way.


## 1. THE DISCRETIONARY CONFERRAL — [RULED]

#RULE-BLESSED-PERMISSION-TEST

**An object belongs to the Blessed because Soliera gave it that status, either
directly or through Ember or Sera's delegated judgment.** This is permission-only
membership, not a test; who physically made it and every observable property are
irrelevant to admission.

This is a discretionary conferral, not an authorship criterion. It can admit
objects Soliera did not personally forge, but only because she chose to grant
the status or allowed Ember or Sera to grant it on her behalf.

The reference case is Vesper. DM004_2b records it as Niv's craft, with Soliera's
touch on the barrel lattice and "Soliera's permission baked at the rail level."
Under the old permission-test wording Vesper was ambiguous — a mortal engineer's rail gun with
a divine finish. Under the permission test it is a clean member, and the entry
already says why.


## 2. THE CONTINUOUS BLESSING — [RULED]

**Anything made by Soliera or the Sanctum carries her implicit blessing and her
power as source of context and ability.**

The blessing is not a rare stamp applied to a short list of objects. It is
continuous and universal across Sanctum-made things, because the source is the
same in every case. This is why the corpus has always been able to speak of
"blessed equipment" for the Waterdeep Vanguard's civic kit (DM009_0), trinkets
"carrying divine imbued effects" for the Zhentarim at the Mustering Fields
(DM031_0), and Wren's blessed wand, without any of those readings being wrong.

**The Blessed, as a named tier, is the top of that continuum, not a separate
mechanism.** Capitalized and used as a class noun, it names tier membership.
Lowercase "blessed equipment" and "Sanctum-blessed" in existing files carry the
ordinary imbued or faction sense and are not claims of membership. Both are
correct usages of the same underlying truth at different degrees.

**CLOSED — the boundary is arbitrary by design.** Ordinary blessed kit remains
ordinary unless Soliera, Ember, or Sera grants the named tier. The grant can be
deliberate, casual, or made in response to a situation; the object does not
earn it by passing a test. Do not retroactively promote Vanguard kit or
Zhentarim trinkets without such a grant.

> **Guard against collapse.** The corpus already carries one term doing double
> duty — see §4 of this file on "Steward." Do not let "blessed" become a second.
> When precision matters, write *the Blessed* or *Blessed tier* for membership
> and *blessed kit* or *Sanctum-blessed* for the ordinary sense.


## 3. DISCRIMINATORS — [HISTORICAL / RETIRED CONTEXT]

These three once attempted to separate the tier from 5e artifact rarity. They are
Claude-generated historical scaffolding, not operative framework material. **Do not cite them as
law and do not rule from them at the table until Corey locks them.**

### First — these objects grow

A 5e artifact is finished the day it is made. Objects in this tier are
continuous with their bearer and change as the bearer changes.

The corpus already documents this without naming it. At the T537.0 uplift,
Wren's Wand of Fireball became an indestructible Staff of the Magi and her Robe
of Stars became the white-and-gold Robe of the Archmagi carrying the stars, the
Astral door, and a permanent crown of stars. Neither happened by re-crafting.
The objects moved because she did.

### Second — no drawback

5e pays for artifact-grade power with curses, sentience conflicts, and
attunement penalties. These objects have none.

They are gifts. The absence of a price is structural, not an oversight, and it
is not an invitation to supply one later.

### Third — numbers stop deriving

Values on these objects are flat. They do not compute from the bearer's sheet.

This is the tier's mechanical signature, and it retroactively explains several
existing numbers that have read as anomalous: Sunset's DC 30, the Staff of the
Magi's indestructibility, and Wren's ward behaving identically at any range.

**Recording rule (applies regardless of ratification).** Where a tier object's
number is already recorded, store it flat. A flat value stored as a derived one
will drift the first time anyone recalculates from a sheet.


## 4. CALIBRATION FOR THE NAMED TIER — [RULED]

The named Sanctum officers — Letha, Niv, Velin, Korin, Kessla, and the rest of
that band — are **overpowered by design. Their limit is judgment, imagination,
and interpretation, never capability.** Letha can kill heads of state in the
Mergeverse. Vesper is functionally one of the most broken objects in that
universe.

Two guards on this.

**First, "Steward" must not collapse.** The term does double duty in the corpus.
This calibration applies to the *named* tier only. **Doran and Wren are Tier-3
field Stewards, deliberately in the mortal band, and they are where the stakes
are actually paid.** Nothing in this file raises them. Their membership in §5 is
a statement about their gear, not about them.

**Second**, per the 2026-08-03 external-IP shorthand policy, comparative
shorthand from conversation does not go to disk. The plain functional
description above is the canonical form.

## 6. SUNSET — TIER READING

Current mechanics are owned by DM004_2b. This section records only the tier
reading of Sunset's history.

**RULED.** The T32 record stands. DM005_0 line 552 records the grant as a lute
"capable of stacking bardic effects and projecting sound 200-300ft." DM007_1
line 306 records its T101-T120 appearance. **Neither is overwritten.**

**The difference between T32 and T537 is growth, not contradiction.** Sunset
stacking two or three bardic riders at T32 and casting three full spells of any
level by T537 is the tier demonstrating its first discriminator on the record.
This preserves the historical entries intact without converting growth into an
admission criterion.

The alternative reading — that the T32 entry was always an under-description
requiring retroactive correction — was considered and **rejected by Corey on
2026-08-09.** It is closed, not open.


## 7. RELATIONSHIP TO THE LAW OF DIVINE SPECIFICATION

DM042_0 §12 (#RULE-DIVINE-SPECIFICATION) governs objects **directly made by the
divine family**: Soliera, Sera, Ember, Deashi, or their joined action. A made
object is a finite description; omitted variables were never part of the object.

The two laws are adjacent but not coextensive.

- **Divine Specification is an authorship test. The Blessed is a permission
  test.** Vesper is a member of this tier and is *not* divine-family-made — it
  is Niv's craft. §12's specification law does not reach it by that route.
- Most members are covered by both. Doran's plate is divine-family-made and
  Blessed; §12's authoring rule and this file's register both apply.
- **§12's authoring rule survives contact with §3's "no drawback."** Recording
  an object's specification and boundaries *at creation* is required. Inventing
  an omitted variable *after a loss* remains forbidden. The proposed absence of
  drawback is not a claim that these objects have no finite description — it is
  a claim that they carry no curse, no attunement penalty, and no sentience
  conflict.

**FUTURE-OPTIONAL.** Whether tier membership modifies, supersedes, or merely
sits beside §12 can be decided if a live object creates that question. It is
not an active contradiction.

## 9. QUICK CONTRADICTION TESTS

- If an object is admitted to the tier because of who made it rather than
  whether it was permitted, the ruling is wrong.
- If Vesper is excluded because Niv built it, the ruling is wrong.
- If a tier object's number is recalculated from its bearer's sheet, the number
  is wrong.
- If a curse, attunement penalty, or drawback is attached to a member object,
  the ruling is wrong.
- If a specification gap is invented for a member object after a loss, the
  ruling is wrong — see DM042_0 §12.
- If the tier is described as making its members stronger than they already
  were, the reading is wrong.
- If a discriminator in §3 is cited as settled law, the citation is wrong.
- If lowercase "blessed equipment" in an existing file is read as a claim of
  tier membership, the reading is wrong.
- If Doran or Wren are moved out of the Tier-3 mortal band because their gear
  is registered here, the reading is wrong.
- If Korin's or Velin's equipment is treated as a member, the roster is wrong.
